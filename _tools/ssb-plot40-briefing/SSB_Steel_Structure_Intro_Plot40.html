1. Project Context — Where Is Plot 40?

The Big Picture: RSGP at RSHT-2

The Ruwais Sulfur Granulation Plant is a 9,900 TPD liquid sulfur receiving, granulation, storage, and export facility in the Ruwais Downstream Sulfur Complex. The SSB (Sulphur Storage Building) is the core solid-logistics structure — a massive arched space-frame shed housing the stacker/reclaimers and conveyor systems.

  • Type: Arched steel truss space frame — bolted spherical joint system

5. Construction Methodology — Hydraulic Synchronous Cumulative Sliding

Why Sliding?

The SSB (320m long) is assembled in 8 modules on a central scaffold platform, then slid longitudinally into final positions. This method avoids the need for full-length scaffolding and allows the Stacker/Reclaimer lifts to happen concurrently in the cleared end zones.

S/R Portal Lift — Concurrent Operation

  • S/R portal sections shifted into position through the SSB gable access opening (foundation held open)
  • Portal sections fitted-up and welded into a single unit on the ground between travel units
  • Tandem lift: 650T crawler + 400T mobile + 100T mobile — portal rotated to vertical, lifted ~0.5m above head beam, lowered onto bearings
  • Modules 1, 2, 8, 7 temporarily parked in adjacent bays to keep S/R lift zones clear
  • After S/R lifts complete → 2nd sliding of M1/2 (after S/R-B) and M8/7 (after S/R-A) to final positions

Sliding System Details

  • Computer-controlled synchronized hydraulic push system (YS-PP-11 pump source)
  • Sliding jack machines and fixing cables: Contractor-supplied
  • Sliding structure installation & operation: Subcontractor scope
  • Sliding rods replaced with permanent rods after base plate welding
  • Temporary cable fixing at outer nodes over retaining wall during slide-out
  • Non-sliding column base plates at slightly lower elevation; stiffener plates cut & re-welded post-slide
  • Spanwise synchronization tolerance: L/1000, max 30mm

9. Key Points for the Subcontractor

Why This Package Matters

  • Specialized scope: The SSB space frame + sliding methodology is a niche skill set — this isn't general MEI work. It requires experience with large-span space frame structures, hydraulic synchronized sliding, and tandem heavy lifting.
  • Clean interface: The carve-out is designed with a clear boundary — steel structure + equipment only, no E&I field works. Material supply is predominantly Contractor free-issue, minimizing subcontractor procurement risk.
  • Schedule critical: The SSB is on the critical path for the entire RSGP facility. Any delay directly impacts granulation start-up and first sulfur export.

Risk Factors to Consider in Pricing

  • Site congestion: Plot 40 is the most congested spatial corridor (4 packages: CP1 + MEI-I + MEI-II + CP3). Coordinate with adjacent contractors for crane paths, laydown, and access.
  • High ambient temperature: Ruwais summer conditions (40–50°C) require bolt torque coefficient recalibration and heat-stress management
  • Wind constraints: Normal ops ≤10 m/s, suspend high work at 10–15 m/s, full shutdown >15 m/s. Plan for wind downtime in schedule.
  • S/R concurrent operations: Modules 1/2/8/7 must be double-handled (temporary park → 2nd slide). Price this double handling.
  • Heavy crane mobilization: 650T crawler crane logistics in Ruwais require advance planning (ground bearing, access roads, assembly area)
  • Scaffold design liability: Method statement & calculation for the 25m-wide assembly scaffold must be certified by an approved Third Party Inspector (TPI)
