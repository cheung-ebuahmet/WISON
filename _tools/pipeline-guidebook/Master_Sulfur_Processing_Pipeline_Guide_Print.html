🚫 THE SUPREME GATEKEEPER

ADNOC Quality Release Certificate (QRC) / Inspection Release Notice (IRN)

This document is the ultimate commercial choke point. Without ADNOC’s official QRC/IRN sign-off, every downstream milestone billing application and IPC (Interim Payment Certificate) is legally frozen. No QRC = No Payment. No exceptions.

⚠ Documentation gaps, incomplete test packs, or missing owner inspection signatures create a payment blockade that cascades across all Civil and MEI packages simultaneously.

🧊 What happens here: Molten sulfur is pumped onto rotating sprayers inside the granulator units. Dispersed across water-cooled steel belts, it solidifies instantly into dust-free, hard yellow pellets (黄豆硫磺 / "yellow beans"). ⚠ This is the single most critical Civil-to-MEI handover point in the entire pipeline. CP2 must pour and cure the high-strength slabs before MEI-I can bolt down the granulator skids. These units produce intense vibrational dynamic loads — undersized or improperly cured foundations cause equipment misalignment and premature bearing failure.

🧠 What happens here: Unit 10 Core is the central nervous system of the entire sulfur processing pipeline. CP3 delivers 8,092 M³ of blast-resistant concrete for the MCR, substations, and LCRs. These enclosures must pass ADNOC blast-resistance testing before any internal fit-out. MEI-II then executes the HV PP5 cable-pulling network, installs DCS cabinets, transformers, and switchgear.

⚠ Single most consequential dependency in the project. If the civil housing leaks or fails owner blast-testing, all downstream cold-commission workflows remain dark, freezing the entire pipeline regardless of progress at Stages 2–5.
⚠ Critical Interdisciplinary Collision Hotspots
HOTSPOT #1
28,158 M³ CP2 → MEI-I Mechanical Rigging (Stage 3)
Rotary granulator units (Zone 220) generate intense vibrational loads. If CP2 lags in pouring the high-strength slabs, MEI-I cannot bolt down machinery skids, halting the entire midpoint line. Concrete cure time is on the critical path.
HOTSPOT #2
Unit 10 Power CNS (Stages 4 & 5 Power Feed)
CP3 must build blast-resistant shells (8,092 M³) before MEI-II can lay down PP5 transformers and route cables. If the civil housing leaks or fails blast testing, critical logic circuitry stays dark. Single-point failure for all DCS processes.
HOTSPOT #3
Greenfield vs. Brownfield PTW Minefields
Erecting space-frame sheds in the Greenfield is straightforward. But Live-Line Tie-ins (underground piping) cross into operational ADNOC lines. Clauses (c) and (d) insulate the Contractor from standby losses when hot-work restrictions are imposed.
HOTSPOT #4
Unit 30/50 Corridor — Triple-Package Collision Zone
The most congested lane on site: CP2 (Red) + MEI-I (Green) + MEI-II (Amber) overlap spatially at the Unit 30/50 interface. Crane paths, cable trays, and pipe racks all compete for the same physical space. Clause (c) mandates unconditional adherence to Contractor unified scheduling.

(c) 机电工程界面重叠与施工协调

(c) 机电工程界面重叠与施工协调：分包商明确知悉并同意，MEI Package I（含 Unit 30/40/50 之硫磺造粒及固体硫磺输送系统）与 MEI Package II（含 Unit 10/10A/20/50 之变电站、LCR 及高压电缆工程）在地理空间与施工工序上存在高度重叠与交叉作业。分包商须无条件服从承包商对上述两个机电标段的统一调度、施工降效协调及现场交接安排。分包商确认，任何因 Package I 与 Package II 施工干扰、工序冲突或工作面交接引发的现场降效，均已完全包含在合同总价中，分包商无权向承包商提出任何额外的费用索赔或工期延长。
English (Official Translation)

(c) MEI Interface Overlap and Construction Coordination

(c) MEI Interface Overlap and Construction Coordination: The Subcontractor expressly acknowledges and agrees that there exists a high degree of spatial and sequential overlap and concurrent operations between MEI Package I (including the sulfur granulation and solid sulfur handling systems across Units 30/40/50) and MEI Package II (including the substation, LCR, and HV cable works across Units 10/10A/20/50). The Subcontractor shall unconditionally adhere to the Contractor’s unified scheduling, construction inefficiency mitigation, and site handover arrangements for both MEI packages. The Subcontractor confirms that any and all site inefficiencies resulting from construction interference, sequence conflicts, or work-front handovers between Package I and Package II have been fully priced into the Subcontract Price, and the Subcontractor shall have no right to advance any additional financial claims or extensions of time against the Contractor.

(d) 装置界区内地下管道碰口（Tie-in）及主控制室（MCR）改造之棕地高危免责：分包商在此确认并同意，所有涉及现有装置区域内的地下管道碰口、MCR 改造及高压电缆拉线等工作均属于棕地项目（Brownfield），具有极高的工业安全风险。分包商须严格遵守阿联酋本地法规及业主（ADNOC）的 PTW（工作许可证）安全审批程序。承包商对因 ADNOC 业主 PTW 审批延误、现场气体超标停工、动火管控或其他出于棕地安全合规原因导致的任何工期滞后及停工损失不承担任何法律与经济责任；该等滞后由分包商自行承担，且不构成本合同项下的可原谅延误。
English (Official Translation)

(d) Exemption of Liability for High-Risk Brownfield Tie-ins and MCR Modifications

(d) Exemption of Liability for High-Risk Brownfield Tie-ins and Main Control Room (MCR) Modifications: The Subcontractor hereby acknowledges and agrees that all works involving underground piping tie-ins within the existing battery limits, MCR modifications, and HV cable pulling constitute Brownfield operations, which carry extreme industrial safety risks. The Subcontractor shall strictly comply with all UAE local regulations and the Owner’s (ADNOC) Permit to Work (PTW) safety clearance procedures. The Contractor shall bear no legal or financial liability whatsoever for any schedule delays or standby losses resulting from ADNOC’s PTW approval latency, gas-exceedance work stoppages on-site, hot-work restrictions, or any other brownfield safety compliance measures; all such delays shall be borne solely by the Subcontractor and shall not constitute an excusable delay under this Subcontract.
🏗 Greenfield vs. Brownfield — Boundary Classification

✅ Greenfield Sector

• Area within designated new development battery limits
• Unobstructed footprint, minimal asset interference
• Clean, straightforward construction workflow
• Covers: Tank foundations, granulator slabs, conveyor A-frames, space-frame sheds
• Stages 2–4 main works fall primarily in Greenfield

⚠ Brownfield High-Risk Sector

• Area intersecting or outside green lines within existing operational refinery
• Includes: MCR modifications, PP5 HV connections, live-line pipe tie-ins
• Full ADNOC PTW compliance mandatory (hot-work permits, gas monitoring, clearances)
• Affects: Stage 1 entry manifold, Stage 5 marine terminal, Unit 10 MCR tie-ins
• All PTW delays are Subcontractor risk under Clause (d)
