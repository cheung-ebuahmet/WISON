PURPOSE 
The purpose of this EXHIBIT is to provide an outline summary of the scope of 
WORK and to list the main documents within the AGREEMENT which more fully 
detail such scope.

SUMMARY SCOPE OF WORK 
The following outlines and summarises the scope of WORK. COMPANY’s 
specific requirements are as specified in and throughout the AGREEMENT. 
COMPANY emphasises that this is a summary and is not a definitive scope.

PROJECT MANAGEMENT AND GENERAL 
• 
PROJECT Management including but not limited to management, control and 
direction of surveys, design and engineering, procurement, construction, 
MECHANICAL COMPLETION, pre-commissioning, COMMISSIONING, 
TEST RUN, etc. 
•

Consultants and LICENSORS, including those novated/assigned by 
COMPANY 
•

in particular, with other contractors executing related projects of COMPANY 
or its AFFILIATES, other contractors working at or near the SITE, operators 
of EXISTING FACILITIES, local authorities, COMPANY, PMC, etc. 
• 
All required duties, taxes, charges, levies, etc.  
• 
All required bank guarantees and bank charges 
• 
All deposits and securities required by local authorities 
• 
All insurances except insurances arranged by COMPANY viz. Construction 
All-risk which shall also include transit insurance and THIRD PARTY liability 
• 
Lodging and follow-up of insurance claims including claims under insurances 
arranged by COMPANY 
• 
Facilities for COMPANY and its PMC team in all engineering and design 
offices including fully fitted-out offices, support staff, utilities, computers, 
printing, communications, data links, refreshments etc.  Assistance with 
visas, accommodation, travel, local transportation, medical etc. 
• 
All required information handover, including electronic data, as specified by 
COMPANY. 
• 
Provision of all notices as required by authority(ies) having jurisdiction
