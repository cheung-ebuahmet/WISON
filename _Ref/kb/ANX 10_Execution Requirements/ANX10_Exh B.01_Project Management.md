B.1 
PROJECT MANAGEMENT 
CONTRACTOR shall provide all management services necessary for proper 
execution of the WORK in order to ensure that the WORK is completed on 
SCHEDULE and in strict compliance with the requirements of the AGREEMENT. 
This shall include but not be limited to: 
 
a) Deploy the right quality and number of personnel, construction plant & 
equipment and other resources in a timely manner in accordance with the 
requirements of the WORK.  
 
b) Prepare and submit for COMPANY’s approval CONTRACTOR’s Organisation 
Charts and Staffing Plans identifying by name/grade all key personnel proposed 
to be deployed on the PROJECT. Provide appropriate replacements to key 
personnel leaving the employment of CONTRACTOR allowing for appropriate 
overlap time for proper handing over of responsibilities. 
 
c) Take necessary corrective action to align the WORK and the PROJECT back 
on track in case of deviations from plans (cost, schedule, quality, HSE, 
performance, operability, maintainability, etc.) including mobilising additional 
resources as required. 
 
d) Lead 
regular 
progress 
review 
meetings 
with 
COMPANY, 
SUBCONTRACTORS, VENDORS, LICENSORS and others to review 
progress, identify problem areas and initiate corrective action. Make 
presentations to COMPANY Management on PROJECT status, as required in 
Abu Dhabi or at locations where the WORK is performed. 
 
e) Prepare Daily, Weekly, Fortnightly and Monthly Reports specifically for 
COMPANY in accordance with the requirements specified elsewhere in the 
AGREEMENT. 
 
f) Ensure that SUBCONTRACTORS, VENDORS and LICENSORS, including 
those novated/assigned by COMPANY, comply with their obligations in 
accordance with the AGREEMENT.  
 
g) Maintain record of all notices, correspondence, reports, documents, etc. during 
the course of execution of the WORK. Ensure that the PROJECT 
DOCUMENTS are maintained for the duration stipulated in the Articles of 
AGREEMENT. 
 
h) Prepare a complete set of Management, Co-ordination, Execution, Quality 
System and HSE procedures for approval by COMPANY, and ensure that the 
approved procedures are complied with by all concerned.
