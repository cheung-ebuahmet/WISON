B.41.1 DOCUMENT AND DRAWING FORMAT PROCEDURE
Document and Drawing Format Procedure shall be developed by
CONTRACTOR and submitted to COMPANY for approval.
Document and Drawing Format Procedure to meet AGREEMENT requirements
shall be developed based on COMPANY standards TE-PR-043- Asset
Information handover procedure, TE-PR-042 Computer Aided Drafting (CAD)
Procedure, TT-ST-001 Engineering Data Deliverable Standard and TE-ST-006
SmartPlant Data Deliverable Standard by CONTRACTOR and submitted to
COMPANY for approval, by CONTRACTOR. Refer to EXHIBIT - B.41
Attachment for the above-mentioned COMPANY documents.
CONTRACTOR shall update into SmartPlant Database of existing facility in
ADNOC Gas Site office for Tie In connections. Details execution strategy to be
finalized after kick off meeting.
CONTRACTOR shall note that, if early design documents deliverable not
developed as per ADNOC Gas standards and procedure specified in above, then
it is the responsibility of EPC CONTRACTOR to comply and deliver all the EPC
projects asset information as per above standards and procedure without cost
impact.
