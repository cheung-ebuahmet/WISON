B.3.1 
GENERAL 
CONTRACTOR shall perform construction and COMMISSIONING planning 
services to ensure development of an optimum overall construction and 
COMMISSIONING plan and procedures as per CONTRACTOR’s scope of work so 
as to facilitate a seamless transition of WORK to the field. 
Upon award of CONTRACT, CONTRACTOR shall assign its Project Construction 
Manager, COMMISSIONING Manager, along with senior construction and 
COMMISSIONING staff to CONTRACTOR’s design office to perform the Home 
Office CONSTRUCTION and COMMISSIONING activities specified in this section.  
All personnel assigned to these activities shall be from CONTRACTOR’s own long-
term staff and approved by COMPANY for the positions nominated for performance 
of the SITE work.   
B.3.2 
HOME OFFICE CONSTRUCTION PLANNING SERVICES  
Home Office Construction Planning Services shall include but not be limited to the 
following:  
• 
Ensuring that the project schedule for detailed Engineering, Procurement and 
Construction 
is 
aligned 
with 
the 
Project 
CONSTRUCTION 
and 
COMMISSIONING Plan. 
• 
Provision of qualified construction personnel for construction planning. 
• 
Preparation of field Organisation Chart for the construction supervision services 
together with job description for each supervisory post. 
• 
Submission of names and C.V.s of candidates up to Superintendent/Engineers 
level for field post for COMPANY’S approval. 
• 
Planning all TEMPORARY WORKS including construction utilities, which 
includes Identifying and supplying in spreadsheet format, estimated Power 
requirements, water requirements and waste management requirements for 
construction.  These requirements are to be defined by each phase of 
construction from start to completion as well as utility requirements for camp, 
construction offices, storage facilities and other facilities.  This spreadsheet is 
to be supplied to COMPANY within 30 days of EFFECTIVE DATE.  Provision of 
all construction utilities is the responsibility of CONTRACTOR. 
• 
TEMPORARY WORKS will also include planning laydown yards and storage 
facilities, camps, construction offices and other facilities required for the 
construction phase of the PROJECT.

B.3.5 
RIGGING STUDIES  
CONTRACTOR shall perform rigging studies through review of available PROJECT 
SPECIFICATIONS, drawings and documents, i.e. plot plans, equipment location, 
structural and civil design and VENDOR drawings.  CONTRACTOR shall submit its 
Rigging Studies for COMPANY approval.  The Rigging Studies shall include the 
following:  
• 
Criteria used by CONTRACTOR to define Heavy Lifts. 
• 
Detailed list of all Heavy Lifts. 
• 
Description of special equipment and rigging needs. 
• 
Rigging plan for each heavy lift. 
• 
Type, rating, and anticipated duration for all lifting equipment. 
• 
Identification and special procedures for lifts over existing piping and 
equipment. 
• 
Heavy lift equipment durations incorporated into the overall project construction 
schedule. 
• 
For detailed rigging requirements refer to EXHIBIT B.22, “Transport and Heavy 
Lift Operations”. 
B.3.6 
TRANSPORT STUDIES 
CONTRACTOR shall determine the requirements of the PROJECT for the 
transportation of heavy or oversized equipment, including modules if proposed, and 
other materials to SITE.  Transport studies shall be completed for each piece of 
heavy or oversized equipment, module, or other materials that cannot be 
transported to SITE by conventional and locally available means of transportation.  
For detailed heavy transport requirements refer to EXHIBIT B.22, “Transport and 
Heavy Lift Operations”.
