B.17.1  
GENERAL  
 
CONTRACTOR shall perform source inspection on materials and equipment 
purchased for incorporation in the WORK. The inspections shall be planned, 
performed, and documented in accordance with EXHIBIT B.10., CONTRACTOR’S 
established inspection procedures, and the following co-ordination requirements. 
 
B.17.2  
CO-ORDINATION  
 
CONTRACTOR shall prepare anticipated weekly testing programs providing 
COMPANY and THIRD PARTY Inspection Agency sufficient advance notice for 
their participation. The notice period shall be fifteen (15) days for testing and 
inspections.  Should any postponement become necessary, CONTRACTOR shall 
provide written notification of such postponement at least forty-eight (48) hours 
prior to the originally scheduled date of testing and inspections for locations within 
the UAE and at least seven (7) days prior to the originally scheduled date of 
testing and inspections for locations outside the UAE. COMPANY shall give 
CONTRACTOR 48 hours’ notice in writing of its intention to attend the test or ask 
for a postponement of not more than 48 hours, if required. 
 
VENDOR shall notify, in writing, CONTRACTOR with parallel copy to COMPANY 
at least 5 working days in advance for all inspection “Witness” and “Hold” points on 
the approved VENDOR inspection and test plan. CONTRACTOR shall be 
responsible for VENDOR understanding and implementation of this requirement. 
 
B.17.3  
RADIOGRAPHY AND NDE WORKS 
 
Radiography and NDE works shall be performed in compliance with the PROJECT 
SPECIFICATIONS. 
 
B.17.4  
INSPECTION GUIDELINES 
 
Inspection intervention points (e.g. “Witness”, “Hold”) for designated inspections 
and tests shall be determined by CONTRACTOR and approved by COMPANY 
based on VENDOR inspection and test plans (refer Exhibit B.10) and in 
accordance with PROJECT SPECIFICATION requirements and the approved 
Class of Inspection.  Provision shall be made for inspection participation by 
COMPANY and an independent THIRD PARTY Inspection Agency directly 
reporting and responsible to COMPANY. 
 
• 
Any inspecting or testing of any part of the GOODS at any place where it is 
located or in preparation which is required under the SPECIFICATIONS 
and Codes and Standards specified in AGREEMENT, shall be the 
responsibility of CONTRACTOR.

COMPANY shall be entitled at all reasonable times during manufacture to 
inspect and witness test on VENDORS’ and SUBVENDORS’ premises, the 
materials and workmanship of the GOODS as far as they are subject to 
inspecting and testing as specified in AGREEMENT.  Such inspection 
and/or testing shall not release the CONTRACTOR from any obligation 
under this AGREEMENT. 
 
• 
COMPANY may appoint an independent inspection authority for carrying 
out such inspection and witnessing of tests. 
 
• 
CONTRACTOR and/or VENDOR shall provide all necessary equipment, 
access, labour, instruments and reference documents/drawings to enable 
the carrying out of such inspection and testing. 
 
• 
If COMPANY desires to re-inspect any portion of the WORKS, 
CONTRACTOR and VENDOR/ SUB-VENDOR shall provide all necessary 
assistance and access.  If such inspection confirms the results of 
CONTRACTORS inspection the cost of this re-inspection shall be to 
COMPANY’S account.  If this re-inspection demonstrates any error or 
omission in CONTRACTOR’S inspection the total cost shall be to 
CONTRACTOR’S account. 
 
• 
Should any part of the materials and equipment inspected or tested not 
comply with the SPECIFICATIONS or sound engineering practice, 
COMPANY shall notify CONTRACTOR and CONTRACTOR shall rectify 
and remove the causes of such objections and inspection or testing shall 
be repeated.  Rejected or objected material, equipment or workmanship 
shall not release CONTRACTOR from any obligation under this 
AGREEMENT.

"Class of Inspection".  In such cases, the higher class of inspection shall

B.17.5  
CLASS OF INSPECTION 
 
The designated Class of Inspection for each item of equipment or materials shall 
be determined by CONTRACTOR based on the requirements of PROJECT 
SPECIFICATION for “Minimum Shop Inspection and Certification Requirements” 
included in EXHIBIT C & EXHIBIT B.10 “Quality Management Requirements” for 
COMPANY review and approval. 
 
B.17.6  
SUGGESTED CLASS OF INSPECTION 
 
The actual class of inspection assigned to a specific piece of equipment or type of 
bulk materials shall be determined by the CONTRACTOR, approved by 
COMPANY, and shall take into consideration the supplier's quality system and 
quality history, the equipment design maturity, and the maturity of the 
manufacturing processes, the type, use, and characteristics of the equipment, 
subject to the minimum class of Inspection requirements given in EXHIBIT B.10 
“Quality Management Requirements” and in PROJECT SPECIFICATION for 
“Minimum Shop Inspection and Certification Requirements” included in EXHIBIT C 
and EXHIBIT B.10. 
 
B.17.7  
INSPECTION REQUIREMENTS 
 
PROJECT SPECIFICATION for “Minimum Shop Inspection and Certification 
Requirements” included in EXHIBIT C and EXHIBIT B.10 “Quality Management 
Requirements” shows COMPANY’s Minimum Inspection Requirements for the 
PROJECT.  CONTRACTOR shall ensure that its inspection procedures and plans 
comply with these requirements.
