B.53 
MECHANICAL COMPLETION AND COMMISSIONING 
SERVICES 
 
MECHANICAL Completion and Commissioning services include in general but 
are not limited to the following:  
• 
Carry out planning activities and prepare detailed schedules for 
Mechanical Completion and Commissioning of the Systems and the 
Installation. The schedules shall take into consideration any interface with 
Existing Facilities, if any, and Company’s other related projects. The 
detailed schedules shall be subject to Company’s approval. 
• 
Plan and mobilize a suitably experienced and qualified team or teams of 
personnel for Mechanical Completion and Commissioning. Coordinate 
with Sub-Contractors, Vendors and Licensors, and ensure that their 
personnel and representatives are available at SITE in order to assist 
and/or 
supervise 
in 
installation, 
Mechanical 
Completion 
and 
Commissioning activities, wherever necessary. The EPC Contractor shall 
provide adequate supervision of any Sub-Contractors, Vendors and 
Licensors to ensure that all activities are carried out in accordance with 
good engineering practices, construction techniques and relevant 
standards. 
• 
Ensure availability of requisite spare parts, consumables, tools, testing 
instruments, calibration devices, testing facilities, etc. at SITE prior to 
commencement of Mechanical Completion and Commissioning activities. 
• 
Develop and implement coordination procedures between the teams 
working on various SYSTEMS and with Company’s operations personnel, 
such that the Mechanical Completion and Commissioning is achieved in a 
phased manner in the order in which the Systems are to be taken into 
operation. 
• 
Develop and implement procedures, checklists and forms necessary for 
Mechanical Completion and Commissioning. 
• 
Develop and submit for Company approval detailed Mechanical 
Completion manual and Commissioning manual. The manuals shall cover 
all work including without limitation, construction, inspection, testing, pre-
commissioning, training and Commissioning, and shall also include the 
necessary checklists and inspection forms. All records of Mechanical 
Completion and Commissioning activities shall be filed in the respective 
manuals. 
• 
Perform SITE HAZOP. Same as "Pre-Startup HAZOP" specified in 
EXHIBIT B.32.

B.53.3 
FLUSHING, PURGING AND LEAK TESTING 
 
EPC CONTRACTOR shall prepare and submit specific procedures for the 
execution of purging and flushing activities and leak testing in compliance with 
EXHIBIT B.38 “Acceptance”. 
 
COMPANY policy requires that all lines in Sulphur, Water, Nitrogen, or 
Hydrocarbon Service (Service Material) are to be leak tested at operating 
pressure prior to the introduction of the Service Material. 
 
EPC Contractor shall develop procedures for leak testing after Service Materials 
have been introduced.  Procedures may include, but not be limited to, leak 
testing with portable testing equipment during stroke testing of valves to ensure 
that glands are not leaking, testing at gland seals of pumps and compressors 
during operation, or at other locations where Service Materials may escape 
owing to wear or poor adjustment of any item of equipment. EPC Contractor shall 
develop post-start-up check lists to record all areas where leak testing will be 
undertaken. 
 
EPC CONTRACTOR shall also comply with Company policy for the Project 
which prohibits the introduction of hazardous substances before Mechanical 
Completion for any System / Installation.  The introduction of hazardous 
substances at Mechanical Completion for any System / Installation will require 
full Company Permit to Work system to be implemented. 
 
EPC Contractor shall note that hazardous substances shall include all chemicals, 
hydrocarbons, inert gases, steam, high pressure water or oil systems and any 
other substances that constitute a danger to personnel or equipment.
