1. 
INTRODUCTION 
As-built drawings/documents include all changes made during construction/commissioning phases of 
the projects. As-built drawings/documents are maintained as MASTER drawings/documents and are 
used as main source of information for plant Operation/ Maintenance activities as well as for future 
projects development. 
It is mandatory to comply with this procedure for handling, controlling & maintaining of As-built 
drawings/ documents, which are considered COMPANY business critical documents. 
This procedure supersedes EPR_SVP (T)/0020 procedure and is applicable for ADNOC Gas Processing 
Projects as this is correlated with the PCR close-out process. The As-built process shall be a controlled 
activity subjected to periodical audits, to ensure accuracy of project handover documentation and/or 
MASTER documentation. 
 
2. 
PURPOSE 
The purpose of this procedure is to establish a uniform approach for the preparation & handling of As-
built documentation of COMPANY’s operating facilities to accurately reflect the actual 
configuration/design of the plant’s assets, equipment, structures etc.  
As part of Process Safety Management and to ensure Integrity of ADNOC Gas Processing plants & 
pipelines, this procedure has been developed to address the following: 
 Handover of accurate/up-to-date project As-built documentation from Contractors and 
Vendors (mainly received Projects). 
 Periodic update of MASTER Documentation by sites to reflect changes that occurred during 
plant modifications, implemented by Projects, with the support from Consultants if required. 
 To verify the correctness of updates in P&IDs, Drawings and Documents" physically at site.

5. 
AS-BUILT REQUIREMENTS 
The process of amending drawings to As-built status is a progressive activity from the start of 
construction through to commissioning/start-up and final handover. At the time of mechanical 
completion and before the commissioning/ start-up phase, appropriate As-built drawings (including 
Vendor drawings) shall be made available to the Commissioning/Start-up team.  These may be either 
the final As-built drawings or redline marked-up drawings, which reflect the actual configuration of 
plant facilities and equipment. 
The following requirements shall be considered during the As-built process and are applicable for all 
Projects: 
 
All changes to AFC drawings/documents shall obtain prior approval from COMPANY through 
DCA’s & Field modification requests. 
 
CONTRACTOR shall submit REDLINE MARK-UP register listing all drawings/documents to be 
marked-up for review/approval of the COMPANY. AFC documentation register shall be used as 
the basis for REDLINE MARK-UP register. AFC drawings/documents which are not to be 
modified shall be deleted from the list. 
 
CONTRACTOR shall prepare & submit REDLINE MARK-UP drawings as construction progresses 
and keep track of the changes to ensure that these activities are not delayed till the end of the 
project. CONTRACTOR shall coordinate with COMPANY to ensure the delivery of the complete 
REDLINE MARK-UP package prior to commissioning. 
 
Modification to underground facilities (piping, civil, electrical/instrument cables, etc.) shall be 
recorded correctly before back-filling. Existing underground facilities which are not shown on 
AFC drawings, but are noticed during excavation (location, size etc.) shall also be marked.  
 
REDLINE MARK-UP preparation shall be periodically checked to ensure that construction 
modifications are incorporated in the AFC drawings/documents. 
 
All drawings/documents which have REDLINE MARK-UP shall be re-issued under a new As-built 
revision which will be indicated in the title block. The drawings/documents for which no 
modification has been made will retain last status but shall be stamped “As-built” to reflect the 
final issue, all revision clouds to be removed in the final As-Built drawings. 
 
The final As Built SmartPlant model and published SmartPlant Foundation based on as-built 
drawings/ documents, shall be submitted preferably before demobilization of the Contractor’s 
team. This activity should comply with TT-ST-001 and TT-ST-002 standards.

6. 
GUIDELINES FOR REDLINE MARK-UP DRAWINGS  
 
Preparation of REDLINE MARK-UP shall be carried out on the latest issue of AFC 
drawings/documents. 
 
REDLINE MARK-UP drawings shall record the changes that have occurred during the construction 
and are done manually or electronically. 
 
During the construction phase of the project, CONTRACTOR may issue DCA / Field modification 
requests which will impact the AFC drawings/documents. These changes shall be reflected as 
REDLINE MARK-UP or revisions. 
 
All site / pipeline changes during construction shall be recorded and a copy of DCA / Field 
modification requests must be attached with the REDLINE MARK-UP drawings. 
 
At the project commissioning stage, the REDLINE MARK-UPs shall be handed over to the COMPANY 
focal point for start-up and trouble–shooting purpose. 
 
Modifications implemented during start-up/commissioning activities, shall also be recorded as 
REDLINE MARK-UPs. 
 
After handover of REDLINE MARK-UP drawings to the Company and after preparation of final As 
Built drawings/documents and design calculations, Contractor to update the SmartPlant Model (all 
the disciplines SP3D, SPP&ID, SPI & SPEL) and complete publishing activity on SmartPlant 
Foundation. This activity should comply with TT-ST-001 and TT-ST-002 standards. 
 
CONTRACTOR shall also arrange with COMPANY to take photos for Existing underground facilities 
which are not shown on AFC drawings, but are noticed during excavation to have an evidence for 
the REDLINE MARK-UP

8. 
UPDATING OF MASTER DOCUMENTATION ORIGINALS 
The MASTER Documentation shall be regularly updated to incorporate As-built changes after 
completion of new projects. Site Documentation Owner should decide on updating of MASTER 
documentation, either in-house or through a SmartPlant approved Consultant after the receipt of 
project As-built documentation. 
 
On receipt of project As-built documentation, Site Documentation Owner shall carryout a 
random check throughout the package to verify that all REDLINE MARK-UP’s are incorporated 
in the As-built documentation.  
 
Site Documentation Owner to ensure that As-built documentation submitted by the 
CONTRACTOR and updating of MASTER Documentation conforms to latest COMPANY formats 
as mentioned in TT-ST-001.  
 
Site Documentation Owner shall check/assess on the volume of additional work between 
MASTER Documentation Originals and Project As-built drawings; 
o Against one project drawing there may be more than one MASTER drawing to be 
updated/prepared. 
o Simultaneous modifications carried out on one area by many CONTRACTORs for 
different projects. 
 
For new project drawings/documents where MASTER Documentation does not exist, the project 
As-built drawing shall be adopted and filed as MASTER Documentation after removing the 
project details (revision history, project code, Contractor’s name, etc.). Where MASTER 
Documentation does exist, the MASTER Documentation Original shall be updated to reflect the 
As-built status, while the project As-built drawing shall be superseded. 
 
When updating, through a SmartPlant approved Consultant (if required and as the case may 
be), the Master Documentation control Register may be used by the Consultant to identify the 
Master Documentation Originals to be updated. The marked-up Master Documentation control 
Register shall clearly show the new MASTER Documentation and the drawings/documents to be 
superseded. 
 
Site Documentation Owner shall check the Master Documentation and control Register 
submitted by the SmartPlant approved Consultant to ensure that project As-built 
documentation is correctly incorporated as per COMPANY procedures. 
 
SmartPlant approved Consultant shall submit MASTER Documentation native files (and 
superseded drawings) along with the project As-Built native files (& hardcopies) to Site 
Document Controller.
