B.51 DOCUMENT CONTROL
CONTRACTOR'S documentation control program and electronic document
management system (EDMS) for this PROJECT shall be submitted to
COMPANY for approval. In addition, CONTRACTOR shall:
e Maintain up-to-date record of drawings and document status and make
regular issue of revisions to all concerned including the
SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS.
e Maintain drawings and documents register on electronic file as established
by Home Office and record all receipts issues and field revisions.
e Maintain and store all drawings and other documents in good condition until
handover to COMPANY.
e Compile handover documentation and record, including electronic files, for
hand over to COMPANY.
COMPANY shall have read-only access, in COMPANY PROJECT Offices, to
CONTRACTOR'S document control system. However, PROJECT DOCUMENTS
shall be stored both in scanned version as well as in soft copy (native and
editable version) for COMPANY'·s review and approval.
CONTRACTOR·s Document Control! Plan shall describe document control on
SITE and include the following:
e Document control group
e Receipt and verification against transmittals
e Management of controlled documents
e Control of interim change documents
e Removal of superseded or voided documents from the workplace
e Document audits
e Notification of document receipt
e Document distribution
e Field document register
e VENDOR and SUBCONTRACTOR Submittals
