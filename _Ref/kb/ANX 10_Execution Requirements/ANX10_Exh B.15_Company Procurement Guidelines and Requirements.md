B.15.1  
INTRODUCTION 
B.15.1.1 
CONTRACTOR shall comply with COMPANY’s procurement guidelines and 
requirements in the performance of procurement services. Changes / revisions / 
modifications considered beneficial or in COMPANY’S interest shall only be 
made with COMPANY’S prior written approval. 
B.15.1.2 
Immediately after the EFFECTIVE DATE, CONTRACTOR shall mobilize a 
COMPANY approved Project Procurement Manager (hereinafter referred to as 
PPM) for planning and execution of all PROJECT procurement activities.  
B.15.1.3 
PPM shall report to CONTRACTOR’S Project Manager and shall maintain a 
close liaison with COMPANY counterpart. PPM shall be responsible for 
organizing, supervising, monitoring and controlling all procurement activities on 
the PROJECT in a professional and efficient manner. 
B.15.1.4 
All procurement documents submitted to COMPANY for approval shall be routed 
through CONTRACTOR’S PPM. CONTRACTOR’S PPM shall ensure that bid 
validity is sufficient to cover the period required to obtain COMPANY approval of 
an award recommendation. For the purposes of this EXHIBIT, the terms 
VENDOR and SUPPLIER are interchangeable. 
 
B.15.1.5 
The requirements of this EXHIBIT are applicable for all the GOODS to be 
procured for the subject PROJECT irrespective of if it is procured directly by 
CONTRACTOR or through its SUBCONTRACTOR. 
B.15.2  
PURCHASING REQUIREMENTS 
B.15.2.1 
Purchasing Policy 
 
It is the policy of COMPANY to require CONTRACTOR to purchase or obtain all 
categories of PROJECT materials and services from sources who comply with 
the requirements stipulated in EXHIBIT B.16.  
Consistent with the above policy, and to the extent they are competitive and 
meet PROJECT schedule and quality requirements, maximum use shall be 
made of Abu Dhabi / U.A.E. supply sources. 
B.15.2.2 
Prequalification of VENDORS 
 
CONTRACTOR shall utilize a pre-qualification procedure, consistent with the 
guidelines set forth in EXHIBIT B.16, in the selection of potential VENDORS, 
wherever not already pre-qualified by COMPANY. This procedure shall include 
all the elements defined within this EXHIBIT with special attention to the 
technical ability, quality system, reference list, capability, capacity and 
compliance of the full requirements of the scope of work anticipated.

Purpose 
 
CONTRACTOR shall develop a detailed Expediting Procedure for COMPANY 
review and approval which shall be followed by CONTRACTOR to ensure that all 
PROJECT equipment and materials ordered on various VENDORS are delivered 
as per contractual delivery dates specified in the relevant Purchase Orders. The 
Expediting Procedure shall permit timely distribution of information (including to 
the COMPANY) ensuring that only the latest information regarding the status of 
equipment and materials is used for interdisciplinary engineering and 
construction decisions.

CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for planning and directing all efforts and 
resources necessary to achieve all expediting efforts required for improving upon 
the contractual delivery dates. All expediting efforts by CONTRACTOR in 
connection with Purchase Orders shall not relieve CONTRACTOR from its 
contractual responsibilities concerning the overall PROJECT SCHEDULE.

1.10.2 
Mechanical Stress 
Since mechanical stress differs, the case for the equipment may have a lighter 
structure. 
However, it is necessary that, after the discharging of equipment out of 
containers, their packaging and packing be appropriate so as to protect 
equipment on site during the storage period, as required in paragraph 1 of this 
Appendix 3/12/1. 
 
1.10.3 
Wedging Stress 
The packer is responsible for stowage inside the container and shall take all 
precautions to ensure that the equipment cases are completely immobilised and 
that correct stowage is performed. 
ISO standards (containers) regulate outer dimensions.  It is thus important in all 
circumstances to consult with the transporters with regard to working 
dimensions, as these can vary considerably with container design. 
In addition to above mentioned specifications regarding containerisation packing, 
the following rules will have to be applied. 
 
1.10.4 
Packing Prior to Placing Into the Containers 
CONTRACTOR/VENDOR shall provide efficient physical and chemical 
protection, as well as protection against mechanical damage.  Depending on 
type, shape, dimensions and weight of the material, such mechanical protection 
may be made with a crate, a watertight cardboard case, appropriate handling, or 
packing on supports. Physical and chemical protection may be either by 
application of varnish and waterproofing, or by a sealed covering.  
CONTRACTOR/VENDOR is notified that condensation produces about 80% 
relative humidity in a container.

Packing in Containers Prior to Loading

•
