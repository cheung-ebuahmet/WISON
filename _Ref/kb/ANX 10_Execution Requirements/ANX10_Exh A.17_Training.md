A.17.1 
TRAINING OVERVIEW 
 
EPC Contractor shall provide training to Company personnel during the execution 
of the Project in accordance with the provisions of this EXHIBIT A.17 and EXHIBIT 
B.54.   
 
EPC Contractor shall provide technical training courses to Company personnel in 
order to provide them the necessary level of knowledge in respect of the process 
and utility facilities, specialist systems and equipment. A list of such courses is 
included in EXHIBIT B.54. In addition, EPC Contractor shall provide any other 
training requirement covered in the relevant design general specifications. 
 
EPC Contractor’s Senior Personnel shall also make presentations at various 
stages of the Project which will provide Company Management, Engineering, 
Operations and Maintenance personnel details of the design, installation and 
maintenance of all Systems included within the new Project facilities and where 
necessary how they are integrated with Existing Facilities.  
 
A.17.2 
TRAINING OBJECTIVES  
 
EPC Contractor shall provide the “Training Objectives” associated with each 
training course for Company review and approval at least six months before the 
commencement of the course. These “training objectives” shall include an overall 
description of the course; the type of participants the course is targeted at 
(managers, supervisors, operators, engineers, and technicians), the expected 
knowledge of application knowledge along with detailed description of the course 
content and the desired output.   
 
EPC Contractor shall provide the methodology that will be implemented in order to 
assess the effectiveness of the courses and level of achievement by participants 
against an established standard. EPC Contractor shall submit for review a monthly 
“Training Report” which will contain details of training activities, attendances and 
assessments.  All Company attendees to training courses shall be given a 
certificate on completion of the course. 
 
All attendees to training courses shall be requested to complete “feedback” forms 
for Company review to measure the effectiveness of the training. EPC Contractor 
shall incorporate Company “feed-back” review actions into later training courses. 
A.17.3 
TRAINING PLAN  
 
EPC CONTRACTOR shall submit a detailed training plan within three months of 
the effective date for Company review and approval. The plan shall include but not 
limited to: 
• 
Title of Course.  
• 
Summary of content. 
• 
Scheduled time.

The “Training Plan” will include all courses listed in EXHIBIT B54 together with any 
additional courses, which the EPC Contractor recommends.  Company shall 
review this training plan and submit comments for inclusion into the plan. EPC 
Contractor shall appoint, with Company’s approval, a qualified Training 
Coordinator who will be the single point of contact for all Project training activities. 
The Training Coordinator shall be as a minimum of middle management level in 
EPC Contractor’s organization, Vendor/ Supplier and OEM Organization as 
required. 
 
EPC Contractor shall allow some adjustment in the training plans to meet the 
availability of Company attendees. 
 
EPC Contractor shall include in the plan details of the training facilities to be 
provided in the Home Office and at SITE for the purposes of training Company 
personnel. This will include the necessary training rooms, teaching aids, course 
material to be provided to participants, computers, projectors, and other visual aids 
to be provided in order to achieve the level of training activities included in 
EXHIBIT B54 as well as any other additional training considered by EPC 
Contractor.  Wherever possible training will be carried out at SITE. However, on 
some occasions it may be necessary to adjust the day and time of the courses to 
accommodate attendees working hours. 
 
EPC Contractor shall clearly indicate in the plan any “out of country” training to be 
carried out detailing description of training to be given, location, timeline and 
duration. 
 
A.17.4

The CVs of the Instructors / Trainer will be subject to prior approval by the 
Company. 
•

• 
Supervisor or Senior Engineer Level. 
•

• 
Have previous experience in conducting similar equipment training 
sessions.

A.17.5 
COMPANY PERSONNEL ATTENDING TRAINING SESSIONS 
 
Training sessions given by EPC Contractor, Licensors, Vendors and specialist 
companies, shall be attended by Company personnel from Senior Management, 
Safety, 
Engineering, 
Operations, 
Maintenance 
and 
Technical 
Services 
Departments depending on the subject and content of the training courses.  
Company attendees to courses will be various levels of seniority and it is essential 
that the correct level of Company personnel attend the training sessions.  For this 
reason it is necessary that a full description of the training is provided.   
 
A.17.6 
TRAINING MANUALS  
 
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for the provision of “Training Manuals” for all 
training courses provided. EPC Contractor shall provide a procedure on 
preparation of training manual for Company review and approval. These manuals 
shall be so detailed in order that COMPANY personnel can use them to provide 
additional courses after the facilities have been handed over.  Completed manuals 
shall be submitted to each course attendee at least four weeks before the training 
session commences in order that the attendees may become fully familiar with the 
content of the course and will be retained by the attendee for future reference.  All 
training materials must be in the English Language. Colour copies of any 
presentation slides used in computer or overhead presentations shall be included 
in each training manual.
