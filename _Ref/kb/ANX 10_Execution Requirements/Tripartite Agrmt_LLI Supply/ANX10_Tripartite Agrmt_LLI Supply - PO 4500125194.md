COMPANY DIRECT PAYMENT UNDERTAKING  
 
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the AGREEMENT or the LLI SUPPLY AGREEMENT, the 
VENDOR shall, subject to this Article 5, be entitled to issue invoices in the form required by Article 
16.4(a) of the LLI SUPPLY AGREEMENT, but addressed to the COMPANY and copied to the EPC 
CONTRACTOR, for any payments due to the VENDOR under Article 16 of the LLI SUPPLY 
AGREEMENT (each an “INVOICE”).  
 
The VENDOR shall, prior to issuing any INVOICE to the COMPANY pursuant to this Article 5, submit 
a draft form of such INVOICE to the EPC CONTRACTOR, copying in the COMPANY, requesting that 
the EPC CONTRACTOR certifies that the corresponding WORK has been completed and that the 
amount of the INVOICE is due and payable under the LLI SUPPLY AGREEMENT. 
 
The EPC CONTRACTOR shall, as soon as practicable, and in any event within five (5) WORKING 
DAYS of receipt of a draft INVOICE pursuant to Article 5.2: 
 
issue to the VENDOR an approval certifying the amount of the draft INVOICE is due and 
payable under the LLI SUPPLY AGREEMENT; or 
 
notify the VENDOR of its rejection of the draft INVOICE together with reasons and relevant 
supporting documentation for such rejection, 
in each case copying in the COMPANY, provided that any claim for any amount due, or claimed to be 
due, by the VENDOR to the EPC CONTRACTOR shall not be a ground for the EPC CONTRACTOR 
rejecting any draft INVOICE in accordance with this Article 5.3. 
 
If the EPC CONTRACTOR fails to approve or reject a draft INVOICE under Article 5.3 within the five 
(5) WORKING DAY period specified in Article 5.3, the EPC CONTRACTOR shall be deemed to have 
approved such draft INVOICE on expiration of such five (5) WORKING DAY period, and COMPANY 
shall have no liability to the EPC CONTRACTOR (whether under the EPC AGREEMENT or otherwise) 
arising out of or in connection with COMPANY’s payment of such invoice deemed to have been 
approved. 
 
Following approval of any draft INVOICE by the EPC CONTRACTOR in accordance with Article 5.3 
or deemed approval of a draft INVOICE by the EPC CONTRACTOR in accordance with Article 5.4, 
the VENDOR may issue the relevant INVOICE to the COMPANY, together with the EPC 
CONTRACTOR's certification and approval or, in the case of deemed approval in accordance Article 
5.4, a certification by the VENDOR that the EPC CONTRACTOR has failed to approve or reject the 
draft INVOICE in accordance with Article 5.3. The COMPANY shall, subject to this Article 5, pay to 
the VENDOR, on behalf of the EPC CONTRACTOR and without any withholding or deduction, all such 
INVOICES within thirty (30) DAYS of receipt by the COMPANY of the INVOICE and the EPC 
CONTRACTOR's certification and approval or VENDOR's certification, as applicable.
