A.8.1.1
General 
CONTRACTOR shall provide secure and dedicated offices for COMPANY’s 
PROJECT Team at the same location as their own staff working on the 
PROJECT. 
The offices for COMPANY’s PROJECT Team shall be located conveniently 
within or adjacent to the PROJECT task force area. The offices shall have 
lockable doors and will be of executive quality as a minimum.  
Where COMPANY has agreed to the use of an affiliate office or second home 
office to support part of the PROJECT, CONTRACTOR shall provide within its 
LUMP SUM PRICE the same facilities, equipment and support personnel as 
stipulated in section 8.1 relative to the size of the team the COMPANY wishes to 
deploy.

Security 
The security arrangements for the entire PROJECT Office shall be subject to 
approval of COMPANY. CCTV camera to cover entire open area spaces with two 
months back up, with display monitor in Secretary/ Administrator office

throughout the year.  Passes and temporary identification permits shall be issued 
and examined and access to any part of the PROJECT Office shall be controlled 
and limited to those who have authorised business associated with the 
PROJECT.
