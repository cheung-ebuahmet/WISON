B.47 MANUFACTURING DATA REPORT (MDR)
CONTRACTOR shall comply with the requirements of this document when making
its submission as required under EXHIBIT B.45 ·- VENDOR Documents and
Manuals.
B.47.1 CONTRACTOR shall ensure that all equipment VENDORS provide documentation
representative of the materials, manufacturing and testing history of the equipment
ordered. This documentation shall be compiled into a Manufacturing Data Report
(MDR) by VENDOR and submitted by CONTRACTOR to COMPANY as part of
the PROJECT documentation requirements.
B.47.2 The content of the MDR will vary dependent upon the type, size and complexity of
the equipment, but it must contain all documentation referenced in the MDR
Specification and all records of testing required by the Purchase Order.
B.47.3 The only exception relates to the radiographic inspection documentation wherein
X-Ray films are not required as part of the record. A report by a competent
qualified operator is to be used where radiography is employed.
B.47.4 The submission & delivery of MDR shall be ensured before or with the delivery of
the equipment / shipment. The MDR will be required at Project site during the
installation, pre-commissioning and commissioning phase.
