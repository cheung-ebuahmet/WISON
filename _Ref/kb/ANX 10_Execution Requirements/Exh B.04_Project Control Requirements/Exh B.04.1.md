PLANNING AND SCHEDULING PROCEDURE FOR EPC CONTRACTS 
 
1.0 
OBJECTIVE 
 
To provide in a structured format all the information necessary for the CONTRACTOR’S 
and COMPANY’S project teams to monitor the project successfully, identify the problem 
areas early, initiate timely corrective actions and to capitalize on the opportunities for 
schedule improvement. 
 
2.0 
SCOPE 
 
This Exhibit B.4.1 covers the methodology to be followed and the requirements to be 
satisfied by the EPC Contractor with regard to the development, updating and statusing of 
all Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Commissioning activities relevant to the 
EPC Contract to ensure effective planning and scheduling during various phases of project 
implementation.

Baseline Schedule shall be consistent with the Contract Schedule and shall be in greater 
detail to reflect the Milestones, sequence and inter-relationships between various activities 
and sub-activities related to each Area / Phase / Discipline of work. 
 
 
Contractor shall adhere to the following guideline requirements while developing the 
Baseline Schedule:  
 
a) 
Baseline Schedule shall be prepared using the “PRIMAVERA Software (Version 
8.3.5 and above)” without any imposed “Constraints” and negative floats. The 
Schedule shall be developed without any “Start-to-Finish” links and with lags not 
more than 2 weeks. All lags of more than 2 weeks duration shall be identified 
as separate activities. 
 
b) 
Each activity in the schedule shall be assigned with the relevant WBS Code, 
consistent with the PROJECT WBS, so as to enable effective organizing, sorting and 
filtering of all critical project activities by Phase / Area / Discipline / Unit / Resource 
etc. WBS / Activity ID to be followed by Contractor for this purpose is given at 
ATTACHMENT-2. Activity ID shall be prepared by using WBS Codes. 
 
c) 
“Activity Duration” Type shall be assigned as “Fixed Duration & Unit” to ensure that 
activity durations remain constant. 
 
d) 
“Activity Type” shall be assigned as Task Dependent (other than Milestones)   
 
 
e) 
Contractor shall include in the Schedule, all activities related to “Company Review/ 
Approval” of all major drawings/documents (including Technical Bid Evaluation 
Reports for Procurement items) by COMPANY, as separate activities in the 
Schedule, with activity durations consistent with the review cycles specified in the 
AGREEMENT. 
 
f) 
Contractor shall develop the Schedule in Primavera P6 based on the following 
working hours: 
 
 
Home Office   
: 5-Days/ Week and 8 hrs/ day (40 hrs/ week) 
 
Manufacturing/ Delivery  
: 7-Days/ Week and 10 hrs/ day (70 hrs/ week) 
 
Construction & Commissioning    : 6-Days/ Week and 10 hrs/ day (60 hrs/ week) 
 
Shut Down                                     : 7 Days/ Week and 24 hrs/ day (168 hrs/ week) 
 
 
Contractor shall give due consideration to the local holidays as well as shorter 
working hours during the holy month of Ramadan, as applicable.
