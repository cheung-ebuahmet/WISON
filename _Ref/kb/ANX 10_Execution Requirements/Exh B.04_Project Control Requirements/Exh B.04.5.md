SCHEDULE RISK ANALYSIS PROCEDURE FOR EPC CONTRACTS 
 
1.0 
OBJECTIVE 
 
Main Objectives of Schedule Risk Analysis include the following: 
 
a) 
To identify activity duration uncertainties and risk events that may cause project 
delays. 
 
b) 
To evaluate above Uncertainties/ Risk Events Qualitatively & Quantitatively and to 
access the probability of meeting the schedule deterministic completion date. 
 
c) 
To plan and implement mitigation measures for the identified risks. 
 
d) 
To monitor and control the risks that may cause Project delays. 
 
2.0 
SCOPE 
 
This Procedure elaborates the methodology to be followed by the EPC Contractor for the 
“Schedule Risk Analysis” and includes the specific details to be submitted by the EPC 
Contractor as a part of the “Schedule Risk Analysis” report.

4.0 
RESPONSIBILITY 
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for carrying out Scheduling Risk Analysis on a Monthly 
or Quarterly basis and submit detail report along with mitigation measures etc. periodically, as 
per the AGREEMENT requirement. 
 
 
EPC Contractor shall organize schedule risk analysis software training for their Project 
Controls team and 6 nos. Company personnel. Also, Contractor shall provide one license 
copy of the schedule risk analysis software (Acumen Fuse 360) to Company with 1 no. 
dedicated latest laptop (intel i9). Both software and laptop will be procured under 
Company name and handed over to Company. Laptop shall have antivirus, latest windows 
operating system, MS Office latest suite and Primavera latest version software installed in 
it, with all software’s having licenses valid up to project FAC period. 
  
COMPANY / PMTC Team shall participate in the discussions with EPC Contractor and 
provide inputs for “Risk Identification” and for development of “Schedule Risk Register”.

6.1 
Step -1: Schedule Validation 
 
EPC Contractor shall thoroughly examine the Project Schedule to ensure its readiness for 
Risk Analysis. A Schedule review checks the Schedule for Logic Errors, Open Ended 
Activities, Negative Lags, Start –to-Finish links and other potential problems and / or 
questionable Scheduling practices that could compromise the Risk Analysis. The 
Schedule review assures the integrity of the Schedule and reliability of the analysis.  
 
In order to avoid errors in activity durations during exporting Primavera Schedule into the 
Acumen Fuse 360 software, all planning units in Acumen Fuse 360 shall be selected as 
“Hours” instead of “Days” or any other unit. 
 
Schedule Validation report shall be submitted by the EPC Contractor in the form as per 
format given at ATTACHMENTS – 1. 
 
6.2 
Step -2: Pre-Analysis Check (Duration Quick Risk) 
 
“Duration Quick Risk” analysis involves preliminary analysis to identify the Schedule 
activities that have the greatest impact on the Project duration. These are the activities 
that are most susceptible to risk and hence will merit the closest attention during the 
detailed Risk Analysis. Pre-Analysis also helps to further validate the Schedule and 
provides an indication of whether the Schedule Finish Date is realistic. 
 
Results of the Pre-Analysis Check shall be submitted by the EPC Contractor in the form of 
“Distribution Graph” & “Tornado Graph” as per formats given at ATTACHMENTS – 2 & 3. 
 
Distribution Graph shall be based on the “Finish Date” while the Tornado Graph shall be 
based on the “Duration Cruciality”.

6.3  
Step -3: Developing Risk Model  
 
Developing a risk model involves estimating the probability and potential impacts of risks 
on the Project and then mapping those impacts to the Project Schedule in the following 
sequence: 
 
i) 
Risk Model based on Duration Uncertainties 
ii) 
Risk Model based on Risk Events 
 
6.3.1  Risk Model based on Duration Uncertainties 
 
“Templated Quick Risk” is used initially for applying the duration uncertainties at the WBS 
Level / Summary Level to determine the impact on the project duration. Probability % 
range (minimum and maximum) to be followed for risk analysis for each summary level 
activity shall be discussed and agreed in advance after discussion with Company. 
 
EPC Contractor shall apply the agreed Correlation Factor (generally 80%) while carrying 
out the Schedule Risk Analysis.  
 
After running the Risk Analysis based on the agreed duration uncertainties, thorough 
review of Distribution Graph (based on Finish Date) and Tornado Graph (based on 
Duration Cruciality) shall be undertaken and duration uncertainties for all major/ critical 
activities shall be adjusted as required, to reflect the extent of uncertainties as accurately 
as possible. 
 
Results of final Templated Quick Risk shall be submitted by the Contractor in the form of 
“Distribution Graph” & “Tornado Graph” as per formats given at ATTACHMENTS – 4 & 5.   
 
Distribution Graph shall be based on the “Finish Date” while the Tornado Graph shall be 
based on the “Duration Cruciality”. 
 
6.3.2  Risk Model based on Risk Events 
 
a) Identification of Risks and Development of Risk Register 
 
EPC CONTRACTOR shall identify risks (external and within the Project Schedule) for the 
entire Project scope covering all phases of the Project, that may impact the Project 
completion date. The risks identified by the EPC Contractor shall be listed in the Schedule 
Risk Register as per format given at ATTACHMENT-6. EPC Contractor shall ensure that 
Risk Register shall contain only the unknown risks (unknown unknowns) which may 
happen only rarely but will have adverse impact on the completion Schedule in the event 
of such risks happening. Inclusion of known uncertainties and / or Assumptions / 
Constraints in the Risk Register will be avoided since all such risks will form part of the 
duration uncertainties. Similarly, known “Issues” will not find a place in the Risk Register. 
Risk events that could disrupt the project shall also be identified and included in the Risk 
Register alongwith their likelihood and potential impact on the project duration.

Senior Project Manager or his nominated representative.

- Map Risks to the specific Schedule Tasks that they impact 
- Determine the impact of risks on the Schedule 
- Capture a Pre-mitigation risk assessment, a mitigation plan and a Post-Mitigation 
assessment.

6.4 
Step – 5 : Run Risk Analysis  
After completing the Qualitative and Quantitative analysis, EPC Contractor shall build the 
impacted Risk Plan by using Acumen Fuse 360 software. This analysis performs multiple 
simulations of the Project using random samplings of the relevant risk impact estimates. 
The simulation generates a range of project duration outcomes along with their statistical 
probabilities, thereby enabling the project team to focus on areas of the schedule that 
pose the greatest risks to the overall project. 
 
Results of Pre-mitigated and Post-mitigated risk analysis shall be submitted by the 
Contractor in the form of “Distribution Graph” & “Tornado Graph” as per formats given at 
ATTACHMENTS – 7 to 11.   
 
Distribution Graph shall be based on the “Finish Date” while the Tornado Graph shall be 
based on the “Duration Cruciality”. In addition, Distribution Analyzers shall be based on 
“Finish Date” reflecting both Pre-mitigated and Post –mitigated results into one sheet, to 
facilitate comparison.
