6.0 
BASIS OF PROGRESS MEASUREMENT 
 
6.1 
Progress measurement shall be based upon the physical progress against each of the 
activities / sub-activities / deliverables according to a set of weighted values, allocated 
according to the effort required to complete.  For working out the weightings of various 
activities / deliverables within each area and within each project phase, the following basis 
will be used: 
 
a) 
Home Office Services 
- Man-hours 
 i) Detail Engineering Services 
- Estimated 
Man-hours 
for 
each 
deliverable. 
 ii) Procurement 
- Estimated Man-hours for each Material 
Requisition (MR) 
 iii) Subcontracting Services 
- Estimated Man-hours for award of 
each subcontract. 
b) 
Manufacturing & Delivery  (Civil 
items and other site procured 
materials excluded) 
- Cost 
(Budgeted cost of each Purchase 
Order) 
c) 
Construction and Commissioning  
- Man-hours 
(Estimated Man-hours for each activity 
based on Physical Quantity of work 
involved) 
 
6.2 
Relative weightings for developing the overall S-curves against the above three phases 
and for each area and / or discipline shall be in accordance with the weightings given in 
the PROJECT WBS. 
 
6.3 
WEIGHTED VALUE SYSTEM 
 
6.3.1 Each activity or deliverable listed in the respective Planning Register will be assigned 
estimated man-hours / cost depending on the estimated work content / cost, as applicable. 
 
6.3.2 Activity or deliverable weight factors shall be computed based on the estimated man-
hours/cost as a fraction of the total man-hours / cost for each Area / Phase / Discipline etc. 
and for the whole project. 
 
6.3.3 The weight factors thus computed from Planning Registers shall be rolled upto Level-2 
and Level-1 by Area / Phase / Discipline etc. in line with the PROJECT Work Breakdown 
Structure (WBS).

6.4 
PROGRESS MEASUREMENT 
 
6.4.1 In case of Home Office Services and Manufacturing & Delivery activities, progress against 
each activity / sub-activity / deliverable level shall be based on assigning certain pre-
defined % progress representing the achievement of specific intermediate Milestone 
(Control Point) required to complete the activity / deliverable. Partial completion of any 
intermediate Milestone (Control Point) does not entitle any progress claim against the 
specific intermediate Milestone. 
 
6.4.2 In case of Construction and Commissioning, progress against each activity / sub-activity 
level shall be measured based on the quantity of work completed as a percentage of the 
total quantity of work to be completed. 
 
6.4.3 Overall progress of each project Area / Phase / Discipline etc. shall be computed by 
multiplying the activity % progress by its weight factor and thereafter summing up the 
products. 
 
6.4.4 Overall progress for the whole project shall be calculated by integrating the progress 
against each project Area / Phase / Discipline etc. as described above, with the respective 
weightings.

c)

Contractor’s proposal shall be consistent with the general requirements specified in 
this procedure as well as various other requirements specified in the AGREEMENT.

6.6 
GROUND RULES FOR PROGRESS MEASUREMENT AND PROGRESS REPORTING 
 
6.6.1 Following activities are considered as support functions and will be excluded from the 
Project Progress Measurement System. This would mean that the effort expended against 
the following activities will not be considered for progress computation: 
a) 
Project Management. 
b) 
Project Control. 
c) 
QA/QC 
d) 
Contract Management 
e) 
Mobilization / Demobilization. 
 
6.6.2 Effort expended towards installation of temporary facilities required if any, shall not be 
considered for progress computation unless specified otherwise in the PROJECT progress 
measurement system. 
 
6.6.3 Progress against detail engineering activities shall be measured and reported only against 
the identified deliverables.  Any effort expended by engineering and other personnel on 
the activities, which are not specific to any particular deliverable viz.  Site visits, 
Engineering Supervision & Coordination, Participation in Meetings, Preparation of MOMs 
etc. shall be excluded from the Progress Measurement and Reporting System. 
 
6.6.4 List of intermediate Milestones (Control Points) identified at ATTACHMENTS – 2 to 6 is 
generic in nature and will be adopted to different deliverables / activities without changing 
the weightings assigned to the intermediate Milestones. 
 
6.6.5 If any intermediate Milestone (Control Point) identified in ATTACHMENTS – 2 to 6 is not 
applicable to a specific activity / deliverable, progress will not be claimed against that 
Control Point and progress will become applicable only on achievement of its subsequent 
Control Point. 
 
6.6.6 If any document identified in the Engineering Document Deliverable Register (EDDR) is 
rejected after COMPANY review, such rejected document’s status shall revert to its 
previous control point and weighted progress against such document shall be adjusted 
accordingly. Similar approach will be followed in case of Procurement Services Register 
also, if any TBE is rejected after company review. 
 
6.6.7 For all Procurement activities, progress shall be measured separately against each 
Material Requisition (MR) for the Control Points prior to placement of order.

6.6.8 A minimum of 5 % of total budget man-hours / weighting will be allocated to 
“Miscellaneous Deliverables / activities” in the respective Planning Registers during all 
phases of EPC Contract (separately for each Area, Phase, Discipline), as a contingency 
towards the unidentified activities and/or the anticipated increase in the work load. This will 
eliminate the need for frequent adjustment of weight factors assigned to each activity / 
deliverable.  At an appropriate time during the period of implementation when full details of 
deliverables / MRs / POs and Construction work elements become available, any unused 
portion of this contingency will be progressed and equivalent progress claimed, in 
consultation with COMPANY. 
 
If any of the documents/deliverables identified in the EDDR are deleted during 
implementation stage, the weightings of such deleted deliverables shall be added to the 
weightage against the “Miscellaneous Documents” under the respective disciplines. 
 
Similarly, if any of the MRs/POs identified in the Procurement Register (PR) are deleted 
during implementation stage, the weightings of such deleted MR/PO shall be added to the 
weightage against the “Miscellaneous MRs/POs” under the respective disciplines. 
 
6.6.9 For the purpose of computation of physical progress through Procurement Register, “Issue 
of Letter / Fax of Award” shall be treated as “Issue of Purchase Order” subject to the 
following: 
- 
Approval of Technical Bid Evaluation (TBE) by Company. 
- 
Issue of unconditional LOA / FOA by EPC Contractor. 
- 
Receipt of Unconditional acceptance of LOA / FOA from Vendor. 
 
Similar approach will be followed with regard to award of subcontracts also. 
 
Issue of LOA/FOA by Contractor prior to Company approval of TBE shall not be construed 
as “Issue of Purchase Order”.  
 
Milestone Completion (refer Milestone Completion Schedule, Attachment-3 to Exhibit 
A-6) with regard to “Issue of Purchase Orders” shall be considered as achieved only after 
issue of valid Purchase Order and final updated Purchase Requisition (PR) to Vendor with 
a copy to Company, alongwith a copy of acceptance by Vendor.  Issue of LOA/FOA to 
Vendor shall not be considered as achievement of this Milestone.

6.6.12 Progress shall be computed based on the assigned weightings for each Control Point in 
the Planning Register on a cumulative basis, but not separately on achievement of any 
particular Control Point, since all Control Points are identified in a sequential manner. As 
an example, progress against issue of FOA / LOA will not be considered as achieved, 
even if the commitment has been made to the vendor by the EPC Contractor, unless its 
preceding Control Points (viz. Submission / Approval of TBE) are achieved. 
 
6.6.13 Progress shall not be claimed against any of the Control Points identified in the respective 
Planning Registers (EDDR, SSR, PSR and MDR) unless all the activities/ sub-activities 
relevant to the Control Point are fully completed/ achieved i.e. Progress shall not be 
claimed on partial completion of any Control Point. 
 
6.6.14 As a general rule, progress against Mfg & Delivery activities shall be claimed against each 
Purchase Order (PO) as identified in the Mfg & Delivery Register (MDR). However, in case 
of Package Equipment and other specified large value Purchased Orders, each PO may 
be split into multiple lots (not more than 3 to 4 lots) in consultation and prior agreement 
with Company. No. of lots however depend on the Value/ Size/ Quantity covered under 
each Purchase Order and shall be discussed and agreed with Company on a case to case 
basis. 
 
6.6.15 Award of Subcontracts for various Construction works does not construe completion of 
detail engineering work and/or issue of purchase orders for the materials included in the 
scope of the respective subcontractor.  All major engineering deliverables and materials 
included in the scope of the subcontractors shall be included in the Contractor’s Planning 
Registers (EDDR, PSR, MDR etc.) to facilitate progress measurement, progress reporting 
and monitoring of all project activities in a comprehensive manner. 
 
6.6.16 Prior to commencement of progress reporting against the construction activities, 
Contractor shall submit the “Basis of Construction Progress Measurement” covering all 
major construction activities pertaining to each discipline of work as per the sample format 
given at ATTACHMENT-7 for Company review/approval. Based on the approved “Basis of 
Construction Progress Measurement” system, Contractor shall develop the Construction 
Progress weightings at all reporting levels consistent with the Agreement WBS, as per the 
sample format given at ATTACHMENT-8. 
 
6.6.17 a) Progress details (Plan % Vs Act %) for all Home Office Services (viz. Engineering,  
Procurement activities and Subcontracting Services) as well as for Mfg. & Delivery 
activities shall be computed using the respective Planning Registers (viz. EDDR, PSR, 
SSR, MDR etc.).  
 
b) Progress details (Plan % Vs. Act %) for all Construction activities shall be computed on 
the basis of “Construction Quantity Based Report” as per the format given at 
ATTACHMENT-11 to Exhibit B.4.3. “Construction Quantity Based Report” shall be 
developed on the basis of the approved details as per ATTACHMENTS-7 & 8.
