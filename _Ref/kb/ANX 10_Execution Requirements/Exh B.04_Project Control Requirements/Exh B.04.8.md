B.4.8  
ASSETS REGISTER PROCEDURE 
B.4.8.1 
INTRODUCTION 
On completion of the PROJECT, CONTRACTOR is required to submit a detailed 
Assets Register indicating the split of the total PROJECT Cost over various 
identified Assets installed / built on the PROJECT.  The procedure outlines the 
requirements and specifies the formats to be utilized by CONTRACTOR to submit 
the Assets Registers.  These formats can be modified, to suit to a given Project, 
with COMPANY approval. 
B.4.8.2 
SCOPE 
The Assets to be covered in the Register shall include all permanent Assets 
installed / built under the Scope of the Agreement with the CONTRACTOR. 
B.4.8.3 
COST 
The total basic cost of the Assets will match the final Agreement Price.  To this, 
COMPANY will add its other costs like FEED, Licenses, Free-issue materials if 
any, Project Insurance and Project Management Costs covering COMPANY, 
CONSULTANT/ENGINEER and Third Party Inspection Personnel, on prorata 
basis. 
COMPANY may advise the actual cost of Free-issue material to CONTRACTOR 
for inclusion in the Assets Register to be prepared by CONTRACTOR. 
B.4.8.4 
SPLIT OF ASSETS 
Total Assets will be split as per the following divisions: 
1. 
Physical Location 
2. 
Area / Unit 
3. 
By Tagged Equipment 
4. 
By Buildings 
 
B.4.8.5 
PRESENTATION OF ASSET REGISTER 
CONTRACTOR shall ensure that all the requisite details with respect to each 
identified asset are filled-in as per Attachments 1-4.  CONTRACTOR shall develop 
the Register in MS Excel format and submit to COMPANY the Register(s) in Hard 
Copy as well as in Electronic form. 
CONTRACTOR shall leave the columns relating to COMPANY costs as blank 
which can be added by COMPANY so that the overall total costs match with the 
approved Close-out budget for the PROJECT.

B.4.8.6 
ALLOCATION OF COSTS 
B.4.8.6.1 
Equipment Supply  
Equipment supply cost includes the following:- 
Cost of all Mechanical Equipment and packages including material cost for all 
process equipment to be installed in permanent facility, such as tanks, 
compressors, drivers pumps, exchangers and any corresponding internals. 
Cost of Electrical Equipment including the material cost for transformers, 
switchgear, packaged switch houses and miscellaneous LV equipment. 
Cost of all Instrumentation Equipment including the material cost for DCS F&G, 
ESD, Computers and analysers etc. 
B.4.8.6.2 
Line Pipe Supply  
Pipeline supply cost includes supply cost of pipeline. 
B.4.8.6.3 
Bulk Materials Supply  
Bulk material supply cost includes cost of materials such as pipes, fittings, valves, 
wires and cables, cable trays, light fittings, lighting poles, field instruments etc. i.e. 
all materials not included in equipment supply cost are included in Bulk Materials 
supply.  It is to be noted that material supply for Civil, Structural, painting and 
insulation is not included in Bulk Materials cost.  This is considered part of 
construction cost.  Similarly all materials supplied for construction of building 
including supply of HVAC equipment for building are considered part of 
construction cost. 
B.4.8.6.4 
Construction   
Includes installation and construction costs, it shall cover cost of labour, goods, 
construction plant and equipment, temporary facilities, material & consumables 
required for fabrication, installation, pre-commissioning, COMISSIONING & TEST 
RUN of Installation.  In addition supply of all materials for buildings, civils, 
structural, insulation and painting is also included in construction costs.
