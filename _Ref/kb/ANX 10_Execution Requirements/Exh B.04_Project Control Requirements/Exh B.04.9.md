1. 
PURPOSE 
 
The purpose of the design concession authorization (DCA) procedure is to establish 
a methodology for early identification, authorization and control of potential changes 
from the SPECIFICATIONS, standards or other requirements of the AGREEMENT  
 
This procedure does not alter the content of the AGREEMENT provisions and, in 
case of any conflict, the AGREEMENT will govern. 
 
2.

3. 
SCOPE 
 
The scope of the procedure covers those design deviations and concession 
requests, which do not impact the cost, schedule and / or have detrimental impact on 
quality and / or safety.  
 
Any deviation from the PROJECT AGREEMENT requirements having an impact on 
the cost, schedule and / or have detrimental impact on quality and safety shall be 
dealt with in accordance with the Contract Trending Procedure Document No: --------
------------ 
 
Non-Conformance Reports (NCR) are not covered by this procedure. Only in case 
CONTRACTOR’s remedial action is not acceptable, the NCR shall be considered for 
DCA/ CTN , as applicable.   
 
This procedure shall be applicable to specification deviation requests from all 
sources viz. CONTRACTOR, SUB-CONTRACTORS and SUPPLIERS. 
 
In the event that it is not practicable for any reason to comply with issued COMPANY 
specifications or contractual drawings, a DCA shall be raised by CONTRACTOR for 
approval by COMPANY. 
 
The identification of Changes is under the responsibility of CONTRACTOR.

5. 
RESPONSIBILITIES 
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for coordination with COMPANY and for 
maintaining an updated log of issued Deviation Requests and approvals received 
from COMPANY. 
 
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for coordinating and reviewing the 
Design/Specification Deviation Requests originated by SUB-CONTRACTORS, 
SUPPLIERS etc. and if found acceptable by CONTRACTOR , forward such 
deviations to COMPANY for approval. 
 
 
 
 
Contractor shall not use Technical Queries (TQ) as an alternative to DCA. If any 
change to design is arisen out of Technical Query, then Contractor shall raise DCA 
separately. 
 
6. 
PROCEDURE 
 
6.1 
Processing of DCA 
Processing of DCAs shall be in accordance with this Project Procedure utilizing the 
DCA Form, enclosed at Attachment 1.  
 
CONTRACTOR shall supply all the pertinent information on the appropriate sections 
of the form. Necessary supporting documentation will be provided.  
As a minimum the DCA Form shall include the following: 
 
- 
Reasons for deviation / concession, including technical justification 
- 
Drawings, documents and specifications affected 
- 
Justification for no impact on cost, schedule, quality and safety 
 
6.2 
DCA Classification 
All design deviation / concession request authorization (DCA) shall be identified 
based on classification summarized below: 
 
Design Deviations 
=  
DD 
 
Concession requests 
=  
CR 
  
General Requests       
= 
GR  
 
 
Design Deviations (DDs) cover deviations from the original design premise proposed 
by CONTRACTOR.  
 
Concession Requests (CRs) relate to substitutions in material, repair procedures, 
deletions of a requirement, or change in specification and equipment raised by 
SUPPLIERS or SUBCONTRACTOR’s and deemed acceptable by CONTRACTOR.  
 
General Requests (GRs), issued by CONTRACTOR, include additions or deletions 
to the contractual scope of work and/or those not covered by Design Deviations or 
Concession Requests.

6.3 
Completion of DCA 
CONTRACTOR shall complete the form, providing as much detail as possible of the 
deviation(s) requested, the reason for the request and the technical justification(s) as 
well as justification for no impact on the Agreement Price, Schedule, Quality and/or 
Safety in support of the request, to enable COMPANY to review the request and 
adequately determine its acceptability. The DCA shall be submitted to COMPANY 
for approval at least twenty one (21) working days prior to the scheduled 
implementation of the proposed design deviation/concession.  
 
Where there is a potential impact on cost, schedule or a detrimental impact on 
quality and the DCA is accepted subject to approval of a CTN, CONTRACTOR 
should also initiate a CTN in accordance with Project Contract Trending Procedure. 
In the event DCA has been approved as subject to Negative CTN, CONTRACTOR 
shall not implement that DCA until the Negative CTN has been issued and 
COMPANY’s approval-in-principle to proceed has been given. 
6.4 
Referenced & Affected Documents 
The number and title of the principal documents affected by the Deviation Request, 
and relevant section number within the specification, must be clearly identified on the 
Deviation Request form. 
 
The number will include an area code to identify the area/plant to which the 
specification deviation applies. 
 
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for assessing the impact on secondary 
documents and for propagating the impact of the DCA to all relevant documents.  
CONTRACTOR Managers shall ensure that all affected disciplines are aware of the 
impact and requirements of the DCA   
 
6.5 
DCA Numbering  
All Deviation Requests shall be uniquely referenced 
 
Deviation Request numbers shall be formatted as follows: 
 
 
 
YYYY- DCA-XX -001 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Sequence number (3 digits) 
 
 
Document Type (DD / CR / GR) 
 
 
Project Number 
 
The Deviation Requests shall be numbered sequentially starting from 001 
irrespective of the document types. ie.,  
YYYY-DCA-DD-001, YYYY-DCA-CR-002,

6.6 
CONTRACTOR Review / Approval 
CONTRACTOR shall carefully review each DCA Request, if any, and obtain 
CONTRACTOR Engineering Manager (EM) and CONTRACTOR’s representative 
signature before submission to COMPANY for review and approval.   
 
6.7 
COMPANY Review/Approval 
COMPANY shall review DCA and shall advise CONTRACTOR that the DCA 
Request is approved, or otherwise, within 15 working days. Contractor shall not 
implement the proposed change unless approved by COMPANY in writing. 
 
7. 
DCA  REGISTER 
CONTRACTOR shall maintain a DCA register as per the formats included in 
Attachment 2 covering the DCA requests submitted by CONTRACTOR and whether 
or not approved by COMPANY. The register will be kept up-to-date and issued to the 
COMPANY regularly. As a minimum, the updated DCA register will be included in 
the CONTRACTOR‘s monthly report. The DCA register included in the monthly 
report will also be sorted by DCA status. 
 
8.  
AGES/DGS ADDENDUMS, TBE, TB AND DAS 
Changes to the company AGES/DGS are generally not encouraged during project 
phases. However, to suit the requirements of project scope and to capture the lessons 
learnt from other projects, the contractor may need to revise or change certain technical 
requirements of company AGES/DGS. 
Section 2.4 of Company Management of Change Procedure – Project Phase (OE-PR-
048) shall be followed for  
• 
AGES/DGS Addendums 
• 
Changes to Technical Bid Evaluation 
• 
Changes to Tender Bulletin 
• 
Changes to Document Amendment Sheet (DAS) 
A register shall be maintained as per attachment 3 by contractor.
