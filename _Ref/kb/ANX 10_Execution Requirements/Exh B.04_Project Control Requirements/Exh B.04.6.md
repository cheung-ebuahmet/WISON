PROGRESS MEASUREMENT PROCEDURE FOR 3D MODELLING 
 
1.0 
OBJECTIVE 
 
To ensure uniform implementation of the Physical Progress Measurement System for 3D 
Modeling Work. 
 
2.0 
SCOPE 
 
This procedure is intended to facilitate establishment of a structured physical progress 
measurement system for 3D Modeling work to be implemented by all Contractors in a 
uniform manner as applicable, during the EPC Phase of the Project.

6.0 
PROGRESS MEASUREMENT FOR 3D MODELING 
 
Progress Measurement System for 3D Modeling shall be independent of the progress 
measurement system for the other engineering activities where progress is measured through 
EDDR. Overall detailed engineering progress shall be computed taking into consideration the 
progress from EDDR (Engineering Document Deliverable Register) as well as the progress 
from 3D Modeling for all applicable Areas & Disciplines (viz. Piping, Civil/Structure, Electrical 
and Instrument & Control).

Contractor shall submit to Company their proposed split-up of progress weights separately 
against the engineering deliverables measured through EDDR and 3D Modeling activities 
based on their estimated man-hours, as per the format given at ATTACHMENT-1. These 
details shall be reviewed and approved by Company prior to implementation by Contractor. 
 
Progress against 3D Modeling shall be measured based on the quantities of the objects to be 
modeled during each stage of the modeling, i.e. 30%, 60%, 90% etc. and progress will be 
considered as achieved on completion of the specified intermediate Milestones/Control Points. 
 
Cumulative progress weightings (Weight%) to be followed for each engineering discipline 
during the different stages of modeling are given at ATTACHMENT-2. 
 
At each modeling stage the following intermediate Milestones / Control Points shall be used for 
progress measurement with progress weightings as given below:

100% 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Contractor shall develop “Progress Measurement Charts” separately for each Area, covering 
all modelling activities related to each engineering discipline, as per the sample formats and 
guidelines specified at ATTACHMENTS-3 to 6. 
 
Based on the progress details computed through the above formats (ATTACHMENT 3 to 6), 
Contractor shall submit discipline wise 3D Model progress summary covering all Areas of work 
as per the sample format given at ATTACHMENT-7 & 8 . 
 
Contractor shall also prepare and submit Progress ‘S’-Curves for modeling activities, 
separately for each discipline alongwith the Overall ‘S’-Curve, as per the sample format given 
at ATTACHMENT-9.
