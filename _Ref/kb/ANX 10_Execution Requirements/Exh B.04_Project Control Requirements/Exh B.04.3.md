PROGRESS REPORTING PROCEDURE FOR EPC CONTRACTS 
 
 
1.0 OBJECTIVE 
 
To define the requirements of periodic progress reporting (Viz. Daily, Weekly and Monthly 
Progress Reports) to be followed by EPC Contractor during the EPC implementation stage 
of the project. 
 
 
2.0 SCOPE 
 
Scope of this Exhibit B.4.3 essentially covers the contents and formats to be followed by 
EPC Contractor for the Daily, Weekly and Monthly reporting to COMPANY alongwith the 
cut-off dates for progress reporting as well as the frequency of Progress Review Meetings 
etc. as per the agreed Project Calendar.

Daily Construction Manpower Return

6.2 Weekly Progress Reports 
 
CONTRACTOR shall submit weekly progress reports by “Monday” every week, based on 
the progress achieved as on the “Friday” of the previous week (Cut-off Date), in line with 
the agreed Project Calendar.   
 
Weekly progress reports shall contain the following details separately against each Area/ 
Phase/System/Discipline etc., as required. 
 
i. 
Narrative Description of major activities completed / achieved during the reporting 
period alongwith reasons for progress shortfall, if any with delay analysis and 
mitigation action plans to recover shortfall. 
ii. 
Major activities achieved during the reporting week and planned during the 
subsequent week. 
iii. 
Areas of Concern and Corrective Action. 
iv. 
List of major activities (with less than 20 days float) delayed by more than 2 weeks, 
alongwith forecast completion dates for the same and impact of such delays if any, 
on the Milestone/Project completion and action plans to mitigate the delays  (As per 
the sample format at ATTACHMENT – 4). 
v. 
Overall Progress Statistics (As per the sample formats at ATTACHMENT – 5, Pages 
1 to 16). 
vi. 
List of Critical Drawings/Documents awaiting COMPANY review for more than 2 
weeks (As per the sample format at ATTACHMENT - 6). 
vii. 
Progress S-curves showing Plan vs. Actual progress (weekly and cumulative), as per 
the PROJECT Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) and Overall Project Progress (As 
per the sample format at ATTACHMENT-7). 
viii. Updated Summary status of Contractual Milestones (ATTACHMENT-14). 
ix. 
Summary Status of “Engineering Document Deliverable Register” indicating the 
number of documents “Approved/Issued for Construction”, separately for each Area / 
Phase / Discipline etc. (As per the sample format at ATTACHMENT - 16). 
x. 
Updated “Engineering Document Deliverable Registers” (As per sample format at 
ATTACHMENTS-9 & 10 to Exhibit B.4.1)

7.0 PROGRESS REVIEW MEETINGS 
 
Progress review meetings shall be conducted on weekly/monthly basis as per the agreed 
Co-ordination Procedure.  These meetings shall be attended by the CONTRACTOR’S 
Project Manager, Engineering Manager, Construction Manager, Contracts Manager, 
Project Control Manager, Procurement Manager and other discipline Engineers as may be 
required. Contractor shall ensure availability of Subcontractors’ key personnel also to 
attend all such meetings wherever felt essential by COMPANY. Monthly Progress Review 
Meetings will be attended by CONTRACTOR’S Project Sponsor also, wherever applicable. 
Topics to be discussed in these meetings generally include the following:- 
 
▪ 
HSE issues. 
▪ 
Areawise / Phasewise / Disciplinewise / Systemwise Progress (alongwith Progress 
Statistics, Reasons for Shortfall, Areas of Concern, Actions for Recovery of Slippage 
etc.) 
▪ 
QA/QC Issues. 
▪ 
CTN / Variation Order Status. 
▪ 
Other issues. 
These meetings shall normally be held at COMPANY’S Offices or at Contractor’s Offices 
as per convenience and prior agreement.  Detailed presentations shall be made by 
CONTRACTOR during the Monthly Progress Review meetings highlighting Areawise / 
Phasewise / Disciplinewise / Systemwise Project Status, Areas of Concern, Actions Taken, 
Actions Required etc.  Presentation formats shall be prepared and agreed in advance by 
COMPANY. 
 
 
8.0 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 
 
8.1 COMPANY reserves the right to add/delete/modify the above reporting requirements as 
deemed necessary, for ensuring effective control & monitoring. COMPANY shall inform all 
such changes/requirements to CONTRACTOR and CONTRACTOR shall implement the 
same at all times, till project completion. 
8.2 CONTRACTOR shall provide COMPANY access to all applicable planning and scheduling 
data to ascertain the accuracy of reporting. COMPANY shall have the right at any time to 
review and audit contractor’s performance, books and records related to planning, 
scheduling, progress measurement and resource details etc. 
8.3 Reporting of all progress figures shall be limited to 2 decimal places only.
