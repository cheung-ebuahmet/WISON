PRE-COMMISSIONING & COMMISSIONING PROCEDURE FOR EPC CONTRACTS 
 
1.0 
OBJECTIVE 
 
To ensure uniform implementation of the Physical Progress Measurement System 
across various phases of the project consistent with the Work Breakdown Structure 
(WBS) and other requirements of AGREEMENT. 
 
2.0  
SCOPE 
 
The procedure is intended to facilitate establishment of structured format for all the 
information necessary for the CONTRACTOR’S and COMPANY’S project teams to 
monitor the project successfully, identify the problem areas early, initiate timely corrective 
actions and to capitalize on the opportunities for schedule improvement. 
 
This procedure covers the methodology to be followed and the requirements to be 
satisfied by the EPC Contractor with regard to the development, updating and statusing 
of all Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Commissioning activities relevant to 
the EPC Contract in a uniform manner as applicable during the EPC Phase of the project 
to ensure effective planning and scheduling during various phases of project 
implementation.

6.1 
PROGRESS MEASUREMENT 
 
a) 
Agreed weightings for each intermediate Milestone / Control Point for each system / 
subsystem shall be presented in the Pre-commissioning Progress Register (PPR) and 
Commissioning Progress Register (CPR). These agreed weightings for each 
intermediate Milestone together with the estimated Man-hours (Weightage) for each 
System / Subsystem shall form the basis for Progress measurement and reporting 
(Plan % Vs. Act %) against each system / subsystem and consequently for each 
discipline / group of systems. 
 
b) 
Planned completion dates against each System / Subsystem shall be in accordance 
with the approved Schedules for Pre-commissioning and Commissioning works. 
 
c) 
While developing the above Progress Registers (PPR and CPR), a provision of 
minimum 5% of total man-hours (Weight%) shall be allocated towards “Miscellaneous 
Systems / Subsystems” against each discipline / group of systems, as a contingency 
towards the likely addition of systems / subsystems. At an appropriate time during the 
period of implementation when full details of systems / subsystems become available, 
any unused portion of this contingency will be progressed and equivalent progress 
claimed, in consultation with Company. 
 
d) 
Progress shall be computed based on the assigned weightings for each Control Point 
on a cumulative basis, but not separately on achievement of any particular Control 
Point, since all Control Points are generally identified in a sequential manner.

6.3  GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 
 
6.3.1) COMPONY reserves the right to add/delete/modify the above reporting requirements 
as deemed necessary and depending upon the progress status, for ensuring effective 
control & monitoring.  COMPANY shall inform all such changes/requirements to 
CONTRACTOR and CONTRACTOR shall implement the same at all times, till project 
completion. 
 
6.3.2) CONTRACTOR shall provide COMPANY access to all applicable planning and 
scheduling data to ascertain the accuracy of reporting.  COMPANY shall have the right 
at any time to review and audit contractor’s performance, books and records related to 
Planning, Scheduling, Progress Measurement and Progress Reporting. 
 
6.3.3) Reporting of all progress figures shall be limited to 2 decimal places only. 
 
6.3.4) CONTRACTOR shall submit to COMPANY  all schedules, reports and other planning 
related documents in requisite copies, together with soft copies, in the same formats as 
originally developed by the CONTRACTOR (Not in .pdf formats), at the time of initial 
submission as well as during the subsequent revisions/updates.
