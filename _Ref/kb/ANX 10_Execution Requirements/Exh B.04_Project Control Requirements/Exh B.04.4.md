PROGRESS REVIEW / CERTIFICATION PROCEDURE FOR EPC CONTRACTS 
 
 
1.0 
OBJECTIVE  
 
To identify the relevant PMT Personnel responsible for verification & approval of Monthly 
Physical Progress Certificates and to specify the supporting documentation required to be 
submitted by the Contractor to enable Certification / Approval of physical progress at the 
end of each month. 
 
 
2.0 
SCOPE  
 
The Exhibit B.4.4 covers the methodology to be followed and the requirements to be 
satisfied by the Contractor with regard to submission of “Monthly Physical Progress 
Certificates” and their subsequent review / approval by the concerned Project 
Management Team (PMT) members.

4.0 
RESPONSIBILITY  
 
a) 
EPC Contractor 
 
EPC Contractor will be responsible for submission of progress details as specified in 
the Progress Reporting Procedure together with all necessary supporting documents 
and to provide additional details / justifications where required. 
 
b) 
Project Management Team (PMT) 
 
Identified PMT personnel will be responsible for review / certification of progress (or) 
rejection of Contractor’s submission, if requisite justification and/or supporting 
documents have not been submitted or the submitted details do not support the 
progress figures claimed by EPC Contractor.

PROGRESS REVIEW / CERTIFICATION AND REQUIREMENT OF SUPPORTING 
DOCUMENTS 
 
CONTRACTOR shall submit Physical Progress Certificate for each month to Company, 
alongwith all supporting documentation for the progress claimed, in line with the approved 
Progress Measurement and Progress Reporting Procedures, to enable progress review 
and verification / approval by COMPANY.  Progress Review / Verification sheets to be 
submitted by CONTRACTOR shall include the details of progress achieved during each 
month and shall be as per the formats specified below:- 
 
a) 
Physical Progress Certificate (ATTACHMENT-1). 
b) 
Overall Project Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT-2). 
c) 
Detail Engineering Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT-3). 
d) 
Procurement Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT-4). 
e) 
Sub-Contract Services Progress Summary (Attachment-5) 
f) 
Manufacturing & Delivery Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT-6) 
g) 
 Construction Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT-7) 
h) 
Construction Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT- 8) 
i) 
Construction Progress Summary – (ATTACHMENT-9) 
j) 
Pre-Commissioning Progress Summary – (ATTACHMENT-10) 
k) 
Pre-Commissioning Progress Summary (ATTACHMENT-11) 
l) 
Summary Details of Milestones Achieved During the Month  (ATTACHMENT-12) 
m) 
Summary Details of Milestones Achieved till current Month (ATTACHMENT-13) 
n) 
Summary Details of Ordering for Long Lead Items (LLIs) (ATTACHMENT-14) 
o) 
Summary of ICV Certification (ATTACHMENT – 15) 
 
Physical progress details submitted through the above formats shall be consistent with the 
progress details furnished alongwith the respective Monthly Progress Reports, wherein 
physical progress is calculated as per the approved Progress Measurement Procedure.
