EPC Contractor shall establish a Pre-commissioning and Commissioning Team 
to prepare all documentation required for all Mechanical Completion activities 
(including 
Pre-commissioning) 
and 
Commissioning 
activities. 
This 
documentation shall include but not be limited to the following: 
 
• 
Mechanical Completion (incorporating the Pre-commissioning) Manual 
• 
Identify Systems and sub-systems for Pre-commissioning and 
Commissioning and prepare test and inspection records  
• 
Mechanical Completion Checklists 
• 
Mechanical Completion Inspection Forms 
• 
Ready for Commissioning Checklist 
• 
Commissioning Manual 
• 
Operating Manuals (see EXHIBIT B.49 “Contents of Operating and 
Maintenance Manuals”) 
• 
TEST RUN Procedure 
• 
Training Manuals 
• 
Execution plan for Pre-commissioning / Commissioning 
• 
Company involvement program for Mechanical Completion, Pre-
commissioning and Commissioning 
 
B.50.2 
EPC Contractor shall perform all Mechanical Completion and Commissioning 
planning and preparation of all documentation. These activities shall include, 
but not be limited to, the following:  
   
 
B.50.2.0 
EPC Contractor shall note that RSHT-1 is a brown field project. The EPC 
CONTRACTOR shall review all early design Documents such as SIMOPS 
Reports, EPC Detailed schedule, all Constructability, Demolishing and 
construction sequencing documents along with these Exhibits. The RSHT-1 will 
continue with its normal Production, (without any interruption) during the 
construction & commissioning phase.   For example, the Granulators relocation, 
dismantling of old Granulators and installation of new Granulators, Conveyer 
upgrades, replacement & installation, Reclaimer & Ship Loader replacement 
and upgrades etc. will be carried out, while the Plant is in Normal Production.  
EPC Contractor shall evaluate the proposed strategy and will further develop the 
detailed plan to fulfil the requirement of plant production and COMPANY 
requirements in consultation with Company Project, Operation & Maintenance 
team. 
 
B.50.2.1 
EPC Contractor shall mobilize as part of its Home Office task force team, 
qualified personnel to perform the Commissioning planning activities as outlined 
in this Agreement.

B.50.2.2 
Before commencing any SITE activities the EPC Contractor shall prepare a 
numbering plan for the complete facilities which can identify Systems and sub-
systems. The numbering plan will enable accurate recording of results 
inspection and test records for the appropriate System or sub-system. The 
numbering plan shall be approved by the Company before implementation. 
 
B.50.2.3 
Company Operators’ involvement during Commissioning shall be included by 
EPC Contractor. The magnitude of such involvement shall be finalized prior to 
Commissioning activities commencement and shall have no impact on EPC 
Contractor’s responsibilities under this Agreement.  
B.50.2.4 
EPC Contractor shall prepare and submit to Company for approval, detailed 
Area-wise / System-wise / Unit-wise Commissioning Schedules with Logic 
Block Diagrams. These schedules shall include all contractual responsibilities 
related to these activities and shall integrate Vendor Representative Schedules. 
Chemicals Schedule shall also be included as part of Commissioning Schedule. 
 
B.50.2.5 
EPC Contractor shall prepare and submit for Company’s approval, the field, 
Mechanical Completion including Pre-Commissioning, and Commissioning 
organisation and deployment schedule, together with Job Description of each 
position in the organisation and key personnel resumes. 
 
B.50.2.6 
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for communicating with all Vendors to 
obtain any clarification, explanation and additional information regarding their 
supplied materials/equipment for the purpose of developing the Mechanical 
Completion including Pre-Commissioning, and Commissioning planning 
activities. 
 
B.50.2.7 
EPC Contractor shall arrange presence of all Vendor personnel required as per 
Agreement.  
 
B.50.2.8 
EPC Contractor shall identify the need for all special equipment/tools, materials 
and temporary equipment required for the execution of the Pre-commissioning 
and Commissioning activities. 
 
B.50.2.9 
EPC Contractor shall prepare Mechanical Completion, including Pre-
commissioning manuals which include all relevant procedures, Specifications, 
technical data, quality control forms and system acceptance criteria, to 
Company satisfaction. These manuals shall be produced at least six (6) months 
before commencement of Pre-commissioning and submitted to Company for 
approval. A typical index of such manual is outlined in EXHIBIT B.53. 
 
B.50.2.10 
EPC Contractor shall prepare specific Quality Control forms outlining, stepwise, 
all relevant checks/tests required to complete Pre-commissioning within 
Mechanical Completion and Commissioning activities 
 
B.50.2.11 
EPC Contractor shall ensure that the design addresses the special 
requirements for the Systems and GOODS necessary to perform the Pre-
commissioning and Commissioning activities in a safe and smooth manner.

B.50.2.15 
EPC Contractor shall prepare detailed and comprehensive Operating, 
Maintenance and HSE Manuals (refer to EXHIBIT B.49 and B.29, respectively) 
which include valve-to-valve operating description for initial start-up, normal

Commissioning activities.

B.50.3 
EPC CONTRACTOR shall develop and submit to Company for approval, the 
TEST RUN procedure(s), include test logs, calculation methods, Laboratory 
tests, and sampling, testing and analysis methods wherever applicable. 
 
B.50.4 
EPC CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for the provision of “Training 
Manuals” for all training courses provided. EPC Contractor shall provide a 
procedure on preparation of training manual for Company review and approval.  
These manuals shall be so detailed in order that Company personnel can use 
them to provide additional courses after the facilities have been handed over.  
Completed manuals shall be submitted to each course attendee at least four 
weeks before the training session commences in order that the attendees may 
become fully familiar with the content of the course and will be retained by the 
attendee for future references.  All training materials must be in the English 
Language.
