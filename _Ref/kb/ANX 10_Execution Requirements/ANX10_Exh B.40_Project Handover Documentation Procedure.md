ATTACHMENT B.40.2.1
REQUIREMENTS FOR DIGITIZATION OF PIPELINE AND FOR
ON SITE PIPING WELD JOINT RADIOGRAPHIC REPORTS AND FILMS
All radiographic films and reports for pipeline and on site piping weld joints, and all other
weld joint NDE (non-destructive examination) (UT, MPI, Hardness etc..) and PWHT (post
weld heat treatment) reports, shall be handed over in an agreed digitized format on CD-
ROMIDVD (2 copies) with navigation facilities. This is additional to any other AGREEMENT
requirements for handover of these items. The CONTRACTOR shall submit to the
COMPANY a detailed proposal for this activity, with a minimum of three (3) VENDORS
being reviewed, who have proven software/experience in this field.
A trial/demonstration shall be made to COMPANY on the digitization system before
COMPANY approval.
CONTRACTOR shall prepare and submit for COMPANY approval a detailed procedure/
method statement for digitization work. This shall include, but not be limited to, the following
minimum requirements:
1. Digitization operators with certified formal training on the proposed digitization
equipment and work process.
2. List of equipment requirements with detailed specifications.
3. Digitization work process description and related precautions during work execution.
4. Provisions for verification and audit of digitization work.
5. Digitized radiographic film image resolution requirements.
6. Data structure and indexing system to facilitate navigation and retrieval of radiographic
film images or NDE reports for any specific weld joint.
7. DVD/ CD-ROM labelling details.
8. Provision of two sets of navigation software package along with licences for viewing
digitized radiographic film images, and related user training at COMPANY offices.
9. Quality records of the digitization work process and verifications/ audits on specific
record formats to be included in the procedure/ method statement.
10. Provision of two (2) fireproof safes for storage of the DVD/ CD-ROMs.
11. List of deliverables to COMPANY.
