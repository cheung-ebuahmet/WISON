B.14 
PROCUREMENT SERVICES 
B.14.1 
GENERAL 
CONTRACTOR shall perform all procurement activities necessary for 
completion of the WORK, including without limitation the activities defined in 
this EXHIBIT B.14 “Procurement Services”, EXHIBIT B.15 “COMPANY 
Procurement Guidelines and Requirements”, EXHIBIT B.16 “Selection of 
SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS” and EXHIBIT B.46 “Spare Parts and 
Equipment Data Capturing Procedure”. 
The requirements in this EXHIBIT B.14 shall, therefore, be read and construed 
in conjunction with the requirements stipulated in EXHIBIT B.15, EXHIBIT 
B.16 and EXHIBIT B.46 or with such other requirements as may be stipulated 
elsewhere in the AGREEMENT. Procurement activities shall include, but not 
be limited to, all activities relating to the procurement of all GOODS required 
for the WORK, and activities relating to the procurement by CONTRACTOR of 
any subcontracted services. 
The requirements of this EXHIBIT are applicable for all the GOODS to be 
procured for the subject PROJECT irrespective of if it is procured directly by 
CONTRACTOR or through its SUBCONTRACTOR. 
B.14.2 
PROCUREMENT OF GOODS 
B.14.2.1 
PLANNING 
 
B.14.2.1.1 
CONTRACTOR’s PROJECT Procurement Manager shall be responsible for 
mobilizing and establishing the PROJECT Procurement Team in line with 
CONTRACTOR’s proposed organisation chart and proposed key personnel 
down to the level of Coordinators, Expeditors and Traffic Leaders (including 
Custom Clearance Coordinator), which shall be subject to COMPANY 
approval. 
B.14.2.1.2 
Within thirty (30) days of the EFFECTIVE DATE, CONTRACTOR shall submit a 
detailed procurement plan to COMPANY for approval. The plan shall include a 
detailed schedule of procurement activities with respect to organizing the 
PROJECT procurement functions and their respective durations for all 
PROJECT materials requirements. This plan shall also be developed in 
conjunction with the PROJECT scheduling group for the timely and efficient 
procurement of GOODS. The plan shall define all procurement activities 
necessary to ensure that all packaged units, equipment items, and bulk 
materials are delivered to the SITE prior to their "Required at SITE" dates and 
that the construction requirements for materials are addressed in the proper 
sequence to complement planned field construction activities. The plan will also 
compliment construction/subcontracting plans to engage SUBCONTRACTORS 
for pre-selected portions of the WORK or WORK packages.

TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION SERVICES 
 
B.14.2.7.1 
GENERAL 
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for organizing, coordinating, and

GOODS, to include permits, escorts, load limits, signs and diversion construction.

import controls and port procedures.

of CONTRACTOR.

OVERSEAS TRANSPORTATION

directed by CONTRACTOR.

documents are for information purposes only and in no way release 
CONTRACTOR from any obligation.

SPARE PARTS AND SPECIAL TOOLS

INTRODUCTION 
 
CONTRACTOR shall procure all spare parts and special tools, and ensure

INSURANCE SPARES

store and maintain such insurance spares at its own cost.

TWO YEARS OPERATING SPARE PARTS (ON REIMBURSABLE BASIS)

list of two-years operating spares provided by the VENDORS on the G-SPIR 
package.  Until the two-years operating spares are transferred to the custody 
of COMPANY, CONTRACTOR shall appropriately store and maintain such 
spares at its own cost.
