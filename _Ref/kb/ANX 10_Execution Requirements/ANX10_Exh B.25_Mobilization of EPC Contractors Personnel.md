B.25.0 
INTRODUCTION 
This EXHIBIT outlines the procedure for mobilization of EPC contractor's 
personnel to the Project at the EPC Contractor Home Office or such other 
office(s) where Detailed Design Engineering will be carried out and at the 
SITE(S).  
B.25.1 
KEY PERSONNEL 
Prior to commencement of the Detailed Design Engineering or Fieldwork EPC 
Contractor shall propose and provide information on its Key Personnel and 
obtain Company approval prior to mobilization.  
B.25.1.1  
EPC CONTRACTOR Key Personnel are: 
1. Project Manager 
2. HSE Manager (Home Office) 
3. Senior QA/QC Engineer (Home Office / SITE) 
4. Project Engineering Manager 
5. Project Engineer(s) 
6. Senior Projects Control Engineer (Home Office / SITE) 
7. Procurement and Sub Contracts Manager 
8. Construction Specialist 
9. Commissioning Manager 
10. Lead Discipline Engineers 
11. Project Interface Coordinator 
12. HSE Lead – SITE 
13. Lead Procurement Engineer – SITE 
 
B.25.1.2  
Details required will be: 
B.25.1.2.1 
A comprehensive employee curriculum vitae which shall include, name, position 
in EPC Contractor's organization, job title for the Project, sex, nationality, 
qualifications and experience. 
B.25.1.2.2 
Anticipated mobilisation and demobilization dates. 
B.25.1.2.3 
Once approved by Company, EPC Contractor shall not replace or demobilise 
Key Personnel without prior written Company approval.

B.25.2  
LETTER OF ASSISTANCE 
B.25.2.1 
In accordance with Article 6 of the Agreement, where EPC Contractor requires 
Company assistance to obtain statutory permission for Personnel, Vehicles or 
Equipment to enter restricted areas at site, a Request for Letter of Assistance 
Form shall be completed by EPC Contractor and submitted to Company. 
B.25.2.2 
EPC Contractor shall allow at least ten working days for Company to prepare a 
Letter of Assistance for Personnel and 15 working days for construction plant/ 
equipment/ vehicles etc.  
B.25.2.3  
A proforma Request for Letter of Assistance is included as Attachment B.25.4. 
B.25.3 
PROCEDURE FOR ISSUING LETTERS OF ASSISTANCE 
The following minimum requirements shall be met by EPC Contractor to enable 
Company to issue Letters of Assistance in favor of EPC Contractor and their 
Sub-Contractors addressed to Oilfield Security Department: 
• 
Compliance with Company registration requirements.  
• 
Colour Copy of Abu Dhabi Municipality and Chamber of Commerce & 
Industry Licenses to provide services at Oil Fields to be provided. (Both in 
English and Arabic Version) 
• 
Copies of staff’s Abu Dhabi residence visas to be provided. Details of 
equipment and vehicles to be provided. 
• 
Copy of necessary insurance certificates for personnel and equipment, as 
applicable   
EPC Contractors/Sub-Contractors shall provide in advance, the actual 
requirements and details of activities along with the numbers of their staff and 
equipment. 
The guidelines for issuance of e-LOA is enclosed at Attachment-1. 
 
NOTE: Company has implemented online electronic system for implementation 
of Letter of Assistance. Company shall provide the details of this system upon 
award and EPC Contractor shall comply with the same.
