1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Computer-Aided Drafting procedure is to describe the minimum requirements
of CAD systems set-up and the procedure for producing new drawings for AD NOC Gas Processing.
All drawings produced by CONSULTANTS, CONTRACTORS and ADNOC Gas Processing in -
house shall comply with the requirements of this procedure unless it is extracted from SmartPlant
Tools. It is the responsibility of the concerned parties to familiarize themselves with the content
of this procedure and follow the same in their work whenever applicable.
2; -SCOPE
CAD procedure has been developed to cover the requirements set-up and guidelines for preparing
the new CAD drawings using the following systems:
> AutoCAD
> MicroStation
This procedure has been developed to achieve the following goals:
¢ To improve communication between the ADNOC Gas Processing and the contractors.
· To ensure drawings uniformity in all ADNOC Gas Processing drawings.
· To avoid time losses during projects activities.
This procedure shall replace all other AutoCAD/ Microstation drafting procedures and/ or
standards that may be currently in use by ADNOC Gas Processing and shall be the only reference
as ADNOC Gas Processing Drawing Standard for AutoCAD/ Microstation drafted drawings.

9. SIGNATURES
Drawing issuance takes place following any check prints and any drafting. The designer will need
to acquaint themselves with the back approver and enter their initials. Blue ink signatures are
entered on the original hardcopy of the drawing. The designer is responsible for entering this
information into the drawing title / revision block in the electronic file prior to issuance. Once
names are entered into the title block, they shall not be changed for subsequent revisions. The
only modifications to the revision block shall be the addition of new names / initials as the drawing
is taken to the next revision. Where revisions exceed 5 the top block will always be the latest
revision with the preceding revisions cascading down.

11.4 MEASUREMENT SYSTEM
11.4.1 LINEAR DIMENSIONS
When dimensions are given, they will be expressed in whole numbers, as follows:
38400 7150 890
Decimal numbers shall generally indicate meters: whole numbers shall indicate
millimetres.
E.g. Dimensions in meters not requiring decimal accuracy shall have the m suffix (24m).
11.4.2 DECIMAL POINT ACCURACY
Dimensions in millimetres requiring accuracy to a number of decimal places shall be
expressed with the mm (millimetre) suffix, as in the following example:
756.345mm.

All non-linear measurements such as areas and volumes shall be followed by the unit
symbol.
