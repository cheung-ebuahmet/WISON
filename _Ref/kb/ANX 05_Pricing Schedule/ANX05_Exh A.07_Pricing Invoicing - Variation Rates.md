The coverage of the costs under each of the headings is explained below:

	Home Office Services

The cost of Home Office Services covers all activities to be performed in/from the Home Office(s) of CONTRACTOR or its SUBCONTRACTORS including but not limited to project management, engineering, design, procurement, contracting, project control, etc.

It also includes the cost of the facilities to be provided by CONTRACTOR to COMPANY’s personnel in CONTRACTOR’s Home Office(s).

It also includes all procurement services (including, but not limited to, ordering, expediting and forwarding to SITE) for pre-commissioning and COMMISSIONING spares, two years Operating Spares, and Insurance Spares.

It also includes any costs associated with work away from CONTRACTOR’s Home Office but incurred in order to facilitate the Home Office activities, e.g. verification of existing plant (tie-ins etc).

	Equipment and Packages

This covers the cost of all equipment and packages of each and every kind and type to be supplied by CONTRACTOR for the PROJECT and includes the cost of freight up to SITE. Spares required during pre-commissioning and COMMISSIONING of the INSTALLATIONS form part of the cost of Equipment.

Equipment Cost includes but not limited to the following:

Cost of all Mechanical Equipment including the material cost for all process equipment to be installed in the permanent facility, such as tanks, compressors, drivers, pumps, exchangers and any corresponding internals, ladders and platforms.

Cost of all Electrical Equipment including the material cost for transformers, switchgear, packaged switch-houses and miscellaneous LV Equipment.

Cost of all Instrumentation Equipment including the material cost for RTU/SCADA, F&G, Computers and Analysers, etc as applicable.

Bulk Materials

Bulk Material supply cost includes cost of materials such as line pipes, pipe fittings, valves, wires and cables, cable trays light fitting, lighting poles, field instruments etc. i.e. all material not included in equipment supply is included in Bulk materials 

supply.  It is to be noted that material supply for civil, structural, painting and insulation is not included in Bulk Materials cost. This is considered part of construction cost.  Similarly all material supplied for construction of building including supply of HVAC equipment for building is considered part of construction cost

The above costs exclude design and engineering service costs, which are included in Home Office Services costs.

	Construction / Pre-commissioning / COMMISSIONING / TEST RUN

This covers the cost of labour, construction plant and other materials and consumables required for the fabrication, installation, pre-commissioning, COMMISSIONING and TEST RUN of the INSTALLATIONS. The costs of supplying water, power, compressed air, fuel, etc. are also included here as per the provisions of the AGREEMENT. Relevant communication and transportation resources and any other costs also form part of these costs.  

It also covers the cost of all necessary temporary SITE facilities (camp, accommodation, messing, offices, workshops, warehouses, relevant communications and transportation resources, site material handling facilities and other construction facilities) needed by CONTRACTOR for performance of the WORK.

Construction costs also cover the supply of all materials for civil, structural, insulation, painting and buildings, including supply of HVAC equipment for buildings.

	Field Management and Supervision

This includes the cost of all activities required to manage and supervise construction, pre-commissioning, COMMISSIONING and TEST RUN activities.  It covers each and every cost and expense such as manpower (expatriate and local hire), running costs of offices, camps, office equipment, travel, relevant communication and transportation etc. 

 In addition, it also covers the costs of VENDORS’ representatives needed during construction, pre-commissioning, and COMMISSIONING, as well as the cost of services by specialist parties including QA/QC/ Inspection, and costs of any other type in connection with SITE activities.

	Insurance Spares – Not to be quoted separately.

This includes the cost of Insurance Spares required to be supplied by CONTRACTOR as detailed in A.7.7.

	Cost of Training

This includes the cost of training as per the requirements stipulated in EXHIBITS A.17 and B.54, and as detailed in A.7.9.
