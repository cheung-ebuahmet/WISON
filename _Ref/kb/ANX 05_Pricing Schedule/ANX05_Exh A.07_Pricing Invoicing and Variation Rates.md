The coverage of the costs under each of the headings is explained below:
Home Office Services
The cost of Home Office Services covers all activities to be performed in/from the
Home Office(s) of CONTRACTOR or its SUBCONTRACTORS including but not
limited to project management, engineering, design, procurement, contracting,
project control, etc.
lt also includes the cost of the facilities to be provided by CONTRACTOR to
COMPANY ·s personnel in CONTRACTOR·s Home Office(s).
It also includes all procurement services (including, but not limited to, ordering,
expediting and forwarding to SITE) for pre-commissioning and COMMISSIONING
spares, two years Operating Spares, and Insurance Spares.
It also includes any costs associated with work away from CONTRACTOR·s Home
Office but incurred in order to facilitate the Home Office activities, e.g. verification
of existing plant (tie-ins etc).
Equipment and Packages
This covers the cost of all equipment and packages of each and every kind and
type to be supplied by CONTRACTOR for the PROJECT and includes the cost of
freight up to SITE. Spares required during pre-commissioning and
COMMISSIONING of the INSTALLATIONS form part of the cost of Equipment.
Equipment Cost includes but not limited to the following:
> Cost of all Mechanical Equipment including the material cost for all process
equipment to be installed in the permanent facility, such as tanks, compressors,
drivers, pumps, exchangers and any corresponding internals, ladders and
platforms.
> Cost of all Electrical Equipment including the material cost for transformers,
switchgear, packaged switch-houses and miscellaneous LV Equipment.
> Cost of all Instrumentation Equipment including the material cost for
RTU/SCADA, F&G, Computers and Analysers, etc as applicable.
Bulk Materials
Bulk Material supply cost includes cost of materials such as line pipes, pipe fittings,
valves, wires and cables, cable trays light fitting, lighting poles, field instruments
etc. i.e. all material not included in equipment supply is included in Bulk materials
