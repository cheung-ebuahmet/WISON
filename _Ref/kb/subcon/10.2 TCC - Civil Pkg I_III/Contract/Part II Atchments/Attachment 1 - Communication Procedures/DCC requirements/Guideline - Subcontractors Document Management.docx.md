* 1. Reason For Issue

All documents shall, depending on their class, pass through the following stages:

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Abbreviation** | **Reason for Issue** | **Description** |
| ICR | Issued for Company Review | Means documents or drawings are issued to COMPANY for review |
| IFI | Issued for Information | Means documents or drawings are issued for information only. |
| IFA | Issued for Company Approval | Means documents or drawings are issued to COMPANY for approval |
| IFE | Issued for Enquiry | Means documents are issued for Material Requisition. |
| IFP | Issued for Purchase | Means the documents are issued for Purchase Order, Subcontract or Letter of Intent. |
| IFC | Issued for Construction | Documents or drawings are ready for issuance to the site for construction. |
| ASB | Issued for As Built | When documents or drawings are certified after the completion of construction work, they will be submitted with this issue purpose. Digital or handwritten Signatures of each party are mandatory. |
| IFF | Issued for Final | Means documents are issued for final use. |

Eventually all technical documents have to reach the IFC / IFE / IFP status.

* 1. Review and Approval Process

### Class-1 Document:

* + - * Class and Category of Document to be indicated on the document.
      * First Issue to Company to be ICR.
      * In case of Code-1 comment on ICR, reissue as ICR until Code-2/Code-3 is received.
      * In case of Code-2/Code-3 comment on ICR, issue as IFA after incorporating comments (if Code-2). Specialist / Independent Review comments on Category-1 Documents should have been incorporated prior to submitting IFA.

Following Code-3 comment on IFA, document to be issued IFC (or IFP/IFE as applicable).

### Class-2 Document:

* Class of Document to be indicated on the document
* First Issue to Client to be ICR.
* In case of Code-1 comment, reissue as ICR until Code-2/Code-3 is received.
* Following Code-2/Code-3 comment, document to be issued IFC after incorporating Company comments (if Code-2).

In case IFC does not actually incorporate Code-2 comments, document to be reissued IFC after incorporating comments.

### Class-3 Document:

* Class of Document to be indicated on the document
* First Issue to be IFI
* In case of comments, document to be reissued IFC after incorporating comments.

In case IFC does not actually incorporate comments, document to be reissued IFC after incorporating comments.
