1 GENERAL
Introduction
This Design Basis describes the work to be performed for the design and preparation of the
architectural contract documents to be used for the complete construction and procurement for
Onshore buildings (including Islands) and Offshore buildings on platforms in the scope of work.
The CONTRACTOR shall develop a complete set of architectural contract documents which shall
include, but not be limited to, all necessary specifications, drawings, schedules etc. required to
construct and / or extend and / or refurbish the buildings, all based on this Specification and on
the drawings.
The Works shall be designed to ensure satisfactory operation in which continuity of existing
operations and service is the first consideration.
Purpose
The purpose of the document is to describe all works for the buildings identified in the contract and
conceptual drawings including, where relevant, the following:
a) Interior furnishings and furniture, including signage (external and internal).
b) Laboratory fixtures / cabinets, equipment, utensils, and tools.
c) Workshop equipment, storage shelving and tool cabinets.
d) Equipment and tools for light vehicle service station.
e) Exterior and interior lighting.
f) Personnel access to each building or facility structure, yards, and parking lots shall be through
pavements or walkways, and through landscaping (where appropriate for site location) wherever
possible.
g) Demolition as required.
h) Any items not listed above but required to accomplish the completeness as set out in the contract
shall also be considered.

6 TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
6.1 General Requirements
The buildings shall be designed and constructed to house the staff and equipment in order to
meet all the requirements of functionality, operation, safety, security, convenience, accessibility,
ergonomics, cost effectiveness, preservation, and sustainability.
CONTRACTOR shall study the design and method of construction of each building and select
the optimum method based on functionality, degree of pre-fabrication possible, cost and
maintenance requirements.
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for developing detailed architectural specifications and
deliverables, considering the following key performance parameters, as a minimum:
i. Performance requirements of materials / systems / assemblies
ii. Submittals
iii. Mock-up Requirements
iv. List of Approved Vendors
v. Local and imported materials / ease of availability
vi. Durability and ease of maintenance
vii. Spare part availability
viii. Quality Assurance Provisions
ix. Value Engineering / cost
x. Sustainability and ‘Green’ building requirements for the Project
xi. Energy optimization
xii. Acoustics / noise control
xiii. Net / Gross Area Ratio
xiv. Functional Proximity Chart and Design Efficiency Assessment
xv. Proximity of inter-related functions / separation of incompatible functions
xvi. Zoning requirements with respect to building heights / set-backs, if any
xvii. Passive and active fire protection design
xviii. Building footprints and potential for horizontal / vertical expandability with respect to the
Project site area and configuration
xix. Potential for Modular Design
xx. Delivery, Storage and Handling Provisions

9 GENERAL BUILDING SYSTEMS
9.1 Blast Protection Requirements
Buildings located within the effects of blast overpressure from potential explosion taking place in
the plant (new plant or existing plant) shall be designed to withstand the identified Blast Loads.
CONTRACTOR is responsible for developing a Project specific narrative for the building types
(e.g. ‘Normal’ construction, ‘Blast Resistant’ construction etc.) based on Fire and Explosion Risk
Assessment (FERA) / Quantitative Risk Analysis (QRA) results / recommendations, staffing
requirements, space requirements etc. for the Project under execution.
As minimum requirements:
i. Blast Resistant criteria shall be applied to Instrument Equipment Shelters (IES), Field
Auxiliary Rooms (FARs) and Control Rooms which are essential for safe and orderly
shutdown of a plant process after an explosion.
ii. Blast Resistant criteria shall be applied to other manned buildings and process buildings
housing critical equipment (such as Substations, Analyzer houses, etc.) subjected to a
Blast Loading (peak side-on overpressure) equal to or greater than 2.1 kPa.
The CONTRACTOR shall compare the above requirements with the results of the project specific
Fire and Explosion Risk Assessment (FERA) / Quantitative Risk Assessment (QRA) and propose
blast design criteria for COMPANY review / approval.
The following are the minimum general design guidelines for the buildings and their components
subjected to Blast Loads:
i. The buildings and their structural components shall be designed in accordance with AGES-
SP-01-003 Structural Design Basis - On Shore Specification.
ii. Buildings shall be limited to single storey construction and may have a basement if
sufficient space is not available; multi-storey construction shall not be used without prior
written approval from the COMPANY. In such cases, the CONTRACTOR shall submit a
necessary study to demonstrate the adequacy of this option in terms of design optimisation,
cost effectiveness, etc.
iii. Buildings shall be as compact as possible, rectangular in shape without re-entrant angles
or protruding canopies. Exceptions to these are permitted only with prior approval from
COMPANY.
iv. The roof shall be flat or have a maximum pitch of 10°. The overall height of the building
and the flat span of the roof shall be minimized to limit the effects of an explosion. Roofs
shall not be covered with gravel or loose concrete tiles or other objects that could become
flying hazards in the event of an explosion. Parapet heights shall be kept to a minimum.
v. Elevated floor slabs with open space under them shall not be allowed unless there is a
technical requirement for the same and adequate design measures are provided and
proven to be satisfactory.
vi. Air-conditioning units shall be located either on the ground floor or in the basement where
possible. Where this is not possible, roof mounted HVAC equipment may be considered
where agreed with COMPANY and where adequately protected from the effects of
potential blast by the inclusion of blast walls extended above equipment height and suitable
fixings / anchor points.

9.32 Anti-Termite Treatment

or renovation.
