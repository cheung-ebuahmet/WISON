Supply Water System
7.3.1 General
a. CONTRACTOR shall study the existing potable supply water network at site with its pipe size,
pressure availability, pipe material used etc., and propose suitable pipe size to be connected to the
existing pipe network to meet the project supply water demand and the tie-in location on the existing
main pipe network and obtain approval from COMPANY.
b. Based on the quantity of potable supply water produced at site, CONTRACTOR shall propose
independent storage tank below ground and/or at roof of building facility with water pressurization
pumps. Depending on end user/project requirement, additional filtration unit shall be proposed by the
CONTRACTOR to remove any suspended particles arising due to erosion, corrosion, etc. on the main
supply water network and obtain approval from COMPANY.
c. A water meter shall be installed before the supply enters the water storage tanks. The meter will be
complete with by-pass and isolating valves.
d. Piping and fittings shall be such that back-siphonage is excluded. The entire piping system shall be
capable of being drained at conveniently located low points. Dismantling points shall be provided at
easily accessible locations. All pipes shall be firmly fixed to the building.
e. Generally, the main supply water network shall be designed & sized to provide a minimum water
pressure of 2.0 bar (30 psi) until the tie-in/interface of new building facility in order to supply water
directly to the plumbing fixtures without having any additional pressurization pump.
f. When the water pressure in the main network/other source of supply cannot provide a residual water
pressure of more than 1.0 bar (15 psi or 103 kPa) to plumbing/sanitary fixture, after allowing for friction
losses and all other pressure losses, pressure booster pump with tank or other means to provide
pressure shall be installed in the building facility. When special plumbing/sanitary fixtures are installed,
which require residual pressure exceeding 1.0 bar., then the minimum residual pressure for the fixture
shall be provided by the booster pump. Pressure booster pump sets shall have variable speed pumps.
The pump set shall consist of two pumps; one run pump and one standby pump. Should one pump
fail, or should a low pressure persist, then the second shall automatically come into operation. The
pumping set shall have each pump controlled with individual pressure sensor and variable speed
pump. Pump sequencing and operation will be dependent upon low pressure persisting within the
system. Booster pump set shall have pressure vessel to satisfy low demand and to prevent the pump
from restart for low demand period. All non-return valves serving domestic water pressure booster
pump sets shall be anti-slam spring assisted type, to prevent hydraulic shock/noise on closure. The
pump set control panels shall contain the pump starters and controls.
g. When static water pressure in supply water pipe line is above 5.5 bar (80 psi or 552 kPa), a pressure
regulator preceded by an adequate strainer shall be installed on supply line to reduce & control the
pressure to all plumbing fixtures outlets in the building facility.
h. All taps shall be fitted with hot water supply on the left and cold water supply on the right as viewed
by the user.
i. Service valve shall be provided and located in accessible positions so as to enable the flow of water
to individual or groups of appliances to be controlled and to limit the inconvenience caused by
interruption of supply during repairs.
