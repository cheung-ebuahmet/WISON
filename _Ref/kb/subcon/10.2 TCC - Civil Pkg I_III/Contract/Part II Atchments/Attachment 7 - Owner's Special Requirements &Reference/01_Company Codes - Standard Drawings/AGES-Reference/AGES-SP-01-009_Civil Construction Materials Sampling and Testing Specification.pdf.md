GENERAL
Introduction
This specification provides the minimum requirements for sampling and testing of Civil construction
materials in ADNOC projects.
Purpose
This specification contains the minimum requirements for the accreditation procedures, type and
frequency of tests, personnel requirements, quality control measures and equipment calibration in order
for the CONTRACTOR or its SUBCONTRACTOR to establish a laboratory with the quantity and type of
equipment required to perform the tests. The purpose of the laboratory testing is to verify that the
earthwork, bituminous asphalt materials, paving, concrete, concrete materials, reinforcement and anchor
bolts conform to the Project Drawings and Specifications.
Civil construction materials not covered under this specification shall be tested in accordance with other
COMPANY / Project Specifications and material MANUFACTURER / SUPPLIER recommendations. All
procured items should be covered by the manufacturer’s accreditation.
Definitions and Abbreviations
