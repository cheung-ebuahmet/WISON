CHAIN LINK FENCE MATERIAL
All materials shall be new and shall conform to BS 1722 Part 10 unless noted otherwise herein.
Fabric
a. Fence fabric shall be galvanized and PVC coated in conformance with BS EN 10245-2.
b. All fabric shall be chain link, with a 50 mm diamond mesh woven continuously without splices. The
top and bottom selvedges shall be machine twisted with the wire cut on a bias to form a barbed
finish. The base metal of the fabric shall be 3.55 mm diameter steel with a minimum tensile strength
of 410 N/mm2 and shall conform to BS EN 10223-6. The overall diameter after PVC coating shall
be 4.75 mm.
c. Mesh for removable fence shall be zinc or Class A zinc alloy coated wire conforming to BS EN
10223-6 and shall be plastic coated. The size of the mesh shall be 50 mm x 50 mm. The galvanized
wire shall be 2.55mm in diameter and the overall diameter after PVC coating shall be 3.55mm. The
colour of the chain link mesh shall be green (RAL-6005) or approved color.

Barbed Wire
Each strand shall consist of two twisted strands each of not less than 3.0 mm wire with 4 barbs (points)
of not lighter than 2 mm wire spaced 60 ±15 mm apart. The barbs shall be wrapped so as to interlock
with both main stands. The barbed wire shall be galvanized and shall conform to BS EN 10223-1 (Type
BWE).
Line Wire
a. Line Wire shall be galvanized and PVC coated in conformance with BS 4102.
b. Line wire shall have a galvanized core of 3.55 mm diameter and PVC coated overall 4.75 mm
diameter in number of rows as shown on the COMPANY standard drawing
c. Line wire shall have a minimum tensile strength of 410 N/mm2
d. Ratchet winders fixed to assembly flanges of straining posts shall be used to strain line wires.
Stirrup Tie Wire and Staples
a. Stirrup Wire and Tie Wire shall be galvanized and PVC coated in conformance with BS 4102.
b. Stirrup wire shall be 2.5 mm diameter galvanized and PVC coated to overall diameter 3.55 mm to
secure line wire to all fencing posts.
c. Tie wire shall be 2.24 mm diameter galvanized and PVC coated to overall diameter 3.5 mm to
secure fence fabric to line wire at 300 mm centres.
d. “Hair-pin” staples for fastening down the bottom of the chain link fencing to the concrete sill beam
with 10mm diameter galvanized bar. They shall be cast into a depth of not less than 150 mm and
at a spacing not exceeding 750 mm. Hair-pin staples shall be threaded over the bottom row of
mesh. Method of fastening chain link fencing with hair-pin staples is subject to COMPANY approval.
Posts
a. Pipe or hollow section material shall conform to ASTM A-53, API 5L, BS EN 10255 or BS EN 10210-
2.
b. Post and Braces shall have a uniform hot-dipped galvanized zinc-coating to conform to BS EN ISO
1461. Zinc coating on articles of iron or steel hardware (such as Bolts and Hinges) shall conform to
BS EN ISO 1461.
c. Post and Braces shall have 1 mm thick PVC coating in an approved colour in conformance with
ASTM F668.
d. All tubular members shall be sealed against ingress of moisture.

Concertina Barbed Tape (For Type 1B Fence)
a. One concertina medium blade barbed tape, standard 600 mm diameter shall be provided on top of
the fence between the Y-shaped arms; for attachment and tie spacing MANUFACTURER’s
specification shall be followed. Attachment spacing shall be at 225 mm centres minimum.
b. Concertina medium blade barbed tape shall be 600 mm diameter, clipped version with 15mm tape

SS-304 stainless steel. It shall be permanently cold clenched around a 2.5mm dia. ASTM A764
galvanized core wire, class 3, finish 2.
c. Core wire shall have a tensile strength of 153 MPA. The barbed tape shall have a minimum of
230°wrap around the core wire.
d. The stainless steel strip extending out from the wire between barb clusters shall be 8.26 mm
minimum. These flanges shall taper off in the immediate vicinity of the barb cluster. Barb clusters
shall consist of 4 barbs spaced 100mm on centre. Each barb length, from tip to tip shall be 60 mm.
Concertina shall have (13, 19) barb clusters per loop, and each row shall consist of 33 loops. Each
row covers a maximum of 10 m with spacing of 300 mm ± 50 mm loop to loop.
