6.1 Doors General Requirements
a. In occupied buildings all main entrances and other entrances that are frequently used shall have
airlocks with adequately separated doors to control the indoor temperature.
b. Entrances shall be designed to prevent sand accumulation for buildings installed in desert conditions.
c. External doors should open outwards and in the direction of the escape route. Revolving doors and
sliding doors shall not be used for emergency exits.
d. Sliding doors shall be designed in such a way that an accumulation of dust and sand does not
influence the proper functioning of the doors. The tracks shall be easily accessible for cleaning.
Sliding doors shall be electrically operated, having a facility for quick disconnection of the electric
driver, after which easy manual opening shall be possible.
6.2 Materials Selection
a. The references of materials, specified in this document are intended to establish a standard of quality
or a level of performance and are not intended to prevent the use of equivalent or superior products.
b. Other references of materials are acceptable after the approval of the COMPANY for the proposed
application.
c. Colors and finishing tints are generally not specified in this document. When necessary,
manufacturer’s standard palette shall be provided by CONTRACTOR for the choice of the COMPANY.
