GENERAL

Introduction

This specification defines the technical requirements for the design, material, fabrication and erection
of Prefabricated or Pre-Engineered Metal Buildings/Shelters.

Purpose

This specification is to provide the minimum criteria for the design, material, fabrication, shipment and
erection  of  prefabricated  buildings/shelters  suitable  for  desert  conditions  and  marine  coastal
environment as outlined on the CONTRACTOR’S drawings, in the contract and in this specification.
System  is  to  include  the  coverings  and  accessories  required  for  a  completed  envelope.  This
specification does not address foundation and other building systems requirements such as Electrical,
HVAC,  Fire  &  Gas,  Instrumentation,  HSE,  Telecom,  Mechanical  System  (cranes),  etc.,  for  which
relevant discipline specifications shall be referred.

This  specification  does  not  address  special  pre-service  analysis  requirements.  If  it  is  proposed  to
construct  and  transport  the  building  unit  in  a  modular  form,  relevant  pre-service  analyses  shall  be
carried  out  by  the  CONTRACTOR  and  submitted  for  COMPANY  approval  along  with  execution
methodology.

The CONTRACTOR shall be fully responsible for the design and stability of prefabricated buildings.
