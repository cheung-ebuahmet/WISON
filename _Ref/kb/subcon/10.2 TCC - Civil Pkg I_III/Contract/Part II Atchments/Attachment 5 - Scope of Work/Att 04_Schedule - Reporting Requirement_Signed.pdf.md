5.2 Baseline Schedule
5.2.1 The Subcontractor shall further develop its Tender Schedule into a
comprehensive, resource-loaded Level 4 Baseline Schedule. This shall be
submitted for the Contractor's review and approval within fourteen (14) calendar
days of the Contract Effective Date.
5.2.2 In addition to fulfilling the Tender Schedule requirements, the Baseline Schedule
must incorporate:
• All critical Project Milestones.
• A detailed plan for preliminary works, site preparation, and mobilization.
• Procurement and delivery schedules for all key materials and equipment (if
applicable).
• A detailed work front handover and interface plan.
5.2.3 An updated Basis of Schedule narrative shall be submitted concurrently with the
Baseline Schedule.
5.2.4 The Contractor shall review the submitted Baseline Schedule within fourteen (14)
calendar days of receipt. Once approved, this Baseline Schedule shall serve as
the official benchmark for all progress control and performance evaluation.
5.2.5 The Contractor's review, comment, approval, or lack of response to any schedule
submission shall not relieve the Subcontractor of any of its obligations to comply
with the Contract requirements, nor shall it be construed as an acceptance of:
 Non-compliance with the KEY MILESTONE DATES.
 Any adverse modifications to the logic or durations within the Baseline
Schedule or its updates.
 Inaccurate logic, constraints, or unreasonable activity durations.
 Any schedule that imposes undue hindrance on the works of other
subcontractors.
 A waiver of the Contractor's rights to demand timely performance or to
exercise its remedies in the event of default by the Subcontractor.
5.2.6 The Baseline Schedule shall be updated weekly to serve as the Progress
Schedule, showing a comparison between the actual/forecast completion of all
activities and the baseline dates. Furthermore, the Subcontractor shall, with the
Contractor's approval, formally update the Baseline Schedule as a Recovery Plan
(with no change to the Completion Date) or a Revised Plan (only when the
