Attachment 2

Change Management

## INTRODUCTION

### This Attachment forms an integral part of the CONTRACT between CONTRACTOR and SUBCONTRACTOR. All capitalized terms used herein shall have the meaning as set forth in the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT. In case of conflict or inconsistency between the provisions set forth herein and the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT, then the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT shall prevail.

## CONTRACTOR’S INSTRUCTION

### CONTRACTOR shall be entitled to issue CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTIONS to SUBCONTRACTOR at any time and for any reason, provided always that only CONTRACTOR’s PROJECT MANAGER shall be entitled to issue and sign CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTIONS on behalf of CONTRACTOR.

### SUBCONTRACTOR shall be obliged to comply with all CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTIONS.

### A CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION may require SUBCONTRACTOR to provide a quotation to CONTRACTOR prior to proceeding with the subject matter of the CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION, in which case SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit a CHANGE PROPOSAL to CONTRACTOR in accordance with **CLAUSE 4** and shall thereafter only proceed with such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION upon receipt of a CHANGE ORDER in accordance with **CLAUSE 4.4**. For all other CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTIONS, SUBCONTRACTOR shall (subject to **CLAUSES 2.5 and 2.6**) comply with such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION within the time period as may be expressly set out in such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION, or (save where expressly stated otherwise) if no time is detailed, a reasonable period of time.

### For the avoidance of doubt, a CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION is not necessarily deemed to be a CHANGE. Save where an CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION requires SUBCONTRACTOR to first submit a CHANGE PROPOSAL to CONTRACTOR (in which case the first sentence of **CLAUSE 2.3** shall apply), if SUBCONTRACTOR is of the opinion that any CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION is or constitutes the basis for a CHANGE, SUBCONTRACTOR shall nevertheless (and subject to **CLAUSES 2.5 and 2.6**) proceed in accordance with the CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION, unless otherwise instructed in writing by CONTRACTOR’s PROJECT MANAGER, and SUBCONTRACTOR shall immediately notify CONTRACTOR with a CAN in accordance with **CLAUSE 3**.

### If in SUBCONTRACTOR’s opinion compliance with an CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION will have a detrimental effect on the WORK or SUBCONTRACTOR’s performance of the WORK (for example, due to missing protection against potential risks or incidents, or due to the quality of CONTRACTOR supplied materials or the quality of services to be provided by other SUBCONTRACTORs), is contrary to APPLICABLE LAW, or is not technically feasible, he shall within seven (7) DAYS of receipt of such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION give written notice to CONTRACTOR specifying such detrimental effect, and the CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION shall not take effect unless confirmed in writing by CONTRACTOR.

### Where CONTRACTOR issues a CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION on behalf of CONTRACTOR verbally (or otherwise than in writing), it shall be of no immediate effect unless and until CONTRACTOR confirms it to SUBCONTRACTOR in writing, whereupon such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION shall take effect from the date and upon the terms of such written CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION. If CONTRACTOR does not confirm such verbal CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION (or CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION issued otherwise than in writing) in the manner stated, but SUBCONTRACTOR nevertheless complies with it, then SUBCONTRACTOR shall not be entitled to a CHANGE ORDER and/or to any extension of time pursuant to **CLAUSE 7** (or to an adjustment, if any, to the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE) and/or to any additional payment in respect of such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION unless and until CONTRACTOR subsequently confirms it in writing (in which case SUBCONTRACTOR’s entitlement (if any) shall be subject to the terms of this THIS ATTACHMENT).

### CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTIONS will be numbered sequentially and shall be listed in the register to be maintained according to **CLAUSE 9**.

## CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION (CAN)

### If SUBCONTRACTOR considers that any CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION has been issued which, in the opinion of SUBCONTRACTOR, constitutes a CHANGE, then SUBCONTRACTOR shall, within seven (7) DAYS of the date of issue of such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION, notify CONTRACTOR by way of a CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION (CAN) in the form set out at **THIS ATTACHMENT, ATTACHMENT 1** stating the full reasons why SUBCONTRACTOR considers that such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION constitutes a CHANGE. Compliance with this **CLAUSE 3.1** shall be a condition precedent to SUBCONTRACTOR being entitled to any extension of time and/or any additional payment in respect of such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION. If SUBCONTRACTOR fails to submit a CAN within seven (7) DAYS of the date of issue of such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION as aforesaid, SUBCONTRACTOR shall not be entitled to any extension of time (or to an adjustment, if any, to the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE) and/or any additional payment in respect of such CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION.

### Within seven (7) DAYS after receipt of a CAN, CONTRACTOR shall either APPROVE or reject the CAN.

### Should CONTRACTOR APPROVE a CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION, then SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit a CHANGE PROPOSAL in accordance with **CLAUSE 4**.

### Should CONTRACTOR reject a CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION, then CONTRACTOR shall notify SUBCONTRACTOR thereof and shall advise SUBCONTRACTOR of the reasons for its decision.

### SUBCONTRACTOR shall either accept a decision of CONTRACTOR given pursuant to **CLAUSE 3.4** or it shall give notice in writing that it reserves the right to submit the matter to dispute resolution according to **ARTICLE 27 of the** **CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT**. Failure by SUBCONTRACTOR to provide such written notice within seven (7) DAYS from the date of issue of CONTRACTOR’s decision and/or failure by SUBCONTRACTOR to submit such matter to dispute resolution according to **ARTICLE 27 of the** **CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT** within thirty (30) DAYS from the date of issue of CONTRACTOR’s decision shall constitute a waiver of any and all further rights and remedies of SUBCONTRACTOR pursuant to the subject matter of such CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION.

### CANs will be numbered sequentially and shall be listed in the register to be maintained according to **CLAUSE 9**.

## CHANGE PROPOSAL AND CHANGE ORDER

### Within seven (7) DAYS after:

###### issue of an CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION which requires SUBCONTRACTOR to provide a quotation in accordance with **CLAUSE 2.3**; or

###### CONTRACTOR’s APPROVAL of a CAN pursuant to **CLAUSE 3**; or

###### CONTRACTOR’s issue of an omission of WORK notice pursuant to **CLAUSE 6**; or

###### SUBCONTRACTOR becoming aware that additional direct cost and/or expense have incurred as a result of the occurrence of a TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT pursuant to **CLAUSE 8**,

#### SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit to CONTRACTOR’s PROJECT MANAGER a CHANGE PROPOSAL in the form set out at **THIS ATTACHMENT, ATTACHMENT 2** which shall advise of the SUBCONTRACTOR’s opinion of the effect and consequences of **CLAUSES 4.1(i), (ii), (iii) or (iv)** (as applicable) on the CONTRACT PRICE (which shall be calculated in accordance with **CLAUSE 4.2**), and/or the effect and consequences of **CLAUSES 4.1(i), (ii), (iii) or (iv)** (as applicable) on KEY MILESTONS and the effect and consequences, if any, on the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE, as applicable.

### Any adjustment to the CONTRACT PRICE to be included in the CHANGE PROPOSAL shall be calculated based on a fair and transparent valuation by using an open book approach and the APPROVED rates set forth in **Attachment 6** or, where not applicable, the actual costs attributable to SUB-TIER CONTRACTOR(S).

### Within [seven (7) DAYS] from CONTRACTOR’s receipt of the CHANGE PROPOSAL, CONTRACTOR shall either APPROVE or reject such CHANGE PROPOSAL.

### If CONTRACTOR wishes to APPROVE the CHANGE PROPOSAL, then CONTRACTOR shall countersign the CHANGE PROPOSAL and provide a copy to SUBCONTRACTOR, provided always that only CONTRACTOR’s PROJECT MANAGER shall be entitled to sign and then issue CHANGE PROPOSALS on behalf of CONTRACTOR. Upon APPROVAL of a CHANGE PROPOSAL submitted in regard to **CLAUSE 4.1(i) to 4.1(ii)** CONTRACTOR shall issue a CHANGE ORDER in the form set out at **THIS ATTACHMENT, ATTACHMENT 3**. Upon APPROVAL of a CHANGE PROPOSAL submitted in regard to **CLAUSE 4.1(iii) to 4.1(iv)** the CONTRACT shall be amended by a CONTRACT AMENDMENT in the form set out at **THIS ATTACHMENT, ATTACHMENT 7**.

### CHANGE ORDERS represent the final adjustment for:

###### any and all amounts due or to become due to SUBCONTRACTOR for CHANGES referred to therein; and

###### any changes to the dates set out in **CLAUSE 4.1(v) to 4.1(vii)** (and any changes, if any, to the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE), it being understood that the final number of CHANGE ORDERS will not retrospectively justify an additional adjustment to the dates set out in **CLAUSE 4.1(v) to 4.1(vii)** (or additional adjustment, if any, to the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE) in general and with regard to a particular CHANGE ORDER; and

###### SUBCONTRACTOR further releases and waives all other claims, if any, for additional compensation regarding such CHANGE under this CONTRACT, including, without limitation, any rights SUBCONTRACTOR may assert or claim for additional compensation arising out of delays or disruption of the WORK as may have arisen prior to the date of issue of the CHANGE PROPOSAL and/or as may arise as a consequence of any WORK to be performed pursuant to the CHANGE ORDER.

### If CONTRACTOR does not agree with the content of the CHANGE PROPOSAL which has been provided by SUBCONTRACTOR, then CONTRACTOR may reject the CHANGE PROPOSAL and require that SUBCONTRACTOR submit a revised CHANGE PROPOSAL for APPROVAL within seven (7) DAYS:

###### if CONTRACTOR disagrees with the scope of WORK proposed in the updated CHANGE PROPOSAL revised and resubmitted in accordance with this **CLAUSE 4.5**, then CONTRACTOR may issue an CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION ordering SUBCONTRACTOR to implement (or continue to implement, as the case may be) the requested CHANGE and **CLAUSE 4.7** **and 4.8** shall apply accordingly;

###### if CONTRACTOR agrees with the scope of WORK proposed in the updated CHANGE PROPOSAL revised and resubmitted in accordance with this **CLAUSE 4.5** but disagrees with the effect on the CONTRACT PRICE and/or on the dates set out in **CLAUSE 4.1(v) to 4.1(vii)** (and/or with the affect, if any, on the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE), as applicable, then CONTRACTOR may issue an CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION ordering SUBCONTRACTOR to implement (or continue to implement, as the case may be) the scope of WORK set out in the requested CHANGE PROPOSAL and **CLAUSE 4.7 and 4.8** shall apply accordingly; or

###### if CONTRACTOR APPROVES the revised CHANGE PROPOSAL, **CLAUSE 4.4** shall apply.

### If CONTRACTOR, prior to accepting any CHANGE PROPOSAL, decides not to proceed with a CHANGE for which a CHANGE PROPOSAL has been provided (by withdrawing the CONTRACTOR’s INSTRUCTION to which the CHANGE PROPOSAL relates, or otherwise), then SUBCONTRACTOR shall have no claim arising out of, or in connection with, such proposed CHANGE or any failure by CONTRACTOR to proceed with such CHANGE.

### Should CONTRACTOR reject a CHANGE PROPOSAL (or should CONTRACTOR, as provided for in **CLAUSE 4.5**, disagree with the scope of WORK proposed in the CHANGE PROPOSAL, or the effect of the CHANGE PROPOSAL on the CONTRACT PRICE and/or on the dates set out in **CLAUSE 4.1(v) to 4.1(vii)** (and/or with the effect, if any, on the other dates in the TIME SCHEDULE), CONTRACTOR shall advise SUBCONTRACTOR of such rejection and shall give reasons for its decision.

### SUBCONTRACTOR shall either accept a decision of CONTRACTOR given pursuant to **CLAUSE 4.7** or it shall give notice in writing to CONTRACTOR that it reserves its right to submit the matter to dispute resolution according to **ARTICLE 27 of the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT**. Failure by SUBCONTRACTOR to provide such notice within seven (7) DAYS from the date of issue of CONTRACTOR’s decision and/or failure by SUBCONTRACTOR to submit such matter to dispute resolution according to **ARTICLE 27 of the** **CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT** within a further thirty (30) DAYS shall constitute a waiver of any and all further rights and remedies pursuant to the subject matter of such CHANGE PROPOSAL.

### CHANGE PROPOSALS will be numbered in accordance with its related CAN, or, if the CHANGE PROPOSAL is not related to a CAN, shall be given a new sequential number and shall be listed in the register to be maintained according to **CLAUSE 9**.

### CHANGE ORDERS will be numbered sequentially and shall be listed in the register to be maintained according to **CLAUSE 9**.

## COSTS AND EXPENSES AND OTHER MATTERS RELATED TO CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATIONS AND CHANGE PROPOSALS

### CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATIONS and CHANGE PROPOSALS shall only be deemed APPROVED if signed by CONTRACTOR’s PROJECT MANAGER.

### Except as otherwise agreed between the PARTIES, CONTRACTOR shall not be responsible for or pay for any costs, charges or expenses incurred by SUBCONTRACTOR in the preparation or modification of a CHANGE PROPOSAL or CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION, whether the CHANGE PROPOSAL or CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATION is APPROVED or not.

### APPROVED CHANGE ALERT NOTIFICATIONS are not an entitlement to payment. They are, however, the basis for preparation of a CHANGE PROPOSAL and respective CHANGE ORDER which may entitle SUBCONTRACTOR to payment in accordance with **CLAUSE 4.4**.

## OMISSION OF WORK

### CONTRACTOR shall have the right to issue at any time and for any reason to SUBCONTRACTOR an omission of WORK notice (OOWN) in the form set out at **THIS ATTACHMENT,** omitting any part or parts of the WORK.

### If an OOWN issued by CONTRACTOR pursuant to this **CLAUSE 6** omits any part or parts of the WORK to be completed by SUBCONTRACTOR:

###### the provisions of **ARTICLE 25 of the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT** shall apply to such part or parts of the WORK;

###### CONTRACTOR may thereafter carry out this work either itself or by engaging a THIRD PARTY;

###### SUBCONTRACTOR shall not be entitled to claim for any loss of profit, loss of contracts or any indirect or economic or consequential losses suffered by SUBCONTRACTOR arising out of or in connection with such omission of WORK; and

###### SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit to CONTRACTOR a CHANGE PROPOSAL in accordance with **CLAUSE 4**.

### OOWNs will be numbered sequentially and shall be listed in the register to be maintained according to **CLAUSE 9.**

## EXTENSION OF TIME DUE TO TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT(S)

### If SUBCONTRACTOR is delayed or is likely to be delayed by a TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT in a manner which will prevent it from achieving the anticipated date of WORK COMPLETION then SUBCONTRACTOR may claim an extension of time.

### Without prejudice to SUBCONTRACTOR’s obligations under **the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT**, to claim an extension of time SUBCONTRACTOR shall immediately upon becoming aware (and in any event not later than seven (7) DAYS after SUBCONTRACTOR became aware, or should have become aware) that the progress of the WORK is or is likely to be delayed by a TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT such that SUBCONTRACTOR may not achieve the anticipated date of WORK COMPLETION**,** submit to CONTRACTOR an EXTENSION OF TIME REQUEST (EOTR) in the form set out at **THIS ATTACHMENT** setting out:

###### its intention to claim an extension to the anticipated date of WORK COMPLETION**,** and SUBCONTRACTOR shall specify in its EOTR which such date are applicable to its claim;

###### fully detailed particulars of the delay and the occurrence causing the delay; and

###### the number of DAYS extension of time claimed to the applicable date or dates which are specified in its EOTR in accordance with **CLAUSE 7.2(i)**, together with the basis of calculating that period, including evidence that SUBCONTRACTOR will be delayed by one or more of the TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENTS (as the case may be) and identifying those TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENTS.

### It is a condition precedent to SUBCONTRACTOR’s entitlement to an extension of time to the anticipated date of WORK COMPLETION that:

###### SUBCONTRACTOR must give the EOTR required by **CLAUSE 7.2** strictly in accordance with the requirements of that CLAUSE; and

###### SUBCONTRACTOR must have actually been delayed in a manner which will prevent it from achieving the applicable date or dates set out in its EOTR issued in accordance with **CLAUSE 7.2**, and the relevant TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT(S) must have caused a delay to the CRITICAL PATH for the WORK.

### Within seven (7) DAYS after receipt of an EOTR, CONTRACTOR shall either APPROVE or reject such EOTR.

### Subject to the other provisions of the CONTRACT, if and to the extent that:

###### the applicable date or dates set out in the EOTR issued in accordance with **CLAUSE 7.2** are or will be delayed by one or more TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT(S), as determined by and with reference to the CRITICAL PATH of the WORK; and

###### the cause of delay does not arise out of a breach of this CONTRACT by SUBCONTRACTOR (including any defect or deficiency),

then SUBCONTRACTOR will be entitled to such extension of time to the applicable date or dates set out in the EOTR issued in accordance with **CLAUSE 7.2** that CONTRACTOR estimates to be fair and reasonable (and the TIME SCHEDULE shall be updated accordingly to reflect the impact (if any) of the extension of time on the other dates set out in the TIME SCHEDULE). The extension of time shall be included in a CONTRACT AMENDMENT. If the PARTIES fail to reach an agreement on the extension of time, SUBCONTRACTOR shall nevertheless proceed with the WORK but shall be entitled to submit such dispute for resolution in accordance with **the CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT**.

### Any extension to the applicable date or dates set out in the EOTR issued in accordance with **CLAUSE 7.2** which would otherwise have been granted under **CLAUSE 7.4** may be reduced to a lower period to be specified in the CONTRACT AMENDMENT to be issued pursuant to **CLAUSE 7.4** to reflect the extent to which:

###### SUBCONTRACTOR caused or contributed to the delay due to any breach, act or omission of SUBCONTRACTOR or any person for whom SUBCONTRACTOR is responsible;

###### SUBCONTRACTOR failed to use best endeavors to prevent the cause of the delay and to avoid, minimize or mitigate the consequences of the delay; and/or

###### any delay caused by one or more TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT(S) is concurrent with another delay for which SUBCONTRACTOR is responsible (in which case the delay caused by such TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT(S) shall not be taken into account when assessing SUBCONTRACTOR’s entitlement).

### Notwithstanding the provisions of this **CLAUSE 7**, SUBCONTRACTOR shall constantly use its best endeavours to prevent delay in the progress of the WORK, however caused, and to prevent the completion of the WORK being delayed or further delayed beyond the anticipated date of WORK COMPLETION. In the event of any delay, SUBCONTRACTOR shall do all that may reasonably be required to the satisfaction of CONTRACTOR to proceed with the WORK.

### For the avoidance of doubt, where SUBCONTRACTOR considers that it is entitled to an extension of time to the anticipated date of WORK COMPLETION as a result of the occurrence of a TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT, it shall issue an EOTR pursuant to the provisions of this **CLAUSE 7** and not pursuant to a CAN.

### EOTRs issued by SUBCONTRACTOR pursuant to **CLAUSE 7.2** will be numbered sequentially and shall be listed in the register to be maintained according to **CLAUSE 9**.

## ADJUSTMENT IN CONTRACT PRICE AS A RESULT OF TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT

### If SUBCONTRACTOR considers that it has incurred additional direct cost and/or expense as a result of the occurrence of a TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT which has materially affected the regular progress of the WORK, it may separately to the EOTR under **CLAUSE 7.2**, immediately upon becoming aware (and in any event not later than seven (7) DAYS after SUBCONTRACTOR became aware, or should have become aware) of the occurrence of such TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT, submit to CONTRACTOR a CHANGE PROPOSAL in accordance with **CLAUSE 4**. In such CHANGE PROPOSAL SUBCONTRACTOR shall specify the full reasons why SUBCONTRACTOR considers such TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT entitles SUBCONTRACTOR to an adjustment to the CONTRACT PRICE in respect of such additional direct cost and/or expense, including details of the amount of the adjustment to the CONTRACT PRICE claimed, the basis for calculating that amount, and evidence of all costs claimed.

### Compliance with **CLAUSE 8.1** shall be a condition precedent to SUBCONTRACTOR being entitled to any payment of additional direct cost and/or expense incurred by SUBCONTRACTOR in respect of any such TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT(S).

### Subject to the other provisions of the CONTRACT, and provided always that the cause of the TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT:

###### was beyond the reasonable control of SUBCONTRACTOR;

###### has materially affected the regular progress of the WORK; and

###### does not otherwise arise out of a breach of this CONTRACT by SUBCONTRACTOR (including any defective WORK),

###### the CONTRACT PRICE will be adjusted by the amount of the reasonable and proper direct costs actually incurred by SUBCONTRACTOR as a direct result of the TIME ADJUSTMENT EVENT as determined by CONTRACTOR and included in the respective CONTRACT which has been issued pursuant to CLAUSE 7.5 and which shall have the effect with regard to the CONTRACT PRICE as set out in **CLAUSE 4.4**. If the PARTIES fail to reach an agreement on the adjustment of the CONTRACT PRICE, SUBCONTRACTOR shall nevertheless proceed with the WORK, but shall be entitled to submit such dispute for resolution in accordance with **the** **CONDITIONS OF CONTRACT**.

### Any adjustment to the CONTRACT PRICE which would otherwise have been granted under **CLAUSE 8.3** may be reduced to a lower amount to be specified in the CONTRACT AMENDMENT to be issued pursuant to **CLAUSE 8.3** to reflect the extent to which the matters referred to in **CLAUSES 7.6(i) to 7.6(iii)** apply.

## REGISTERS

### SUBCONTRACTOR shall keep a register of each document issued during the progress of the WORK which belongs to one of the categories set out in this ATTACHMENT below. The register shall be updated and provided by SUBCONTRACTOR to CONTRACTOR as an attachment to SUBCONTRACTOR’s monthly report.

## Priciple of change settlement

### SUBCONTRACTOR shall be responsible for the correctness of the claimed value in CHANGE PROPOSAL submitted to CONTRACTOR. Provided the claimed value in a CHANGE PROPOSAL is higher than 30% of the CHANGE ORDER value bilaterally finally agreed, CONTRACTOR shall back charge CONTRACTOR 10% of the final CHANGE ORDER value as liquidated damage. The liquidated damage will be offset in the payment of CHANGE ORDER.
