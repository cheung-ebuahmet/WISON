1. INTRODUCTION
ADNOC Code of Practice COP-V4-02 ‘Work Equipment Risk Assessment and Control’ [Ref ‎1]
requires Group Companies to ensure that all work equipment used is fit for purpose,
properly maintained, serviceable and any associated risks in using the equipment are
identified and removed or adequately controlled.
During past few years, GASCO had a number of serious incidents (incl. fatalities) related to
work at height. Fatal accidents resulting from work at height pose a serious challenge to the
industry that we must control.
2. PURPOSE
This document defines the control measures to prevent and mitigate fall of personnel and
material from work at height.
3. SCOPE AND APPLICATION
In accordance with above purpose, the standard applies to all personnel who:
 Gain access to and work at height at 1.8 meter or more;
 Plan work at height;
 Supervise work at height;
 Construct, put in place and/or inspect structures and equipment to facilitate work at
height (which is covered under separate GASCO Scaffolding Procedure [Ref ‎4]; and
 Inspect and maintain fall protection equipment.

Please refer to Appendix-A.

6. ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES
6.1 Vice President (Plant and Pipelines) and Project Managers
Plant and Pipeline VPs and Project Managers are responsible to ensure that protective
measures are in place to prevent fall of personnel and material from work at height.
6.2 Site Project / Maintenance / Planning Department
Responsible to ensure that:
 All work at height is planned, assessed, organised and appropriately supervised;
 Scaffold, work platforms and structures are designed and constructed for purpose and
GASCO Scaffolding procedure is applied while constructing structures and platforms;
 Equipment for work at height are inspected by authorised people;
 The risks from falling objects are properly controlled;
 Those involved in work at height (such as contractors) are trained. They know about
risks, necessary precautions and their actions during emergency; and
 Current register of site authorized scaffolders and inspectors is updated and maintained.
6.3 PTW Authorisers
Responsible to ensure that:
 Personnel supervising and working at height know about the risks, necessary
precautions and their actions during emergency;
 Visually inspect the platforms before start of each day of work including validity of
scafftags; and

 Do regular patrols, spot check the compliance, intervene and stop work if permit
conditions and precautions are not followed and report non-compliances on GIRS as
unsafe conditions.
6.4 Person in Charge of Work (PICW)
Responsible to ensure and verify that:
 It is safe to start work at height;
 Activity risk assessment / PTW precautions are applied and communicated to the
workforce before and during the work; and what to do in case any work environment
conditions change during the work and stop the work when it is unsafe; and
 Ensuring that no uncertified ladders, platforms and scaffolds are used by the execution
crew.
6.5 Persons working at height
Employees and contractors are responsible to:
 Know the risks of working at height;
 Comply with the precautions as mentioned in the activity risk assessment of Permit to
Work;
 Wear fall protection equipment;
 Not let tools and material drop off the height;
 Not use ladders, platforms, scaffolds which are not certified; and
 Report their health condition to PICW, if unwell.
7. REQUIREMENTS
Definition of work at height : All work that is executed at 1.8 meter and above the ground
level in which there is a risk of a person falling from any height from through, into or onto a
place or structure.

A‎place‎is‎‘at‎height’‎if‎a‎person‎could‎be‎injured‎falling‎from‎it,‎even‎if‎it‎is‎at‎or‎below‎
ground level. (Ref: Abu Dhabi Emirates EHS management system COP 23.0).
7.1 Planning of work at height
7.1.1 All work at height shall be properly planned and risk assessed with involvement of the work
executer.
7.1.2 The construction of the work platform / scaffold shall be fit for purpose so that the required
operations can be performed safely at all times. This includes but not limited to following
considerations:
 Loading of the structure e.g. men, equipment and materials;
 Environmental conditions such as wind, storm, rain, heat, etc.;
 Forces imposed upon it by any means of access which is connected to it;
 Forces imposed upon it by any lifting or winching operations in which the high structure
serves as an anchor point, counterweight or tie point;
 Working conditions (e.g. Illumination, temperature);
 Risks to the safety of all those at the work place where equipment is to be used;
 The distance to be negotiated for access and egress;
 Distance and consequences of a potential fall;
 Duration and frequency of equipment use;
 Need for easy and timely evacuation and rescue in emergency. Larger platforms shall
have more than one access/egress for escape purposes;
 Any additional risk posed by the use, installation or removal of work equipment or by
evacuation and rescue;
 Provision of safety net under work platforms so that severity of fall injury would be
reduced;
 Used in a manner that prevents accidental displacement;

 At least be 600mm wide so as to permit free passage of persons and safe use of
equipment and material;
 Constructed so that the surface has no gaps likely to cause injury or through an object
could fall to any person below and without any tripping and slipping hazard;
 Provided with guardrails, toe-boards, handholds and footholds as necessary to prevent a
person falling; and
 Sited on surfaces which are stable and attached to the structures which are secure and
of sufficient strength and rigidity.
7.2 Erection and Dismantling Requirements
Scaffolds shall be erected, moved, dismantled, or altered only under the supervision and
direction of an authorized person.
7.3 Maintenance and Inspection requirements
An authorized person shall inspect all scaffolding, mobile platforms, ladders, fall protection
devices, anchorage points and harnesses.
7.4 Operational Requirements for working at height
General : Each site or plant operation team shall identify any fall hazards and put in place
controls that may be present while accessing plant/equipment for the operating and
maintenance staff.
7.4.1 Operation team will ensure following aspects before approving activity risk assessment:
 Fencing to prevent person falling from edges or objects falling on to them and must be
sufficiently high to prevent falls over or through it.
 Covering to prevent the person from falling into tanks, pits, vats, sumps etc.
 Fixed ladder of sound construction, properly maintained by ensuring that all access
equipment is properly and regularly maintained.

 Safe Working Platform with appropriate toe boards and guard rails shall be provided to
prevent falling objects from height.
7.4.2 Following persons shall be excluded from working at heights: (Ref: Abu Dhabi EHS manual)
1. Suffer from vertigo or who are afraid of heights;
2. Not fit enough to undertake climbing activities;
3. Suffer from dizziness; and
4. Have a physical shape or weight which may affect the safe operation of working at
height
7.4.3 A person at height if potentially exposed to risk due to sun glare or reflection, toxic,
flammable gas release shall be provided with adequate PPE and Life Saving Equipment.
7.4.4 Personnel shall wear fall protection equipment 100% of the time. Safety harness with double
lanyard having shock absorber shall be used. Personal fall equipment to be attached to a
stable and acceptable tie-off point using fit for purpose harness and lanyard. Stable and
acceptable tie off points shall be communicated to all personnel who need to know these
points.
Fall protection is not required in following situations:
 Climbing up or down ladders less than 6.1m with 3 point contact.
 Using a ladder climbing safety device such an inertial reel fall arrestor when climbing up
or down un-caged ladders 6.1 meter or longer.
Fall protection equipment and ladders shall be visually inspected before each day of use.
Safety harness shall be available where required and personnel trained in their use.
7.4.5 Area below work at height shall be barricaded with clear display of safety warning signs.
7.4.6 Establish exclusion zone and enforce authorized access to the area of work. Work shall be
stopped when people traverse the exclusion zones. It will be ensured that personnel
required shall be in exclusion zones such as platform erectors, persons holding ladders and
banks man, etc. wear hard hats and other personal protection equipment.

7.4.7 Tools and equipment used at heights shall be prevented from fall by securing them with
lanyards. It will be ensured that personnel use bolt bags and tool carriers to carry small
items and tools when working at height.
7.4.8 Hand tools and equipment shall be fully secured. Tools, equipment shall be kept at such
locations that they do not represent a tripping hazard and cannot fall or be knocked down
the structure;
7.4.9 Rescue arrangements shall be in place to deal with in case any person falls and become
suspended by their safety harness.
7.4.10 Housekeeping and cleaning of work platform shall be ensured at all times. Upon completion
of the day's work, all ropes and tackles shall be securely tied back to a permanent structure.
7.5 Requirements for Ladders
[Extracted from Ref : Abu Dhabi EHS Manual]
 Ladders shall be industrial rated;
 Able to extend at least 1 m higher than the highest level that need to be accessed;
 The slope shall be maintained between 70 and 80 degrees (1m horizontal for 4m vertical
ideally);
 Capable of supporting the greatest load to be imposed;
 No longer than thelengths specified below:
o 6.1 meters for an industrial stepladder
o 9.0 meters for a single ladder
o 15 meters for an industrial extension ladder
 Self-made ladders or access equipment is prohibited;
 Ladders or step-ladders shall not be used in the event of high winds or inclement
weather;

 Ladders shall be inspected before use. Defective and damaged ladders shall be removed
from service;
 Ladders shall not be used near the edge of an open floor, penetration, or an scaffolding
to gain extra height;
 Ladders shall not be setup in a passageway, doorway or driveway or other place where a
person, vehicle or crane lifted load might strike it, so as to cause instability or collapse;
 Where a ladder is left unattended, signs shall be provided warning against unauthorised
use of ladder;
 Work from ladder shall not be carried out directly over people;
 Combined weight of person and tool when using a ladder shall not exceed 120 kg unless
ladders is designed for such a‎load‎as‎per‎manufacturer’s‎instructions;
 There should be appropriate, stable and non-slippery support for the base of ladders.
7.6 Requirement for Personal suspension equipment
Please refer to section 6.3 of ADNOC COP V04-02 on specific requirements for personal
suspension equipment.
7.7 Requirements for Mobile Elevated Work Platforms
[Extracted from Ref : Abu Dhabi EHS Manual]
Following specific requirements shall apply for the selection and use of MEWP:
7.7.1 MEWP such as scissor lifts, hydraulic platforms, cherry-pickers shall only be operated by
persons who have been trained in their use by‎the‎manufacturers/supplier’s representative.
7.7.2 MEWP shall be examined at six monthly intervals or major repair whichever is earlier, by
authorized person. Copies of such certification shall be kept for record purpose by the
equipment custodian.
7.7.3 The Safe Working Load (SWL) shall be prominently displayed on the MEWP.

7.7.4 Personnel working from MEWP shall wear safety harness securely connected to a suitable
anchor point on the platform.
7.8 Requirements for Roof Work
Where work on roofs constructed of fragile materials is undertaken, fall protection shall be
provided to the people involved in the maintenance of the skylights covers on the
fragile/false roof of the buildings such as warehouses.
8. COMPETENCY AND TRAINING REQUIREMENTS
Definition of Authorized Person : An authorized person is the one who meets GASCO
qualifications of erecting, installing and supervising platforms, scaffolding structures and
whose name appears in site register of authorized persons as per Section 8.2 below.
8.1 Training for Personnel Working at Height
A site training program shall be provided to ensure that all persons involved in work at
height acquire the understanding, knowledge and skills necessary for the safe performance
of all duties.
GASCO Training specifications SP-42 shall be used for training content.
8.2 Site Authorised Scaffolders/Inspectors
GASCO Staff and Contractors appointed as scaffolders and inspectors shall comply with
qualification criterion specified in Abu Dhabi Emirates EHS manual for Work at Height. Site
Civil Maintenance Dept./Project Dept. will maintain a register of such certified staff after
proper verification of their competences.
9. PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT AND INDICATORS
Level-1 audit conducted/year by each site with no‎“HIGH”‎finding.

APPENDIX - A : TERMS AND DEFINITIONS
Terms Definitions
ALARP As low as reasonably possible
Anchorage Point A secure point of attachment for lifelines, lanyards, or deceleration devices.
An anchorage must be capable of supporting a minimum dead weight of five
thousand (5,000) pounds (2,268 kilograms) for each person attached to it.
An anchor point is often a beam, girder, column, or floor.
COMAH Control of Major Accidental Hazard report
Fall Arrest System A system used to arrest a person's fall, consisting of an anchorage,
connectors, or body harness and may include a lanyard, deceleration device,
lifeline, or combination of these.
Harness A design of straps which may be secured about the employee in a manner to
distribute the fall arrest forces over at least the thighs, pelvis, waist, chest,
and shoulders, with a means for attaching it to other components of a
personal fall arrest system.
Lanyard A flexible line of rope, wire rope or strap which has a connector at each end
for connecting the body harnesses to a deceleration device, lifeline, or
anchorage point.
Lifeline A vertically suspended rope with one end attached to a stationary object
(such as a structural member), capable of supporting at least five thousand
(5000) pounds (2,268 kilograms) of dead weight and the other end attached
to a lanyard or safety harness.
MEWP Mobile Elevating Work Platform
PICW Person in Charge of Work
PTW Permit to Work
Shock Absorber Device built into a fall protection device to limit or reduce the forces applied
to the falling body, consisting of a tear web type fabric, designed to slow the
rate of fall and reduce the force applied to the body to less than 6 kN.
SWL Safe Work Load
