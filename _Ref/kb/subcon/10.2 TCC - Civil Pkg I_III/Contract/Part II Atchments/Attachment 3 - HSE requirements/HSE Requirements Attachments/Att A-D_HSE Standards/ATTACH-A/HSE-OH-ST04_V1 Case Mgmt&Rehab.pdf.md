1 INTRODUCTION
Case Management and workplace rehabilitation is a managed process involving early intervention with
appropriate and timely services based on assessed needs which is aimed at maintaining and returning the
injured or ill employees to suitable/restricted/alternate employment until complete recovery including claim
settlement, as applicable. It is seen as a system in which the employee of primary importance is the injured
or ill personnel (IP), that every reasonable and effective attempt is made for return of employee from injury
and/or illness to their pre-injury state.
2 PURPOSE
This Occupational Standard specifies ‘mandatory requirements for workplace rehabilitation and return-to-
work to ensure that employees receive timely, appropriate treatment and benefits, including assistance in
returning to their normal duties after a workplace injury and/or illness.
3 SCOPE
This Standard stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to ADNOC Group (Directorates & Functions
at HQ, Group Companies and Affiliates) and its Contractors.
ADNOC Group and Contractors shall ensure that all expectations listed herein are fully understood,
implemented and thoroughly monitored.
4 LAWS AND REGULATIONS
UAE Legislations applicable to this Standard includes but not limited to:
(a) Federal Law No. 8 (1980), Chapter V: Safety, Protection, and the Health and Social Care of the
Employees; Article (91).
(b) Ministerial Order No. (32), 1982 – Determination of Preventive Methods and Measures for the
Protection of Workers from the Risks of Work.
(c) FANR (REG-24 Version 1) – Basic Safety Standards for facilities and activities involving Ionizing
Radiation and other than in Nuclear Facilities.
(d) UAE Federal Law No 24 of 1999 (Protection and development of the Environment).
(e) UAE Federal Law No 1 of 2006 (Organization and Controlling the Use of Radiation Sources).
(f) Federal Law No. 8 (1980), Chapter VIII: Compensation for Occupational Injuries and diseases; Article
(142 -153).
ADNOC Group shall ensure that their activities and the activities of their contractors comply with all relevant
Federal and Abu Dhabi laws and regulations at all times, including any that may be introduced after the
publication of this Standard.

7 REQUIREMENTS
CASE MANAGEMENT
Occupational health case management is the process of assessing, planning, facilitating and evaluating an
employee’s occupational health needs in order to achieve overall employee health in a cost-effective and
efficient manner.
The Case Management for an employee commences from the time of joining ADNOC Group/Contractor until
exit from the Company. It allows case managers to coordinate care and foster a timely & healthy return to
work.
Case Management will enable occupational health team to:
Integrate the Site Clinic Visit and HSE Incident Management process post identification/occurrence of

any injury/illness during the course of employment to enhance communication and ensure record
accuracy;
Record and track an employee’s progress related to occupational and non-occupational

injuries/illnesses including their absences, work restrictions and expected return date;
To ensure timely and evidence based validation of classification of all types of occupational and non-

occupational injuries/illness, including but not limited to fulfilling UAE Labour Law No. 8 Guidelines
[Ref 9];
Streamline regulatory compliance and respond to regulatory and corporate body inquiries on the spot

by having validated case details readily available;
Directly access integrated disability duration guidelines and injury/illness classification guidelines for

each specific diagnosis to reference evidence-based supporting documents and other important
information regarding the relevant medical condition.

REHABILITATION
Workplace rehabilitation is defined as a managed process involving early intervention with appropriate and
timely services based on assessed needs and which is aimed at maintaining injured or ill employees in, or
returning them to, suitable/alternate available employment. It is seen as a system in which the person of
primary importance is the injured or ill employee and that every reasonable attempt is made to return the
employee from injury and/or illness to their pre-injury state [Ref 6].
Rehabilitation is part of occupational health case management and is required to ensure rapid and effective
recovery of an IP as identified during the periodic/transfer/extension/post injury illness return to fitness
medical examination and health screening and surveillance examinations assessments (refer to ADNOC
Health Screening and Surveillance Standard HSE-OH-ST05)
Rehabilitation is meant for cases where workplace injuries, and/or illness that are likely to result in a disability,
or have led to disability or death; and include a process to ensure injured/ill personnel (employee) are not
dismissed from employment solely or mainly because of their injury as well as appropriately compensated
as per Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation regulations, which includes Federal Law 8 for 1980.
ADNOC Group/ Contractor shall provide suitable duties to employees during rehabilitation and return-to- work.
Suitable duties (also known as light or alternative duties) shall be utilized when an injured/ill personnel
(employee) can perform some duties but cannot perform all of their normal day-to-day duties until complete
recovery as applicable.
Suitable duties shall be meaningful tasks selected from the injured/ill employee’s normal task or another
comparable role. Suitable duties shall be agreed between the employee and the ADNOC Group/Contractor
and shall take into account the employee’s normal job, age, education and training, work experience, the
nature of the injury, restrictions and other information as per the recommendation from the treating LHCP/
ADNOC Group Medical Committee.
The ADNOC Group and Contractor Rehabilitation and Post injury/illness Return-to-Work is depicted in the
above described Case Management and Rehabilitation Process Flow Chart in Figure Nos..7.1.1 and 7.1.2.
