1. INTRODUCTION
This ADNOC Standard sets out the minimum requirements for Contractor Welfare by providing welfare
management initiatives as stipulated in this Standard. The objective is to improve contractor welfare
conditions in their work locations and camps, including accommodation, recreation, catering, medical and
other facilities. It also focus on the remuneration, wages, leaves and end of services requirements that
may affect contractor employee performance.
The activities of ADNOC Group and their Contractors are carried out in diverse areas across United Arab
Emirates spanning from onshore to offshore; some of which are accessible while others are in remote
locations. Irrespective of location of its activities, any operating ADNOC Group or its Contractors have a
Duty of Care to ensure that all reasonable and practicable steps are taken to maintain the on-going
welfare, wellbeing, health and safety of its employees and visitors.
Human welfare shall take into consideration living environments and attendant services for Contractors.
All accommodation facilities and welfare arrangements with respect to Contractor shall achieve at least
the minimum requirements set out in this Standard in order to avoid adverse physical and moral effects
on personnel. Conversely, Public Health, Emergency and First Aid facilities shall be suitable and sufficient
to meet the needs of Contractors at any ADNOC location.
Employee Welfare is a fundamental requirement for any employer that also has responsibilities for good
living conditions, catering, accommodation and remuneration. The main elements involved in Contractor
Welfare management are illustrated in below Figure 1.1.

2. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Standard is to:
Define the minimum Welfare Management objectives to be met;

Develop a strategy for proactive management of Contractor Welfare;

Highlight the benefit of effective proactive approaches, particularly prior to tendering, mobilization

and during the execution of works;
Describe a planned approach to Management of Contractor Welfare that will ensure a continual

improvement in welfare performance for all Contractor activities and facilities;
Describe the roles and responsibilities of key personnel in Contractor Welfare Management;

Provide Contractors with Welfare standards and best industry guidance to ensure that the Health

and Welfare of their employees are not affected;
Address key aspects of Welfare including but not limited to; accommodation, remuneration, camp

facilities, medical emergencies, first aid facilities and control of pests.
3. SCOPE
This document stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to ADNOC Group (ADNOC and Group
Companies) and its Contractors.
ADNOC Group and Contractors shall ensure that all expectations listed herein are fully understood,
implemented and thoroughly monitored.
This Standard is applicable to Welfare aspects for all types and forms of contract services within the
ADNOC Group. The term Contractor as previously defined also extends to any sub-contractor engaged
by the Contractor to perform contracted activities.
The overall objective of this Standard is to:
Instil mutual welfare management awareness; both the Contractor and the Contract Administrator

shall be mutually aware of both parties' minimum obligations to welfare management and these
obligations shall be within mutually agreed contractual terms;
Provide means to monitor the implementation of Contractor Welfare requirements;

Provide prescribed means to rectify welfare management non-compliances or in extreme cases

exercise intervention and stop the work if required;
Ensure clarity between ADNOC Group and Contractor regarding responsibilities; and

Determine the Welfare standard related to accommodation, transportation, medical and other

facilities for Contractors and means to monitor the same.
4. LAWS AND REGULATIONS
ADNOC Group and their Contractors shall ensure that their activities comply with all relevant location
specific (where ADNOC operations are conducted), Federal and Abu Dhabi laws and regulations at all
times as applicable, including any that may be introduced after the publication of this Standard.
It is expected that ADNOC Group and its Contractors, as a minimum meet, or else exceed the
requirements of these regulations in any way that is applicable to their specific facilities and operations.

7.7. ADNOC DRUG AND ALCOHOL POLICY [REF 5]
The management of risk factors including misuse of drugs and alcohol in ADNOC Group is a serious
issue that extends beyond physical safety to include impairment to decision making. Poor judgment in a
high-stakes situation could result in injury to personnel and even death, substantial damage to assets/
aircrafts and the environment. When proper judgment is impaired by substance use and key decisions
shall be made, the risks increase dramatically.

workplace and regardless of the type of work being performed.

alcohol in ADNOC Group, as a serious misconduct. Abuse of alcohol, use of illegal drugs, or misuse of
legal drugs (such as, prescription medication) can impair performance at work, can be a serious threat
