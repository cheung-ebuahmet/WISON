1  PROJECT DESCRIPTION

1.1

Introduction

The  Ruwais  Sulphur  Handling  Terminal  2  (RSHT-2)  is  owned  by  ADNOC  and  operated  by
ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in
the  Ruwais  Industrial  Area.  RSHT-2  facility  is  designed  to  handle  bulk  granulated  sulphur
coming  from  Shah  and  Habshan  plant  via  railcars  and  transported  via  new  federal  railway
(Union Railway) to Ruwais Terminal (SHT-2).

Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2 (Ruwais
Sulphur Granulation Plant-RSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of receiving
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This new plant
will be located to the East of the BOROUGE terminal-3 & to the west of RSHT-2 as depicted
in Fig.1.1 below.

Figure 1: New RSGP Site Location near Existing RSHT-2 Facility

1.2  Project Objective and Scope

The  primary  objective  of  the  RSGP  Development  project  at  RSHT-2  is  to  accommodate
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves
constructing  new  infrastructure  for  liquid  sulphur  receiving  storage  and  transfer,  sulphur
forming (Granulation), Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and
export to existing ship loaders. The project also includes implementing dust control systems,
sulphur  remelting  unit,  utility  systems  and  integration  with  existing  facilities.  The  developed

1.3  Purpose of This Document

The  purpose  of  this  procedure  is  to  stipulate  CONTRACTOR’s  minimum  requirements  to
SUBCONTRACTORs for the processes pertaining to Inspection & Testing during Construction
in order to ensure that Inspections and Tests are planned, arranged, conducted and recorded in
a systematic manner to meet project quality requirements.

This  procedure  outlines  the  minimum  requirements  for  inspection  and  testing  of  materials,
components,  equipment  and  permanent  installations  that  shall  be  conducted  during  the
construction, pre-commissioning & commissioning stages.

This procedure shall be used along with PR-5312-6000-QA-0001 Project Quality Plan.

7  MATERIAL QUALITY CONTROL

7.1  General

CONTRACTOR will perform Field Receiving Inspection of materials and equipment. Once the
CONTRACTOR  issue  or  dispatch  materials  and  equipment  to  the  SUBCONTRACTOR  for
assembly, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall ensure a proper storage and preservation of equipment
and components in accordance with all requirements included in the Subcontract and the best
professional practices, including protection against weather hazards, water, humidity, sand, fire,
theft,  vandalism,  etc.  SUBCONTRACTOR  shall  allocate  dedicated  preservation  crews  in
sufficient number to check preservation compliance.

The  SUBCONTRACTOR  shall  address  in  the  written  procedure  all  contractual  provisions  for
receiving  inspection,  storage,  maintenance  and  preservation,  traceability  and  handling  of
materials and equipment supplied by CONTRACTOR.

and be available during audits/assessments conducted by CONTRACTOR Site QC Team and
COMPANY.

7.2  Material Identification and Traceability

SUBCONTRACTOR  shall  maintain  an  identification  procedure  for  materials,  parts  and
components, including partially fabricated assemblies. Identification shall be addressed either
by  using  tags,  stamps,  color  coding,  stencils  and  labels  or  by  traceability  records  throughout
fabrication, erection, installation, repair, modification and use of materials and equipment. The
location and the method of identification will not affect the function or quality of the item being
identified. Verification of correct identification of material, parts and components, shall be made
and documented prior to installation.

10.3  Inspection & Test Reports

SUBCONTRACTOR is responsible to maintain and update the inspection and test records.

Daily  entry  is  required  for  all  the  inspection  and  test  records.  Details  shall  be  provided  to
COMPANY upon request. SUBCONTRACTOR shall provide a weekly/ monthly status report to
demonstrate that entry has been maintained and is up to date, CONTRACTOR shall provide the
same to COMPANY in weekly/ monthly report.

3rd Party Reports shall be issued within 5 working days upon completion of related inspection
and/or test.

These  inspection  &  test  reports  shall  be  maintained  and  submitted  to  COMPANY  as  part  of
Turnover Document.

10.4  Statistical Analysis Inspection & Test Reports

SUBCONTRACTOR shall  conduct  statistical  analysis  of the  percentage  of  inspections  / tests
that are rejected and/or missed causing reschedule per discipline.

The  maximum  percentage  for  rescheduled  shall  be  5%  per  discipline,  and  the  maximum
percentage for rejections per discipline shall be 3%.

If the figures are exceeded, SUBCONTRACTOR’s Site Quality Manager shall conduct detailed
analysis to identify the root causes so that necessary remedial action can be undertaken.
