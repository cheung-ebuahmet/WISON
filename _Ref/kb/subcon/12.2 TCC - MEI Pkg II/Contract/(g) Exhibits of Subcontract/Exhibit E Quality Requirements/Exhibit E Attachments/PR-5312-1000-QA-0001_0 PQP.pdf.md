1 PROJECT DESCRIPTION
1.1 Introduction
The Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) is owned by ADNOC and operated by
ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in
the Ruwais Industrial Area. RSHT-2 facility is designed to handle bulk granulated sulphur
coming from Shah and Habshan plant via railcars and transported via new federal railway
(Union Railway) to Ruwais Terminal (SHT-2).
Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2 (Ruwais
Sulphur Granulation Plant-RRSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of receiving
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This new plant
will be located to the East of the BOROUGE terminal-3 & to the west of RSHT-2 as depicted
in Fig.1.1 below.
Figure 1: New RSGP Site Location near Existing RSHT-2 Facility
1.2 Project Objective and Scope
The primary objective of the RRSGP Development project at RSHT-2 is to accommodate
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves
constructing new infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur
forming (Granulation), Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and
export to existing ship loaders. The project also includes implementing dust control systems,

9 RISK MANAGEMENT
9.1 General
The management of project risks deals with uncertainties throughout the project. This enquires
a structured approach / strategy that shall be documented in a risk management procedure.
Project risk management shall be carried out in accordance with the approved risk
management procedure.
The structured risk approach / strategy consists of
➢ Determining risk sources and categories
➢ Define the parameters used to analyze and categorize risks, and the parameters used to
control the risk management effort.
Uncertainties are also related either to the project processes or to the project's product. The
risk‐related processes are :
➢ Risk identification,
➢ Risk assessment,
➢ Risk treatment, and
➢ Risk control
The risk‐related processes aim to minimize the impact of potential negative events and to
take full advantage of opportunities for improvement. All units shall bring risk base thinking
approach to become pro-active and preventive in their respective areas.
9.2 Risk Identification
Risk identification shall be performed at the initiation of the project, at progress evaluations
and other occasions when significant decisions are made.
9.3 Risk Assessment -Analysis/ Evaluation
Risk assessment is the process of analyzing and evaluating identified risks to the project
Processes and to the project's product.
9.4 Risk Treatment
Risks are handled and mitigated through mitigation plan, where appropriate, to reduce Adverse
impacts achieving objectives. Solutions to eliminate, mitigate, transfer, share or accept risks,
and plans to take advantage of opportunities shall preferably be based on known technologies
or data from past project experience.
9.5 Risk Control
Throughout the project, risks shall be monitored and controlled by an iterative process of risk
identification, risk assessment and risk treatment.

➢ Reviewing nonconformities (including Customer complaint);
➢ Determining the cause of nonconformities;
➢ Evaluating the need for action to ensure that nonconformities do not recur;
➢ Determining and implementing action needed;
➢ Records of the results of action taken;
➢ Reviewing corrective action taken.
The preventive action eliminating the cause of potential nonconformities in order to prevent
their occurrence shall be appropriate to the effects of the potential problems, and the procedure
shall include;
➢ Determining potential nonconformities and their cause;
➢ Evaluating the need for action to prevent occurrence of non-conformities;
➢ Determining and implementing action needed;
➢ Records of results of action taken;
➢ Reviewing preventive action taken.
Relevant organization shall request appropriate organization in charge to issue Corrective
Action Request (CAR).

issue CAR which requests the receiving organization to take necessary action in due date
designated on the CAR.
CAR receiving organization shall prepare document describing the cause and action plan or
the result of action taken, and request CAR issuing organization to review it.
CAR receiving organization shall take necessary action within due date, confirm resolution of
problems by self-evaluation and transfer the CAR with attaching evidence document of the
result.
