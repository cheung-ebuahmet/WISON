02CODEM1.Cleaning&DEGREASING&Pre-passivation
1. WorkDefinition
This work item is defined as the chemical cleaning, degreasing and/or pre-passivation of
processpipingandequipmentforallFacilities/Plants.
The SUBCONTRACTOR shall be fully responsible for providing all manpower, supervision,
equipment, chemicals, temporary facilities, accessories, small tools and consumables required
throughout the entire pre-commissioning period as specified in the Schedule, prior to
Mechanical Completion.Chemical cleaning shall strictly comply with all applicable codes,
procedures,andspecifications.
2. MeasurementMethodandUnitQuantities
The quantities defined herein should be cubic meter (M³) of net volume of process pipe and
equipment required for chemical cleaning. Wastewater disposal shall not be measured
separatelybutdeemedtobeincludedinunitprice.
Theunitprice(percubicmeter)shallbefixedandshallnotbesubjecttoanyadjustmentdueto
the elevation, size, or location of piping and equipment, or due to any auxiliary works or
materialsusedduringthechemicalcleaningactivities.
