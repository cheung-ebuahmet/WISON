CODE N. COMMISSIONING& PRE STARTUP

CODE N1. External Service Fee

This work code is defined as commissioning& Pre startup work for mechanical work, E&I work in
accordance with specifications and drawings. The definitions of Pre-commissioning and
Commissioning shall be in accordance with the Company’s Requirements as specified in Exhibit
B.38.

N1.1 Man-hour for Assistance Work

1) Definition

This work item shall include as follows

 Assist Vendor Specialist(s) / Contractor(s) employed by Owner and/or Contractor


Provisional Supervisor, Labor, Construction Equipment, Tool and Consumables to complete
the commissioning work
Supply, Installation and removal of all the necessary temporary and scaffolding materials and
whatsoever required for the completion of the work.



 Manhour rate shall include all the premium of Overtime, Holiday, and Sunday working hours

to be paid in accordance with applicable relevant Local Laws.

 All labor used for changes and which do not resemble the classifications listed in Appendix D-
4 – All Inclusive Labor Rates, must be approved by Owner/ Contractor prior to their use.
 All Inclusive Man-hour Rates shall include, but not limited to, all wages, payroll burdens,
fringe benefits, travel and subsistence allowances, insurance, overhead and profit, bonuses and
forms of extra payment, vacations, protective and/or special clothing and construction
consumables.

2) Measurement Method and Unit of Quantities.

Daily Man-hour record sheet shall be jointly confirmed and signed by Owner and/or EPC Contractor
and the Man-hour rate shall be applied for measurement of and payment for the work.

No measurement shall be made for Overtime, Sunday, Holliday working allowance separately.

Any outstanding, incomplete, defective or punch list works attributable to the SUBCONTRACTOR,
including but not limited to leftover works, omissions, rejected works, cleaning, rectification and
reinstatement required during or after trial run and commissioning, shall be fully executed and
cleared by the SUBCONTRACTOR at its own cost.No additional measurement, re-measurement, or
payment shall be made for such works, and all associated costs shall be deemed included in the
Subcontract Unit Rates and Price.
