I1.2 Anti-corrosions
1. General Work Description:
The section covers following items, and term used hereinafter has been defined as:
Exterior surface corrosion prevention on piping & equipment above ground, either Painting or
Coating, is described as “Coating/Painting”
For Under Ground piping & other equipment where needs coating/wrapping layers for exterior
surface corrosion prevention, either Coating or Wrapping, described as “Coating/Warpping”.

is described as “Lining”.
