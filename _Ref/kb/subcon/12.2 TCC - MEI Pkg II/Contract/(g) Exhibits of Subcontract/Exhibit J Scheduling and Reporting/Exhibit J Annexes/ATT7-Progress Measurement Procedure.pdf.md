1 PROJECT DESCRIPTION
1.1 Introduction
The Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) is owned by ADNOC and operated by
ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in
the Ruwais Industrial Area. RSHT-2 facility is designed to handle bulk granulated sulphur
coming from Shah and Habshan plant via railcars and transported via new federal railway
(Union Railway) to Ruwais Terminal (SHT-2).
Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2 (Ruwais
Sulphur Granulation Plant-RSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of receiving
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This new plant
will be located to the East of the BOROUGE terminal-3 & to the west of RSHT-2 as depicted
in Fig.1.1 below.
Figure 1: New SGP Site Location near Existing RSHT-2 Facility
1.2 Project Objective and Scope
The primary objective of the RSGP Development project at RSHT-2 is to accommodate
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves
constructing new infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur
forming (Granulation), Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and
export to existing ship loaders. The project also includes implementing dust control systems,
sulphur remelting unit, utility systems and integration with existing facilities. The developed

6.3 WEIGHTED VALUE SYSTEM
6.3.1 Each activity or deliverable listed in the respective Planning Register will be assigned esti-

6.3.2 Activity or deliverable weight factors shall be computed based on the estimated manhours/cost

whole project.
6.3.3 The weight factors thus computed from Planning Registers shall be rolled up to Level-2 and
Level-1 by Area / Phase / Discipline etc. in line with the PROJECT Work Breakdown Structure
(WBS).
6.4 PROGRESS MEASUREMENT
6.4.1 In case of Home Office Services and Manufacturing & Delivery activities, progress against
each activity / sub-activity / deliverable level shall be based on assigning certain pre-defined %
progress representing the achievement of specific intermediate Milestone (Control Point) re-
quired to complete the activity / deliverable. Partial completion of any intermediate Milestone
(Control Point) does not entitle any progress claim against the specific intermediate Milestone.
6.4.2 In case of Construction & Pre-commissioning and Commissioning & As built, progress against
each activity / sub-activity level shall be measured based on the quantity of work completed as
a percentage of the total quantity of work to be completed.
6.4.3 Overall progress of each project Area / Phase / Discipline etc. shall be computed by multiply-
ing the activity % progress by its weight factor and thereafter summing up the products.
6.4.4 Overall progress for the whole project shall be calculated by integrating the progress against
each project Area / Phase / Discipline etc. as described above, with the respective weightings.

6.6 GROUND RULES FOR PROGRESS MEASUREMENT AND PROGRESS REPORTING
6.6.1 Following activities are considered as support functions and will be excluded from the Project
Progress Measurement System. This would mean that the effort expended against the follow-
ing activities will not be considered for progress computation:
a) Project Management.
b) Project Control.
c) QA/QC
d) Contract Management
e) Mobilization / Demobilization.
6.6.2 Effort expended towards installation of temporary facilities required if any, shall not be consid-
ered for progress computation unless specified otherwise in the PROJECT progress measure-
ment system.
6.6.3 Progress against detail engineering activities shall be measured and reported only against the
identified deliverables. Any effort expended by engineering and other personnel on the activi-
ties, which are not specific to any particular deliverable viz. Site visits, Engineering Supervi-
sion & Coordination, Participation in Meetings, Preparation of MOMs etc. shall be excluded
from the Progress Measurement and Reporting System.
6.6.4 List of intermediate Milestones (Control Points) identified at ATTACHMENTS – 2 to 8 is ge-
neric in nature and will be adopted to different deliverables / activities without changing the
weightings assigned to the intermediate Milestones.
6.6.5 If any intermediate Milestone (Control Point) identified in ATTACHMENTS – 2 to 8 is not appli-
cable to a specific activity / deliverable, progress will not be claimed against that Control Point
and progress will become applicable only on achievement of its subsequent Control Point.
6.6.6 If any document identified in the Engineering Document Deliverable Register (EDDR) is re-
jected after COMPANY review, such rejected document’s status shall revert to its previous
control point and weighted progress against such document shall be adjusted accordingly.
Similar approach will be followed in case of Procurement Services Register also, if any TBE is
rejected after company review.
6.6.7 For all Procurement activities, progress shall be measured separately against each Material
Requisition (MR) for the Control Points prior to placement of order.
