8.3 Delay Analysis and Variance Analysis
CONTRACTOR will monitor the planned versus actual start/complete date of activities to anal-

When any delay is predicted or happens, CONTRACTOR will analyze the impact on succes-
sors and project schedule based on critical activities, total float and free float.
1) If it is a critical activity, the delay will impact successors and project schedule. CONTRAC-
TOR will take corresponding measures as below:
➢ Improving the activity procedures and shortening the duration.
➢ Increasing labors to recover the delay.
➢ Improving the construction plan and using advanced construction techniques and
measures.
2) If it is not a critical activity, follow the next steps.
➢ When the absolute value of this delay is more than total float, this delay will impact
successors and project schedule. CONTRACTOR will make suitable adjustments as
per the severity of this impact. The duration shortening measures will be taken to-
gether in order to reduce the negative impact on the project schedule.
➢ When the absolute value of this delay is equal or less than the total float, this delay
will not impact project schedule.
➢ When the absolute value of this delay is more than free float, this delay will impact
successors. CONTRACTOR will make suitable adjustments as per the successors’
bearing ability.
➢ When the absolute value of this delay is equal or less than free float, the delay will not
impact successors and project schedule. It is no need to make any adjustment.
