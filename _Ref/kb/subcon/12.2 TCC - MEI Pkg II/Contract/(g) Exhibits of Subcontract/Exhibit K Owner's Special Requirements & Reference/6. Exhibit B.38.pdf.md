B.38.1 MECHANICAL COMPLETION INCLUDING PRE-COMMISSIONING
B.38.1.1 CONTRACTOR shall provide a detailed MECHANICAL COMPLETION manual
for COMPANY's review and approval no later than six (6) months prior to the
scheduled date of MECHANICAL COMPLETION. The MECHANICAL
COMPLETION manual shall cover all work required for MECHANICAL
COMPLETION including without limitation diagrams showing the sequence of
construction, inspection, testing, pre-commissioning, training activities,
MECHANICAL COMPLETION checklists and MECHANICAL COMPLETION

Attachment B38.8.10 hereto and Ready for COMMISSIONING checklists for

For the avoidance of doubt, MECHANICAL COMPLETION is considered to
incorporate the associated pre-commissioning activities set out in EXHIBIT B.53.
CONTRACTOR shall note that COMPANY policy prohibits the introduction of
hazardous substances before MECHANICAL COMPLETION for any SYSTEM /
unit / plant. The introduction of hazardous substances at MECHANICAL
COMPLETION for any SYSTEM / unit / plant will require COMPANY full Permit
to Work SYSTEM to be implemented.
CONTRACTOR shall note that hazardous substances shall include all chemicals,
hydrocarbons, inert gases, liquid sulphur, steam, high pressure water or oil
SYSTEMS and any other substances that constitute a danger to personnel or
equipment.
B. 38.1.2 The MECHANICAL COMPLETION manual shall be organised in an integrated
format to link all individual inspections to each test pack, subsystem and
SYSTEM(S) of the INSTALLATION. All records of MECHANICAL
COMPLETION activities shall be filed in the MECHANICAL COMPLETION
manual.

B.38.2.5 Faultless Right First Time Start-Up (FRFTSU)
COMPANY emphasizes the importance of a faultless start-up program for the
PROJECT.
Objective
COMPANY and CONTRACTOR have the sole project goal of delivering the
Facility on time, without fault, and fully capable of efficient and safe first time right
operational service. This encompasses a systematic approach to ensure a
successful COMMISSIONING, start-up and first cycle operation of the facility. It
means for instance that it is expected that more than 99.9% of the functionality
needs to be first time right on demand.
Responsibilities and Organization
CONTRACTOR will coordinate and manage implementation of the FRFTSU
program throughout all phases of EPC execution of the PROJECT.
CONTRACTOR shall establish a suitable organization, plans, reporting routines
and management systems to efficiently implement the review and pro-active
action process of the FRFTSU.
CONTRACTOR shall clearly allocate roles and responsibilities covering both the
discipline specializations and the complete WORK scope. Further,
CONTRACTOR shall nominate a senior specialist responsible for implementation
and monitoring of all issues raised regarding FRFTSU.
CONTRACTOR shall conduct the WORK in such a way to enable
accomplishment of first-time- right start-up and operation of the facilities. A
database shall be maintained by CONTRACTOR and made available to
COMPANY. COMPANY may separately raise risk issues and instruct
CONTRACTOR to address.
Program definition
The formalized program to achieve the objective of world-class
COMMISSIONING, start-up and operational performance for the total PROJECT,
including first cycle operation will be implemented by the CONTRACTOR. The
FRFTSU will develop the process by which risk to this objective will be identified,
assessed and addressed during the engineering and site implementation in a
pro-active manner.
Schedule
CONTRACTOR shall start the FRFTSU program as from the EFFECTIVE DATE
and develop and submit for COMPANY consent latest 1 month after the
EFFECTIVE DATE an Implementation Plan to describe how risks will be
mitigated. This plan should be part of the standard PROJECT work processes.
