* 1. Risk Control

If the COSHH Assessment concludes that there are risks to health from storage or use of the product, then action shall be taken to prevent or reduce the risk to an acceptable level. Due consideration must be given to:

* + - 1. Substance Elimination: changing the process or activity so that the hazardous substance is not required or generated.
      2. Reduction of quantities stored or used.
      3. Substitution with a safer alternative or a safer form of the same substance (for example, pellets in place of powder, or more dilute solutions).
      4. If prevention of the hazard through one of the above means is not reasonably practical then a control strategy shall be selected from the following list (singly or in combination):
* Total enclosure of the substance (such as a glove box);
* Partial enclosure and local exhaust ventilation;
* General ventilation (Including natural ventilation if effective);
* Special procedures.
* Reliance on personal protective equipment (PPE) such as respiratory protection should only be used as a last resort if all the above strategies result in an unacceptable level of risk. However, PPE shall be worn, as appropriate, commensurate with the requirements of the activity being conducted.
