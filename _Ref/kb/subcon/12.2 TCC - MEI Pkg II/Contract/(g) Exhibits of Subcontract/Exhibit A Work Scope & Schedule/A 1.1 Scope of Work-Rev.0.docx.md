General
 General Description of the Project
The Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) is owned by ADNOC and operated by ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in the Ruwais Industrial Area. RSHT-2 facility is designed to handle bulk granulated sulphur coming from Shah and Habshan plant via railcars and transported via new federal railway (Union Railway) to Ruwais Terminal (SHT-2).

Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2 (Ruwais Sulphur Granulation Plant-RSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of receiving 9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This new plant will be located to the East of the BOROUGE terminal-3 & to the west of RSHT-2 as depicted in Fig.1.1 below.


Figure 1: New RSGP Site Location near Existing RSHT-2 Facility
Project Objective and Scope
The primary objective of the RSGP Development project at RSHT-2 is to accommodate 9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves constructing new infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur forming (Granulation), Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and export to existing ship loaders. The project also includes implementing dust control systems, sulphur remelting unit, utility systems and integration with existing facilities. The developed facility aims to ensure efficient and environmentally sound sulphur processing, support ADNOC's sulphur production, and maintain operational safety and reliability.
The overall scope includes the following units & infrastructure (but not limited):
Liquid sulphur receiving for Hail & Ghasha 
Liquid sulphur storage and transfer 
Sulphur forming includes filters, day vessels & pumps and Enersul granulation package
Granulated sulphur transfer and storage 
Stacking and reclaiming, associated system inside Linear storage
Integration to existing RSHT-2 linear storage & ship loading system
Sulphur remelting package 
Dust suppression and dust extraction packages
Biocide injection package 
Waste water collection, treatment & disposal system
Sewage collection, treatment & disposal system
Evaporation/settlement pond
Air compressor package, driers, receivers & distribution system
Process & potable water storage, transfer & distribution system, Chlorination package 
Steam reduction system & distribution system
Condensate collection, transfer, coolers & polishing units
Utility interfaces for power, steam, condensate/water, nitrogen from existing Borouge facility
Utility Interfaces for process water & potable water from existing GUP/GUP header.
Utility interface for fire water from existing RSHT-2 facility.
