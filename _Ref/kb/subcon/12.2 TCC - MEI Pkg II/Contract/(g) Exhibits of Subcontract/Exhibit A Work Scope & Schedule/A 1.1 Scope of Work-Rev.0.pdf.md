1 General
1.1 GeneralDescriptionoftheProject
The Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) is owned by ADNOC and operated by
ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in
the Ruwais Industrial Area. RSHT-2 facility is designed to handle bulk granulated sulphur
coming from Shah and Habshan plant via railcars and transported via new federal railway
(Union Railway) toRuwaisTerminal (SHT-2).
Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2
(Ruwais Sulphur Granulation Plant-RSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of
receiving 9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This
new plant will be locatedtothe East of the BOROUGEterminal-3& to thewest of RSHT-2as
depictedin Fig.1.1below.
Figure1: NewRSGPSiteLocationnear Existing RSHT-2Facility
1.2 ProjectObjectiveandScope
The primary objective of the RSGP Development project at RSHT-2 is to accommodate 9,900 TPD of
liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves constructing new
infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur forming (Granulation),
Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and export to existing ship loaders.
The project also includes implementing dust control systems, sulphur remelting unit, utility systems
and integration with existing facilities. The developed facility aims to ensure efficient and
environmentally sound sulphur processing, support ADNOC's sulphur production, and maintain
operationalsafetyandreliability.
