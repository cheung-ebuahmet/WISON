CONTRACTOR and SUBCONTRACTOR to be hereinafter jointly referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party"

Whereas, ADNOC GAS OPERATIONS AND MARKETING – L.L.C. (hereinafter referred to as the “Owner”) wishes to construct Sulphur Granulation Plant at Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) for Hail & Ghasha Project in Ruwais, UAE (hereinafter called the “Project”) and has awarded the EPC Agreement for the Project (hereinafter referred to as the “Prime Contract”) to Contractor;

Whereas, the Contractor requires the construction of Mechanical, Electrical, and Instrumentation Installation Works for the Project execution, including but not limited to structural steel, piping (including underground pipelines), equipment, painting, insulation, electrical works, instrumentation, telecommunication, fire protection, and HVAC works (“Works”); and

Whereas, Contractor is willing to engage the Subcontractor execute and complete the Works, including with respect to the engineering, procurement, construction, installation and testing in relation to the Works; and

Whereas, Subcontractor wishes to perform the Work (as defined below) associated with the Project in accordance with the terms and conditions of this Subcontract (as defined below); and

Whereas, Subcontractor represents that it is willing, qualified and capable of performing the Work in compliance with all such terms and conditions.

Now, Therefore, Contractor and Subcontractor hereby agree as follows:

# Article 2 Performance of Work

The Subcontractor expressly warrants and undertakes that all Works shall be performed in strict accordance with the terms of this Subcontract. The specific scope of Work required to fulfil the Subcontractor’s obligations hereunder is more particularly described in Article 3-Scope of Work hereof.

# Article 3 Scope of the Work

The Subcontractor’s Works shall comprise complete engineering, procurement and construction field service for Mechanical, Electrical, and Instrumentation Installation Works, including all necessary and ancillary activities and services required for proper execution of the Works and fulfillment of all Subcontractor`s obligations under this Subcontract, as more particularly stipulated in the conditions of Subcontract and related Exhibits.

# Article 4 Time Schedule

The Subcontractor shall commence and complete the Works in accordance with the schedule and requirement specified in Exhibit A.2 Work Schedule and all applicable terms and conditions set forth in the of Subcontract.

The detailed breakdown of the Subcontract Price shall be in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Conditions of Subcontract and Exhibit D hereto.

# Article 7 Binding Effect

The Subcontract shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Parties hereto and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

# Article 8 Entire Subcontract, Amendments

The Subcontract constitutes the entire and exclusive agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, negotiations and understandings, whether written or oral. No modification, waiver, or discharge of any provision shall be valid unless made in writing and duly executed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

# Article 9 Governing Law, Dispute Resolution

As far as legally possible, the validity, interpretation and implementation of the Subcontract shall be governed by and construed in all respects in accordance with the laws of the England and Wales without recourse to its conflict of law rules. All disputes resulting from, concerning the validity of, or arising in connection with the Subcontract shall be finally settled in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions as set forth in the Conditions of Subcontract (including the agreement of the Parties on settlement by arbitration).

# Article 10 Headings and Captions

The headings and captions for the Articles and Clauses of the Subcontract shall be solely for the convenience of the Parties and shall not affect the substance or interpretation of those Articles and Clauses. References in the singular shall apply equally to the plural and vice and versa.

# Article 11 Severability

If one or more of the provisions or conditions of the Subcontract or a part thereof shall be invalid, void, illegal or unenforceable in any respect by operation of law or otherwise, the validity, legality or enforceability of the remaining provisions or terms or part thereof shall not, in any way whatsoever, be affected or impaired thereby and the Parties shall make good faith efforts to reword the Subcontract to give effect to their original intent.

# Article 12 Waiver

Unless expressly set forth otherwise in the Subcontract, failure or delay of either Party at any time to take action, against the other Party within the agreed periods of time and/or as otherwise provided for in the Subcontract Documents shall constitute a final waiver by such Party of any such right or remedy arising out of or in connection with this Subcontract. However, the waiver by any Party of its rights and remedies resulting from a breach of any provision of the Subcontract shall not constitute a waiver of its rights resulting from any subsequent breach.

# Article 13 Assignment

Contractor may assign or transfer any of its rights and/or obligations hereunder to any third party. Subcontractor shall not assign or transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Subcontract to any third party without the previous written consent of the Contractor.
