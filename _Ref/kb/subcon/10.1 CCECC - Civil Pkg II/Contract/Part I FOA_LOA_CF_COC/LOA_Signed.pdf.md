6. Clarification

Both Parties agree that Clauses 1, 2, 3. 4 and 19 under Section “3. No Deviation Declaration”
of this LOA shall be amended as follows:

1, Clauses 1 (Advance Payment & Guarantee) : The Advance Payment shall be made within
fourteen (14) days after execution of the Subcontract and receipt of the Subcontractor’s Advance
Payment Invoice, Advance Payment Bond, and Performance Bond. The Advance Payment shall
be recovered by deducting ten percent (10%) from each payment certificate to the Subcontractor
until fully repaid.

2, Clauses 2/3 (Performance Bond / Warranty Period & Bond) : During the interim payment
stage, the five percent (5%) Warranty Money shall no longer be deducted from each payment.
Upon Work Completion, the Subcontractor shall, as required by the Contractor, submit a
Warranty Bond in an amount equivalent to five percent (5%) of the Contract Price. Submission of
the Warranty Bond shall be a condition precedent to the Contractor's release of the Performance
Bond. All other provisions shall remain unchanged.

3, Clauses 4(Retention Money): Retention Money shall be ten percent (10%). Of this amount,
seven point five percent (7.5%) shall be deducted from each interim payment. The remaining two
point five percent (2.5%) shall be secured by a Contractor-accepted bond equal to 2.5% of the
Contract Price, to be submitted by the Subcontractor at the time of the first interim payment.

4, Clauses 19 (ICV): During the interim payment process, the Contractor shall no longer
withhold the five percent (5%) ICV reserve from each payment. All other provisions shall remain
unchanged. Should the Subcontractor fail to achieve the required ICV target, the Contractor shall
be entitled to deduct the corresponding amount from any outstanding payments due to the
Subcontractor, and any deficiency shall be recoverable from the Subcontractor.
