1 PROJECT DESCRIPTION
1.1 Introduction
The Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) is owned by ADNOC and operated by
ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in
the Ruwais Industrial Area. RSHT-2 facility is designed to handle bulk granulated sulphur
coming from Shah and Habshan plant via railcars and transported via new federal railway
(Union Railway) to Ruwais Terminal (SHT-2).
Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2 (Ruwais
Sulphur Granulation Plant-RSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of receiving
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This new plant
will be located to the East of the BOROUGE terminal-3 & to the west of RSHT-2 as depicted
in Fig.1.1 below.
Figure 1: New SGP Site Location near Existing RSHT-2 Facility
1.2 Project Objective and Scope
The primary objective of the RSGP Development project at RSHT-2 is to accommodate
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves
constructing new infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur

9.3 General Directive on The Numbering of Pages & Sheets
Each DRAWING sheet (size A0 to A2) shall be assigned unique and shall have dedicated
revision table.
When the content of a DRAWING cannot be shown on a single DRAWING sheet, the resulting
DRAWING sheets shall be regarded as separate DRAWINGS as far as the issue process is
concerned, but they shall have same BOROUGE document number with different suffixes (01,
02, 03, etc.).
Multiple sheets DOCUMENTS are allowed for A3 and A4 formats. For such DOCUMENTS, as
far as the revision process is concerned, four cases may apply:
• Type A1: General specifications, procedures, process data sheets, etc., documents which
are composed by many sheets, usually size A4 text files, which are revised as a whole: the
revision table is shown only in the first sheet (cover page), and each sheet shall carry the
same Document Numbers and Revision indexes, in addition, each sheet shall show the sheet
number and the total number of sheets. Wherever it is not possible to indicate total number
of sheets on each sheet (e.g., IN Tools Instrument Data Sheets), the total number of sheets
will be indicated on cover sheet. The document shall carry an index sheet when there are
more than 10 sheets.
• Type A2: Engineering mechanical drawings (issued by static equipment department)
composed by several sheets, usually A3 size, which are revised as a whole: the revision
table is shown only in the first sheet, and each sheet will show identical revision index. First
sheet will include the list of the sheets. Each drawing shall have identical BOROUGE number
with different sheet suffixes.
• Type B: Electrical and instrumental schemes, DCS/ESD logic diagrams, material lists,
typical drawings, etc., which are composed by several drawing sheets, each one being liable
of separate update: the drawings shall be preceded by one or more cover pages, containing,
besides the general revision table which shows the document revision history and status, a
table (index) showing the list of the following sheets with the update status of each of them;
such cover sheets shall be revised in case of any modification carried out in the following
sheets: each sheet shall show the common Document Numbers (with suffix for BOROUGE’s
number); only the common Document Numbers shall be reported on EDMS.
Remark: Numbering of Instrument Data Sheet shall be done in ascending order of tag numbers.
Some spares will be left in between areas to take care of additional tags getting added
subsequently. Sheet numbering for such additional tag numbers will be adjusted as far as
possible in the spares left at the end of each unit. If the same is not possible, additional tag
numbers will be placed at the end.
