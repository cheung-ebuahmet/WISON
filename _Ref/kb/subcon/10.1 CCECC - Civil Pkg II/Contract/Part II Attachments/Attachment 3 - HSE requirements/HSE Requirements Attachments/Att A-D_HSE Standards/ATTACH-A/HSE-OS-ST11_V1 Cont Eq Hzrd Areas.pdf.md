1.

INTRODUCTION

Hydrocarbon  under  pressure  is  the  primary  Major  Accident  Hazard  (MAH)  at  ADNOC  and  its  Group
Company  facilities.  A  loss  of  flammable  gas/dust  containment  and  subsequent  accumulation  has  the
potential for fire  and/or explosion  if an ignition source is present in the vicinity and  can lead  to  major,
catastrophic  and  even  disastrous  consequence  in  extreme  cases.  Facility  areas  with  the  potential
formation  of  a  flammable  or  explosive  atmosphere  are  classified  as  hazardous  areas.  Control  of
equipment  in  those  classified  hazardous  areas  can  significantly  reduce  the  likely  presence  of  ignition
sources and can prevent extreme consequences. Therefore, it is vital for ADNOC and Group Companies
operations  to  effectively  control  equipment  in  classified  hazardous  areas  as  part  of  the  overall  major
accident management.

2.

PURPOSE

The purpose of this standard is to provide standard requirements on controlling temporary equipment and
ignition sources, to be used in classified hazardous areas within ADNOC and Group Company operating
facilities.

3.

SCOPE

This document stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to ADNOC Group and its Contractors.

ADNOC  Group  and  Contractors  shall  ensure  that  all  expectations  listed  herein  are  fully  understood,
implemented and thoroughly monitored.

REQUIREMENTS

7.1.  OVERVIEW

A  hazardous  area  is  a  three-dimensional  space  in  which  an  explosive  atmosphere  is  present  due  to
gas/vapor or dust, or may be expected to be present, in quantities such as to require special precautions
for the construction, installation and use of equipment. Areas  are subdivided  into zones based  on the
likelihood  of  occurrence  and  duration  of  an  explosive  atmosphere.  All  other  areas  are  non-hazardous
areas.

Hazardous Area Classification (HAC) is a method of analyzing and classifying the areas of a facility into
hazardous areas and non-hazardous areas and the subdivision of the classified hazardous areas into
zones as Zone 0, Zone 1 and Zone 2 if the explosive atmosphere is likely because of a gas/ vapor; and
Zone 20, Zone 21 and Zone 22 if the explosive atmosphere is likely because of dust. The main purpose
of HAC is to facilitate the proper selection of equipment to be used safely in classified hazardous areas,
taking into account the properties of the flammable materials that will be present.

The specification of temporary equipment to be used in classified hazardous areas within ADNOC and
Group Companies shall be determined by the classification of the area in which it is, or is going to be,
located/ used and temperature class of the hazardous material present or likely to be present in that area.

HAC drawings are developed to determine the classified hazardous areas in the facility. Those drawings
shall be referred to identify the extent of the classified hazardous area(s) to comply with requirements
mentioned in this standard.

Refer to ADNOC HSE Design Philosophy Standard [Ref. 12] for further information.

The temperature classification shall be marked on items. If the classified hazardous area in which the
equipment  is  being  installed  has  gases  or  vapours  present  with  a  low  auto  ignition  temperature,  then
equipment with a higher T-number will be required to ensure that equipment hot surfaces will not cause

For example, if a flammable gas has an auto ignition temperature of 180°C, then it would be safe to use
equipment which is marked either with T6 or T5 or T4. It will not be safe to use equipment classified as

atmosphere.

Generally, equipment is only certified in ambient temperatures up to 40°C. If the ambient is consistently
higher, the stated T-Class is exceeded; safety components within the equipment will be operating outside
of design parameters and could fail. If it is expected that equipment will be subjected to temperatures
above 40°C (e.g. in direct sunlight), equipment which is certified for a higher ambient temperature shall
be installed.

7.5.  TEMPORARY EQUIPMENT CERTIFICATION

Temporary equipment required to be used in Zone 0/20 (category 1/a) and Zone 1/21 (category 2/b) shall
be certified to ATEX or equivalent by accredited 3rd party. Category 3/c equipment does not require 3rd
party certification.

Note: For Zone 1/21 and Zone 2/22, if due to unavoidable/exceptional operational circumstances where
a non-certified equipment is required to be used or the required ATEX or equivalent certification is not
available,  authorization  shall  be  subject to  a  risk  assessment  and  implementation  of  adequate control
measures. All such activities shall be governed by a PTW as per the ADNOC Work Management System
(WMS) Framework [Ref. 10].
