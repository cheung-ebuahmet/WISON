1 PROJECT DESCRIPTION
1.1 Introduction
The Ruwais Sulphur Handling Terminal 2 (RSHT-2) is owned by ADNOC and operated by
ADNOC Gas (AG) and is located approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City in
the Ruwais Industrial Area. RSHT-2 facility is designed to handle bulk granulated sulphur
coming from Shah and Habshan plant via railcars and transported via new federal railway
(Union Railway) to Ruwais Terminal (SHT-2).
Based on ADNOC recent directives, the Sulphur Handling Scenario at Ruwais SHT-2 (Ruwais
Sulphur Granulation Plant-RSGP) will be developed further due to consideration of receiving
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur from Hail & Ghasha to the new plant, via pipeline. This new plant
will be located to the East of the BOROUGE terminal-3 & to the west of RSHT-2 as depicted
in Fig.1.1 below.
Figure 1: New RSGP Site Location near Existing RSHT-2 Facility
1.2 Project Objective and Scope
The primary objective of the RSGP Development project at RSHT-2 is to accommodate
9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP. This involves
constructing new infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur
forming (Granulation), Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and
export to existing ship loaders. The project also includes implementing dust control systems,
sulphur remelting unit, utility systems and integration with existing facilities. The developed

9 PTW TRACKING REGISTER
The PTW (Permit to Work) Tracking Register is a critical tool used in managing and
documenting the lifecycle of work permits within a project or organization. It's essentially a
digital logbook where each issued permit is recorded, updated, and tracked, from the moment
it's LIVE until it's CLOSED. The PTW tracking register refer to Appendix 6.
There are two programs you can use for this purpose. The first option is to use the traditional
Microsoft Excel and set up a shared folder to store all the necessary documents for processing
the permit. The second option is to use a registered version of Microsoft Lists, which allows
you to create, manage, and track information in a structured format. Microsoft Lists is
specifically designed to organize data in a list format and offers various features for
collaboration and data management.
Purpose of PTW Tracking Register:
The primary purpose of the PTW Tracking Register is to provide a real-time snapshot of all
active work permits, which assists in managing safety and operations in the work area. By
logging all permits, the PTW Tracking Register helps ensure that all necessary work approvals
have been obtained, safety checks have been completed, and work is carried out within the
parameters set by the permits. It also acts as a historical record for future reference or for
audits.

14 REMOTE FIELD OPERATIONS
In alignment with Greenfield Projects that have limited manpower, specifically fewer than 100
personnel on a specific off-site/remote area, the Remote Field Operations (RFO) feature can
be a valuable tool. It enables the Area Authority (AA) to delegate complex tasks to a designated
Permit Issuer (PI). This delegation includes authorizing the PI to manage daily permit issuance
and revalidation at the worksite. The AA accomplishes this by formally noting the PI’s name,
indicating their responsibility for these tasks.
For projects with a large number of personnel specifically SGP AT RSHT 2 FOR H&G, the
RFO feature remains equally beneficial. It aids the CONTRACTOR in initiating work promptly
by delegating a Permit Issuer to act on behalf of the AA.
The Area Authority is allowed to delegate a maximum of 2 Permit Issuer to act on his behalf.
These assigned individuals will be responsible for carrying out their duties, providing sufficient
coverage for both the day and night shifts.
The practice of formally writing the PI’s name in full by the AA during delegation serves a
crucial purpose. It ensures the AA is fully aware of all delegated actions, correctly transferring
responsibilities to the PI, while still retaining accountability for the entire permit process, even
if they are not physically present at the worksite.
Ultimately, the RFO feature will streamline the revalidation process without necessitating the
AA’s physical presence at the worksite. However, through proper delegation, the permit
process remains robust, fool proof, and maintains its integrity. By implementing this strategy
will enhance progress efficiency and responsibility tracking.

16 FORMAL RISK ASSESSMENT (FRA)

(ALARP).

using the Formal Risk Assessment (FRA) approach.
