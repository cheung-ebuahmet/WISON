TOILET COMPARTMENTS
General

TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION FOR STEEL HEAVY DUTY SHELVING SYSTEM
1. GENERAL
a. Shelving system proposed is of an adjustable type, designed to be extremely adaptable in operation
and pleasing in appearances. All shelves shall be easily adjustable vertically on a 38 mm module and
yet remain secure on a specially designed clips and beam or beam brackets which should enable
shelves to be added or subtracted when required.
b. The front face of the uprights shall be smooth and completely free from holes.
c. All shelving material shall be zinc phosphated for rust protection to BS EN 12476 and then stove
enamelled.
d. Basically the shelving system should comprise of:
i. Frames (two uprights joined together)
ii. Shelves
iii. Beam or Beam Brackets
iv. Accessories
2. FRAME CLADDING
a. Frame cladding shall be fabricated from steel Grade CR4 to BS EN 10130, 0.6 mm thick and is
degreased and stove enamelled light grey.
3. UPRIGHTS
a. Size of the uprights should be 619 x 13.5 x 0.9 mm thick with 19.7 mm lips.
b. Slot Pattern Uprights shall not have slots on their front face, thus offering a clean and attractive
appearance. Both lips are perforated with 5.1 mm diameter holes at 19 mm center.
c. The upright back shall be perforated with two rows of key holes slots at 38 mm centers with 5.1 mm
diameter holes midway between them.
4. MATERIAL
a. Material used to fabricate uprights shall be steel Grade CR4 to BS EN 10130 with minimum proof
stress of 16.2 ton f/IN2 (250 N/mm²).
5. FINISH
a. Uprights are degreased and zinc phosphated to BS EN 12476 phosphate conversion coatings of
metals.
b. Method of specifying requirements and stove enamelled. Colour to be cobalt blue to BS 381 C.
6. CLADDING TRIM
a. Cladding trim shall be used to join the frame cladding sheets. It shall be an “H” section 23.4 mm deep
by 4.1 mm width.
b. Cladding trim is fabricated from rigid P.V.C.
