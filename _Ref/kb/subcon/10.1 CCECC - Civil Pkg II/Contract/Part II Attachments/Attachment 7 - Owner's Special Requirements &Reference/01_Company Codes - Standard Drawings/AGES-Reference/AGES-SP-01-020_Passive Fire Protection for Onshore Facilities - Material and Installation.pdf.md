shall comply with the key aspects specified in it.

This specification describes fire proofing material, extent of fireproofing and installation requirements
for the specified duration of protection time against a hydrocarbon fire (pool fire/jet fire).

SECTION B – TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS
GENERAL & TECHNICAL DEFINITIONS
a. The fireproofing protection is necessary for steel support structures where their sudden collapse
due to fire would seriously endanger personnel, lead to the release of large quantities of flammable
products and/or toxic materials, lead to consequences beyond the property limit (including
environmental damage), or endanger major equipment.
b. Fireproofing also serves as cold splash protection in that it offers an effective and well proven
means of protection against severe embrittlement and fractural failure due to refrigerated or
cryogenic liquefied gases escaping to the atmosphere
c. The fireproofing material shall be selected from this specification and shall be as specified on
Project documents.
d. The following can be used as standard fireproofing material.
i. Lightweight vermiculite concrete or normal concrete (Grade 25 FP with light weight aggregate)
for Onshore steel structures.
ii. Epoxy intumescent coating for Onshore steel structures where specified on project drawings.
iii. Epoxy intumescent coating for steel structures on Offshore Islands. Lightweight vermiculite
concrete may be considered where specified on project drawings and approved by COMPANY.
iv. Epoxy intumescent coating for critical control and electric cables.
e. The extent of fireproofing shall be as shown on the design/construction drawings. Fireproofing
details shall be in accordance with COMPANY standard drawings.

GENERAL REQUIREMENTS
a. Passive Fire Protection (PFP) is intended to improve the fire resistance of structures that support
pressure vessels, piping and other equipment located in FPZs. PFP is provided by the application
of concrete or other approved protection materials to structural steel supports. PFP offers protection
against the adverse thermal effects of fire for a defined period and a defined degree of exposure.
b. PFP should not be considered as a replacement for active firefighting or lead to relaxation of normal
design requirements (spacing and layout considerations) and precautions in operation and
maintenance.
c. The extent of PFP requirements SHALL [PSR] be established on the basis of a Fire Safety
Assessment executed for the particular plant or unit as per relevant HSE standard.
d. The zones where PFP are required are called Fire Protection Zones (FPZ). The extent of the
fireproofing around equipment and structures SHALL [PSR] be indicated on layout and construction
drawings
e. The severity of the fire shall be based on hydrocarbon pool fires using the fire temperature profile
per UL 1709. The hydrocarbon fire test as defined in UL 1709 is applicable to all PFP systems
applied on steel supports and structures. Refer ISO 22899-1 to determine the performance of PFP
material selected for the protection of structures against credible jet fire scenarios.
f. For steel structures, the required protection time shall be established based on the
recommendations of project specific FSA / FERA / QRA studies for both pool fire and jet fire
scenario. However, the fire exposure resistance time shall be of minimum 2 hours duration when
exposed to a hydrocarbon pool fire and minimum 30 minutes duration when exposed to a jet fire
(as applicable). Longer protection times may be warranted or required depending on the equipment,
function and available firefighting measures.
g. The passive fire protection requirements, fireproofing material properties, fire protection duration
and other performance requirements shall also be in line with AGES-PH-03-002, Fire & Gas
Detection and Fire Protection System Philosophy.
h. Extent of fire proofing for structures supporting equipment, pipes, air coolers, etc. shall be in
accordance with API 2218. Also, the additional requirements as per the outcome of FERA study
shall be applied.
i. For all structures located within the FPZ, steel columns, beams, vertical and horizontal bracing,
connection joints, anchor bolts, etc. shall be fireproofed.
j. Bolted moment connections of shop applied, fireproofed, main pipe racks block-outs should be
fireproofed in the field subsequent to erection.
k. For specific requirements of ADNOC Refinery with regard to fire protection duration and specific
structures requiring fireproofing regardless of FPZ refer Appendix E1.

EXTENT OF FIREPROOFING
Fireproofing Zones
a. Only specific structures and equipment located within a Fire Proofing Zone (FPZ) shall be
fireproofed as identified by HSE.
b. The zones shall be identified early in the engineering phase of the Project.
Structures Supporting Equipment
a. Steel structures supporting equipment located within a FPZ shall be fireproofed in accordance with
API 2218.
b. Stairways, walkways, and platforms designed mainly for live loads and top surfaces of beams
supporting floor plates, gratings, or equipment are normally not fireproofed.
Steel Pipe Rack & Pipe Supports
a. Steel structures supporting overhead piping and located in a FPZ shall be fireproofed in accordance
with API 2218, and CONTRACTOR shall identify such supports/structure in the related structure
drawings.
b. When a pipe rack is within a fire-scenario envelope, fireproofing shall be considered for vertical and
horizontal members, up to and including the first level. If a pipe rack carries piping with a diameter
greater than 150 mm (6 inch), at levels above the first horizontal beam; or if hydrocarbon pumps
are installed beneath the rack, fireproofing shall be considered up to and including the level that is
nearest to a 9m elevation
c. In general, the structure columns, beams and bracings located in FPZ shall be fireproofed from
Base Plate level up to minimum 9.0m Elevation from FFL or level as required by HSE studies.
Vessels and Exchangers
a. The passive fireproofing requirements for skirts and saddles supporting Vessels & Exchangers shall
follow the requirements specified in COMPANY AGES-PH-03-002 - Fire & Gas Detection and Fire
Protection System Philosophy.
ESD Valves
a. The passive fireproofing requirements for ESD valves and associated critical control & power
cables shall follow the requirements specified in COMPANY AGES-PH-03-002 - Fire & Gas
Detection and Fire Protection System Philosophy.

General

FP. Alternative proprietary system with proven track record may be used. The passive fire protection
material type shall be as shown on the Project specific scope drawings.
b. The extent of fireproofing shall be as indicated on the design drawings.
c. The material requirements shall be read in conjunction with AGES-PH-003-002, Fire & Gas

Materials
a. General
i. Lightweight vermiculite concrete or normal concrete shall be used as the fireproofing material
approved by the COMPANY.
ii. Normal Concrete fireproofing shall be of Grade 25FP with light weight aggregate in accordance
with Specification for Concrete Supply & Construction, AGES-SP-01-001. All other
requirements for concrete including protection shall be as per AGES-SP-01-001.
iii. The use of alternative materials shall be subject to an economical evaluation based on life cycle
costs. In case of physical limitation for concrete PFP, or where weight of the fire protection
material is a significant issue, alternative materials may be considered. Climatic conditions
during installation shall be included in the selection of materials and application methods.
iv. Alternatives to above is proprietary epoxy intumescent and sublimating materials (Typical
proprietary systems include intumescent coatings, which expand and form an insulation layer
when the temperature increases and subliming mastic materials).
v. For proprietary PFP systems, an approved primer or substrate shall be applied in accordance
with a certified and proven system from the fireproofing coating MANUFACTURER/SUPPLIER.

vii. A topcoat shall be applied according to the MANUFACTURER/SUPPLIER’s recommendation
viii. The capacity of the structure to handle the weight of the fire protection system shall be
assessed.
b. Material Performance
i. Shall provide protection for the specified duration for pool fire case and/or jet fire case as
applicable
ii. Shore hardness shall be at least 40 for cementitious material

SECTION E – APPENDICES
SPECIFIC REQUIREMENTS FOR ADNOC REFINING
1.0 STRUCTURES REQUIRING FIREPROOFING REGARDLESS OF FPZ
1.1 General
Structures listed below shall be fireproofed irrespective of their location relative to the FPZ.
1.2 Furnace Support Structures
Columns shall be fireproofed from grade level to the full height of the column. All structural members
incorporated to reduce the effective buckling length of these columns shall be fireproofed. Main floor
beams of furnaces shall be fireproofed as well.
1.3 Supports of Pressurized Spheres and Bullets
Supporting legs of spheres shall be fireproofed from grade level up to 0.2 m below the intersection
of the leg with the sphere.
All columns and beams supporting bullets and structural members incorporated to reduce the
effective buckling length shall be fireproofed.
1.4 Storage Tank Pipe Bridges
At refrigerated and cryogenic storage tanks, the main frames of steel towers which support pipes
and/or pipe bridges shall be fireproofed.
1.5 Steel Pipe Supports Inside Storage Tank Bunded Areas
Steel pipe supports inside a storage tank bunded area shall be fully fireproofed to the highest level
of supported piping.
2.0 FIRE RESISTANCE DURATION
The duration of fireproofing shall be as per HSE recommendation in FSA / FERA / QRA. However,
three (3) hours duration (pool fire) shall be followed for Steel structures/Pipe racks and Pipe supports
located inside fire proofing zone. Relaxation to two (2) hours minimum may be acceptable on specific
cases subject to COMPANY approval.
