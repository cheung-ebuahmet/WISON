GENERAL
Introduction
The purpose of this document is to describe the technical requirements for the furnishing and installation
of plastering, dry walling, floor & wall tiling, skirting, cladding / lining panels, acoustical ceiling tile,
resilient flooring, carpet, painting, wall coverings and accessories in buildings. It also defines the
technical requirements for the furnishing and installation of all laboratory furniture, shop furniture,
movable wall / partition systems and miscellaneous furniture, furnishings and equipment.
Purpose
This Specification provides minimum requirements for the architectural finishes and furnishings noted
above and as outlined in the drawings and contract documents.

or other approved fasteners. Fasteners shall be located at the dimples or crimps only at every 300
mm canters.

Fasten strip lath over the base as shown on shop drawings.

6.2.3.6 Application
The first scratch coat or dash-bond coat: a thick wet mixture of Portland cement and water, with or
without aggregate, dashed onto the surface of a plaster base of concrete or concrete block surfaces
to improve the mechanical key for subsequent plaster coats. Allow at least one day before
succeeding coat is applied. The thickness of the scratch coat shall be not less than 10 mm. The
scratch coat shall set firm and hard before the brown coat is applied. The thickness of the brown
coat shall be not less than 7 mm.
The brown coat shall be brought out to grounds, straightened to a true surface with rod and darby,
and left rough, ready to receive the finish coat. The brown coat shall set dry and hard before finish
coat is applied.
Scratch and brown coats for Portland cement plaster shall each be moist cured with water for at
least 24 hours or as per manufacturer’s instruction.
Finish Coat: The finish coat shall be laid out to permit the completion of an entire surface in one
operation. Finish coat shall be applied to minimum thickness of 3 mm, or in such thickness as may
be necessary to ensure the full thickness achieved as specified and shall have a sand float finish
free from slick spots, blemishes, and irregularities. Brown coat shall be dampened evenly to provide
suction before applying finish coat.

All plaster shall be protected from premature or uneven drying until thoroughly set. Portland cement

The CONTRACTOR shall use drop cloths and other protective measures to protect all surfaces

shall be repaired. All plaster deposited on surfaces which are not to be plastered shall be removed
as soon as possible to the satisfaction of the COMPANY.
6.2.3.8 Patching

permitted only when acceptable to the COMPANY, and such patching shall match adjacent work in
texture and color.
The CONTRACTOR shall point up and patch as necessary around grilles and other openings,
leaving all work in a neat and finished condition.

General
a. Quality plans shall address all aspects related to local conditions, such as climatic conditions,
backup facilities, spare parts, transport possibilities, storage facilities, local Manufacturers /

‘Quality Management Systems‐Requirements’ and BS EN ISO 9004, ‘Quality management –
Quality of an organization – Guidance to achieve Sustained Success’. Written quality plans and
procedures shall be submitted to COMPANY for review and approval.
c. Materials should be obtained from the same source(s) throughout the work.
Inspection, Testing and Reporting
a. Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs) identifying the tests, frequencies, acceptance criteria, and
responsibilities shall be prepared as part of the quality plan.

Specification.
