B.16.1 INTRODUCTION
This EXHIBIT B.16 summarises COMPANY’s requirements and criteria for
selection of SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS on the PROJECT, and shall be
read in accordance with and as further detailed in the relevant procurement
sections contained in EXHIBITS B.14 and B.15. For the purposes of this EXHIBIT,
the term SUBCONTRACTOR shall not include VENDORS.
B.16.2 SELECTION OF SUBCONTRACTORS
Selection of any SUBCONTRACTOR for performance of any portion of the WORK
shall be based, amongst other factors, on technical merit and the ability to comply
with PROJECT SCHEDULE. The requirements of this EXHIBIT shall apply to
SUBCONTRACTORS of all tiers.

are limited to the following:
• Surveys – Topographic/ Geotechnical
• Detailed Engineering
• Line Pipe Coating Works
• Line Pipe Transportation
• Piping / Structural Pre-fabrication
• Pipeline Construction Works
• Civil Works
• Road Crossing Works
• Building Construction Works
• Mechanical and Piping Erection Works
• Electrical Works
• Instrumentation Works
• CP System Installation Works
• Non Destructive Testing
• Hot Tapping
• Pre-commissioning
• Commissioning

as Attachment-1. Any of the Critical Works not proposed to be subcontracted in
the Subcontracting Plan shall be deemed to be performed directly by
CONTRACTOR. CONTRACTOR may modify its Subcontracting Plan in respect of
Critical Works only with prior written approval of COMPANY, provided that
CONTRACTOR shall not subcontract both engineering and construction work
packages.
