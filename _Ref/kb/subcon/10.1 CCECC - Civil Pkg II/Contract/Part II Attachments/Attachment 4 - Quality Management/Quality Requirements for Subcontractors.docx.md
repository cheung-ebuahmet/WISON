1. General Requirement

This document covers CONTRACTOR’s minimum requirement for the implementation of Quality Management System for the WORK.

SUBCONTRACTORS shall develop and implement a Project Specific Quality Program for the Project’s Quality Management needs and requirements. The Quality Management System shall be in full compliance with ISO 9001 requirements;

SUBCONTRACTOR shall be totally responsible for the quality of WORK required by this Contract.

COMPANY’s Quality requirements should be followed by SUBCONTRACTOR.

EXHIBIT-B.10 Quality Requirement

AGES-GL-13-001 Contractors QAQC Requirement

1. Quality Management System

General

SUBCONTRACTOR shall establish, document, implement and maintain a quality management system and continually improve its effectiveness in accordance with the requirements of ISO 9001.

SUBCONTRACTORs and Suppliers shall be certified ISO 9001 by independent authorized third party certifying bodies;

* 1. SUBCONTRACTOR Quality Plan

Within thirty (30) calendar days after the Commencement Date, SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit a Quality Assurance Program for approval by CONTRACTOR, consisting of the following documents:

1. Project Quality plan;
2. The quality control procedure/ instruction submittal schedule;

The Project Quality Plan shall address all activities relevant to the Work and shall demonstrate how to perform the quality control for the key activities by SUBCONTRACTOR.

The plan shall include an organization chart showing SUBCONTRACTOR’S corporate and Project organization responsible for managing, performing and verifying the Work. The organization chart shall be supported with a reporting and functional description of SUBCONTRACTOR’S corporate and Project organization to identify the quality related responsibilities of key positions.

The plan shall be updated as necessary throughout the site work duration of the SUBCONTRACT to reflect any changes to SUBCONTRACTOR’S documented quality system.

1. Resource Management
   1. Responsibilities of the SUBCONTRACTOR’s QC Manager/Inspectors
2. To communicate these minimum QC requirements to any suppliers and/or Sub-Sub-contractors.
3. To have the authority to stop Work for cause, reject Work, order Work removed, initiate remedial Work, propose solutions, and reject material not in compliance with the Contract.
4. Be present on-site during working hours and assigned "full time" to the Contract. Sub-contractor shall designate alternate individual(s) to assume responsibilities in the temporary absence of the QC Manager/Supervisor.
5. Be completely familiar with the Contract Scope of Work, Drawings and Specifications.
6. Establish and implement QC Programs for Sub-contractor and with its various Sub-Sub-contractors, and monitor their conformance.
7. Inspect existing conditions prior to the start of new Work segments.
8. Conduct a pre-construction Quality Control meeting with Sub-contractor’s responsible field and office representatives prior to the start of each major item of work required by the Contract.
9. Perform in process and follow up inspections on each of the work segments to ensure compliance with the Contract. Upon request, acCOMPANY WISON on such inspections.
10. Coordinate required test, inspections, and demonstrations with WISON or any other authority having jurisdiction. Assure work is accessible for test or inspection.
11. Inspect Sub-contractor purchased materials and equipment arriving at the Site to ensure conformance to the requirements of the Contract. Prepare and submit documentation as required by the Contract.
12. Inspect material “free-issued” to Sub-contractor by WISON to ensure conformance to the requirements of the Contract. Sub-contractor must provide immediate notification to WISON when materials are found to be damaged or nonconforming.
13. Identify, Report and reject defective Work not in conformance with the Contract. Monitor the repair or reconstruction of rejected Work and document corrective action, including all remedial action taken to prevent the re-occurrence of the defective Work.
14. If necessary, retain specialists or Sub-Sub-contractors for inspection of Work in areas where additional technical knowledge is required (mechanical, electrical, electronics, controls, communications, welding, structural, etc.). Submit qualifications of Sub-Sub-contractors and specialists to WISON for approval.
15. Schedule and acCOMPANY WISON on any site visits when requested.
16. Work closely with WISON to ensure optimum Quality Control. Attend meetings as required by COMPANY.
    1. Competency requirements
17. As a minimum, QA/QC inspectors employed by SUB-CONTRACTOR to perform the WORK shall have 10 years of relevant industrial experience, including 6 years’ experience in a given inspection specialty, four of which have been in oil and gas or petrochemical industries; shall be a High School graduate, or equivalent; shall be fully conversant with applicable Industry Standards and Specifications; and shall perform a variety of complex inspection functions with minimal supervision.

SUB-CONTRACTOR shall submit the CV and supporting evidence of qualifications/ professional certifications for CONTROACTOR review and approval before mobilisation. COMPANY shall be the final authority for the determination of equivalency for all qualifications, certifications, or experience.

   1. Inspection, Measuring and Test Equipment
      1. The used Device or Equipment shall be calibrated or verified, or both, at specified intervals, or prior to use, against measurement standards traceable to international or national measurement standards; where no such standards exist, the basis used for calibration or verification shall be recorded.
      2. Every Device or Equipment which is used for the inspection must be calibrated by the third party.
      3. Calibration Log shall be prepared and maintained by the Device Controller. The device situation shall be clearly identified in the log including: Identification No. , Size-Range, Model No., Calibration Frequency Date, Calibrated, Calibration expired Date etc.
      4. Each item requiring calibration shall be individually identified by serial number. A calibration tag or sticker, which includes the serial number and the calibration due date, shall be affixed where physically possible.
      5. Device or Equipment shall be calibrated to or checked basis on manufacture’s specification and international customary practices.
      6. The reference standards used for calibration shall have accuracy levels and ranges that are greater than those of the Device or Equipment being calibrated.
      7. Device or Equipment calibration report shall be issued by third party in accordance with the standard requirement.

    1. Identification & Traceability
11. SUBCONTRACTOR shall maintain an identification procedure of all materials, parts and components, including partially fabricated assemblies. Traceability shall be maintained on all equipment, materials, parts and components in accordance to with Project requirements, specifications and relevant Codes. Full material traceability shall be maintained on records during fabrication and construction. Identification of an item shall be addressed either by using tags, stamps, stencils or labels or by records traceable to an item throughout fabrication, shipment, erection, installation, repair, modification and use of the item.
    1. Dimensional Control
       1. SUBCONTRACTOR shall ensure that full time experienced Surveyors are available for survey work.
       2. SUBCONTRACTOR shall implement effective procedures for controlling the dimensions of major fabricated equipment as well as all field installations. Such procedures shall provide for witness/hold points to independently verify critical dimensions, such as module interface, castings, pipe bends, anchor bolt locations, etc. before proceeding with work that would increase the cost of correcting possible errors or that would lengthen the construction schedule if errors were discovered later in the construction sequence. In case of deviations, CONTRACTOR shall input dimensional data from the equipment vendors reports in order to adjust the layout drawings accordingly.
    2. Special Process Control
       1. The special processes (as per Attachment 1 of EXHIBIT-B.10 QUALITY MANAGEMENT REQUIREMENTS) require TPI review and/or approval of work procedures, personnel qualifications, or personnel qualification procedures.
       2. Sub-contractor shall ensure that these special processes are accomplished under controlled conditions as specified in all applicable standards and specifications. All process parameters shall be identified with acceptance criteria specified and monitored. All Procedures and Personnel Qualification shall be submitted for review and/or approval at least thirty (30) calendar days prior to the start of WORK.
       3. Sub-contractor shall establish KPI with COMPANY/CONTRACTOR direction and approval of targets for all construction processes and quality measurements.
       4. Sub-contractor will provide procedures to control work processes.
