**3. Working Scope and PRM’S Responsibilities**

3.1 RPM shall:

(a) Provide one (1) DOH-approved Registered Male Nurse for day shift duties (10 hours/day, 6 days/week).

(b) Provide one (1) First Aid Trained Driver for day shift duties (10 hours/day, 6 days/week).

(c) Provide one (1) fully equipped, DOH-approved BLS Ambulance (4x4 Landcruiser) available 24/7.

(d) Mobilize and demobilize all personnel and equipment at its own cost.

(e) Ensure all personnel have valid professional licenses, certifications (BLS, ACLS, CMEs as applicable), health certificates ("MOH" equivalent), and security clearances.

(f) Be responsible for all visa costs, travel costs (to UAE point of hire), medical insurance, GOSI, and other statutory insurances for its personnel.

(g) Provide adequate PPE for all its staff.

(h) Comply with all WISON and end client (e.g., ADNOC) HSE requirements, site rules, and regulations, and all applicable UAE laws.

(i) Maintain accurate medical records and provide reports as requested by WISON.

(j)Manage the medical waste through a licensed third-party provider if the waste management service is engaged.

(k) Ensure the ambulance is always maintained in good working condition.

(l) Replace any underperforming or unacceptable personnel immediately upon WISON's request, at no additional cost to WISON.

(m) Be responsible for all transportation costs (to/from site if different from deployment location) and PPE for its personnel.

(n) guarantees that all personnel (Nurse, Driver) will be approved by the Department of Health Abu Dhabi (DOH) and possess necessary qualifications and certifications.

(o) Be responsible for obtaining all necessary gate passes and permits for its personnel and vehicle; WISON will issue necessary Letters of Authorization (LOA).

(p) Provide monthly reports including statistics on treatments, incidents (LTI, medical cases), etc., as required by WISON or the end client (e.g., ADNOC).

(q) shall provide comprehensive onsite medical support services, including but not limited to:

 Primary emergency medical response and first aid.

 Basic life support (BLS) services.

 Management of common illnesses and injuries.

 Referral of cases to higher-level medical facilities when necessary.

 Maintenance of medical records and documentation.

 Health and safety promotion (optional, can be added).

 Operation and maintenance of the provided medical equipment and ambulance.

 Ensuring the clinic/first aid post (if setup is chosen) is always stocked and functional (consumables replenishment to be billed separately after initial stock).

A. Project Management Personnel

RPM must appoint a dedicated account manager or site coordinator for communication and coordination with WISON.

B. Training for Personnel

RPM shall ensure all deployed medical personnel are adequately trained and competent in their respective fields, holding current certifications relevant to their duties (e.g., BLS, ACLS for the nurse).

C. Quality Standard

All medical services, equipment, medicines, and consumables supplied shall meet or exceed the standards required by the Department of Health Abu Dhabi (DOH) and any applicable regulations at the project site location. All medications must be within their expiry date.

(r) maintain appropriate insurance (Professional Liability, General Liability, Vehicle Insurance, Workmen's Compensation).

(s) Any additional hours beyond agreed working hours will be charged based on pro-rata

**5. Warranty**

5.1 All medical equipment supplied by RPM, whether sold to WISON on a one-time basis or provided as part of the leased services, must be accompanied by valid calibration certificates and/or third-party verification certificates proving their compliance with relevant standards and accuracy upon deployment. RPM shall be solely responsible for all costs associated with the initial certification, annual recalibration, and recertification of all equipment throughout the service period. RPM must provide updated valid certificates to WISON upon each renewal. Failure to maintain valid certifications shall be considered a breach of contract. Upon written notice from WISON, RPM shall have a period of five (5) calendar days to rectify such failure. If the failure is not rectified within this period, WISON reserves the right to withhold payment for the related equipment or services until the issue is rectified.

5.2 Obtainment of Necessary Approvals

RPM expressly warrants that all equipment, personnel, documentation, and services supplied under this Agreement shall fully comply with all requirements and standards mandated by the relevant local government authorities and ADNOC for the operation of onsite medical services.

RPM shall, at its own cost and expense:

a) Preparing, submitting, and managing all applications required to obtain all necessary approvals, permits, and licenses from the relevant local government authorities and ADNOC for the services contemplated under this Agreement.

b) Providing all necessary documentation, including but not limited to calibration certificates, personnel qualifications, training records, and equipment specifications, required for the applications.

c) Serving as the primary point of contact and liaising directly with the said authorities throughout the application and approval process.

d) Promptly informing WISON of the application status and promptly rectifying or replacing any item or correcting any deficiency identified by the authorities that prevents or delays the obtainment of approvals.

Any failure to obtain the required approvals due to the non-compliance of items supplied by RPM or RPM's failure to diligently pursue the application process shall constitute a fundamental breach of this Agreement by RPM.”
