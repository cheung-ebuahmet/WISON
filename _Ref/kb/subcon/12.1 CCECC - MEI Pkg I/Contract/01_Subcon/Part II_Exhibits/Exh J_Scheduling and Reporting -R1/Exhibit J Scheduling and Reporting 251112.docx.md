7. WORK PROGRAM
   1. WORK PROGRAM (Baseline Schedule)

In accordance with SUBCONTRACT, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall, within the timeframe specified in This Exhibit, submit a detailed WORK PROGRAM for CONTRACTOR's APPROVAL. This program shall be a Level 4 detailed schedule and serve as the Baseline Schedule. It must be developed using the Critical Path Method (CPM) as defined in the CONTRACT and shall evolve from the schedule submitted with the tender. In addition to the requirements of Sub-Clause 10.8.2 of the CONTRACT, in addition to those specified in Annex 2: WBS and Annex 3: Civil Works Package 1 Schedule:

1. It shall be developed using the Critical Path Method (CPM), with the primary critical path clearly identified (e.g., highlighted in red).
2. It shall incorporate the full Work Breakdown Structure (WBS), with activities detailed down to the construction-step level, in accordance with Annex 4: BASIS OF CONSTRUCTION & PRE-COMMISSIONING PROGRESS MEASUREMENT.
3. It shall clearly define the logical relationships (dependencies) and constraints between all activities.
4. It shall include all KEY MILESTONE DATES and key intermediate milestones.
5. It shall realistically account for resource limitations, weather patterns, public holidays, and other site-specific factors.
6. It shall align with the overall TIME for COMPLETION and the required dates for sectional completion.

The Tender Schedule shall be accompanied by a comprehensive Basis of Schedule narrative to provide the Contractor with a clear understanding of the Subcontractor's execution strategy. This document shall include, as a minimum:

1. The basis for schedule development and the project calendars to be used (in compliance with Annex 5: Project Calendar).
2. A description of the site layout, construction area divisions, sequence of works, and a summary of the construction methodology.
3. Key quantities, planned durations, productivity rates, and the planned deployment of manpower and equipment.
4. A specific section addressing any long-lead materials or equipment.
5. Mitigation measures to ensure schedule adherence and a contingency plan for managing potential deviations.
6. An identification of potential risks, challenges, and required points of coordination.

The SUBCONTRACTOR shall update the WORK PROGRAM weekly and submit it with progress reports in accordance with SUBCONTRACT and the procedures in Annex x of this Exhibit.
