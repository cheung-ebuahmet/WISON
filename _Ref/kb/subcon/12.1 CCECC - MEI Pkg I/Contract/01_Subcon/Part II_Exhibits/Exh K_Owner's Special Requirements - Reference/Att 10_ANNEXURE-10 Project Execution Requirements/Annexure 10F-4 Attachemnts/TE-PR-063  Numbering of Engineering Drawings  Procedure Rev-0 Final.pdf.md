1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this procedure is to establish a unified numbering system for all drawings and
documents developed by the CONTRACTOR or COMPANY during Greenfield projects. Any new
drawing/document shall comply with this procedure.
This procedure shall be followed to facilitate the storage, tracking, sorting or retrieval of
document/drawings plant-wise, project-wise, discipline-wise, etc. Numbering system remains the same
for all phases of the project, with only change being the project number which changes from FEED to
EPC.
This procedure has been developed to meet the following objectives:
 Standardizing the engineering numbering system in the COMPANY.
 Management of Project deliverables by maintaining Quality Assurance practice.
2. SCOPE
This procedure specifies the requirements of numbering system for all drawings and documents.
This procedure covers the following categories of drawings and documents:
 Engineering Design
 Manufacturer, Vendor, Supplier
The exceptions being the documents that are solely for the internal use of one of the parties and the
updating of certain “Brownfield” documents that relate to existing facilities, which must retain their
current plant document numbering format. For drawings/documents generated for modifications
within the existing plant, the existing plant numbering procedure shall be followed.

Numbering format of Engineering drawings and documents shall be as the below table, (during Pre-
FEED, FEED and EPC stages). Respective contractor logos shall be inserted in title block based on stage
of project. When status of project is changed, revision numbering shall be modified.

1. Project No: The project number shall be identified by 5 digits or as directed the COMPANY. This shall not
be changed by EPC contractor unless otherwise directed by Company.
2. Plant Name: Plant name shall be identified by two alphabet letter/digit (See Attachment-4)
3. Unit No: Unit numbers shall be identified by 3 digits, see note 3 below
4. Drawing Type code: Contains three parts. First 2 digits are for Discipline code, second two digits are for
the drawing type and last 3 digits are Serial number or sequence number (See Attachment-2)
5. Drawing Size: Identify the drawing size; ISO Standard sizes A4, A3, A2, A1 and A0 shall be used (See
Attachment-3)
6. Drawing revision: 2 digits, see note 2 below
7. Sheet Number: 2 digits, depending on number of sheets no. of digits may vary (applicable only for group
drawings like isometrics, loop drawings etc.)

the main/cover page centre in bold letters and footers of each page.
6. Titles of documents like data sheets, equipment or material specifications, etc. shall appear on the cover
page as well as on each page header or top of the page. No document shall have more than one title
and no document shall be without a title.
