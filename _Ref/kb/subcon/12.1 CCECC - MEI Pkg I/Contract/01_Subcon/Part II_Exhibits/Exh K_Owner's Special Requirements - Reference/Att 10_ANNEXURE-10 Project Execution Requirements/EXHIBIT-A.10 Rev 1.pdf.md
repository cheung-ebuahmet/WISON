1 A.10.1 PERFORMANCE TEST RUN
The PERFORMANCE TEST RUN of the entire INSTALLATION shall be
achieved in phases as per the system breakdown noted in EXHIBIT B.38.
Performance Guarantee shall be as per EXHIBIT A.11 and Acceptance
Criteria as per EXHIBIT B.38 for overall performance of the unit at name
plate capacity of 12,000 TPD.
The facilities, forming part of installations, covered under each phase are
described in EXHIBIT A.1.
PERFORMANCE TEST RUN can be conducted only after the respective
installation has achieved stable and safe operation, free from leaks and
defects, at not less than 100% capacity for a continuous duration of
seventy two (72) hours and subject to Company having issued the
READY FOR PERFORMANCE TEST RUN CERTIFICATE to EPC
Contractor in accordance with the provisions of EXHIBIT B.38.
The required PERFORMANCE TEST RUNS and the duration thereof are
set out in the table below.
The respective PERFORMANCE TEST RUN for each phase shall apply to
the entire installation facilities covered under that Phase, unless Company
agrees to exclude a particular part or parts thereof from that respective
PERFORMANCE TEST RUN. Such part or parts of the installation shall
be tested separately.
EPC Contractor shall provide to Company, for Company approval, a
detailed PERFORMANCE TEST RUN procedure at least six (6) months
before the scheduled date for respective PERFORMANCE TEST RUN,
which shall also include details of calibration and tolerance of flow
measurement instruments, sampling and laboratory and analytical
procedures.
If a respective PERFORMANCE TEST RUN is interrupted or not
completed for reasons attributable to EPC Contractor it shall be repeated
in full at EPC Contractor's cost. If the interruption is not attributable to EPC
Contractor, Company shall have the option of calling for a full repetition of
that PERFORMANCE TEST RUN or only the incomplete portion of that
PERFORMANCE TEST RUN.

2 A.10.2 TEST REPORTS
Test Reports shall include all data necessary to verify the performance
guarantee conditions have been achieved, including but not limited to
Instrument data analysis data, DCS archive data, as well as calculations
and interpretation of data. Test Reports shall be distributed as agreed
with Company prior to each PERFORMANCE TEST RUN.
Test reports shall be submitted to Company within two (2) weeks after
completion of the respective PERFORMANCE TEST RUN.
EPC Contractor shall coordinate with OEM/ Vendors and review ITP of all
equipment.
3 A.10.3 LABORATORY EQUIPMENT & FACILITIES
EPC Contractor shall provide at its own cost all laboratory equipment or
testing devices that are necessary to achieve accuracy in measurement in
order to prove the performance guarantees have been met. This is in
addition to the requirements specified in EXHIBIT-C regarding scope of
supply of laboratory equipment, if any.
