1. PURPOSE
The purpose of this procedure is to establish a unified tagging system for the new assets of the plant
and for existing assets, which do not have a numbering system (mainly the civil assets).
2. SCOPE
This procedure specifies the requirements of numbering system for assets of the plant and existing un-
tagged assets. This procedure has been developed to meet the following objectives:
 Maintaining quality assurance practice.
 Unifying and standardizing the numbering system in ADNOC Gas Processing.

Numbering of equipment shall be as follows:
Unit number Equip. code identification Serial Number Suffix Code

1. Unit No.: Unit numbers for the new projects should be identified by 3 or 4 digits
2. Equipment code identification: Up to 4 alphabet letter code. (See Attachment-1)
3. Serial number of equipment: 3 digits, running sequentially from 101 for each group of
equipment and for each process unit.
4. Suffix code: A, B, C, D, etc... : Denotes identical equipment for the same category.

11. MANUAL VALVE TAG NUMBERING
All actuated valves, Pressure Safety Valves (PSV), Relief Valves, Motor Operated Valves (MOV) and
Emergency Shutdown (ESD) valves shall be tagged as per instrument tagging procedure (refer section
8 of this document).
Manual valves are considered as a piece of asset which requires maintenance / inspection etc. and
which needs a track history as a Maintainable Equipment. Purpose of manual valve tagging is as
mentioned below;
 To easily identify the valves and raise break down work order from operation
 To register the history of inspection and maintenance record in Maximo
 To comply with asset integrity policy
 To have the data sheets and maintain the spare parts.
Following guidelines are applicable for manual valve tagging philosophy;
 Manual isolation valves size 4” and above, for all service/fluids shall be assigned a tag number.
 Tag numbers shall be shown on P&IDs and in Piping isometric drawings
 In Maximo, each manual valve tag entry shall be associated with the respective line number.
Additional details like vendor reference/drawing, data sheet number can be added.
