B.21.1 DEFINITIONS
1. Area Classification
Company facilities handle flammable, toxic and hazardous materials. In
order to differentiate those areas where these specific hazards exist, the
Company Permit to Work (PTW) System requires that each Plant and
Pipeline Division classify specific areas as either a Restricted Area or an
Unrestricted Area. Additionally, there is a requirement to further define
zones within the Restricted Areas where there is an identified Hydrogen
Sulphide (H S) hazard.
2
1,1 Restricted Areas
These are areas, which are:
i) Operating areas of an oilfield or the Plant and thus require
precautions and controls. These may, or may not, include access
roads
ii) Areas deemed to be hazardous under Electrical Classification
Codes and therefore requiring further controls
iii) Critical for the operation of the facility, e.g. Control Rooms and
Electrical substations
In the Pipeline Division, thirty (30) meters either side of a designated
pipeline, or Pipeline corridor, is defined as a Restricted Area. The intent
is that no work may take place within thirty (30) meters of any Pipeline
containing hazardous material without Company PTW controls being
applied.
1.2 H S Zones
2
These are zones within the oilfield, operational plant, or pipeline,
restricted areas where process streams contain, or are likely to contain,
concentrations of H S in excess of five hundred (500) ppm and where
2
more stringent measures are required for control of access to, and work
within, the defined H S Zoned Area.
2
1.3 Unrestricted Areas
These are all other areas within the oilfield, Plant or Pipeline perimeter
fence, which have not been defined as Restricted, and which do not
normally require formal control under the PTW System.

B.21.2 GENERAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR WORKING NEAR OFF SITE
PIPELINES
EPC Contractor shall comply with the following general instructions when
working near or at Company pipelines:
• Detailed drawings of the intended work to be performed shall be submitted
for Company approval
• Company’s Permit to Work shall be obtained for any work to be performed
within thirty (30) metres of a pipeline.
• EPC Contractor shall review and observe all safety and security regulations
of Company before and while working near Company pipelines. A copy of
these regulations can be obtained from Company on request.
• Prior to any excavation in the vicinity of an existing pipeline, the route of the
pipeline shall be accurately identified and marked by EPC Contractor
• Excavate by hand whenever within a horizontal distance of five (5) metres
or less on either side of a pipeline
• Dewatering of excavated areas near the pipelines by EPC Contractor shall
be subject to Company’s prior approval.
• Utmost care shall be taken when excavating nearby and/or under the
pipelines.
• Where permanent hardtop roads are proposed to cross existing pipelines,
these pipelines must be protected by installing approved reinforced
concrete culverts. These shall be subject to prior Company approval.
• New pipelines and other services must cross beneath existing pipelines with
one thousand (1000) mm minimum vertical separation.
• It shall be EPC Contractor's responsibility to provide, install, maintain and
subsequently remove the temporary supports while excavating under any
pipeline. Design, fabrication/construction and installation of all such
supports shall be subject to prior Company approval.
• No backfilling shall take place before Company’s final inspection and
approval.
• Working at night shall be subject to Company approval.
• Working on Fridays and Public Holidays shall be subject to Company
approval.
• Waste shall be identified and the frequency of removal shall be agreed with
Company.
• Company reserves the right to supervise all work executed within the limits
specified above.

B.21.3 SPECIFIC ADDITIONAL INSTRUCTIONS FOR WORKING NEAR
COMPANY PETROLEUM PIPELINES
B.21.3.1 GENERAL
• Clear distinction applies for working within thirty (30) metres and
unrestricted areas as decided by Company.
• Company will apply its own Work Permit System when work is to be done in
its restricted area.
• Company will issue to EPC Contractor an Authorisation to Work document
to confirm its approval of the work to be carried out.
• Company’s PTW Systems are not normally required if the work is more than
thirty (30) metres away from pipeline facilities otherwise the PTW system
shall be followed.
• Should any pipeline leakage occur during EPC Contractor work performance,
the work should be stopped immediately, the area evacuated and Company
notified.
• For inhabited areas within the restricted area, each case is to be treated
separately.
B.21.3.2 PROCEDURE FOR WORKING IN A RESTRICTED AREA
• Before EPC Contractor commences any work in the restricted area, which
includes thirty (30) metres from Existing Facilities, it shall inform Company
and present to Company all documentation regarding the work to be done
including drawings with locations and vehicles required to enter the area,
date and time of work, etc.
• Equipment and vehicles to be used shall be inspected and approved by
Company prior to entering any restricted area.
• EPC Contractor shall provide supervision and the supervision shall attend
Company PTW training.
• EPC Contractor personnel shall attend an induction course before the work
commences.
• Company, at its discretion will issue the required permits to EPC Contractor.
• EPC Contractor shall only commence the work once the required permits
are in its possession and toolbox talks have been completed.

B.21.3.3 PROCEDURE FOR WORKING IN NON RESTRICTED AREA
• Before EPC Contractor commences work outside thirty (30) metres from
Existing Facilities, it shall inform Company, and present all documentation
regarding the work to be done, and obtain written approval.
• Company will check EPC Contractor’s working activities in the vicinity of its
pipelines.
• After completion of the work, EPC Contractor shall notify Company.
In Pipeline Division, where the distance from the pipeline exceeds thirty (30)
meters, but is less than two hundred (200) meters, Third Parties and EPC
Contractor are required to advise Company of proposed work activities and to
obtain a No Objection Certificate (NOC) prior to commencing work. After
evaluating the risk, the Asset Owner decides what PTW System controls (if
any) are required.
Where the distance from the pipeline or pipeline corridor exceeds two hundred
(200) meters, Third Parties or EPC Contractor need to advise Company of any
proposed work activity but No Objection Certificate (NOC) from Company is not
required for commencing the work.
B.21.3.4 WORKING NEAR NON COMPANY OWNED PIPELINES
When it is proposed to perform work for Company but adjacent to non-
Company owned pipeline, EPC Contractor is to apply for and comply with all
work permit requirements imposed by the relevant Party involved.
B.21.4 ACTION TO BE TAKEN ON EPC CONTRACTOR'S FAILURE TO

Company may stop EPC Contractor’s work if Procedures or Instructions are not
implemented or fulfilled.
Any damage resulting from a breach by EPC Contractor shall be subject to
evaluation by Company and billed to EPC Contractor’s account.

B.21.5 INSTRUCTIONS FOR PROTECTING EXISTING FACILITIES DURING
WORK
B.21.5.1 CROSSING UNDER OVERHEAD ELECTRICAL TRANSMISSION LINES
EPC Contractor shall:
• Give Company a minimum of seven (7) days advance notice of its
intention to work in the vicinity of existing overhead lines.
• Obtain a Permit to Work before carrying out any work within thirty (30)
metres on either side of an electrical transmission line.
• Not excavate within ten (10) metres of an overhead cable support pole.
• Restrict vehicle passage to the recognised and established track
crossings. Obtain relevant permits for heavy vehicles that have to pass
or work beneath overhead cables.
B.21.5.2 EXCAVATION OF/OR NEAR BURIED ELECTRICAL CABLES
EPC Contractor shall:
• Give Company a minimum of forty-eight (48) hours advance notice of EPC
Contractor's intention to excavate in the vicinity of existing buried cables.
• Obtain a permit before carrying out any work within thirty (30) metres on
either side of buried cables.
• Use hand excavation within a horizontal distance of five (5) metres or less
from a buried cable.
• Identify all routes of cables in the vicinity of any proposed excavation under
supervision of a Company Representative.
• Provide adequate, suitable support for excavated cables.
• When laying pipelines beneath all electrical cables ensure a minimum
clearance of one thousand (1000) mm.
B.21.5.3 CROSSING ROADS
EPC CONTRACTOR shall:
• Restrict excavation of roads and recognised tracks to outside normal hours
of road use.
• Give a minimum seven (7) days’ notice to Company if it is necessary to
close an established crossing under the overhead electrical transmission
system or any other established Company access track or road.
