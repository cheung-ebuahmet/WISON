B.20.1 STATEMENT ON QUALITY OF MANPOWER AND FACILITIES
Company is committed to the continuous achievement of quality work and quality
products.
Achieving this requires complete co-operation between all contracting parties in all
Project phases. It is especially necessary when many EPC Contractors/sub-
Contractors or large numbers of personnel are involved.
Effective management of the human resources is critical for all Project activities
and the proper handling of this facet must be ensured from all aspects, including
qualification, experience, services and facilities provided, etc. The target is to
achieve a quality product on schedule through the use of quality human resources.
Temporary Works means all temporary facilities, buildings and contents thereof of
every kind required for or concerning the execution or maintenance of the work.
For avoidance of doubt, the requirements of this EXHIBIT shall apply equally for
EPC Contractor as well as Sub-Contractors, and to also any permanent buildings,
facilities, etc. hired or used temporarily for execution of work.
The following requirements are given to emphasize Company's commitment to this
critical subject and are intended to supplement rather than supersede any other
requirements of the Agreement or sound working practices.
EPC Contractor shall ensure that the following are satisfactorily provided and
managed and corrective action taken immediately if necessary:
• SITE Facilities, Services and Safety
− Proper transportation of personnel, e.g. by busing
− Availability of hygienic facilities e.g. toilets, hot water, etc.
− Clean offices, working locations, etc.
− Hygienic and clean sites.
− Healthy food presented at the correct time. To be coordinated with camp
catering supplier.
− Proper working time and rest roster, i.e. working hours shall generally not
exceed 10 hours per day.
− Provision and correct use of Personal Protective Equipment.
− Rest Shelters / Cooling Shelters in Summer
− Prayer room and ablution facilities
− Appropriate use of known HSE signs, Posters (in appropriate languages)
for all the jobs/tasks performed.

B.20.2 COMPANY GENERAL STANDARDS OF WORKING CONDITIONS FOR
EPC CONTRACTOR'S PERSONNEL
EPC Contractor shall comply with the following general standards of working
conditions:
B.20.2.1 Office, Workshop, and Camp Facilities
EPC Contractor shall provide its personnel with a safe and hygienic working
environment. Accordingly, camp accommodation, mess facilities, office, workshop,
storage facilities, etc. shall comply with Company HSE Standards and Procedures
as well as with the Local Governmental Regulations as applicable.
The facilities shall be fenced, adequately ventilated, lit and air-conditioned.
The facilities shall be appropriately furnished and kept tidy at all times. Moreover,
sufficient and suitable storage facilities shall be provided for all equipment, tools,
etc.
The facilities shall be well maintained and in good repair at all times.
B.20.2.2 Sanitation facilities
EPC Contractor shall provide toilets and bathrooms for its personnel:
• In sufficient numbers and equipped with suitable sanitary fittings.
• Isolated from all other facilities and shall not have direct access to
accommodation and office units and kitchen.
• Provided with sufficient and continuous supply of hot and cold water.
• Kept Clean
EPC Contractor shall provide adequate sewage collection, treatment and disposal
facilities. EPC Contractor shall install and maintain a treatment and disposal
system meeting Company and local Regulatory requirements.

B.20.2.3 Cleaning
All SITE accommodations, offices, workshops, storage facilities including fixtures,
fittings, surfaces, structures, equipment, tools, utensils, etc., shall be kept clean at
all times. EPC Contractor shall submit a cleaning schedule along with EPC
Contractor’s proposed methods and materials for Company approval.
All cleaning equipment and material shall be kept in a sanitary condition at all
times and kept away from food preparation, storage and services areas when not
in use.
Suitable and sufficient refuse bins for domestic, technical and catering areas must
be provided. Such bins shall always be kept clean by using hot water and
detergents/disinfectants, and be kept covered. The refuse shall be collected daily
and disposed off at site(s) designated by Company, or by the local authority, as
garbage dump site(s).
B.20.2.4 Safety Measures
EPC Contractor shall provide its personnel with suitable and sufficient high
Standard PPE equipment such as safety boots, hard hats, overalls, gloves, H2S
Monitors and goggles as per Company HSE requirements / recognized Standard.
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for providing a safe working and living
environment for its personnel and shall comply with all Government and Company
HSE requirements regarding personnel, equipment, vehicles, and camps.
B.20.2.5 Transportation of Personnel
Transportation shall be provided using appropriate vehicles that shall always be in
sufficient numbers and in safe, roadworthy condition and complying with Abu
Dhabi traffic regulations.
Transportation schedules and frequency of trips shall take into consideration the
number of EPC Contractor personnel to be transported and the work schedules at
the various SITES.
B.20.2.6 Remuneration
EPC Contractor shall comply with the requirements of the U.A.E. Labor Law with
regard to the timing and method of payment to its personnel of their salaries,
wages, overtime entitlements, allowances and other benefits.
EPC Contractor shall be obliged to pay its personnel the amount of salary/wage
and overtime stated in their individual contract of employment. Company will have
the right to audit EPC Contractor’s relevant books to ensure compliance with such
requirements.

B.20.2.7 Work Schedule
Should EPC Contractor’s personnel be required to work extra hours, the number
of such extra hours worked by each person per day shall be controlled to avoid
having any impact on safety and the person's performance and efficiency.
EPC Contractor shall comply with the leave schedule(s) of its personnel as
provided in the employment contract of its personnel, or in U.A.E. Labor Law,
whichever is applicable.
Working hours for outdoors activities during July & August shall comply with the
UAE Labor Ministry Regulations.
B.20.2.8 Health Standards
EPC Contractor shall, at its sole cost and expense, comply with and adhere to the
Ministry of Health requirements and regulations and the provisions of the U.A.E.
Labor Law with regard to the provision of a healthy and hygienic working and living
environment, issuance/renewal of medical certificates and health cards, provision
of medical treatment, first aid boxes, medication and vaccination, etc. Company
shall have the right to inspect EPC Contractor’s premises at any time to ensure
EPC Contractor’s adherence to such regulations/requirements and EPC
Contractor shall immediately affect any remedies as may be instructed by
Company.
B.20.2.9 General
Company may at its discretion report recurrent violations of the Labor Law
provisions relating to these requirements to the Government authorities for official
action.
The words adequate, sufficient, and appropriate as used in this document shall
require no further definition and shall be interpreted at Company discretion.

• Daily cleaning and dust control of construction access roads
• All equipment and material required for housekeeping, cleaning and janitorial

maintenance of the Temporary Works.

B.20.2.10.2 Maintenance of Temporary Works
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for regular inspection, routine maintenance and
any repair/replacement of all Temporary Works required for the Work including but
not limited to:
• All external and internal components of the Buildings, e.g. roofs, doors, windows,
walls, floors, ceilings, carpets, furniture, fixtures and fittings etc.
• All fixed equipment, e.g. air conditioning units, fire detection/alarm and firefighting
equipment etc.
• The plumbing systems and their components, e.g. sanitary equipment, water pipe
work, sewage/waste water pipe work, potable water tank, water heater etc.
• The electrical systems and their components, e.g. electrical cabling, outlets,
internal and external lighting etc.
B.20.2.10.3 Operation and Maintenance of Utilities
a. Operation
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for operation of the utilities including, but not
limited to, the following:
• Operation of the utilities on a continuous basis, i.e. 24 hours/day 7 days
/week
• Provision of sufficient competent and fully trained staff to allow continuous
operation
• Supervision of diesel fuel offloading and storage
• Disposal of utilities waste products in an approved manner and location
including such items as sludge from waste tanks, lube oil, filters, used
chemicals, packaging, etc.
• Provision of any mobile firefighting equipment e.g. dry powder, foam
required by the local authorities or regulations
• Provision of any equipment or test instrumentation that is necessary to carry
out the operation of the utilities.
• Disposal of Surplus / unsuitable excavated material shall be moved to an
agreed location within 200 m exclusion zone.
b. Maintenance
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for all maintenance and the provision of all
spares required keeping the utilities and all ancillary equipment fully operational
including:
• Provision of sufficient competent and fully trained staff to carry out the
maintenance work on a continuous basis

B.20.3 GENERAL CONDITIONS OF PERFORMANCE
EPC Contractor shall carry out the Work in accordance with the Project
Specifications.
EPC Contractor shall provide personnel, procure, purchase and deliver supplies
for the performance of the work and shall be responsible for the transportation,
delivery, storage, safety of such personnel and supplies.
Company reserves the right to inspect at all reasonable times all phases of EPC
Contractor’s operations to ensure conformity to Project Specifications, conformity
to rules and regulations, to ensure that safe and hygienic practices are being
adhered to at all times and that supplies conforming with the Specifications are
used. EPC Contractor shall promptly rectify deficiencies in that respect as
Company directs.

B.20.4 HEALTH AND SECURITY
EPC Contractor shall submit its HSE Plan and program covering the overall Work
program in writing to Company within thirty days of the Effective Date.
All EPC Contractor’s employees and representatives shall comply with the Rules
and regulations issued by Company and, as to the enforcement of these Rules
and Regulations, shall accept instructions issued by Company. EPC Contractor
shall be responsible for ensuring that all its employees and representatives are
aware of all HSE rules and applicable Regulations.
All EPC Contractor personnel shall be trained in fire protection and firefighting
activities.
Medical facilities and First aiders shall be available at Camp and work site.
EPC Contractor shall supply and issue all required recommendations by posters
such as “NO SMOKING”, “GAMES Prohibited IN THIS AREA”, “NO PARKING”,
etc. inapplicable languages as required.
Health Risk Assessment and COVID-19 procedures shall be prepared and updated as
per UAE Government regulations and ADNOC guidelines.
