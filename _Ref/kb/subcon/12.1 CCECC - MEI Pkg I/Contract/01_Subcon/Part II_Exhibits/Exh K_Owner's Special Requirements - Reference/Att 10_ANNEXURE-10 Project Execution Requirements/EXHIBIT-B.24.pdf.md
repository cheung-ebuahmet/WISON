B.24.1 INTRODUCTION
This procedure is intended to assist CONTRACTOR in the process of selecting
and supplying lubricants/grease for the PROJECT and defines the scope,
requirements, responsibilities, and procedures to be followed for the selection,
recommendation, and purchase of lubricants/greases for the PROJECT.
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for the co-ordination of all VENDORS’ and
SUB-CONTRACTORS’ lubrication requirements into a consolidated document for
final presentation to COMPANY.
This procedure applies to all equipment supplied for the PROJECT that requires
lubricants/greases.
EPC CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for 1st fill and subsequent fills till the
facilities are officially handed over to the End User.
B.24.2 CONTRACTOR REQUIREMENTS
CONTRACTOR shall:
• Supply all lubricants for equipment within CONTRACTOR’s scope of supply up
to PROVISIONAL ACCEPTANCE.
• Develop a Consolidated Lubrication Summary Report for all CONTRACTOR
furnished equipment in accordance with this procedure.
• Recommend lubricants for further warehouse inventory (first year of
INSTALLATION operation).
• Wherever possible, CONTRACTOR shall supply lubricants manufactured by
ADNOC FOD. If ADNOC FOD lubricants are not available, the lubricants
manufactured by ESSO/EXXON shall be used. Any deviation from these
sources of supply shall require COMPANY approval.
• Take all reasonable steps to ensure use of COMPANY specified products and
sources where appropriate.
B.24.3 SUPPLY OF LUBRICANTS/GREASES
The scope of lubricants to be supplied by CONTRACTOR shall include as a
minimum:
• Lubricants/greases required for initial fill of equipment
• Lubricants/greases required for flushing/replacement/consumption during pre-
commissioning, COMMISSIONING and TEST RUN up to PROVISIONAL
ACCEPTANCE.

B.24.4 CONSOLIDATED LUBRICATION SUMMARY REPORT
No later than three months prior to MECHANICAL COMPLETION, CONTRACTOR
shall prepare a Consolidated Lubrication Summary Report, per the format
provided in Appendix 1, for submittal to COMPANY. The report is in spreadsheet
format allowing detailed reports to be produced by performing various sorts (i.e.
equipment description, lubricant brand, or tag no.).
The Report shall contain a total list of the different types of lubricants/greases
required for the PROJECT to facilitate the exercise of consolidating and limiting
the number of different types of lubricants on the PROJECT.
The Report shall be completed so that each lubrication point-lubricant type
combination is listed on a separate line with tag number identification and a
separate column showing the amount of lubricant per point and the number of
points per unit in order to enable proper summing.
No allowance shall be made in this Report for the possibility of loss of lubricant
due to unforeseen events such as contamination, spillage, or leakage.
For actual lubrication and maintenance of the equipment, the VENDOR’s
Installation, Operation and Maintenance (IOM) Manual shall always be consulted.
The following guidelines will assist CONTRACTOR in compiling a complete
Consolidated Lubrication Summary report:
• Obtain completed Lubrication Data Sheet in electronic format from VENDOR
• Review and compare Lubrication Data Sheet with available information in
VENDOR’s IOM Manual
• Consult VENDOR regarding conflicts, errors, or questions and resolve all
issues
• Consolidate all VENDOR submittals into the Consolidated Lubrication
Summary report
• Consolidate lubricant types where possible
• Submit final completed report to COMPANY in both hard copy and electronic
format.

B.24.5 RECOMMENDED LUBRICANTS FOR FIRST YEAR OF OPERATION
CONTRACTOR shall recommend lubricants for further warehouse inventory (first
year of operation) via column D2 of the Consolidated Lubrication Summary report.
This new recommendation shall to the extent reasonable, prefer the products and
sources specified by COMPANY.
B.24.6 LUBRICATION DATA SHEET
During the equipment Request for Quotation stage, VENDORS shall be advised of
the PROJECT Lubrication Data Sheet (B.24.6.1) requirements, and given the
PROJECT Lubrication Requirements Specification. A diskette containing the
Lubrication Data Sheet shall be issued by CONTRACTOR to the successful
VENDORS with the equipment Purchase Order.
