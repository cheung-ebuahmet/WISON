A.11.1 GENERAL
This EXHIBIT defines the performance guarantees and other performance criteria,
which CONTRACTOR guarantees that the INSTALLATION shall satisfy. The
responsibility for meeting these guarantees rests with CONTRACTOR.
The guarantees shall apply to the INSTALLATION as a whole and also for the
respective INSTALLATIONS to be completed in Phases as described in EXHIBIT
B.38.
The conditions at which the respective TEST RUN is to be conducted to
demonstrate the performance guarantee and other performance criteria are
furnished in this document.
If the INSTALLATION is operated with different feedstock conditions and/or flow
rates than those specified herein, then the performance guarantees shall be
adjusted by mutual consent between COMPANY and CONTRACTOR. Such
adjusted performance guarantees shall be developed by CONTRACTOR as
approved by COMPANY and the respective TEST RUN is to be conducted with
these conditions.
The INSTALLATION performance shall be conducted in accordance with the
procedures given in EXHIBIT A.10 (TEST RUN Conditions) and demonstrate that
the INSTALLATION is:
1. Sufficiently stable in operation so as to avoid objectionable fluctuations in
stream flows during normal operation; and
2. Capable of operating satisfactorily without the routine use of spare
equipment, repeated or frequent equipment failures, excessive pressure,
or excessive manning requirements.
CONTRACTOR guarantees that the design has been developed in accordance with
the SPECIFICATIONS and warrants that the design and engineering has been
skilfully performed in accordance with professional engineering practice.
