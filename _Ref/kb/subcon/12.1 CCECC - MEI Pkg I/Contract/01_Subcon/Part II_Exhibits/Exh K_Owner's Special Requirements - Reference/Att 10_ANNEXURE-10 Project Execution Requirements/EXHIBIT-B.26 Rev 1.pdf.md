B.26.1

INTRODUCTION

This  EXHIBIT  covers  the  minimum  requirements  for  Field  Services  to  be
executed  by  CONTRACTOR  in  accordance  with  the  AGREEMENT  and
CONTRACTOR’s  PROJECT  Policies  and  Procedures,  approved  by,  or  to  be
approved  by  COMPANY  and  which  are  to  be  adhered  to  during  the
performance of the WORK.

B.26.2

SCOPE OF FIELD SERVICES

In general, CONTRACTOR shall provide all services including but not limited to
the  provision  of  qualified  and  skilled  labour,  provision  of  tools,  construction
equipment,  facilities,  indirect  materials,  direct  materials  and  consumables,
provision  of  testing  facilities,  provision  of  supervision  and  management  staff,
provision of other technical and professional services and each and every other
item  necessary  for  the  completion  of  all  activities  and  works  necessary  to
achieve  MECHANICAL  COMPLETION,  COMMISSIONING,  TEST  RUN,
PROVISIONAL  ACCEPTANCE  and FINAL  ACCEPTANCE  in  accordance  with
the procedures and requirements stipulated in the AGREEMENT.

Field Services  shall  conform  to  the requirements  set  out  in  EXHIBIT  B.26  and
the  AGREEMENT.  Field  Services  shall  include  but  not  be  limited  to  the
following:

B.26.2.1

PROVISION OF PERSONNEL

CONTRACTOR  shall  provide  all  personnel  including  but  not  limited  to  Field
engineers,  construction  labour,  supervisors,  inspectors,  managers,  etc.  as
necessary  for  the  proper  and  timely  execution  of  the  WORK.  CONTRACTOR
shall  provide  for  COMPANY’s  review  and  approval  an  Organisation  Chart  as
stated  in  B.26.3.  CONTRACTOR  shall  provide  at  SITE,  sufficient  number  of
adequately qualified and experienced personnel for management and control of
labour relations.
