B.45 VENDOR DOCUMENTS AND MANUALS
B.45.1 Purpose
The purpose of this document is to provide guidance on the documentation to be
provided by VENDORS. The CONTRACTOR shall provide the VENDOR
documents in an “As built” state as part of the final handover to the COMPANY.
B.45.2 Documents Content Requirements
The documents submitted by VENDORS shall contain sufficient detailed
information to provide CONTRACTOR / COMPANY with a complete
understanding of the supplied equipment/system operation:
• Manufacture / assembly
• Quality Systems
• Inspection and testing
• Sub VENDORS
• Packing & Shipment
• Storage
• Installation
• Interfaces
• SITE testing
• Operation
• Maintenance
The documents shall meet the recognized industry standard and the format to be
in accordance with the PROJECT standard as detailed in the Material
Requisition / Purchase Order.
B.45.3 Review Process of VENDOR Documentation
VENDOR documents shall be submitted to CONTRACTOR / COMPANY for
review in the agreed method and format in accordance with requirements
detailed in the Material Requisition / Purchase Order. The use of electronic
review shall be encouraged in order to reduce the review cycle time and the filing
of paper copies etc.
CONTRACTOR shall follow COMPANY documents: TE-PR-043 - Asset
Information Handover Procedure and TE-PR-050 - As-Built Procedure

The VENDOR document review will contain the following levels coding:
Code 1 Rejected, VENDOR to revise and resubmit. Work cannot
proceed.
Code 2 Comments as noted. Work may proceed subject to compliance
with incorporation of comments.
Code 3 No comments. No further comments. Work may proceed.
Code 4 Information only. Review not required.
All comments must be incorporated into documentation by the VENDOR in order
to receive final approval. Any comments not incorporated by VENDOR shall only
be after written agreement with CONTRACTOR / COMPANY waiving the
requirement for the comments to be incorporated in the document.
B.45.4 List of documents to be submitted
The document requirements from VENDORS shall be in accordance with the
detailed requirements listed in each Material Requisition / Purchase Order and
shall depend on the type of equipment and or systems being procured.
Attachment No. 1 is a general guide to the types of documents to be submitted
by package VENDORS. This shall be expanded / developed on each particular
Material Requisition / Purchase Order. Attachment No. 1 details the following:
Column A – Document to be submitted by VENDOR. This the type of
documents required by CONTRACTOR / COMPANY for
review / approval and incorporation into manuals.
Column B – Documents, which require approval before work may
proceed.
Column C – Documents to be included in Manufacturers Record Books.
Column D – Documents to be included in the Operations & Maintenance
Manual.
All VENDOR documentation will be included by CONTRACTOR in the final
handover package.
B.45.5 Attachment No. 1
Documents to be submitted by VENDORS.
Final equipment data sheets shall be submitted in native files. Code calculations,
pipe stress calculations and thermal calculations also to be submitted in native
file.
