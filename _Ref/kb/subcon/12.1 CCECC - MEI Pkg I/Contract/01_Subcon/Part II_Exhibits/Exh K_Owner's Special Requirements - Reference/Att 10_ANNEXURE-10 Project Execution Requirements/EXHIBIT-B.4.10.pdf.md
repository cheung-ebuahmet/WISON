1.0 INTRODUCTION
This Invoicing Procedure describes and details various aspects of the invoicing
requirements to be followed by the CONTRACTOR during the execution of the WORK.
This Procedure is to be used as a guideline for CONTRACTOR to produce a PROJECT
specific procedure to be submitted within 15 days of the EFFECTIVE DATE.
2.0 REFERENCES
▪ Articles : Articles 23 and 24 of AGREEMENT
▪ EXHIBIT B.4.0 : Project Completion Schedule
▪ EXHIBIT A.4.0 : Agreement Price, Invoicing & Payment and
Rates For Variations
▪ EXHIBIT B.4.10 : Invoicing Procedure for EPC Contractors
▪ EXHIBIT B.4.4 : Progress Review / Certification Procedure
The Procedure is generally in accordance with the provisions of the AGREEMENT. In the
event of a conflict, the provisions of the AGREEMENT shall prevail.
3.0 AGREEMENT PRICE
The AGREEMENT PRICE is defined in EXHIBIT A.4.1 and as subsequently required to
be amended in accordance with the provisions of the AGREEMENT.
4.0 TYPE OF INVOICES
There are three (3) types of invoices to be submitted by the CONTRACTOR, namely:
a) Invoice for Advance Payment
b) Invoices for the LUMP SUM PRICE against achievement of payment Milestones
in the month.
c) Invoices for the Reimbursable Items.( If any)
5.0 INVOICE CURRENCY
All invoices shall be in US Dollars. In case reimbursable costs are incurred in a currency
other than US Dollars, then conversion will be effected by referring to the closing
midpoint market rate for the dollar spot range in the country of expenditure on the last
business day during which the cost was incurred, as detailed in the “currencies, money
and capital market section” of the Financial Times.
In the unlikely event of de-pegging of UAE Dirhams, if CONTRACTOR elects to be paid
in UAE Dirhams in lieu of US Dollars, CONTRACTOR shall request COMPANY in writing
for the same. However, the payment of UAE Dirhams in lieu of US Dollars shall be based
on the proportion of UAE Dirhams component to the balance LUMP SUM PRICE in US
Dollars as mentioned in EXHIBIT A.4.2.5.10.

7.0 INVOICING AND PAYMENT
7.1. ADVANCE PAYMENT
CONTRACTOR shall submit an invoice for an advance payment equal to ten percent
(10%) of LUMP SUM PRICE against the provision by CONTRACTOR of an advance
payment bank guarantee in the form given in EXHIBIT A.2 from a prime bank registered
in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi and approved by COMPANY.
The advance payment shall be recovered by COMPANY at the rate of ten percent (10%)
of every subsequent invoice of LUMP SUM PRICE. The recovery from the last invoice(s)
will be such that the entire amount of advance payment made is recovered by
COMPANY.
The advance payment bank guarantee shall be equivalent to 10% of the LUMP SUM
PRICE and shall be of fixed value and valid only for one year from EFFECTIVE DATE.
Subject to receipt by COMPANY of CONTRACTOR’s correct and complete invoice for
the advance payment and of the performance bank guarantee and the advance payment
bank guarantee, COMPANY shall make payment to CONTRACTOR of the advance
payment within thirty (30) days after the EFFECTIVE DATE or thirty (30) days after
receipt of the invoice, the performance bank guarantee and the advance payment bank
guarantee, whichever is the latest.

7.2. LUMP SUM PRICE
Within 15 days following the end of each month, CONTRACTOR shall submit one
invoice against the LUMP SUM PRICE in accordance with the achievement of payment
milestones in the month, as further described in EXHIBIT A.4. CONTRACTOR shall
submit the invoice and COMPANY certified Milestone Completion Certificates with
details as per EXHIBIT B.4.4. COMPANY will make payment to CONTRACTOR by direct
transfer to the bank nominated by CONTRACTOR within 30 days of receipt of
CONTRACTOR’s correct invoice by company at Controller Accounts Payable,
COMPANY HQ, Abu Dhabi.
In the event that the PROJECT cut-off date for the first calendar month based on the
PROJECT calendar is equal to more than fifteen (15) days after the EFFECTIVE DATE,
the first calendar month will be considered as the first invoicing month. In the event that
the PROJECT cut-off date for the first calendar month based on the PROJECT calendar
is less than fifteen (15) days after the EFFECTIVE DATE, the second calendar month will
be considered as the first invoicing month.
7.3. REIMBURSABLE ITEMS ( Not Applicable)
CONTRACTOR shall submit separate invoices for each of the following at monthly
intervals in respect of costs incurred for reimbursable items during the preceding period
and not previously invoiced to COMPANY.
- Two Years Operating Spares
- Chargeable Costs
7.4. PAYMENT TERMS AND INVOICING INSTRUCTIONS
7.4.1. CONTRACTOR shall submit, within fifteen (15) days following the end of each month, an
invoice for LUMP SUM PRICE to COMPANY. However, CONTRACTOR can submit the
invoices for REIMBURSABLE ITEMS to the COMPANY as and when it becomes due.
7.4.2. All Invoices shall be addressed as follows:

8.0 BACK-UP DATA / SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS
8.1. LUMP SUM PRICE
As back-up data and supporting documentation for the LUMP SUM PRICE invoice, the
CONTRACTOR shall attach COMPANY approved Milestone Completion Certificate(s).
8.2. REIMBURSABLE ITEMS ( Not Applicable)
8.2.1. (i) Two Years Operating Spares
Invoices for two years operational spares shall comprise the actual landed cost
of the Two Years Operating Spares in accordance with the provisions of the
AGREEMENT and shall be supported by:
• Photocopy of the VENDOR’s invoice and other shipping documents
• Photocopy of relevant pages of “The London Financial Times” published
tables for all invoices containing currency conversion calculation, if
applicable.
• Material receipt report issued by COMPANY
• Copy of relevant Purchase Order
• Proof of Payment made to Vendor
Separate invoices shall be raised by the CONTRACTOR for each Purchase
Order.
(ii) The actual landed cost of Two Years Operating Spare will be calculated based on
its Ex works cost as invoiced by the VENDOR plus all inclusive 5% mark up
which is to cover the following cost:
▪ Cost of transportation from ex-works to port of embarkation
▪ Cost of material handling at port of embarkation
▪ Cost of sea-freight to port of entry in Abu Dhabi
▪ Cost of material handling at port on entry in Abu Dhabi
▪ Cost of Customs Clearance and levies (excluding Customs Duty)
▪ Cost of inland transportation to SITE (including unloading costs)
In the event, two year operating spares is procured locally from UAE, the
aforementioned mark up shall not be applicable for calculation of the actual
landed cost for that two year operating spare and such costs will be deemed to
be included within the LUMP SUM PRICE.
(iii) The cost of Two Year Operating Spares shall be reimbursed based on stage
payments as per the following:
- For an SPIR with value less than or equal to US$ 250,000 – Full Payment
shall be payable upon delivery at SITE.

document.

invoices containing currency conversion calculation, if any.
