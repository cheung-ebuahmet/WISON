B.49 OPERATING AND MAINTENANCE MANUALS
B.49.1 Introduction
This EXHIBIT provides a typical sample of contents with appropriate
explanations for guideline purposes only. There are also some requirements
detailed herein which CONTRACTOR shall comply with. Preparation of
Operating and Maintenance manuals by CONTRACTOR shall require close
interaction with COMPANY in order to ensure compliance with COMPANY
requirements. CONTRACTOR shall, therefore, jointly develop the Table of
Contents and obtain COMPANY approval prior to proceeding with preparation of
the manuals.
CONTRACTOR shall refer to the COMPANY Maintenance and Inspection Policy,
Maintenance Standard, Operating Procedure Development Guideline, Critical
Documents Management Procedure which are provided as Attachment 1 to
EXHIBIT A.2.4 Design Policy.
CONTRACTOR and SUB-CONTRACTORS / VENDORS shall prepare detailed
manuals for both Operating and Maintenance activities which includes all the
requirements contained within the COMPANY Maintenance and Inspection
Policy.
CONTRACTOR shall check COMPANY’s existing facilities’ Emergency
Procedures and Standing Orders to ensure compatibility prior to integration by
COMPANY.
CONTRACTOR shall check the existing Emergency Response Plans to ensure
that interfaces are properly identified and coordinated.
B.49.2 Documentation - General
CONTRACTOR shall comply with COMPANY procedures for the coding, format
and final completion of documentation, drawings and certification.
CONTRACTOR shall prepare procedures covering the preparation of the
requisite manuals for review and approval by COMPANY.
To achieve a smooth handover from the PROJECT to the Operating Teams, it is
important that all stages of the PROJECT are properly recorded to ensure that
correct practices have been followed. The appointment of a Quality Assurance
Group within the PROJECT Team will assist both in the identification, and
defining, of correct practices and that monitoring is complied with. The records
and documents generated during the project shall be included in the transfer of
responsibilities from construction and COMMISSIONING to operations.
Complete document packages for each SYSTEM shall be available before the
transfer of responsibilities takes place. Computerized records shall be utilized
where appropriate instead of hard copy documentation. Equipment data,
engineering specifications and maintenance data shall be provided for all
equipment for electronic input into COMPANY Maintenance Material
Management System (MAXIMO). Where a SYSTEM handover could be delayed

B.49.3 Operations Manual
This is statutory reference document for Operations Management. It shall
describe the INSTALLATION and contain the basic data which determines the
methods of protecting, operating and maintaining INSTALLATION. It shall
contain the operating parameters, including safe working limits of all vessels and
pipelines. It shall contain area classification plans. Plans take into account
access routes for emergency and rescue vehicles and other items to be agreed
with COMPANY.

B.49.4 Operating Procedures
This shall be a collection of detailed operating procedures relating to
INSTALLATION or to each SYSTEM as appropriate. The types and nos. of
operating procedures to be determined with COMPANY’s Operations staff.
The operating procedure shall describe the INSTALLATION in sufficient detail to
the level of a competent operator. (Refer “Management of Operating Procedures”
guidelines (OEX-GU-022) for more details)
In general, the contents of Operating Procedures as follows:
• Title page,
• Table of Contents,
• Purpose & Scope,
• Definitions/
• Interrelationships/customers,
• Process Responsibility,
• Work Procedure,
• Documents and records, and
• Assurance & Control, and
• References

B.49.5 Maintenance Manuals
These shall provide sufficient information to the Maintenance Engineer and be
produced in a form which will facilitate the setting up of a maintenance
management system for INSTALLATION. Excess information shall be discarded
after agreement with COMPANY. The final format and content will be decided by
COMPANY to reflect site policies.
The manuals shall contain the appropriate data to allow the Maintenance
engineer to perform his function effectively and include VENDOR information,
equipment description, detailed drawings, spares lists, references, re-ordering
procedures and maintenance procedures for normal and major overhauls.
