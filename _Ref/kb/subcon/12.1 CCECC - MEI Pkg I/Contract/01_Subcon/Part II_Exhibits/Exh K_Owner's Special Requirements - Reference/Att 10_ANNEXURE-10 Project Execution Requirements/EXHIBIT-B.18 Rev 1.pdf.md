B.18.1 GENERAL
Immediately after the Effective Date, EPC Contractor shall begin planning for the
Temporary Works required at SITE to support the construction phase, from initial
mobilization through demobilization on completion of the work. As temporary
works are a major cost, care must be taken to plan efficient and practical
facilities. These facilities shall be designed to support the field effort but shall be
removed after Project completion and the area restored to its original condition.
EPC Contractor’s construction manager shall lead the development of Temporary
Works necessary to support the Work at SITE.
B.18.2 TEMPORARY WORKS
B.18.2.1 TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION FACILITIES
Temporary construction facilities include, but are not limited to the following:
• Administrative and field offices for EPC Contractor and Company
• First aid, ambulance and medical services
• Temporary Prayer Room suitably equipped for climatic conditions
• Change buildings, sanitary facilities, waste water treatment and sewage
disposal
• Access Roads
• SITE fences, gates, and area lighting
• SITE fabrication facilities
• Warehouse and lay down yards
• Personnel and material transport
• Concrete Batch Plant
• Communication facilities
• Security services and gate house
• Utilities
• Electrical Power
• SITE lunch facilities
Company expects that lunch facilities be provided at the SITE, in order that
bussing between the SITE and the temporary living accommodation is avoided at
lunch times.

B.18.2.2 CONSTRUCTION CAMP ACCOMMODATIONS
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for providing accommodation for its staff
and workforce, include those of its Sub-Contractors and Vendors. EPC
Contractor shall be fully responsible for all arrangements related to construction
camp accommodation, including identification of a suitable location and approval
thereof from all authorities having jurisdiction. Company will not allocate any land
or space for construction of camp accommodation. EPC Contractor’s
construction camp accommodation shall comply with all requirements specified in
the Agreement including but not limited to Company Group HS&E Management
Guidelines “EPC Contractor Camps and Temporary Buildings” (Latest Edition)
and EXHIBIT B.27 “Camp Accommodations” as well as those of local regulatory
bodies having jurisdiction over the matter.
EPC Contractor shall establish its accommodation camp location in accordance
with Company guidelines – a key document in this respect is the Company
HS&E Management Guidelines ”EPC Contractor Camp and Temporary Buildings
(Latest Edition) and EXHIBIT B.27 “Camp Accommodations”. Company may
consider a location closer to a risk source than that defined in the Company
guidelines providing it meets Company risk acceptance criteria.
B.18.2.3 TEMPORARY SITE FACILITIES AND LAYDOWN AREAS
EPC Contractor shall be responsible for providing temporary SITE facilities and
lay down areas for execution of Project Work scope.
EPC Contractor shall be fully responsible for all arrangements related to
temporary SITE facilities and lay down areas, including identification of a suitable
location and approval thereof from all authorities having jurisdiction. Company
will not allocate any land or space for temporary SITE facilities and lay down
areas.
EPC Contractor shall check the area’s and is fully responsible for its development
to suit its requirements including but not limited to levelling, preparation and
fencing to suit his requirements.
When laying out his temporary facilities areas and establishing traffic flow
patterns to respective Work fronts EPC Contractor is to take recognition of the
fact there may be other EPC Contractors working in the vicinity at the same time
and to minimise disruption to ongoing operations.
B.18.2.4 SPACE REQUIREMENTS
EPC Contractor shall lay out his temporary facilities and lay down areas which will be
subject to Company approval. EPC Contractor shall take into consideration all Sub-
Contractors, VENDOR representative, equipment/material receipt and handling,
fabrication, painting, subassembly, testing requirements and workshop etc. to
support the needs of the Work.

B.18.2.5 MEDICAL FACILITY
Provision of medical facilities at SITE, accommodation camps and any other area
where Work is being performed shall be EPC Contractor’s responsibility. Based on
the number of resident, fully qualified Medical Staffs will be available to provide first
aid to the personnel at construction site as well as camp.
B.18.2.6 FIRST AID AND EMERGENCY SERVICES
EPC Contractor shall provide and maintain first aid and emergency
equipment/ambulance to respond to any medical emergency involving EPC
Contractor. Sub-Contractor personnel, Company or PMC Personnel. Handling of all
medical emergencies is EPC Contractor’s responsibility. This facility shall be
provided at SITE, accommodation camps and any other area where Work is being
performed
B.18.3 ACCESS ROADS
B.18.3.1 ROUTES
The Ruwais Industrial Complex, within which a portion of the Work is to be executed,
is a hydrocarbon processing facility with its own internal network of existing paved
roads. EPC Contractor can, subject to traffic flow patterns agreed by Company,
utilise these roads during construction of the Work. Damage to or debris deposited
on the roads resulting from the EPC Contractors activities will be the responsibility of
the EPC Contractor and EPC Contractor shall be liable for the repair and making
good of any damage to said roads and removal for all debris associated with his
works.
EPC Contractor shall note he will have shared use of the existing road network within
the Ruwais Industrial Complex with the operation staff and other EPC Contractors.
Other temporary access roads, whether inside or outside the Ruwais Industrial
Complex, shall be the responsibility of EPC Contractor with the intent to enhance
construction mobility while mitigating the effects of traffic on existing roads.
B.18.3.2 SAFETY
Safety is a major consideration in planning for construction traffic. Therefore traffic
lights or other appropriate measures are suggested wherever construction traffic
must cross-existing and/or temporary construction roads. Also, construction traffic
must be limited to specific routes, which minimizes contact with, and impact on
existing roadways and other EPC Contractors/operations within adjacent areas.
