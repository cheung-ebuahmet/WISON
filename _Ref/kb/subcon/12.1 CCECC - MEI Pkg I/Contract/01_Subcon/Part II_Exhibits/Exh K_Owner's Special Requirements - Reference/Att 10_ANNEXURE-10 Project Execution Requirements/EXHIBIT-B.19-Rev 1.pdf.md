B.19.1 WORK PERMITS
Introduction
The Project is a major development involving significant construction activities
and, therefore, associated potential HSE hazards. In order to eliminate or reduce
this risk to an acceptable level, construction activities need to be closely
monitored and controlled, so that Existing Facilities and/or Third Party adjacent
facilities are not affected.
This document provides guidance on the use of Company / EPC Contractor
permits to work and defines the fire & safety requirement for construction areas.
Objective
The main objective of SITE Work Procedures is to ensure that all activities which
will be carried out by EPC Contractor are in compliance with Company Permit to
Work System and appropriate equipment is provided for handling emergencies
at construction areas.
All work performed adjacent to or within any Existing Facilities limits shall be
carried out under Company Permit to Work System, Latest Edition. The
Company Work Permit is a written document, normally issued on a daily basis
that authorize EPC Contractor to carry out work within a defined area and in
accordance with specified conditions.
EPC Contractor shall provide a detailed Method Statement and supporting Risk
Assessment to Company seven (7) days before the start of work for activities in
or affecting Existing Facilities. The Method Statement shall include the duties
and responsibilities of the Supervision personnel responsible for the work, a
detailed narrative execution plan, schedule, risk assessment (HSE) and quality
requirements, Specifications, and drawings. EPC Contractor shall agree with
Company activities that require a Method Statement. EPC Contractor shall plan
its submission of Method Statements allowing time for Company review in
accordance with durations specified in the Agreement. Company requires an
approved Method Statement prior to issuing Work Permits for HSE critical
activities.
The Work Permit provides warning of potential hazards, precautions to be taken
to mitigate those hazards, procedures to be followed and special personal
protective gear or equipment to be used.
EPC Contractor shall provide an Activity Sequence Control Form and follow
Activity Sequence Control where applicable. The EPC Contractor shall also carry
out a Tool Box Talk as relevant to the activity.

adjacent to Existing Facilities:

B.19.2 BLOCK PERMITS
A Block Permit valid for one month will be authorized by Company for (in
accordance with ADNOC HSE standard HSE-OS-ST02 and WMS as prevailing
at the time of execution):
• EPC Contractor ’s laydown area and office location;
• A fenced-off construction area (if applicable).
• A clearly defined work area at a remote location (if applicable).
The Block Permit is applicable for all required activities including hot work.
EPC Contractor shall introduce its own Work Permit System for the areas where
a Block Permit has been issued.

B.19.3 FENCED OFF CONSTRUCTION AREA
EPC Contractor shall isolate the construction area by means of a security fence
installed to Company requirements. Specific details as to location of fence line,
gates, escape routes, muster points, etc. are to be co-coordinated by EPC
Contractor with Company.
Within the fenced-off area, a specific location shall be reserved for smoking if
safe to do so subject to Company approval.
Apart from any allocated smoking location, smoking is prohibited.
The fenced-off area shall have a 24 hour guarded main gate for normal entry/exit
of all construction personnel/materials. This gate shall be guarded by EPC
Contractor security personnel.
If required, a second gate shall be provided for entrance to Existing Facilities, as
an emergency escape route, and for EPC Contractor’s personnel who have to
work outside the fenced-off area. This second gate shall be manned by
Company security personnel. Entrance to Existing Facilities via the second gate
shall require a separate identification badge. All requirements of the Oil Field
Security Police or any other authority having jurisdiction with respect to access to
restricted areas shall be complied with.
All activities outside the fenced-off area and under Company/Third Party
responsibility shall require a daily permit, which shall be submitted to
Company/Third Party operational team at least 48 hours in advance.
The fenced-off construction area shall be provided with the following facilities:
• Fixed gas detection (including SO2) along the fenced-off area if required as a
result of the QRA. Company will advise the location of the detectors.
• The gas detectors shall activate a local audible alarm (Klaxon) and shall be
connected to the alarm system of the Company’s Main Control Room (if
practical). Company will advise where connection can be made to the
existing Company alarm system.
• A hot line telephone connection shall be provided between the construction
area and the Company Main Control Room for emergency situations.

B.19.4 REMOTE UNFENCED WORK AREA
EPC Contractor shall, subject to Company approval, install fixed temporary
marker posts so positioned that the limits of the construction area subject to
work permit control can clearly be defined i.e. cooling water discharge
lines/outfall and flare area. Notices shall be clearly displayed advising that
restrictions apply to gain entry to the area and contact details of the supervising
control. The limits of the marked area shall not encroach into the exclusion
zone around any existing Company and/or other Parties facilities.
Access and egress points are to be established for the movement of
construction plant, equipment, materials and construction labor resources which
are subject to approval by the Company. Such equipment and the movement of
other materials and resources shall not deviate from defined routes into other
areas of the Ruwais Plant without prior Company approval. All requirements of
the Oil Field Security Police with respect to access to restricted areas shall be
complied with.
Within the marked-off area, a specific location shall be reserved for smoking, if
safe to do, subject to Company approval.
Apart from Company approved smoking location, smoking is prohibited.

B.19.5 NON COMPANY AREAS
Construction of the PROJECT facilities, if it entails work having to be executed
outside an area over which the Company has jurisdiction and/or Work Permit
control. In such cases the EPC Contractor is to liaise with, obtain and comply
with any and all permit requirements imposed by the party having primary
control of the area. This may entail the EPC Contractor having to perform work
under a multi permit system of which the most stringent conditions will apply.
The EPC Contractor is to liaise with and obtain approval of all concerned
parties (CICPA, FERTIL, ADNOC Refinery, etc.) prior to commencing work.
B19.6 IMPORTANT NOTES
1. This procedure is valid only for Project construction activities.
2. This procedure shall be updated in the future to include pre-commissioning
and Commissioning activities, which shall be subject to Company approval.
3 The work permit Procedure shall be updated for the SIMOPS phase of the
Project. The Work Permit Procedure shall be updated based on consultation
with Company Project, Operation and HSE team as applicable.
