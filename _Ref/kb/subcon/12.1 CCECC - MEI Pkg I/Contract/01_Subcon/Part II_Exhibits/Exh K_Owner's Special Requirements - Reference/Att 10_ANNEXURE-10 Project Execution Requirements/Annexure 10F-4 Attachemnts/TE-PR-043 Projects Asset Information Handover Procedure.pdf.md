1. INTRODUCTION
The Asset Information Management ensures the integrity of technical drawings, documents and geospatial
data across systems and the associated people during the lifecycle of an asset. As-built drawings and
documents are maintained as Master drawings and documents and are used as main source of information
for the Plants Operation and Maintenance activities as well as for future projects development. During the
Management of Change (MOC), the Asset information are created or updated by CONTRACTORs or
VENDORs. The Engineering and SmartPlant Data Deliverable Standards (TE-ST-005 and TE-ST-006) will
provide the requirements for the creation or updating Asset information during
2. PURPOSE
The purpose of this document is to define the requirements for the handover of asset information generated
during projects, and to ensure it is complete, consistent, and fit for operations, maintenance and projects.
3. SCOPE
This procedure covers Asset Information handover requirements for greenfield and brownfield projects and
throughout all its stages of Assess, Select, Define and Execute until commissioning as well as MOC.

6. ASSET INFORMATION HANDOVER- ELECTRONIC REQUIREMENTS
This section will outline the requirements for asset information handover in electronic formats:
6.1. CONTRACTOR shall follow all software requirements listed in Exhibit B13, TE-ST-005, IT Metadata
attributes requirements and other exhibits.
6.2. CONTRACTOR shall provide all source and native files of all project documents which was generated
during the project execution to COMPANY at various stages as per COMPANY requirements. COMPANY
have the right to add or remove documents in this classification during the project execution.

6.3. All electronic files, documents and drawings contents, structure, and formats to be followed as per
COMPANYʼs Codes, Standards, Procedures, and AGES etc.
6.4. CONTRACTOR shall provide licensed copy of all electronic and softwareʼs source codes, customization
files, configuration files to the COMPANY to operate, maintain and update electronic data and files
smoothly during Operation and Maintenance phase.
6.5. After the project completion and handover, if COMPANY found electronic information, files and data
provided by CONTRACTOR is not working, CONTRACTOR must provide all required information and
support to operate the electronic files smoothly and as intended.
6.6. All project documents shall be handed over as per COMPANYʼs metadata and structure requirement.
Refer Attachment (3) for a standard metadata template & structure.
6.7. All projectsʼ documents to be handed over in As-Built revision. Vendor documents to be handed over as
per the requirement stated above; it is the responsibility of Assess, SELECT, DEFINE, and EXECUTE
Contractor/ Consultant to ensure all VENDOR documents are handed over in electronic formats as per
COMPANY requirements.
6.8. CONTRACTOR shall sign ADNOC Gas Processing Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), that specifically
states commitments of the contractor and its sub contractors about information handling (Soft & Hard
copy), Retention period (as applicable) and secure disposal of all copies at the end of retention.
6.9. CONTRACTOR shall ensure secure disposal of information that is being processed by contractorʼs
systems/tools such as mail server, file server…etc.
6.10. Data needs to be encrypted if provided using HDD/SSD or any media.
6.11. If electronic data will be provided through Data transfer, this activity needs to be done through ADNOC
Gas Processing share file solution.
6.12. To deliver confidential information such as source code, it shall be handed face to face or through secure
transfer channel selected by ADNOC Gas Processing.
6.13. Secure packaging/Vault shall be used to deliver confidential physical documents to ADNOC Gas
Processing.
6.14. Information provided by the CONTRACTOR to the Company should have a classification labelling
(Confidential, Internal, Proprietary etc.) dependent on the type of information and its use within the
different teams.
6.15. Minimum 2 sets of HDD/SSD.
6.16. All the PDF documents/drawings shall be delivered in searchable format (OCR).

7. ASSET INFORMATION HANDOVER-HARD COPY REQUIREMENTS
For Brownfield projects, CONTRACTOR to handover all required asset information documents and data
generated during the project in accordance to the respective Site/Pipeline procedural requirements. For
Greenfield projects, this section will outline the Asset Information Handover requirements for hardcopy
submission,

technical data, drawings, manuals, correspondence, Deviations to engineering standards" (DCA
Register), Process Safety Information (PSI) as per ADNOC AIPS Manual Element 5, journals, accounting
records and other documents submitted by CONTRACTOR to COMPANY in the course of performance
of WORK as required by the COMPANY.

documents in this classification during the project execution. CONTRACTOR shall prepare final Asset

Review and Approval.

7.9. Specification for Handover Documents: Hard copies of all handover documentation must be on Metric
size paper and bound in sturdy white binders. International standard sizing listed below shall be
applied:
 A4 - 210 X 297 mm
 A3 ‒ 297 X 420 mm
 A2 - 420 X 594 mm
 A1 - 594 X 841 mm
 A0 - 841 X 1189 mm
A2, A1 and A0 shall be used selectively. All P&IDs, GA drawings shall be prepared on A3 drawings
and all dimensions, details shall be legible when prints are taken.
7.10. All documentation presented as printed material shall utilize paper of suitable quality for long term
storage (minimum acceptable quality of paper: 80g/sq. m). All documentation shall be of a suitable
quality to provide clear and legible reproduced copies.
7.11. Files shall be numbered as per below format.
 <Division No.>
 <Doc. Title>
 <File No.> is the unique number given to any specific Document which may contain more than 1
volume
7.12. All documentation shall be bound in European format A4 size binders with uniform size (refer
Attachment - 2). Drawings shall preferably be reduced to A3 size; however, if larger size drawings are
required for clarity, they shall be folded and placed in clear plastic sleeves within the binders. The
binders shall comprise:
 Strong white plastic covers. Plastic sleeves (for storage of large size drawings within binders).
 Transparent jackets for identification labels are to be used on the spine and cover sheets
 (Refer to Attachment-2). Binder cover sheet and spine identification label layouts shall be
submitted for COMPANY approval. Identification labels shall include COMPANY name & logo,
PROJECT title and no., CONTRACTOR name & logo and folder number.
7.13. The binders shall be robust and not susceptible to distortion by impact during shipping. The binders
shall not be overfilled/ under-filled and shall contain only a suitable number of documents to enable
convenient handling.
7.14. Tag numbers shall be indicated on the binder cover for each volume/ book (where applicable).
