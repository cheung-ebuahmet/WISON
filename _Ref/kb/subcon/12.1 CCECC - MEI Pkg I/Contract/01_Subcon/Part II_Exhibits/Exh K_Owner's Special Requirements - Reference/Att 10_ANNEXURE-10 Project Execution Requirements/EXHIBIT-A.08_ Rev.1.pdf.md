A.8 INTRODUCTION
During the execution of WORK, COMPANY shall station its PROJECT Team
personnel (including PROJECT MANAGEMENT CONSULTANT and
COMPANY’s inspection/certification personnel) in CONTRACTOR's home
office(s) and SITE office(s). COMPANY personnel may also visit and be
stationed at SUBCONTRACTOR's offices and manufacturer's establishments.
CONTRACTOR shall provide to COMPANY PROJECT Team, without limitation,
the facilities and services identified herein. The cost of providing and maintaining
the facilities and services for the phases/durations specified is included in the
LUMP SUM PRICE, except where specifically mentioned in this EXHIBIT A.8 as
reimbursable by COMPANY. In the event CONTRACTOR fails to execute the
WORK in accordance with the SCHEDULE specified in EXHIBIT A.6,
CONTRACTOR shall be responsible for continuing to provide the facilities and
services for the extended phases/durations at no additional cost to COMPANY.
CONTRACTOR shall submit detailed layouts of all facilities and accommodation
to be provided for approval of COMPANY prior to COMPANY mobilizing its
personnel.
All items supplied to COMPANY shall be new, of good quality, fit for purpose,
and to a standard equivalent, as a minimum, to that being utilised by
CONTRACTOR’s management / senior staff.
In respect of facilities and services to be provided by CONTRACTOR, the
numbers stated in this EXHIBIT may vary during the execution of the PROJECT.
CONTRACTOR shall perform a reconciliation on completion of the PROJECT of
the actual facilities and services provided. Any variance, both upwards and
downwards, shall be subject to a VARIATION in accordance with the rates
contained within EXHIBIT A.7.
All the facilities mentioned are generally to be provided by CONTRACTOR from
the EFFECTIVE DATE until the last PROVISIONAL ACCEPTANCE
CERTIFICATE except where specifically mentioned otherwise in this EXHIBIT
A.8.
Except as otherwise stated herein, ownership of the facilities provided to
COMPANY as described in this EXHIBIT A.8, including consumable items shall
rest with CONTRACTOR. CONTRACTOR shall retake possession of all items
on an "as is where is" basis when discharged by COMPANY. CONTRACTOR
shall not be entitled to any claim because of damage or loss to any item.
