**ATTACHMENT OF SUBCONTRACT AGREEMENT**

**Insurance Requirements**

Insurance Requirements

Refer to OWNER’s insurance requirements as applicable to CONTRACTOR and since SUBCONTRACTOR will subcontract from CONTRACTOR and undertake the Subcontract Work of the Project, SUBCONTRACTOR shall procure any insurance applicable to the aforesaid Work and SUBCONTRACTOR’S staff, equipment and apparatus etc. for execution of the Work.

SUBCONTRACTOR shall procure the said insurance from those insurers qualified and acceptable to CONTRACTOR and/or OWNER.

If SUBCONTRACTOR specifically procures any insurance for this SUBCONTRACT where applicable CONTRACTOR and OWNER will be named as co-insured. SUBCONTRACTOR shall provide insurance to his employees as per local law requirements.

Before SUBCONTRACTOR’S insurance procured for the Health, Safety and Environment Impact Assessment Work takes effect, CONTRACTOR and OWNER will be given the chance to review the terms of the insurance.

If the Subcontractor fails to effect and keep in force any of the insurances it is required to effect and maintain under the Subcontract, or fails to provide satisfactory evidence and copies of policies in accordance with this Section, the Contractor may (at its option and without Prejudice to any other right or remedy) effect insurance for the relevant coverage and pay the premiums due. The Subcontractor shall pay the amount of these premiums to the Contractor and the Subcontract Price shall be adjusted accordingly.

Nothing in this Exhibit limits the obligations, liabilities or responsibilities of the Subcontractor, under the other terms of the Subcontract or otherwise. Any amounts not insured or not recovered from the insurers shall be borne by the Subcontractor in accordance with these obligations, Liabilities or responsibilities.

Provided always that the Contractor and the Owner shall be indemnified from any economic and legal responsibilities for the Subcontractor`s failure or insufficient to effect and/or maintain the required insurances including but not limited to those for its manpower, machinery, equipment and third-party liability etc.

Below are terms on insurance for CONTRACTOR as included in the Prime Contract and quoted hereunder for SUBCONTRACTOR’S reference and applicable compliance.ARTICLE-41. INSURANCE

41.1 In the event any SUBCONTRACTOR PERSONNEL are performing WORKS in the UAE, without limitation to its obligations and responsibilities, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall obtain and maintain Workmen’s Compensation Insurance (Employer’s Liability) with a limit of liability of not less than USD 1,000,000 per occurrence.

41.2 In the event any SUBCONTRACTOR PERSONNEL are performing WORKS outside the UAE, without limitation to its obligations and responsibilities, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall obtain and maintain Workmen’s Compensation Insurance (Employer’s Liability) or insurance coverage of a comparable nature in accordance with the applicable LAW and/or the contracts of employment of the SUBCONTRACTOR PERSONNEL.

41.3 In the event the SUBCONTRACTOR uses motor vehicles in the performance of the WORKS in the UAE, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall obtain and maintain Motor Vehicle Third Party and Passenger Liability Insurance which shall be:

(a) as per the applicable LAWS of the UAE for death or injury to any THIRD PARTY; and

(b) for damage to PROPERTY of a THIRD PARTY with a limit of not less than USD 1,000,000 per occurrence.

41.4 Without limitation to its obligations and responsibilities, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall obtain and maintain All Risk Insurance on SUBCONTRACTOR CONSTRUCTION EQUIPMENT covering the full value thereof.

41.5 The SUBCONTRACTOR shall obtain and maintain:

(a) Third Party Liability Insurance with a limit of not less than USD 10,000,000 per occurrence; and

(b) Pollution Liability Insurance to cover the risk arising from the performance of WORKS and any pollution emanating/originating from the SUBCONTRACTOR CONSTRUCTION EQUIPMENT with a limit of not less than USD 10,000,000 per occurrence,

noting that the two insurance coverage requirements named in Articles ‎41.5(a) and ‎41.5(b) may be addressed in one insurance policy.

41.6 The SUBCONTRACTOR shall ensure that all insurance policies provided by the SUBCONTRACTOR shall:

(a) be obtained on an additional insured basis in the names of the PARTIES with the exception of the insurances required under Articles ‎41.1 and ‎41.2; and

(b) contain a waiver of subrogation against its contractual principals which shall include the OWNER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES,

to the extent of the liabilities assumed by the SUBCONTRACTOR under the AGREEMENT.

41.7 Upon the OWNER’s request, SUBCONTRACTOR shall provide the OWNER with certificates of insurance or other documentary evidence, satisfactory to the OWNER, of the insurance cover and conditions required under this Article ‎41 and the AGREEMENT. The OWNER’s acceptance of such evidence does not constitute a waiver, release or modification of the required insurance.

41.8 If at any time the OWNER considers that any insurance certificate provided by the SUBCONTRACTOR is not sufficient to evidence that such insurance policies have been obtained and maintained, the OWNER may request, and the SUBCONTRACTOR shall promptly provide, any further information the OWNER requires to verify that such insurance policies have been obtained and maintained and/or an undertaking from the SUBCONTRACTOR confirming that such insurance policies have been obtained and maintained. The OWNER may take any steps necessary to ensure that the insurance policies shall remain in force throughout the term of the AGREEMENT.

41.9 All deductibles or self-insured retentions that are applicable under insurance policies to be procured by the SUBCONTRACTOR or which the SUBCONTRACTOR is to require any SUBCONTRACTORS to procure under the AGREEMENT shall be for the account of and paid for by the SUBCONTRACTOR and/or SUBCONTRACTORS, including any deductibles or self-insured retentions applicable to coverage of claims made against the OWNER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES for which the SUBCONTRACTOR is responsible.

41.10 If any insurance policy is cancelled, or if there is a material change which may affect the OWNER’s interest, or if the SUBCONTRACTOR fails to effect or maintain any policy which it is required by the AGREEMENT to effect and maintain, the OWNER may at its sole discretion effect and maintain any such insurance or additional insurance as the OWNER shall consider necessary and recover the cost from the SUBCONTRACTOR.

41.11 The SUBCONTRACTOR shall either:

(a) require its SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS to obtain and maintain the same level of insurance as the SUBCONTRACTOR is obliged to take out under the AGREEMENT for performing any part of the WORKS, in proportion to the value of the WORKS that are to be performed by such SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS, save and except that the SUBCONTRACTOR shall not require such SUBCONTRACTORS to carry insurance that would duplicate the coverage of the insurance carried by the SUBCONTRACTOR; or

(b) ensure that such SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS are insured under the SUBCONTRACTOR’s relevant insurance policies.

41.12 The SUBCONTRACTOR’s compliance with the insurance requirements set out in this Article ‎41 shall not be construed as limiting the SUBCONTRACTOR’s liability to the OWNER and others as set out in Article ‎42. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall save, indemnify, defend, release and hold harmless the OWNER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES from and against all losses, liabilities, damages and costs (including legal costs) arising out of or in connection with any claim by any PERSON against the OWNER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES in respect of any shortfall in insurance cover it is obliged to obtain under the AGREEMENT or any failure to effect or maintain such insurances specified by the AGREEMENT or out of any act or omission, which invalidates the said insurances or in the event that liability for any loss or damage be denied by any underwriter, in all or in part, because of breach of said insurance requirements by the SUBCONTRACTOR, to the extent of the liabilities assumed by the SUBCONTRACTOR under the AGREEMENT.

41.13 The SUBCONTRACTOR shall:

(a) as soon as possible inform the OWNER in writing of any occurrence that may give rise to a claim under an insurance policy required by the AGREEMENT;

(b) keep the OWNER informed of subsequent developments concerning the claim; and

(c) ensure that the SUBCONTRACTORS and VENDORS similarly inform the SUBCONTRACTOR and the OWNER in respect of occurrences which may give rise to a claim by them.

41.14 Any insurance arranged in accordance with this Article ‎41, shall be obtained in accordance with Article 26 of Federal Law No. (6) of 2007, as far as possible. However, the OWNER may at its discretion accept insurance policies from other competent insurance companies provided that such insurance policies meet all other requirements mentioned in this Article ‎41.

41.15 Not used.

41.16 The OWNER shall effect the insurance specified in ‎ANNEXURE 7 A for the period specified in ‎ANNEXURE 7 A and shall provide the SUBCONTRACTOR with a copy of the relevant insurance policies. The insurance referred to in ‎ANNEXURE 7 A shall be subject to the exclusions, conditions and deductibles noted on the policies. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall:

(a) satisfy itself of the nature and extent of the OWNER’s insurance;

(b) if required by the OWNER, effect insurance to:

(i) insure any risks not insured by the OWNER’s insurance; and/or

(ii) cover any exclusions, conditions or deductibles in that insurance;

(c) where it bears the risk of the relevant loss, damage or liability or is required to indemnify the OWNER pursuant to Article ‎42, bear the cost of any deductibles in the OWNER’s insurance;

(d) compensate the OWNER for all losses, damages and costs arising out of or in connection with the OWNER’s inability to recover under any of the insurance policies effected by the OWNER under this Article ‎41 as a result of a vitiating act by the SUBCONTRACTOR. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless the OWNER from and against all losses, liabilities, damages and costs (including legal costs) arising out of or in connection with any claim by any PERSON in relation to the same; and

(e) ensure adherence to the OWNER’s insurance practices in connection with the transition from a ‘construction-phase’ insurance programme prior to the issuance of the PROVISIONAL ACCEPTANCE CERTIFICATE to an ‘operational phase’ insurance programme. To evidence the SUBCONTRACTOR’s compliance with this requirement, the SUBCONTRACTOR must provide the INSURANCE DECLARATION to the OWNER pursuant to Article ‎17.1(a)(vi) prior to the issuance of the PROVISIONAL ACCEPTANCE CERTIFICATE.

41.17 Without limitation to its obligations and responsibilities, if the FORM OF AGREEMENT states that the SUBCONTRACTOR is required to take out additional insurance above what is stated in Articles ‎41.1 to ‎41.15, the details of such additional insurance shall be set out in ‎ANNEXURE 7 and the SUBCONTRACTOR shall:

(a) for the period specified in ‎ANNEXURE 7, effect the insurance specified in ‎ANNEXURE 7:

(i) with insurers approved by the OWNER and with minimum long-term credit ratings not lower than A- rated by S&P and/or AM BEST;

(ii) on terms satisfactory to the OWNER (acting reasonably);

(iii) containing an additional insured clause for OWNER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES, where such clause is stated to be required in this Article ‎41; and

(iv) containing waivers by insurers of all rights, remedies or relief to which they might become entitled by way of subrogation against the OWNER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES;

(b) fifteen (15) DAYS prior to the relevant date specified in ‎ANNEXURE 7 (or if no date is specified, from the COMMENCEMENT DATE), and within seven (7) DAYS of any subsequent request as may be issued from time to time by the OWNER to do so, provide the OWNER with a copy of any required insurance policy and evidence satisfactory to the OWNER that the policy is current;

(c) inform the OWNER whenever it receives from the insurer a notice under or in connection with the policy, which in the case of a notice of cancellation shall be given to the OWNER no later than thirty (30) DAYS prior to the cancellation of the policy; and

(d) ensure that it:

(i) does not do anything which prejudices any insurance;

(ii) if necessary, rectifies anything which might prejudice any insurance;

(iii) reinstates an insurance policy if it lapses;

(iv) does not cancel, vary or allow an insurance policy to lapse without the prior written consent of the OWNER;

(v) immediately notifies the OWNER of any event which may result in an insurance policy lapsing or being cancelled;

(vi) immediately notifies the OWNER if the long-term credit rating of any insurer falls below the rating required by Article ‎41.17(a)(i) or such other rating requirements specified in ‎ANNEXURE 7;

(vii) gives full, true and particular information to the insurer of all matters and things, the non-disclosure of which might in any way prejudice or affect any such policy or the payment of all or any benefits under the insurance; and

(viii) require its SUBCONTRACTOR and VENDORS to:

(A) maintain insurance in accordance with this Article ‎41, save and except that the SUBCONTRACTOR shall not require such SUBCONTRACTORS to carry insurance that would duplicate the coverage of the insurance carried by the SUBCONTRACTOR; and

(B) otherwise comply with this Article ‎41.

41.18 Wherever insurance is effected under the AGREEMENT in the joint names of the PARTIES, or on terms containing provisions for indemnity to principals, the PARTY effecting such insurance shall ensure that the insurance policy provides that:

(a) insofar as the policy may cover more than one insured, all insuring agreements and endorsements (with the exception of limits of liability) shall operate in the same manner as if there were a separate policy of insurance covering each named insured;

(b) the insurer waives all rights, remedies or relief to which it might become entitled by way of subrogation against any of the persons covered as an insured, and that failure by any insured to observe and fulfil the terms of the policy shall not prejudice the insurance with respect to any other insured;

(c) any non-disclosure by one insured does not prejudice the right of any other insured to claim on the policy; and

(d) a notice to the insurer by one insured person shall be deemed to be notice by all insured persons.

41.19 Where the OWNER procures insurance hereunder for the benefit of both PARTIES, claims management shall be OWNER-driven and the SUBCONTRACTOR shall not, and shall take all steps necessary to ensure that any SUBSUBCONTRACTOR or VENDOR do not, report any claims to the insurer without coordinating with the OWNER. For the avoidance of doubt, the insurer’s claim investigation process shall not be considered as a hindrance to, or be a reason for delay in the execution of the WORKS.
