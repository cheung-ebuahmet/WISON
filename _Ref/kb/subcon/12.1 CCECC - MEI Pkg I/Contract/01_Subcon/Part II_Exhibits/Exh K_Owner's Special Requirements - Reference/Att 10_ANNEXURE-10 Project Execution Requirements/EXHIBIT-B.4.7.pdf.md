1. PURPOSE
The purpose of the EPC Contract Trending Procedure is to establish a
methodology for early identification, authorization and control of potential
changes from the AGREEMENT, through continuous monitoring of
developments. The desired outcome of such a procedure is communication
resulting in corrective action/resolution prior to problems; expediency in
addressing issues of cost and schedule; and successful settlement of changes
during the course of the AGREEMENT.
2. SCOPE
This procedure outlines requirements for the identification of potential changes,
preparation of the trend notices, responsibilities and administration of trends.
The scope of the procedure covers those changes to the AGREEMENT
requirements that could potentially impact the cost, schedule and / or have
detrimental impact on quality.
For the sake of clarity it shall be noted that those design Deviations and
Concession Requests that do not impact the cost, schedule and / or have
detrimental impact on quality and HSE are excluded from the scope of this
procedure and shall be dealt with in accordance with Project MOC Procedure
OE-PR-048 section 2.5.
Non-Conformance Reports (NCR) are not covered by this procedure. Only in
case CONTRACTOR’s remedial action is not acceptable shall the NCR be
considered for a CTN.
3. PROCEDURE
3.1 Identification of Trends
Only those potential changes that fall outside the AGREEMENT and could be
construed to impact cost, schedule or do not have detrimental impact on quality
and HSE should be considered for a trend notice.
3.2 Timing
After a potential change is identified, the Contract Trend Notice (CTN) should be
initiated promptly. Within ten (10) calendar days after the event which causes a
potential change, a CTN should be prepared and issued by the CONTRACTOR.

3.6 COMPANY Initiated Contract Trend Notice (CTN)
COMPANY shall have the right to request CONTRACTOR and CONTRACTOR
shall prepare and issue CTNs for COMPANY review and approval. Where
CONTRACTOR refuses or fails to issue a CTN as requested by COMPANY,
COMPANY shall have the right to issue the CTN itself.
3.7 CONTRACTOR Initiated Contract Trend Notice (CTN)
CTNs will be prepared and delivered to COMPANY within ten (10) calendar days
after the event which prompted a potential change. COMPANY will:
1. review the CTN
2. conclude that cost, quality and/or schedule deviation is acceptable or not.
3. deliver a written response to CONTRACTOR.
3.8 Contract Trend Notice Preparation Standards
CONTRACTOR shall supply all the pertinent information on the appropriate
sections of the CTN. Any CTN supplied by CONTRACTOR to COMPANY with
incomplete or incorrect information, will be returned to CONTRACTOR.
CONTRACTOR shall resubmit the CTN, with necessary corrections, forthwith.
Detailed supporting documentation will be provided with each CTN including
copies of referenced letters, memos, drawings, minutes, etc.
3.9 COMPANY Approved Trends
CONTRACTOR will update the status of the CTN register by incorporating
COMPANY’s response and other details.
3.10 Delivery of Contract Trend Notices
CTNs shall be delivered in accordance with the PROJECT procedures
concerning correspondence to COMPANY
4. AUTHORIZATION
CONTRACTOR shall proceed with the change upon receipt of approval of the
CTN from COMPANY in writing, either on the CTN form or through a separate
communication. However, in certain cases considering the expediency of
circumstances COMPANY may instruct in writing and CONTRACTOR shall
proceed with implementation of the change while the cost and schedule portion
of the trend are under review and/or finalization.

5. COST OF TREND PREPARATION
The full cost of preparing and processing of a CTN, whether ultimately approved
or rejected by COMPANY shall be borne by the CONTRACTOR and shall not be
reimbursed by COMPANY
6. COST EFFECTS
The value of a CTN will generally be expressed as a fixed lump sum amount in
accordance with the AGREEMENT. To support the requested lump sum price,
CONTRACTOR shall provide full back-up of cost details. The details should
cover such items as Engineering, Procurement, Equipment, Bulk Materials,
Freight, Construction, Field Supervision, etc. and should clearly show the man-
hours, quantities, prices, rates, etc. used in developing the lump sum price. All
prices, rates etc. used should be supported either by provisions of
AGREEMENT, copies of quotation or any other suitable means so that rates
used are justified to the satisfaction of COMPANY. Full back-up documentation
for quantities and prices should be provided. COMPANY may request additional
details from CONTRACTOR. CONTRACTOR will supply such requested
information in a timely manner so as not to delay decision from COMPANY.
7. SCHEDULE EFFECTS
The trend section applicable to schedule will be completed by CONTRACTOR.
CONTRACTOR shall endeavor to maintain the schedule as contained in the
AGREEMENT.
In the event of a potential schedule deviation, CONTRACTOR shall evaluate the
extent of change in any MILESTONE and/or Overall Completion Date.
COMPANY requires CONTRACTOR to provide all relevant schedule data,
related to the change, in order to demonstrate that the work cannot be executed
within the agreed time frame. CONTRACTOR shall supply COMPANY with a
CPM Network including the basis for original and revised logic and durations.
8. TREND MEETINGS
Regular Meetings, at mutually agreed intervals, will be held between COMPANY
and CONTRACTOR representatives to review the CTNs.
The updated CTN Register will be the basis for discussion during the trend
meeting.
The general purpose of these meetings will be to:
1. Verify that all trends have been identified
2. To review trends in process.
3. To review formalization of trends that have been approved since the last
meeting.

9. AMENDMENTS TO AGREEMENT
Approved CTNs will ultimately be incorporated into the AGREEMENT in the form
of an Amendment to the AGREEMENT.
10. FINAL SETTLEMENT
Each Amendment to the AGREEMENT, signed by both parties, shall be deemed
as final settlement of cost and schedule impact for those changes incorporated.
11. DOCUMENT UPDATE
CONTRACTOR shall update all relevant contractual documents and procedures
as a result of the approved trend. Within two (2) weeks of receiving approval
from COMPANY, progress reports, cost reports, plans and schedules, Trend
Logs, and other relevant documents will be updated.
