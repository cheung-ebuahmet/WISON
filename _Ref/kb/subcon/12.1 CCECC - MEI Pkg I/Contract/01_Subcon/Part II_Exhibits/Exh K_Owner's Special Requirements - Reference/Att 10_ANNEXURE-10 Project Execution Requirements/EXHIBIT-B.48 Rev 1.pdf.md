CONTENTS OF MECHANICAL CATALOGUES

•  The  Mechanical  Catalogues  are  a  bound  compilation  of  drawings  and  data  which  the  EPC
Contractor  shall  furnish  as  a  ready  reference  to  all  important  equipment  items.  They  are
needed for start-up, operation, maintenance, future modification, performance checking, etc.

•  The quantities of drawings, manufacturer’s literature or other data required for the catalogues
is  additional  to  that  required  for  the  normal  document  distribution.  The  required  number  of
copies of Mechanical Catalogues is as specified in Exhibit B.39.

•  The  completed  catalogue  will  comprise  of  a  number  of  volumes.  It  shall  be  available  to
Company  three  months  prior  to  start-up.    It  may  be  compiled  by  System,  by  plant  unit  or  by
equipment category as agreed between EPC Contractor and Company.
