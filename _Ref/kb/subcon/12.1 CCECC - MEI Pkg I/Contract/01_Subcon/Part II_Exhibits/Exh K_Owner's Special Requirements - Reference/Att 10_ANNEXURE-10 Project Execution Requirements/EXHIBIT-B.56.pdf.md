B.56

VIDEO DOCUMENTARY

A  video  documentary  shall  be  developed  to  chronicle  the  PROJECT.  The  final
video shall be 30 - 60 minutes in length with both English and Arabic versions of
commentary.  The  video  shall  be  on  a  DVD  with  format  suitable  for  U.A.E.    The
video shall cover four basic areas of the PROJECT, as detailed below:

CONTRACTOR shall submit within thirty (30) days from the EFFECTIVE DATE of
the AGREEMENT its proposed script and story book for this documentary.

B.56.1

Introduction

Introduction  to  Abu  Dhabi,  introduction  to  COMPANY,  introduction  to  the
PROJECT with views of EXISTING FACILITIES.

B.56.2

Home Office

Sequence  of  shots  of  Home  Office  activities  in  progress  in  CONTRACTOR’S
Offices.  Include  typical  progress  meetings,  model  reviews  (CAD  and  physical),
COMPANY  PROJECT  Management  Team  at  work  and  weekly  meetings,  etc.  at
two  stages  in  the  progress  of  the  work  -  early  at  10-20  %  and  late  at  70-80  %
completion.

B.56.3

Fabrication

Views  of  fabrication  of  major  equipment  (Granulators,  Remelters,  Reclaimer,
vessels, pipelines etc.). Worldwide procurement shall be featured.

B.56.4

Construction and Commissioning

Various stages of construction on SITE in Abu Dhabi, through to the inauguration
ceremony  (four  stages  at  least).  To  include  transfer  of  major  equipment  from
docks to job SITE and erection of major equipment onto foundations.
