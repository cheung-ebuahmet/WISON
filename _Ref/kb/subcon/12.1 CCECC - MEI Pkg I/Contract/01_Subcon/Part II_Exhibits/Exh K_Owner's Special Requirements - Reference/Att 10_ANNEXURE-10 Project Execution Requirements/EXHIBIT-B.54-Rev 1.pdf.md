B.54 TRAINING SERVICES TO COMPANY’S PERSONNEL
B.54.1 Training Program Outline
B.54.1.1 Introduction
The objective of the Training Services to be provided by EPC Contractor / Vendor
is to train Company’s operations, maintenance and technical services personnel to
understand the new process facilities, equipment and systems in order to be able
to operate and maintain them safely and efficiently. The training will enable
Company personnel to deal with the new process and utility facilities, equipment
and systems in normal operation and emergencies, identify faults and rectify them
(trouble shoot) efficiently, thereby ensuring minimum disruption to operation. The
training will enable them to acquire understanding of all aspects of the systems
and equipment used on the facilities. For general details on training, refer to
EXHIBIT A.17.
B.54.1.2 Classification of Training Services
The training requirements are broadly classified as follows:
Class Room Training
This will include structured and properly organized class-room training to
Company’s engineers, supervisors, operators and technicians at SITE, Company
Head Quarters in Abu Dhabi or other locations, as more particularly described in
Attachments 1 to 5 covering the schedule of training requirements identifying the
training description, the number of trainees, the number of sessions, the duration
per session, location, etc. The tables shall be used as a guide for the EPC
Contractor to develop a detailed program, which shall be submitted for Company’s
review and acceptance. Any change, both upwards and downwards, in the training
requirements included herein shall be subject to a Variation in accordance with the
unit rates stipulated in EXHIBIT A.7. For the avoidance of doubt, any change in the
number of trainees per session shall not lead to a Variation. Generally, the training
session will be conducted before the Commissioning of any system or facility
unless otherwise agreed between Companies.
On-job Training at SITE
This will include on-job training to Company’s operators and technicians during the
pre-commissioning and Commissioning of equipment and Systems. A team of six
to eight Company personnel, and occasionally more depending on the equipment
or System, may be associated with EPC Contractor’s pre-commissioning and
Commissioning teams for each equipment item and System. EPC Contractor shall
allow Company personnel to observe and involve in the Commissioning process
and provide hands-on training on various facets of the pre-commissioning and
Commissioning.

On-job Training at EPC CONTRACTOR’s Home Office
As part of the development of assigned Company personnel, and as a separate
requirement to the specific needs of the Project, Company requires the placement
of suitably qualified and experienced personnel at EPC Contractor’s home offices
on a secondment basis within the EPC Contractor’s organisation at different
stages of the Project execution. This program may include Company personnel
being assigned to different departments and engineering disciplines, however
Company would require that the pl acements receive training in all areas during the
Project development. The potential placements will be graduates with typically 2+
years’ experience and would be fully integrated into the EPC Contractor’s
organisation working on the Project.
EPC Contractor shall provide a full time training coordinator at senior engineer
level, office space, computers and the relevant Project software. A training
program shall be developed for each placement to include the proposed training
by EPC Contractor and their particular requirements. EPC Contractor shall fully
monitor the placements training program and carry out regular assessments with
each placement and provide these assessments to the Company.
Within one month of the effective date, EPC Contractor shall provide details of the
most suitable areas of the Project that would provide the most benefit to the
placements, provide details of the training programs that will be available within
their organization and a matrix showing proposed placement positions, durations
and numbers to Company for review and approval”. Mobilization of Company
trainees will occur shortly after approval of EPC Contractor plan. Placements are
to be within the engineering disciplines and thus EPC Contractor needs to show
proposed locations in his plan for Company review & approval. The EPC
Contractor shall implement continuous assessment and reporting program of
training objectives for Company personnel assigned to training and provide
detailed final report.
EPC Contractor’s scope includes training to the following Company personnel at
its home office:
• Twelve (12) short-term trainees for a duration of 3 months each
EPC Contractor’s LUMP SUM PRICE includes the cost of all training services,
including but not limited to the costs related to provision of office and office
facilities (including computers) to Company personnel, training instructors, training
course material, training venue, teaching aids, computers, projectors and other
visual aids. Company personnel shall make their own arrangements for travel to
and boarding expenses at the venue of training.
Should the number of trainees or the duration change from those mentioned
above, the Agreement Price shall be adjusted in accordance with the unit rates
stipulated in EXHIBIT A.7.

B.54.1.3 General Requirements
The general requirements governing the provision of training services are
stipulated in EXHIBIT A.17.
EPC CONTRACTOR’s LUMP SUM Price includes the cost of all training services
stipulated in the Agreement, including but not limited to the costs related to training
instructors, training course material, training venue, teaching aids, computers,
projectors and other visual aids. Company personnel shall make their own
arrangements for travel to and boarding expenses at the venue of training.
EPC CONTRACTOR shall provide suitably qualified Instructors (the CV of trainer /
Instructor will be subjected to Company review and approval) to provide training.
Where necessary in Company’s opinion, Training Instructors from Vendors,
suppliers, OEM and Sub-Contractors or Licensors shall be arranged. Training by
the commissioning / site engineer is not acceptable.
Clear and concise materials for training shall be made available at least four
weeks before the commencement of any training session. Each attendee will
receive a course instruction and reference manual (color copy on specific
requirements) in English Language, consisting of equipment descriptions and
operating and maintenance instructions. Due recognition shall be given in
preparation of the course content to the composition and level of the trainees.
Also, EPC Contractor shall provide a competency assessment framework aims to
assess and certify Company’s staff. EPC Contractor to:
− Provide a minimum hours of training per Operator. The minimum hours will be
identified during the finalization of training plan.
− Conduct assessment and certification to the Operators.
− Provide a list of DCS vendor training instructors and their credentials for
Company’s approval.
