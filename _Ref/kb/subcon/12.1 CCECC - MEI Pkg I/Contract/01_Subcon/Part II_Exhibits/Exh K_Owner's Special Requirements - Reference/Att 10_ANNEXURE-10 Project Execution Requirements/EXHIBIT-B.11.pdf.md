For Acoustic Induced Vibration (AIV) assessment, CONTRACTOR shall
suitably assess the pressure drop across all the pressure reducing devices
like Pressure Safety Valves, blow down valves, pressure control valves,

assessment shall be performed by CONTRACTOR to mitigate any AIV
related concerns. Some of the options that may be considered while
designing such pressure reducing devices are as follows:
• Operating philosophy of number of pressure reducing devices shall have
adequate number of operating and stand-by sources, to have an optimal
mass flow rate in the system.

respective headers may be used. FEA analysis shall be performed to
establish and verify the design solution (i.e. sizing requirement

improper design / manufacturing / installation of FRP systems result in
substantial cost and resource burden for the COMPANY, besides having impact
on production and HSE aspects. To enhance the performance of FRP piping
systems following is recommended to be considered in addition to the
requirements of AGES-SP-09-014, consider the revised standard drawing
numbers 5312-00-A1-5038 and 5039 for FW hydrant / monitor installation. In
the revised drawings the FRP-CS flanged interface is located in the above

responsibility shall be given to FRP Vendor / Manufacturer, so that he is
