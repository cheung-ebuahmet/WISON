B.37 VERIFICATION OF LESSONS LEARNED
B.37.1 INTRODUCTION
The attached compilations provide a list of lessons learned from the execution of
COMPANY’s previous projects, as well as a list of lessons learned generated based
on early design documents. Those which are appropriate based on early design
documents to be incorporated, and this is reflected in the documentation
contained elsewhere within the EPC Package.
The EPC Contractor is required to develop a procedure for continuing the
implementation and verification of the lessons learned during the EPC phase, and
to ensure that all lessons learned have been satisfactorily closed out, whenever
applicable,
COMPANY shall, from time to time, issue Recommendations and Corrective Actions
e.g. arising from accident investigations within ADNOC companies. On receipt,
CONTRACTOR shall demonstrate how it intends to implement the
Recommendations and Corrective Actions to prevent similar incidents associated
with the PROJECT activities.
CONTRACTOR shall provide the lessons learnt to COMPANY upon completion of
each phase of the subject PROJECT i.e. Engineering, Procurement, Construction and
Commissioning Phases. COMPANY shall review in consultation with CONTRACTOR
applicable Lessons Learnt for each phase and agreed lessons learnt shall form part
of the Close Out Report to be submitted by CONTRACTOR for issuance of
Provisional Acceptance Certificate.
Lessons Learnt Workshop sessions shall be arranged with Company to share the
ideas and thoughts to prepare the LL reports.
B.37.2 VERIFICATION
Verification of the resolution and its implementation of the “Lessons Learned” will
be part of Contracts and Quality audits.
Discussions on the status of the Lessons Learned implementation shall be included
as one of the agenda of the Monthly Management Meeting.
