B 57 REQUIREMENTS FOR PIPING AND EQUIPMENT DECOMMISSIONING, DEMOLITION
AND   ABANDONEMENT

B 57.1        INTRODUCTION

The Piping and equipment demolition  requirements defined within this EXHIBIT shall
be implemented by EPC Contractor alongside and integral with all of EPC Contractor’s
other  discipline  activities,
its  SUB-Contractors,  and  Company’s  other  EPC
CONTRACTORs.

COMPANY  recognizes  that  all  Demolition  must  be  followed  with  the  proactive
participation of suitably qualified and experienced Operations and Maintenance (O&M)
and Construction personnel to meet Company’s safety and integrity objectives. EPC
Contractor is responsible for ensuring the Facilities provided meet optimum standards
of  safety,  integrity,  operability,  reliability  and  maintainability  and  to  provide  all  the
documents accordingly.

All aspects of EPC Contractor support services activities shall consider the protection
of O&M personnel during normal operations and during decommissioning, demolition,
shutdown and emergency situations.

All  document  deliverables  required  shall  be  subject  to  review  cycles  involving
Company’s PMT Team and incorporation of all comments by EPC Contractor into the
Decommissioning  and  Demolition  Philosophy  of  Piping  and  Equipment.  EPC
Contractor’s decommissioning and demolition philosophy shall be based on the FEED
document no. RP-5312-00-PR-030 Decommissioning and Demolition Philosophy.

B 57.2       OBJECTIVE

The objectives are to:

Complete  Decommissioning,  demolition    activity  maintaining  safety  and  Integrity  of
existing assets / facilities
Support the Demolition, restoration and handover to  operations meeting Company’s

HSE requirements.

Update relevant plant information and documents

B 57.3       SCOPE

This document provides Company’s requirements that shall be implemented by EPC
Contractor  as  part  of  the  Agreement.  These  requirements  shall  be  progressed
alongside,  and  be  an  integral  part  of,  the  Decommissioning,  demolition  &  support
services provided by the EPC Contractor. All requirements related to Decommissiong,
and  demolition    are  covered  under  FEED  document  no.  RP-5312-00-PR-030
Decommissioning and Demolition Philosophy.

report. This is replaced with required HSE risk studies, including Environment Impact
Assessment (EIA), as required for decommissioning, demolition and site restoration.

B 57.5 Piping and equipment Decommissioning

Refer OE procedure OE-PR-037

Preparation of P&IDs for Decommissioning

Before  decommissioning,  EPC  Contractor  shall  prepare  P&ID  in  consultation  with
Company for spading and blinding requirements for all P&IDs. The P&IDs shall clearly
demarcate  the  section  of  the  piping  and  equipment  to  be  physically  isolated  and
decommissioned from the plant.

Piping and equipment Process Isolation and depressurization

     Company shall ensure isolation before handover to EPC Contractor for cleaning

Piping and equipment Cleaning and Purging

Ensure  that  the  existing  liquid  or  gas  piping  and  equipment  is  free  from  sulphur
deposits  and  hydrocarbons  by  cleaning  it  using  appropriate  methods  approved  by
Company.

Make necessary arrangements for collection and disposal of waste. Treat all solid and
liquid waste or give it to an approved third party for disposal as per HSE-ST-001.

B 57.6 Demolition of Piping and equipment and Associated Facilities

After  successful  decommissioning,  demolition  of  the  piping  and  equipment  and
associated  facilities.    Following  steps  to  be  followed  for  demolition/abandonment:
Refer OE procedure OE-PR-037

Demolition Plan Preparation

EPC  CONTRACTOR  shall  finalize  the  demolition  plan  in  consultation  with  the
Operations and Maintenance team from the piping and equipment’s, as appropriate.
The  demolition  plan  shall  include  the  details  of  mechanical  equipment,  electrical
equipment, piping, instrumentation,  and civil structures to be demolished. It shall cover
updating the documents and drawings from all disciplines including detailed plans for
the removal of tags and alarms from the system.

EPC CONTRACTOR shall prepare the Demolition Philosophy, Demolition P&IDs and
the Demolition Method Statement, covering all the above requirements, and present
them to the TE, Operations and Maintenance teams for validation.

Piping and equipment Purging with Nitrogen

The piping and equipment to be demolished shall be purged with Nitrogen .

Electrical Demolition

Electrical demolition includes the demolition and retrieval of all related electrical cables,
lighting,  earthing  cable  network,  solar  power  units,  power  supply  units  and  all

The scope of electrical demolition shall also include the demolition of related electrical
instruments and their associated cables, existing junction boxes, etc., and removal of

The demolition of electrical equipment includes the following steps:

  EPC CONTRACTOR shall take the necessary clearances from the Abu Dhabi
Distribution Company, if required, before undertaking any electrical demolition.
  EPC CONTRACTOR shall ensure that the electrical equipment are isolated and

de-energized before demolition.

  EPC CONTRACTOR shall carry out physical separation of electrical cables from
the  source  with  appropriate  measures  (earthing  of  static  electricity  from  the
equipment). Electrical demolition shall be carried out only after completing these
steps.

  EPC  CONTRACTOR  shall  mark-up  all  related  electrical  drawings  for  removal

and updating.

Instrumentation and Control System Demolition

The scope of instrumentation and control system demolition includes the demolition of
related  instruments  and  their  associated  cables,  existing  junction  boxes,  etc.,  and
removal of their configuration and tag database from the SMC/SCADA/DCS systems
(as applicable).

The demolition of the instrumentation and control system includes the following steps:

  EPC  CONTRACTOR  shall  ensure  that  all  alarm  cables,  which  conform  to  no
alarms at SCADA/DCS, are physically separated from the respective terminals.
  The EPC Contractor shall prepare the detailed method statement and demolition
drawings  as  per  the  site  conditions  and  get  them  endorsed  by  Company
Operations and TE teams.

  EPC  CONTRACTOR  shall  take  all  necessary  approvals  and  clearances  from

Etisalat before demolishing the RTU.

  EPC  CONTRACTOR  shall  Mark-up  all  related  instrumentation  drawings  and

documents for removal and updating.

Civil and Structural Demolition

The demolition of civil and structural items include the following steps:

  Demolish all civil and structural items associated with the existing

piping/equipment, i.e., foundations, existing berms, safety barriers,
identification slabs, piping and equipment markers, drain pits, hardstand pipe
supports and sleepers, roads and fencing (where applicable), etc., and dispose
them of to the approved disposal area as per HSE-ST-001

  After  demolition,  backfill,  grade  and  reinstate  the  entire  area  to  the  existing

levels.

  Dispose  of  the piping  and  equipment  and  associated  facilities  recovered  from

site as per HSE-ST-001.
