B.39.1 SCOPE
This EXHIBIT B.39 outlines COMPANY’s minimum requirements for the
handling of documentation on the PROJECT and the action to be taken by the
CONTRACTOR.
The attached Guideline Document Classification Table provides guidelines to
be adopted for classification of documents on the PROJECT. CONTRACTOR
shall, within three (3) weeks of the EFFECTIVE DATE, submit for COMPANY’s
review and approval a detailed Document Classification Table which shall be
followed on the PROJECT.
B.39.2 DOCUMENT CLASS
All drawings, documents and manuals to be submitted by CONTRACTOR will
be classified in one of the following three categories for the purpose of review,
comment and approval.
Class 1 All documentation in Class 1 shall require formal COMPANY
approval.
Class 1 documents shall be further categorized as follows:
Cat-1:
Documents requiring Specialist / Independent Reviews
Cat-2:
Documents not requiring Specialist / Independent Reviews
CONTRACTOR shall categorise ALL Class 1 documents as per
above and submit for COMPANY approval accordingly
Class 2 Documentation in Class 2 shall be issued to COMPANY for
comment, if comments are not received within stipulated period.
CONTRACTOR shall inform COMPANY in writing of its intention
to proceed with work.
Class 3 Documentation in Class 3 shall be issued to COMPANY for
information only. However, comments made on Class 3
documents shall also be considered when received in a timely
manner or advised that they are being developed.

B.39.3 REVIEW CODES
COMPANY’s comments on the submitted documents shall carry one of the
following codes:
Code 1 Rejected Revise and resubmit, Work shall not proceed.
Code 2 Comments as noted Work may proceed subject to compliance
with and incorporation of comments.
Code 3 No Comments Work may proceed.
Code 4 Information only Accepted for information only.
B.39.4 DOCUMENT APPROVAL PROCESS
All documents prepared by CONTRACTOR shall, depending on their class,
pass through the following stages:
IFR: Issued for Internal Review
ICR: Issued for Client Review
IFD01 Issued for HAZOP Review
IFD02 Issued for SIL Review (If required)
IFA: Issued for Approval
IFI: Issued for Information
IFE: Issued for Enquiry
IFP: Issued for Purchase
IFC: Issued for Construction
Eventually all technical documents have to reach the IFC / IFE / IFP status.
The following guideline procedure shall be followed for review and approval of
documents on the PROJECT:
Class-1 Document:
• Class and Category of Document to be indicated on the document.
• First Issue to Client to be ICR.
• In case of Code-1 comment on ICR, reissue as ICR until Code-2/Code-3 is
received.
• In case of Code-2/Code-3 comment on ICR, issue as IFA after incorporating
comments (if Code-2). Specialist / Independent Review comments on
Category-1 Documents should have been incorporated prior to submitting
IFA.
• Following Code-3 comment on IFA, document to be issued IFC ( or
IFD/IFP/IFE as applicable).
