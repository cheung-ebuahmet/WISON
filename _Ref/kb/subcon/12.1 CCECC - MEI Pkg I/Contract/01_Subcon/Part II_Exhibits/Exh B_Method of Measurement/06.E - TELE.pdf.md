E1 CCTV System Equipment

1.1 Definition

This work item is defined as the installation works of CCTV system equipment in accordance w
ith the drawings, technical specifications and manufacturer’s instruction manual at the Site, incl
uding unloading of the owner supplied materials at the owner designated locations, site transpor
tation, hauling, rigging, fitting, setting, assembling, bolting, welding if necessary, alignment, ins
pection, calibration, Functional Testing, pre-commissioning and assistance for commissioning/s
tart-up and operational test.

This work item shall also include, but not limited, the following;

 Installation of parts/components/supports shipped separately for transportation
 Installation of mounting accessories, boxes and bracket, etc.
 Installation and connection of vendor cable/wire, including supply of terminal lug
 Furnishing and installation of anchor bolt/nut/washer
 Test and inspection as per the project specification and inspection procedure

Further details of this work item are listed below. But all other associated activities required unt
il issuance of Final Acceptance Certificate shall also be included in the scope of this work item.

 Receiving, storing and maintaining the owner supplied materials
 Material transportation from/to the owner’s warehouse and laydown areas, and installation

locations

 Preparation of work plans and procedures and submission for the owner’s approval prior to
starting the work (work procedure, construction method statement, methodology,
inspection/test plan and procedure, etc.)

 Removal of existing obstacles to installation work and restoration work upon completion
thereof. Reinforcement or protection work for existing facilities, as required by the owner

 Preassembling work prior to the commencement of the installation work, as necessary
 Removal of reinforcing or protection materials used for the transportation or installation of

equipment, as necessary

 The following works related to the installation of the work item on concrete foundation :

 Checking anchor bolts and foundation with respect to location, size, level, etc.
 Chipping concrete foundation
 Setting mortar pads and liner plates
 Adjusting anchor bolts
 Setting and grouting box-out anchor bolts
 Mortar grouting and curing

 Welding washers and/or liner plates, as required by technical specifications and/or drawings
 Opening and re-closing manholes and/or handholes for cleaning and inspection
 Rust prevention during the construction work and timely removal of such rust and

prevention materials, if any

 Cleaning equipment inside/outside as required
 Application of necessary anti-seizing solvent/compound/paste for bolts and nuts
 Application of touch-up paint
 Preparation and submission of inspection/test record as per inspection/test plan

1.2.1 General

The contractor Prices shall not be adjusted for the differences in installation method, size, el
ectrical rating, weight, depth of burial, type of insulation, type of equipment and elevation/lo
cation of working places, etc. All of works descripted in above Definition works shall not be
measured separately but deemed to be included in unit price.

1.2.1 Measurement method and unit of quantities

The quantities shall be measured by number of complete set of CCTV system equipment fro
m the construction drawings.

Any boxes, mounting accessories and brackets will not be separately measured.

Furnishing, installation, and dismantling of Scaffolding should not be measured separately, b
ut deemed measured and included in the unit price of this work.

No measurement shall be made for any rework, furnishing and installation of field fabricated
steel support material, excavation, concrete work and backfilling .
