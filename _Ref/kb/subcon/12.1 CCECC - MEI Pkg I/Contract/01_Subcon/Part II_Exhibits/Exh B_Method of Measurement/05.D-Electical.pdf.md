1.2 Measurement method and unit of quantities

1.2.1 General

The Sub-contractor Prices shall not be adjusted for the differences in installation method, size,
electrical rating, weight, type of equipment and elevation/location of working places, etc. All of
works descripted in above Definition works shall not be measured separately but deemed to be
included in unit price.

1.2.2 Measurement method and unit of quantities

The quantities shall be measured by number of complete set of equipment, where specified by
SET, and by length of net installed cable excluding any waste or allowance, where specified by
Linear Meter (LM) in accordance with IFC drawings.

Any accessories, components and parts will not be separately measured.

Furnishing, installation, and dismantling of Scaffolding should not be measured separately, but
deemed measured and included in the unit price of this work.

No measurement shall be made for any rework.

The quantities measured above shall be broken down into the following activities with the
