Appendix - Key Personnel & Liquidated Damages

1. General
   1. The Key Personnel of the SUBCONTRACTOR are the employees specified in this Appendix. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall nominee the candidates with resume incorporated in its proposal.
   2. The Key Personnel shall be as indicated in this Appendix and any other personnel which directly reports to the Contractor’s Representative.
2. KEY PERSONNEL MOBILISATION

The SUBCONTRACTOR shall mobilize the KEY PERSONNEL to project indicated in SECTION 4 of this Appendix **within 30 days** after the LOA.

The SUBCONTRACTOR shall ensure that SUBCONTRACTOR’s Key Personnel engaged in the performance of the Contract is involved in the performance of the Project exclusively for the purpose of the Project.

**Should SUBCONTRACTOR fail to mobilize Key Personnel to project within 30 days after LOA，the CONTRACTOR is entitled to claim KEY PERSONNEL LIQUIDATED DAMAGES as set out in the table of this Appendix**

1. REPLACEMENT OF KEY PERSONNEL
   1. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall notify the CONTRACTOR:
      1. subject to Sub-Clause 3.1.2, within a reasonable period but in any case not later than twenty (20) Business Days in advance of any proposed replacement of any member of Key Personnel; and
      2. where a member of Key Personnel dies, becomes seriously ill, resigns from the employment of the SUBCONTRACTOR, or needs to leave for any other serious matter - within a reasonable period, but in any case not later than ten (10) Business Days after the occurrence of such situation.

The notice under article 3.1 shall indicate the reasons for the replacement of the relevant member of Key Personnel.

* 1. Together with the notice under article 3.1.1 and within ten [10] Business Days after the notice under article 3.1.2, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit for the CONTRACTOR’s approval the CV of the substitute nominee proposed by the SUBCONTRACTOR for replacement of the relevant member of Key Personnel.
  2. The CONTRACTOR shall, within ten (10) Business Days following receipt of the CV under article 3.2 either approve the nominee proposed by the SUBCONTRACTOR or provide a reasoned and substantiated refusal to approve the nominee. If the CONTRACTOR does not reply within this period, the nominee shall be deemed approved.
  3. In case of CONTRACTOR’s reasoned and substantiated refusal to grant approval, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall, within ten (10) Business Days following such refusal, propose a new nominee and this article 3.3 will re-apply.
  4. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall ensure the replacement of Key Personnel in such a way that within the first ten (10) Business days of the nominee the nominee is accompanied by the replaced employee, who shall handover all the affairs regarding the Project to the nominee, except in cases of article 3.1.2.
  5. If the SUBCONTRACTOR fails to obtain the CONTRACTOR’s consent that should not be unreasonably withheld for the replacement of a member of Key Personnel in the manner set out in articles 3.1 to 3.3 above, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall be liable to pay the CONTRACTOR liquidated damages for each instance of such failure in the amounts stipulated in Appendix.
  6. Upon CONTRACTOR reasonable and substantiated request SUBCONTRACTOR shall replace a member of Key Personnel within thirty (30) days after receipt of such request following the procedure stipulated in this Appendix.
  7. Together with the notice under article 3.1.1 and within ten [10] Business Days after the notice under article 3.1.2, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall submit for the CONTRACTOR’s approval the CV of the substitute nominee proposed by the SUBCONTRACTOR for replacement of the relevant member of Key Personnel.
  8. The CONTRACTOR shall, within ten (10) Business Days following receipt of the CV under article 3.2 either approve the nominee proposed by the SUBCONTRACTOR or provide a reasoned and substantiated refusal to approve the nominee. If the CONTRACTOR does not reply within this period, the nominee shall be deemed approved.

In case of CONTRACTOR’s reasoned and substantiated refusal to grant approval, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall, within ten (10) Business Days following such refusal, propose a new nominee and this article 3.3 will re-apply.

* 1. The SUBCONTRACTOR shall ensure the replacement of Key Personnel in such a way that within the first ten (10) Business days of the nominee the nominee is accompanied by the replaced employee, who shall handover all the affairs regarding the Project to the nominee, except in cases of article 3.1.2.
  2. If the SUBCONTRACTOR fails to obtain the CONTRACTOR’s consent that should not be unreasonably withheld for the replacement of a member of Key Personnel in the manner set out in articles 3.1 to 3.3 above, the SUBCONTRACTOR shall be liable to pay the CONTRACTOR liquidated damages for each instance of such failure in the amounts stipulated in Appendix .
  3. Upon CONTRACTOR reasonable and substantiated request SUBCONTRACTOR shall replace a member of Key Personnel within thirty (30) days after receipt of such request following the procedure stipulated in this Appendix.
