1. INTRODUCTION
Health, Safety and Environment Impact Assessment (HSEIA) is a systematic process of identifying
hazards/impacts and adequate control measures to ensure that risks are managed to As Low As
Reasonably Practicable (ALARP) level to safeguard people, protect environment, prevent property
damage and maintain reputation for all ADNOC projects, facilities and operations. The HSEIA Reports &
HSE Studies shall demonstrate in relation to the project, facility, site or activities:
That all HSE hazards/aspects have been systematically identified, inclusive of relevant risk/impact
classification (e.g. High, High Medium, Medium and Low) in accordance with principles that are
compatible with those provided in relevant ADNOC HSE Standards.
(a) That all potential or actual (for an existing facility/operations) Significant (adverse) Environmental
Impacts have been identified, suitably analyzed and assessed for significance (exceeding
environmental limits). It shall be demonstrated that relevant control, mitigation and recovery
measures are incorporated into the design (for projects), or are being implemented (for existing
facilities or operations), or that a suitable plan exists for implementation in accordance with ADNOC
Environment Impact Assessment Standard [Ref. 3].
(b) That all applicable Social Impacts with respect to surrounding public facilities and communities
have been identified, suitably analyzed and addressed adequately. It shall be demonstrated that
appropriate actions for community engagement and protection of social environment have been
implemented or that a suitable plan exists for implementation in accordance with ADNOC
Environment Impact Assessment Standard [Ref. 3].
(c) That all Major Accident Hazards have been identified and suitable control, mitigation and recovery
measures are proposed, engineered and are implemented. It shall be demonstrated that
operations can be achieved within the ADNOC quantitative criteria and ALARP levels shall be
demonstrated in accordance with principles in ADNOC COMAH Standard [Ref. 12].
(d) That all High & High Medium Occupational Health Risks have been systematically identified and
suitable actions to mitigate these risks and to protect employees from these risks have been, or
will be, taken in accordance with ADNOC Occupational Health Risk Management Standard [Ref.
9].
(e) That there is an implementation plan that shows how the control, mitigation and recovery measures
for Significant Environmental Impacts, Major Accident Hazards and High, High-Medium
Occupational Health Risks will be implemented and managed throughout the facility lifecycle.
(f) That Medium and Low Risk Hazards - will be managed and controlled via the Group Company or
project/site/operations specific HSE Management System and associated HSE Systems &
Procedures.
(g) That Emergency Response Plans (on-site and off-site where necessary) in relation to Major
Accident Hazards have or will be prepared or updated based on credible emergency scenarios,
with the necessary stakeholder consultation.

2. PURPOSE
This document provides the requirements to develop/conduct a HSE Impact Assessment (HSEIA) study
for various projects, facilities, sites and activities to ensure safe and reliable operations across ADNOC
Group. It outlines the requirements of HSEIA study components such as Environmental Impact
Assessment (EIA) including social impacts as applicable, Control of Major Accident Hazards (COMAH)
Report and Occupational Health Risk Assessment (OHRA) and specifies when these HSEIA study
components are to be prepared by the ADNOC Group and to what level of detail.
It also outlines the roles and responsibilities of the various parties within ADNOC Group who have
responsibilities for HSEIA matters, the common framework and control procedures through which
preparation and approval are managed within the ADNOC Group. It also highlights the various interfaces
between ADNOC, Group Companies & external governmental agencies such as Environment Agency Abu
Dhabi (EAD), Fujairah Oil Industry Zone (FOIZ) Codes of Practice, Dubai Environmental Agency, etc.
This Standard also specifies the assurance processes for the ADNOC Group to ensure effective
implementation of the control measures identified through HSEIA process.
3. SCOPE
This standard stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to the ADNOC Group and its Contractors.
The HSEIA process is applicable across all ADNOC projects and facilities/sites that entail hazardous
operations and/or have potential for adverse HSE impacts due to high HSE risks.
ADNOC Group and its Contractors shall ensure that all requirements listed herein are fully understood,
implemented, complied with and monitored at all times.

QRA "Quantitative Risk Assessment; It is a formal and systematic approach of
estimating the likelihood and consequences of hazardous events, and
expressing the results quantitatively as risk to people, the environment, asset
or reputation.
A structured approach to assessing the potential for incidents and
expressing this potential numerically. In QRA statistical values are derived
for potential loss of life and damage to resources and environment. Note:

losses.

based on experience, statistical failure and incident rates representing an

preventable.

prevent the potential incidents from happening. QRA is not to be used to

7.2. HSEIA PROCESS
7.2.1. LIFECYCLE HSEIA REPORT AND HSEIA DOSSIER
(a) HSEIA is a “Living Document”, which is developed and updated during the full lifecycle of a project
as detailed in Appendix 2. The process starts at early design stage (FEED) and is updated
throughout the lifecycle of the project through operations. The requirements of HSEIA and HSE
studies are initially established via HSEIA screening process at concept stage and later during
each HSEIA update.
(b) HSE risks related to construction and demolition phases are managed through ADNOC HSEMS,
GC specific procedures, project construction HSE plan and other associated HSE risk studies as
identified necessary.
(c) HSEIA Report and associated HSE studies constitute the HSEIA dossier, which shall also be
updated during project lifecycle. Overall structure of the HSEIA and HSEIA dossier is available in
Appendix 3.
(d) The requirement of HSEIA screening study/review is described in Section 7.2.2 and minimum
content for HSEIA Reports during project lifecycle are described in Sections 7.2.3 through 7.2.6
below.
7.2.2. HSEIA SCREENING STUDY/REVIEW
(a) At the Concept stage of all projects, irrespective of their cost or value, a HSEIA screening study
shall be carried out to determine the content of the HSEIA Report and/or relevant HSE studies to
be carried out as part of the HSEIA. HSEIA screening study shall also be carried out for activities
requiring Combined Phase HSEIA.
(b) The HSEIA screening shall be based on a thorough review of all the relevant information relating
to the project, the anticipated HSE risks and the sensitivity of the surrounding environment. These
shall be identified and documented in HAZID, ENVID & Qualitative OHRA (OHID).
(c) Refer to Appendix 1 for the HSEIA Screening Report format which includes the Screening Checklist.
Group Company may amend the checklist to suit to the project scope in consultation with CHSE
as required. The final HSEIA Screening study shall be approved by GC CHSE. Screening study
shall be further updated and approved beginning of the FEED stage as necessary.
(d) This HSEIA Screening Report shall include following:
(i) Coarse HAZID, ENVID, Qualitative OHRA Review; and
(ii) HSEIA Screening including HSE Studies to be carried out in project life cycle.
(e) The conclusions of the HSEIA Screening Study shall define the scope of COMAH, EIA and OHRA
including all relevant HSE studies. HSEIA screening shall also highlight if any Non-ADNOC
Regulatory Approval which may be required during various project lifecycle such as EAD approval,
Civil Defence, etc.
(f) When conceptual phase is near completion, PHSER 1 shall be carried out to ensure that the
recommendations from all HSE studies carried out in concept are addressed.

8. COMPLIANCE ASSURANCE
(a) As part of the assurance process, ADNOC Group HSE Function, at their discretion, will carry out
assurance audits (refer Appendix 2) during project lifecycle to verify compliance with the HSEIA
process, correctness of the HSEIA dossier updating, and technical verification of the documents.
(b) If any discrepancies are observed, improvement notices with recommendations will be issued by
ADNOC assurance audit team to GC, following which GC shall submit the action plan within 45
days.
(c) ADNOC assurance audit team may recommend withdrawal of the regulatory and technical
approval, depending upon risk levels and failure to demonstrate ALARP. In such event, GC shall
demonstrate how risk levels will be managed to ALARP by implementing immediate mitigation
measures.
(d) If the action plan and demonstration of the ALARP is found to be inadequate, ADNOC may
recommend to put the project on hold or to suspend the operation of the facility/equipment until
adequate risk mitigation measures are implemented to demonstrate ALARP.
