1. INTRODUCTION
The Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) HSE Occupational Health Framework provides an
overview of occupational health management system by which ADNOC Group directs and control its
functions and relate to its stakeholders in order to manage its business, achieve its missions and
objectives and meet the necessary standards of accountability, integrity and propriety. It is a key aspect
in improving efficiency and accountability as well as in enhancing openness and transparency. A
significant aspect of the ADNOC’s occupational health reform is the strengthening of governance and
accountability arrangements for ensuring protection of employees from workplace health hazards across
ADNOC Group and Contractor.
The suite of documents enlist the minimum standards of occupational health for implementation provides
an overview of the of occupational health documents.
2. PURPOSE
The purpose of this Standard is to provide the framework to address the following aspects of occupational
health:
(a) the protection and promotion of the health of workers (employees) by preventing and controlling
occupational diseases and injuries, by eliminating occupational factors and conditions hazardous
to health and safety at work;
(b) the development and promotion of healthy and safe work, work environments and work
organizations;
(c) the enhancement of the physical, mental and social wellbeing of workers and support for the
development and maintenance of their working capacity, as well as professional and social
development at work; and
(d) enabling workers (employees) to conduct socially and economically productive lives and to
contribute positively to sustainable development.
3. SCOPE
This document stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to ADNOC Group [Directorates &
Functions (in ADNOC Head Quarter), ADNOC Group Companies and Affiliates] and its Contractors.
ADNOC Group and Contractors shall ensure that all expectations listed herein are fully understood,
implemented and thoroughly monitored.
