1. INTRODUCTION
Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC), as part of its Health, Safety and Environment (HSE) policy,
commits to ensure:
(i) Health and safety of all persons, to minimize damage to its assets and to safeguard the
natural environment in accordance with Local, Federal and International Laws.
(ii) All new projects or activities take full account of HSE requirements.
(iii) It is also a requirement of ADNOC Integrated HSE Management System Manual [Ref. 2] to
ensure that all HSE issues related to project activities have been identified for each stage of
a project and corrective actions implemented.
In order to meet these requirements, ADNOC intends to ensure that Project Health, Safety and
Environmental Reviews (PHSERs) are conducted at each stage of the project lifecycle.
PHSER involves reviewing the HSE aspects of a developing project from conception to post-
commissioning including decommissioning of the facilities in order to determine any shortcomings or non-
compliances.
2. PURPOSE
The purpose of this standard is to define the requirements for conducting PHSERs within ADNOC Group.
This standard establishes the consistent practices for planning, conducting and documenting PHSER
studies.
The PHSER process consists of up to seven (07) formal reviews scheduled for each project stage. These
reviews are conducted by a team of specialists, whose objectives are to verify that the project has
identified and fully addressed all the HSE and process safety aspects that are relevant to the project
stage.
This standard applies to all major projects in ADNOC Group. Requirements for carrying out PHSER at
various stages of a project are in accordance with the ADNOC HSE Impact Assessment (HSEIA)
Standard, HSE-RM-ST02 [Ref. 1].
3. SCOPE
This document stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to the ADNOC Group and its
Contractors.
ADNOC Group and its Contractors shall ensure that all requirements listed herein are fully understood,
implemented, complied with and monitored at all times.

technology (for e.g. additional identical process train, etc.).

environmental impacts for project feasibility. Suitable preliminary hazard analysis and environmental
screening techniques shall be used. Qualitative risk assessments may be required to assess compliance
with ADNOC’s Risk Tolerability Criteria.

7.3. PHSER 2 – FEED STAGE

risk;
(viii) To confirm that appropriate HSE studies have been carried out for project including those
listed in ADNOC HSE Impact Assessment (HSEIA) Standard, HSE-RM-ST02 [Ref. 1] have
been completed, their recommendations are being tracked, and actions potentially affecting
the design have been completed and are incorporated into the project design;

(ix) To verify that an appropriate programme of safety reviews throughout construction period

7.5.2. PHSER 4 – KEY REQUIREMENTS
PHSER 4 shall be conducted at 50% of the construction stage, typically soon after commencement of
significant construction at site. For projects in which the majority of construction takes place during a
turnaround, it may be practical to combine some aspects of the construction stage PHSER with the
commissioning stage PHSER.
The review shall also provide a detailed HSE assessment of the project construction to ensure the new
facilities meet ADNOC and regulatory HSE requirements.
Site visit shall be mandatory for construction stage PHSER and the team shall verify the contractor HSE
implementation in the site physically and collect evidence through interviews with the personnel. Site visit
shall be performed for the following typical locations/activities:
(i) Civil demolition, excavation and foundations;
(ii) Fabrication workshops;
(iii) Sites with construction of major structures, buildings, pipe racks, process vessels, columns,
tanks, fire hydrant ring main, fire and safety systems/ equipment;
(iv) Chemical storage and handling areas;
(v) Laboratory;
(vi) Radioactive sources storage and usage areas;
(vii) Medical facilities, clinics;
(viii) Escape and evacuation routes, muster points;
(ix) Accidental Oil Containment (AOC) drains, closed drains, flow channels;
(x) Heavy lifts and working at heights; and
(xi) Accommodation camp, catering areas.
For offshore projects, construction and installation can be separate and sequenced activities involving
different workforces, sites and HSE risks.

PHSER 5 shall be conducted at 90% of the construction stage before commissioning. It shall cover the
final aspects of construction stage and ensure that all agreed HSE related issues have been fully
addressed before commissioning.
For offshore projects, it may be appropriate to conduct the PHSER 5 in two stages:
(i) First stage before the sail of the rig.
(ii) Second stage prior to start-up.
Site visit shall be required to acquire the updated information regarding implementation of HSE plan.
Appendix 5 provides the key activities to ensure that above objectives are met.
7.7. PHSER 6 – OPERATION STAGE
7.7.1. PHSER 6 – OBJECTIVES
The objective of PHSER 6 are:
(i) To confirm that actions from the PHSER 5 – Commissioning Stage and post start-up actions
of Pre-Start-up Safety Review (PSSR) have been closed or are being suitably progressed
to closure;
(ii) To confirm that appropriate HSE studies have been carried out for project including those
listed in ADNOC HSE Impact Assessment (HSEIA) Standard [Ref. 1] are completed, their
recommendations are being tracked, and actions potentially affecting the operations have
been completed. To verify that HSE performance of the operating facility meets the design
intent and ADNOC, regulatory, and HSE contractual compliance requirements;
(iii) To confirm that the operating facility has adopted and embedded HSE design philosophies,
safeguards, processes, and tasks established by the project for ongoing HSE performance
of the operating facility and to meet contractual and regulatory obligations such as HSEIA;
and
(iv) To ensure that HSE lessons learned from the execution of the project and early operation of
the plant and/or asset are captured and shared across the ADNOC Group.

7.7.2. PHSER 6 – KEY REQUIREMENTS
PHSER 6 shall be conducted between 1 year and 2 years after the facility has been in operation and first
Operation Phase HSEIA has been completed and technically approved by GC CHSE. It shall discuss the
key HSE aspects related to the continued operation of the facility. It shall ensure that all HSE issues are
identified, plans are established for mitigating HSE risks during continued operation and programmes are
developed for managing long term HSE concerns and emerging risks. It shall also ensure that actions
from Operation Phase HSEIA are being addressed.
PHSER 6 shall also provide an insight on HSE issues in operation as the lessons learned to be considered
in future similar projects.
Site visit shall be mandatory in order to:
(i) Identify any abnormal conditions at facilities that pose threat or signs of deterioration on
quality of engineering, workmanship, etc.; and
(ii) Review of operation logs, checklists, and maintenance records maintained post start-up.
Appendix 6 provides the key activities to ensure that above objectives are met.
7.8. PHSER 7 – DECOMMISSIONING STAGE
7.8.1. PHSER 7 – OBJECTIVES
The objective of PHSER 7 are:
(i) To confirm that the proposed decommissioning is consistent with the ADNOC HSE MS
requirements;
(ii) To confirm that appropriate HSE studies have been carried out for decommissioning
activities including those listed in ADNOC HSE Impact Assessment (HSEIA) Standard [Ref.
1] are completed, their recommendations are being tracked, and actions potentially affecting
the decommissioning activities have been completed; and
(iii) To provide assurance that adequate identification of the HSE issues associated with the
decommissioning has been carried out.
7.8.2. PHSER 7 – KEY REQUIREMENTS
PHSER 7 shall be conducted at 50% of the decommissioning stage (refer to the note at the end of this
section). It shall ensure that all HSE issues related to decommissioning operations have been identified,
plans have been established for mitigating HSE risks, and programmes are developed for managing long
term HSE concerns.
The abandonment/demolition raises new issues of safety and environmental protection. PHSER 7 shall
ensure that HSE studies have been conducted to identify key safety and environmental hazards and
associated risks and plans are in place to close action items.
Site visit shall be conducted to verify the compliance to demolition HSE plan and acquire updated site
HSE information.
Appendix 7 provides the key activities to ensure that above objectives are met.
Note: For certain decommissioning projects based on activities and feedback from GC CHSE, PHSER
can be carried out in two phases i.e. first prior to starting work and the second at 50% progress.
