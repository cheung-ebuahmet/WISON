1.

INTRODUCTION

Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC), in alignment with the Government of Abu Dhabi’s 2030 vision.
ADNOC launched its long term strategic initiative to assume a shaping and supporting role in promoting
sustainability and energy efficient economy in the United Arab Emirates (UAE). ADNOC has established
its Health,  Safety and  Environment (HSE)  Standards  System to  govern  and guide  Group Companies.
Energy Management System (‘EnMS’) standard aims to achieve the establishment and maintenance of
EnMS across the ADNOC Group.

This Standard provides information and guidance on how energy management should be addressed and
governed  in  ADNOC  Group.  It  is  based  on  the  latest  requirements  and  guidance  detailed  in  the
international standard ISO 50001:2018 [Ref 1]. Implementation of this Standard across ADNOC Group
will enable energy performance to be effectively managed,

ADNOC Group shall establish and develop EnMS in compliance with ISO 50001 requirements.  ADNOC
Group may develop their EnMS as a stand-alone system, or as an integral component of their Integrated
Management System (IMS).

ADNOC Group shall maintain the certification of ISO 50001 with the latest standard. This document has
been  structured  based  on  the  requirements  of  ISO  50001:2018  and  ADNOC  Energy  Management
System’s  Guidelines.  ADNOC  Group  shall  implement  the  requirements  of  this  document  and  EnMS
guidelines.

2.

PURPOSE

The  purpose  of  this  document  is  to  provide  guidance  to  ADNOC  Group  on  the  procedures  to  assure
effective establishment, implementation, and maintenance of EnMS across ADNOC Group. It will enable
ADNOC  Group  to  follow  a  systematic  approach  in  achieving  continual  improvement  of  energy
performance and the EnMS.

3.

SCOPE

This  document  stipulates  the  mandatory  requirements  applicable  to  ADNOC  Group  [Directorates  &
Functions at headquarters (HQ), Group Companies and Affiliates].

ADNOC  Group  shall  ensure  that  all  expectations  listed  herein  are  fully  understood,  implemented  and
thoroughly monitored.

(a)

Energy Boundaries and Periods

ADNOC has established a guideline for defining the measurement energy boundaries and periods
to allocate efforts and resources in energy management and energy performance improvement.

ADNOC is managing the measurement boundaries and periods at corporate level, organizational
level  and  for  its  building  facilities  in  order  to  measure  energy  performance  and  performance

ADNOC Group shall establish and manage their measurement boundaries and period in-line with

(b)

Legal and Other Requirements

ADNOC Group shall identify and implement all applicable legal and other requirements relevant to
energy use, consumption and efficiency. They shall also determine how these requirements are
considered in establishing, implementing and maintaining the EnMS.

ADNOC has established governing guideline that describes how to identify, comply to and maintain
legal and other requirements applicable to ADNOC Group and its building facilities.

ADNOC  shall  list  all  applicable  legal  and  other  requirements  and  maintained  a  register  with
respective compliance status. ADNOC Group shall list applicable legal and other requirements and
maintain compliance register in accordance with the requirements of ADNOC governing guideline.
The legal requirements relevant to this Standard shall also be reviewed at regular defined intervals
i.e. at least once per annum by ADNOC Group.

(c)

Energy Information and Data Gathering

ADNOC has established a governing guideline on how to determine and gather energy data for
different purposes as required by the EnMS processes.

ADNOC shall determine and gather energy data for different purposes including conducting energy
review, defining EnPIs, establishing baselines, setting targets, measuring energy performance and
performance improvements; and conducting benchmarking for existing facilities and new projects.

ADNOC  Group  should  determine  and  gather  useful  energy  data  as  required  by  the  ADNOC
governing guideline.

(d)  Data Accuracy and Tolerances

ADNOC has established a governing guideline for determining the criteria for ensuring reliability
and accuracy of data collected for calculating meaningful energy savings results.

ADNOC shall set and document an acceptable level of uncertainty criteria before determining its
energy  savings,  so  as  to  minimize  the  impact  of  statistical  variations  on  the  validity  of  energy
savings.

ADNOC Group shall use uncertainty criteria and acceptable tolerances in order to minimize the
statistical variations during calculating and validating energy saving.

(a)  Operational Control

Operational  level  indicators  are  important  to maintain  focus  and  ensure  energy  continues  to  be





the defined operational criteria; and

ADNOC shall develop, record and maintain an operational control plan of its buildings and facilities.

ADNOC  Group  shall  develop,  document  and  implement  operational  controls  for  their  SEUs  in

(b)  Design

The  design  of  a  facility  has  a  major  influence  on  energy  performance.  It  follows  that  energy
efficiency  and  consumption  shall  be  given  full  consideration  during  the  design  phase  of  new
projects. It is equally important to consider energy efficiency when upgrading/expanding existing
facilities or infrastructure.

Energy Efficient Design (EED) involves considerations such as:











The  use  of  efficient  energy  converters  (e.g.  electric  motors,  burners,  variable
speed/frequency drives);

Minimisation of heat/power/pressure losses;

Optimisation of pipe sizes to minimise life-cycle costs;

Heat and power integration, including optimum use of waste heat;

Integration/optimisation based on risks and opportunities with neighbouring facilities;



Application of best practice in building design and construction; and

Use of advanced longer-life lighting systems (e.g. light emitting diodes (LEDs)).

Energy performance improvements and operational controls shall be considered in the design of
all new or modified ADNOC Group projects or facilities (e.g. ADNOC HQ, Sheikh Khalifa Energy

The  future  costs  of  energy  should  also  be  taken  into  account  in  project  economics  so  that  the
measurable benefits of energy efficiency can be accounted for over the lifetime of projects.

Another important EED consideration is life cycle energy performance. For example, flow volumes
in  upstream  oil  and  gas  operations  can  change  significantly  over  time;  ramping  up  to  peak
production  and  then  declining  as  the  field  ages.  Designing  an  energy  system  only  for  peak
production will not optimise energy performance. However, if the design allows for adaptation to
load  changes  over  time;  this  is  likely  to  result  in  higher  energy  efficiencies  and  significant  cost
savings over the lifetime of the asset.

ADNOC  has  established  a  governing  guideline  for  EED  which  is  applicable  to  greenfield  and
brownfield projects and relates to its facilities or infrastructure which have a significant impact on
energy performance.

ADNOC shall implement the guidelines and consider energy performance risks and improvement
opportunities  during  new  design  as  well  as  during  upgrading,  expansion  of  existing  facilities  or
infrastructure which have a significant impact on energy performance.

ADNOC Group shall consider energy performance risks and opportunities during new design as
well  as  upgrading,  expansion  of  existing  facilities  or  infrastructure  in  accordance  with  the
