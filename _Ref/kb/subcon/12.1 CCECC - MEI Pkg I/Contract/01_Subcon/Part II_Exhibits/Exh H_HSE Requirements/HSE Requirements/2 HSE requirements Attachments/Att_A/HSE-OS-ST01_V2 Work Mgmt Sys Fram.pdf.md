1.

INTRODUCTION

Work  Management  System  (WMS)  is  a  safety  tool  to  ensure  that  the  work  is  properly  controlled,
coordinated and communicated. It takes a holistic and robust approach to job safety analysis inclusive of
formal risk assessment, energy isolation, permit to work, temporary defeat and simultaneous operations
with an objective of standardizing these practices across ADNOC Group Companies.

Key objectives of WMS include the following:

(i)

ADNOC  Group  Companies  are  no  longer  required  to  maintain  a  different  set  of  system
procedure.

(ii)

Harmonizes terminologies and processes under the Work Management System processes.

(iii)  Process  of  communication  and  enforcing  the  lessons  learned  across  the  organization

becomes simplified and effective.

(iv)  Familiarity  of  the  contractors  on  a  single  system  working  for  across  ADNOC  Group

Companies.

(v)

The increased ability to move core personnel around ADNOC Group Companies becomes
free from system limitations.

ADNOC  Work  Management  System  (WMS)  Framework  depicted  in  the  below  figure  indicates  the
components for facilitating implementation and continuous improvement of Work Management System
across ADNOC Group Companies.

2.

PURPOSE

ADNOC Work Management System (WMS) framework describes the key aspects and requirements to
facilitate harmonization and integration of HSE critical processes that include Job Safety Analysis, Permit
To Work, Energy Isolation, Temporary Defeat, and SIMOPS across all ADNOC Group Companies.

This standard defines the structure of Work Management System (WMS), which takes into account of the
components for facilitation and  continuous improvement during the implementation in ADNOC and  its
Group Companies.

WMS  framework  standard  also  provide  details  of  governance  requirements  of  WMS  elements  across
ADNOC Group of Companies.

3.

SCOPE

This  document  shall  be  applicable  to  all  ADNOC  group  companies,  employees,  contractors,  facilities
(including interface areas), activities and services without exception.

Contractors  shall  be  responsible  for  ensuring  that  all  activities  performed  within  ADNOC  operating
facilities in the course of carrying out their contractual requirements are managed in accordance with the
requirements of this document and reported, when applicable, to the contract holder.

In  cases,  where work  is  to  be  carried out  on  non-ADNOC  facilities/greenfield locations, work  may  be
managed under an alternate work management system. Where Contractor work management system is
implemented, Contract Administrator shall ensure that it is compliant with ADNOC requirements.

WMS framework standard shall be used and read in conjunction with other WMS standards.

4.

LAWS AND REGULATIONS

There are no specific laws and regulations applicable to this standard.

7.  WMS FRAMEWORK

7.1.  KEY COMPONENTS OF WMS FRAMEWORK

The WMS is a eight (8) stage process that integrates all five critical processes in WMS i.e. Permit to Work
(PTW), Job Safety Analysis, Energy Isolation, Temporary Defeat and SIMOPS.

(a)  Work Request & Planning

A  comprehensive  planning  process  that  include  Integrated  Work  Activity  Planning  (IWAP)  process,
providing  clear  description  of  work  scope  details  and  carrying  out  initial  job  analysis  to  decide  further
course of actions.

(b)

Identify & Approve Controls

A formal process of conducting Job Safety Analysis involving all relevant personnel basis the initial job
analysis for identifying controls necessary to reduce the risk level to ALARP followed by a final approval
by authorized personnel to proceed.

(c)  Work Site Controls & Implementation

A critical process for ensuring safe working conditions prior to work permit issue involving implementation
of the controls as per the Permit requirements. The various steps involved in the process are identification
and completing all worksite preparations (energy isolations, temporary defeats, etc.).

(d)  Authorize Work Permits

Process of authorization of the work permit by Area Authority (AA) ensuring implementation of necessary
permit controls.

(e)  Communicate Controls & Commence Work

(f)

Dynamic Risk Assessment

An on-going process of identification whether safe working conditions exist during execution of work. All

7.3.1.  ADNOC GROUP COMPANY AND EMPLOYEES:

ADNOC Group Companies and employees, the users of WMS Standards are responsible for managing,
planning, supervising or executing the work on ADNOC facilities.

7.3.2.  CONTRACTORS AND THIRD PARTIES:

Contractors  shall  be  responsible  for  ensuring  that  all  activities  performed  within  ADNOC  operating
facilities in the course of carrying out their contractual requirements are managed in accordance with the
requirements of WMS Standards and reported, when applicable, to the contract holder.

Where an ADNOC Group Company is in a JV arrangement, the use of alternate safe system of work is
allowed provided that it meets the mandated requirements given in WMS standards.

In cases, where work is to be carried out by a third party contractor on non-ADNOC facilities/greenfield
locations, work may be managed under an alternate safe work system however, ADNOC representative
shall ensure that  controls equivalent to this standard are applied.

7.3.3.  REGULATORS:

ADNOC has an obligation to comply with all applicable laws, regulations when applying WMS Standards.
WMS Standards incorporate applicable regulations to ensure that underlying principles and requirements
of WMS are aligned to all mandatory requirements from the regulatory perspective. The requirements in
ADNOC WMS Standards are intended to meet or exceed regulatory requirements.

ADNOC Group HSE shall review applicable regulatory requirements pertaining to WMS Standard on a
periodic basis to periodically to identify any requirements in addition to that defined in the WMS Standards
to meet or exceed regulatory compliance.

7.5.  CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT

This stands for a process of actively identifying and realizing improvement opportunities that will better
enable  ADNOC  to  achieve  the  intended  outcomes  of  WMS.  The  potential  sources  of  improvement
opportunities include periodic review of WMS Standards, incorporation of the results of review, analysis
on HSE performance, lessons learned during WMS implementation, audits and WMS steering committee
reviews. Improvement can also be effected through innovation practices or by company reorganization
and/ or process transformation.

7.5.1.  PERIODIC REVIEW OF WMS STANDARD:

WMS  Standards  shall  be  reviewed  every  year  by  ADNOC  Group  HSE  for  edits,  enhancements  and
continuous improvement opportunities.

ADNOC Group HSE shall maintain a register of changes requested from the Group Company on WMS
Standards, to appropriately adjust it in the standards. Decisions on accepting or rejecting change requests
from a Group Company will be with ADNOC Group HSE who are required to ensure that the proposed
changes shall not impact other Group Company activities across ADNOC and  the safety of personnel
and  protection  of  the  environment  /  property  must  not  be  jeopardized.  Accepted  changes  are  either
immediately  changed  and  amended  in  the  Standard  following  the  Document  Control  Procedure  of
ADNOC or captured for revision at the next scheduled review cycle depending on their criticality.

7.5.2.  WMS AUDITS:

This component represents having an established audit program to cover all requirements under WMS.
The  purpose  of  audit  is  to  provide  information  whether  the  existing  practices/  processes  conforms  to
requirements of WMS elements and are effectively implemented and maintained.

The implementation of the WMS Standards shall be subjected to a structured audit program in addition
to routine monitoring carried out by Asset Owner and his delegates. Auditing shall be carried out in such
a manner that all aspects of the WMS Standards are covered. Auditing shall be carried out by a personnel
considered to have sufficient knowledge and experience of the WMS to fulfil the requirements of the audit.
Evaluation of audits shall be conducted by experienced and knowledgeable personnel, who shall report
and advise on the state of the system and provide advice on trends, deficiencies and area of improvement.

The objectives of Monitoring and Audit of the WMS Standards are to:

(i)

Check  compliance  with  WMS  Standards  requirements  and  improve  performance  where
