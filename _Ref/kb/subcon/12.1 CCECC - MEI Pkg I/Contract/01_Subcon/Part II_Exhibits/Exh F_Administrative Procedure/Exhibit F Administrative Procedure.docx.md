**1.0 INTRODUCTION**

The main goal of this Attachment is to specify the coordination procedures to be employed by Subcontractor in the performance of this Subcontract.

* 1. The Subcontract Price is written in Part II and it includes the provision of supervision, manpower, equipment, materials and facilities necessary for Subcontractor to operate in accordance with this Attachment.   2. In case a contradiction with other terms and conditions of this Subcontract, it may be changed only after Contractor has been notified of and has resolved any such contradiction or on the express instruction of Contractor.

Contractor (WISON) and Subcontractor shall establish communication through meetings, reports, letters and documents transfer. 1. **SUBCONTRACT SITE INSTRUCTIONS (SSI)**

Subcontract Site Instructions (SSIs),will be issued by Contractor to Subcontractor defining minor changes to the Work that are not included by AFC drawings and for which finalized pricing has not been agreed. The SSI will define the payment basis. Subcontractor shall proceed with the work (unless instructed otherwise on the SSI) and within ten (10) working days, shall submit to Contractor the cost and schedule effects of the SSI with all requisite pricing details.

* 1. SSIs will be numbered sequentially.
  2. A daily time sheet covering the previous day for approval for any SSI that is issued on an open-ended time and material basis will be submitted by the Subcontractor on or before 10:00 a.m.
  3. Contractor will periodically consolidate the agreed costs of SSIs into Subcontract Modifications. The SSI is not an authority for payment. It is, however, the basis for preparation of a Subcontract Modification, which is the only authority for payment.
  4. Generally SSIs are for low value (under $5,000) works. Works of a higher value, if not covered by SSI, will be covered by a Subcontract Modification and will not be performed until the written approval is issued in accordance with Change Clause set forth in Part I to the Subcontract.

1. **REQUESTS FOR INFORMATION (RFI)**

Request for Information (RFI) should be submitted to Contractor in case Subcontractor finds some portion of a drawing, specification or the contract document that needs clarification or interpretation.

* 1. Using the approved RFI form (attached hereto), Subcontractor shall clearly and concisely set forth the following information:
     1. Subcontract number, RFI number, referenced documents. RFIs will be consecutively numbered beginning with CXXX-RFI-001.
     2. The issue for which clarification is required and why an answer is needed from Contractor.
     3. Subcontractor’s analysis or understanding of the Subcontract requirements together with reasons therefore and any proposed resolutions.
  2. Subcontractor anticipates that this is a complex project, and it is expected that Subcontractor will issue RFIs to Contractor throughout the life of the Subcontract to question and or seek clarification of the Work.
  3. After completion of the form, Subcontractor submits the RFI to Contractor’s Field Engineer with a copy to the Subcontract Administrator.
  4. Contractor will review all RFIs to determine whether or not they are RFIs within the meaning set forth in this Attachment. If Contractor determines that the RFI is inappropriate, it shall be returned within five (5) working days of receipt, without response except to state one of the following:
     1. Information to resolve the request that exists in the Subcontract.
     2. Information to resolve the request that exists on an issued drawing.
     3. Information to resolve the request that exists in an issued Specification or Document.
     4. The use of the RFI form is incorrect and the subject should be covered by a letter, transmittal or other as defined in this Attachment.

RFIs returned for any of the foregoing reasons shall be clearly shown in Contractor’s RFI register as “Not Applicable” meaning that they comprise incorrect use of the RFI procedure.

* 1. Contractor shall answer to RFIs within five (5) working days after receiving by Contractor, unless Contractor determines that a longer period is required to fully review and respond. In this case, Subcontractor will be advised of the longer period required within five (5) working days of initial receipt.
  2. If Subcontractor submits an RFI within five (5) working days or less of float on the agreed Subcontract Schedule, Subcontractor will not be given extension of time due to the time taken for Contractor to respond to the RFI.
  3. Contractor’s Field Engineer shall register when RFIs are returned to Subcontractor. The RFI register shall be reviewed at each progress meeting.
  4. Contractor's response to RFIs are considered to be within the Scope of Work of the Subcontract, will not change any requirements of the Subcontract and do not in themselves constitute agreement or authorization to perform a change.
  5. If Contractor considers that the response to an RFI comprises a change to the Subcontract Price, Contractor will issue a corresponding Subcontract Site Instruction or a Subcontract Modification, and this shall be referenced on the RFI response. Only the Subcontract Site Instruction or Subcontract Modification constitutes authorization to proceed with the RFI response if it is a change.
  6. If Subcontractor receives answer to an RFI that Contractor has not registered as a change, but which Subcontractor believes that there is a basis for a change, Subcontractor shall not proceed in accordance with the response. Subcontractor shall immediately notify Contractor in accordance with Clause 21 of the Conditions of Subcontract and shall not proceed with the Work, which is believed to be changed, without the receipt of a Subcontract Site Instruction from Contractor.

1. **MEETINGS**

Contractor or his nominated representative shall chair all meetings. Subcontractor shall prepare an agenda and review material required for each meeting. The weekly coordination meeting will take place at Site during the construction phase. When a meeting other than the scheduled monthly/weekly meeting is requested, a meeting notification sheet with agenda is to be prepared by the requesting party. This notification sheet will be signed by authorized personnel before issuing to concerned parties.

When Contractor requests a meeting for clarification of technical matter and/or the Subcontractor requests to obtain approval of scope, documents, etc., a meeting will be held by prior arrangement between both parties.

Coordination meetings with other interfacing Contractor on an as needed basis shall be held to clear any interface matters.

Meeting between parties will be held as requested by Contractor, for the purpose of:

* Keeping all parties fully informed of all aspects of the Work
* Reviewing progress, status, scheduling and HSE aspects of the Work, and
* Reviewing technical or financial subjects.

Technical documents for discussion and progress report narrative, together with other relevant documents as considered appropriate to Contractor, shall be submitted with a proposed agenda at least seven working days in advance of such meetings by Subcontractor.

* 1. Major Meetings
     1. In addition to formal and informal communications or meeting between Contractor and Subcontractor, regular formal routine meetings are needed throughout the duration of the Subcontractor's performance in order to assure continued communication and record such interfaces for the future.
     2. Meetings possibly scheduled at any time to resolve problems. Major meetings will be scheduled on a regular basis and will be determined as the Subcontract work develops. Some major meetings to be scheduled may include:
* Kick-off Meeting
* Quality Control & Audit Meetings
* Health, Safety and Environmental (HSE) Meetings
* Regular Progress Review Meetings
* Subcontractor Coordination Meetings
* Special Meetings
* Subcontract Closeout Meetings
  + 1. Work site normally is the regular location of the meeting. However, it may occasionally be necessary for Subcontractor to participate in meetings at other locations, e.g., Contractor or Owner’s office.
  1. Regular Progress Review Meetings
     1. Progress review meetings will be held on a regularly scheduled basis, usually weekly. These meetings will provide a discussion on reviewing of Subcontractor's operations, evaluation of progress and schedule, discussion and resolution of problems facing the Subcontractor and Contractor, and coordination of the activities of all parties concerned.
     2. In general, these meetings require a minimum of participation to achieve the maximum positive results.
     3. Subcontractor shall prepare minutes of meeting in English and submit within two days for approval.
  2. Subcontractor Coordination Meetings

Subcontractor Coordination Meetings will be scheduled as required to discuss problems requiring Subcontractor interfaces and coordination. Minutes of Meeting will be taken by the Party who called for this meeting.

* 1. Special Meetings
     1. Special meetings will prearrange as necessary to discuss and plan Work and resolve problems, etc.
     2. Further, Subcontractor shall conduct a program of safety motivation and training; including regular safety meetings (tool box meetings) for its personnel in accordance with Attachment D. Subcontractor shall minute these meetings and submit to Contractor for information.
  2. Subcontract Closeout Meeting

At finishing point of Subcontractor's Work or as soon thereafter as practicable, a Subcontract Closeout Meeting will be scheduled between Contractor and Subcontractor. The principle of this meeting is to reach an agreement on all outstanding items, i.e. Subcontract Modifications, final payment amounts, etc.

* 1. Minutes of Meeting

Subcontractor shall prepare minutes of meeting for all meetings mentioned above (except toolbox meetings). The Minutes of Meeting will be complete with all significant aspects of the meeting recorded and will be presented to the Contractor for signature as soon as practicable after the meeting. It must not exceed 2 days after meeting and it will be officially published after Contractor signed the Minutes of Meeting.
