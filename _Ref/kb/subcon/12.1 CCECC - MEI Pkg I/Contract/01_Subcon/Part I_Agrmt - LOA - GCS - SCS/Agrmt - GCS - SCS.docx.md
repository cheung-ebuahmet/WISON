# SCOPE OF WORK AND SUBCONTRACTOR’S OBLIGATION

1. **GENERAL INFORMATION**
   1. Project Description

The Owner plans to construct the Project (Sulphur Granulations plant) at the Ruwais Industrial Area, approximately 240 km west-southwest of Abu Dhabi City, UAE. The primary objective of the Project is to accommodate 9,900 TPD of liquid sulphur received via pipeline from the Hail & Ghasha OPP.

This involves constructing new infrastructure for liquid sulphur receiving storage and transfer, sulphur forming (Granulation), Granulated sulphur transfer and storage, stacking and reclaiming and export to existing ship loaders.
