**1. SCOPE OF OFF-SITE PREFABRICATION BY THE FABRICATOR / 异地预制范围**

1.1 Fire-fighting piping of nominal diameter 3 inches and greater, which is designed with flanged connections, shall be prefabricated in China, including fabrication, galvanizing, anti-corrosion coating (including FBE i.e., Fusion Bonded Epoxy coating), and all associated works. The total quantity is approximately 67,000 welding inch-diameters.
1.1 公称直径 3 寸及以上的消防管道（设计为法兰连接形式）在中国国内进行预制，包括制作、镀锌、防腐涂层（含 FBE，即熔结环氧粉末涂层）及所有相关工作。总量约为 6.7 万寸径焊接量。

1.2 Fire-fighting piping of nominal diameter below 3 inches, which is designed with threaded connections, shall continue to be fabricated and installed at the Site as originally provided under the Subcontract.
1.2 公称直径 3 寸以下的消防管道（设计为螺纹连接形式）仍按分包合同原约定，在项目现场制作安装。

1.3 Notwithstanding Clause 1.1 above, and in substitution for the arrangement under which Subcontractor was to carry out the off-Site prefabrication in the UAE as its own works, the Parties agree that the fire-fighting piping described in Clause 1.1 shall be prefabricated by the Fabricator inside China under the Direct Fabrication Contract to be executed separately between Contractor and the Fabricator. Correspondingly, Subcontractor's scope of works under the Subcontract shall be reduced (Descope) by the workshop prefabrication segment of the said piping. The Parties confirm that this arrangement shall not constitute a breach, nor shall it diminish, release, or affect any of Subcontractor's obligations or liabilities under this Amendment or the Subcontract.
1.3 尽管有上述第 1.1 条约定，为替代原定由分包商在阿联酋自主实施的异地预制安排，各方同意第 1.1 条所述消防管道由加工商在中国国内依据承包商与加工商另行签署的独立加工合同实施车间预制加工。相应地，分包商在分包合同项下就该消防管道车间预制加工部分对应的工程范围予以核减。各方确认，本安排不构成违约，亦不减损、免除或影响分包商在本协议或分包合同项下的任何义务与责任。

**3. SCOPE REDUCTION AND FINANCIAL SET-OFF / 范围核减与财务对冲扣减**

3.1 Subcontractor expressly acknowledges and agrees that the workshop prefabrication segment of the fire-fighting piping has been descoped from the Subcontract and shall be performed by the Fabricator under the Direct Fabrication Contract executed separately between Contractor and the Fabricator. The corresponding value of such Descope shall be deducted from the Subcontract Price strictly on an actual-cost basis, in accordance with the financial set-off mechanism set forth in Clause 3.2 below.
3.1 分包商明确知悉并同意，消防管道车间预制加工部分已自分包合同工程范围中核减剥离，并由加工商依据承包商与加工商另行签署的独立加工合同实施。该核减范围对应的合同价值应严格依据以下第 3.2 条规定的财务对冲扣减机制，按实从分包合同总价中予以等额调减。

3.2 To maintain the structural financial balance under Subcontract (i.e. No. WISON24108C26005), the Subcontractor's progressive progress billings and upcoming Interim Payment Certificates (IPCs) for the fire-fighting piping installment package shall be forcefully and automatically reduced on an absolute actual-cost net deduction basis. Irrespective of any variation against market rate estimations or the Subcontractor's internal cost projections, and regardless of whether the final cross-border prefabrication arrangement represents a commercial savings or a premium, the structural reduction to the Subcontractor's fire-fighting piping subcontract scope shall be calculated and enforced solely based on the real-world expenditures actually incurred, invoiced, and paid under the Direct Fabrication Contract. The Contractor shall have the absolute contractual authority to set-off and claw back the following two cost components directly from any progress payments due or becoming due to the Subcontractor:
3.2 为维持分包合同（WISON24108C26005）项下的结构性财务平衡，分包商后续申报的与消防管道安装采购包相关的工程进度款/中期支付证书（IPC）应基于绝对按实对冲扣减原则执行自动扣减。无论最终预制成本与市场公允价或分包商自身的成本预估存在何种偏差、亦不论最终结果构成了商业节省还是超支溢价，主合同项下相关消防管线工程范围的核减调减，均须绝对且排他性地依据实际发生、开票并支付的真实支出进行计算与强制扣减。承包商有权直接从任何应付或将应付给分包商的进度款中全额轧差扣除以下两项实际费用：

(a) 100% of Actual Workshop Fabrication Expenditures: The total real-world amounts actually invoiced by and paid to the Fabricator under the Direct Fabrication Contract for its prefabrication processing, rolling, bending, welding, high-precision galvanizing, protective coating, and load-port terminal handling inside China, which shall be deducted equivalent-for-equivalent from the Subcontractor's original subcontract scope value as the definitive descope valuation for the workshop prefabrication segment; and
(a) 100% 的实际加工预制成本：加工商在独立加工合同项下因执行预制加工、卷制、弯曲、焊接、高精度镀锌、防腐涂层及中国境内集港交付而实际向承包商开具发票并由承包商支付的全部真实金额。该笔实际发生费用将等额直接从分包合同原对应的消防管段总价中进行扣减，作为最终预制加工范围核减的最终结算依据；以及

(b) 25% of All Actually Incurred Logistics and Customs Expenditures: The Subcontractor's strict twenty-five percent (25%) proportional share of all actual international transportation segment expenditures (explicitly including, without limitation, all real-world marine cargo insurance, loading port terminal handling charges, destination sea customs clearance fees, UAE import regulatory duties, as well as any and all actually occurring dead freight, port demurrage, container detention, or shipping re-booking cancellation charges) incurred from the loading port in China up to arrival at the UAE designated handover plot.

4.1 承包商依据独立加工合同向加工商支付的就消防管道预制采购包相关的全部款项（包括但不限于预制加工、镀锌、涂层及集港交付成本）以及第 4.3 条所述的全部物流及清关相关支出，均应依据第 3 条约定的财务对冲扣减机制按实全额从分包商的工程进度款中予以抵扣。

4.2 Without limiting any other rights or remedies available to Contractor under applicable law or the Subcontract (including this Amendment), Contractor shall be entitled, at its sole discretion and without prior notice to Subcontractor, to deduct the full amounts described in Clause 3.2 and Clause 4.1 from any project progress payments, or any other sums (or any combination thereof), due or becoming due to Subcontractor under the Subcontract.
4.2 在不限制承包商依法或依据分包合同（含本变更）可获得的其他权利或救济的前提下，承包商有权自行决定且无需事先通知分包商，从分包合同项下应付或将成为应付分包商的任何工程进度款、或任何其他款项（或其组合）中全额抵扣上述第 3.2 条及第 4.1 条所述的全部款项。

As to the costs actually incurred in respect of such international transportation (including marine insurance, port charges, customs clearance fees, and associated shipping charges), Subcontractor shall bear twenty-five percent (25%), and Contractor shall bear the remaining seventy-five percent (75%). The 25%/75% sharing under this Clause 4.3 applies exclusively to the international transportation segment described above.

4.4 Save for the international freight component sharing set forth in Clause 4.3 above, all other cost components associated with the fire-fighting piping prefabrication package — including but not limited to: fabrication processing costs, material handling, galvanizing and coating costs, quality assurance, inspection and testing costs, domestic Chinese transportation, and any other costs or expenses arising out of or in connection with the Direct Fabrication Contract and related logistics services — shall be borne one hundred percent (100%) by Subcontractor and deducted on an actual-cost basis in accordance with Clause 3 and Clauses 4.1–4.2.
