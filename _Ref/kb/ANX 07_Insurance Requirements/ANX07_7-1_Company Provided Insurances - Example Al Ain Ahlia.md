General: 
The COMPANY will maintain construction all risks insurance for the PROJECT,

Sum Insured:

3. 
Insured Parties: 
(i) 
ADNOC and ADNOC GROUP as owner and/or any parent, owned, 
controlled, joint venture, partner, shareholder or any subsidiary 
companies or corporations of the foregoing as they exist now or may 
hereinafter be constituted and/or their affiliates and/or; 
 
(ii) 
Project manager(s) and/or consultant(s) and/or engineer(s) and/or the 
CONTRACTOR and/or sub-contractors of any tier and/or any other 
company, firm, person or party with whom the Insured(s) above or in this 
paragraph have, or in the past had, entered into written agreement(s) in 
connection with the CONTRACTOR and/or;  
 
(iii) 
Manufacturers, vendors and suppliers of equipment to the PROJECT, 
including all professional consultants, technical advisors and architects in 
respect of their manual onsite activities only; and/or; 
 
(iv) 
Any other company, firm, person or party with whom the Insured(s) have, 
or in the past had, entered into written agreements(s) in connection with 
the PROJECT covered hereunder; 
 
together with their respective successors in title and assigns, all for their 
respective rights and interests, and/or as may be more fully defined in the 
wording and/or as required under the AGREEMENT.

Insured Project: 
[insert PROJECT description]

Interest Insured:

transits, loading/unloading, storage, testing, trials, pre-commissioning  and 
commissioning etc. associated and ancillary activities in connection therewith. 
6. 
Coverage: 
Physical loss or damage unless otherwise excluded. 
THIRD PARTY claims arising out of or in relation to the performance of the 
WORKS.

Deductibles: 
The deductible levels are set independently for onshore and offshore projects 
respectively. The indicative levels are attached for information only, and it should
