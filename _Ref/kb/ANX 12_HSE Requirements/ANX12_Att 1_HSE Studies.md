Notes 
a 
Above table provides list of minimum HSE Studies that are required for providing input to various sections under HSEIA identified as required through HSEIA 
screening.  
1 
A separate HAZID, ENVID & Qualitative OHRA register/report shall be prepared for construction & decommissioning activities. All construction related hazards to 
be managed by Construction HSE Plan and CEMP. All decommissioning related hazards to be managed by Decommissioning HSE Plan & DEMP. 
Recommendations arising from HAZID/ENVID/Qualitative OHRA review and other HSE studies which are associated with Construction and decommissioning 
activities shall be implemented 
2 
Detailed engineering HAZID/ENVID/Qualitative OHRA will identify any specific hazards for operation phase and commissioning phase if any.  These 
register/report shall be updated if any major changes are seen to design as part of MOC process/major technical deviations 
3 
Detailed engineering HSEIA dossier/COMAH shall be updated if any major changes are seen to design as part of MOC process/major technical deviations. 
4 
HSEIA study and all studies carried out during given project phase will form the base HSEIA dossier for next phase of the project. 
5 
Based on the size, complexity and HSE issues associated with the project, GC can either perform Coarse QRA or only dispersion assessment as per guidance 
from corporate HSE. 
6 
Update study at operation stage only if there are major modifications or requirement to integrate brown field projects with existing facilities. Study shall be 
reviewed every 5 years to determine if there are significant changes to facilities affecting the study. Study shall be updated based on these changes to ensure 
assessment reflect the updated changes. 
7 
QRA scope shall include SIMOPS QRA objectives (i.e. Impact to construction locations and personnel from existing facilities) as applicable. Where facilities are 
commissioned or planned to be commissioned in phases/units, incremental risk results and assessment shall be provided in QRA Reports. 
8 
Based on the HSECES management system in place, GC can develop separate HSECES register for tag level HSECES identification. 
9 
Update is from Detailed Engineering HSECES PS. 
10 
SBRA/Semi-Quantitative Risk Assessment may be carried out for specific commissioning hazards as part of MOC or major technical deviations. 
11 
Requirement for HAZOP report to be checked during De-commissioning by GC CHSE to ensure no process safety risk are introduced. (Example: - 
Decommissioning of one train; one unit; etc.) 
12 
The detail of deliverables and approval requirements shall be as per ADNOC SRM standard.
