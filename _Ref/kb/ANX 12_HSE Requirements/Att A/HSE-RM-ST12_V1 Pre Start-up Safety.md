1. 
INTRODUCTION 
Pre Start-up Safety Review (PSSR) is a systematic and structured process that is intended to provide 
assurance that all new or modified facilities are constructed in accordance with the relevant design 
specifications.  
 
The objective of the PSSR process is to verify the following: 
 
(i) 
Construction and equipment are in accordance with design specifications; 
(ii) 
Hazard analysis and risk assessment has been performed for the new facilities/intended 
change  and recommendations from the hazard analysis are implemented or resolved; 
(iii) 
HSE, operating, maintenance, interface, coordination and emergency procedures are in 
place and adequate; and 
(iv) 
All necessary training has been completed for relevant personnel. 
The PSSR provides a final check for new and modified equipment/facilities to confirm that all appropriate 
HSE elements have been addressed satisfactorily and the facility is safe to start up. 
  
2. 
PURPOSE 
The purpose of this standard is to define the minimum requirements for conducting a PSSR for all new 
and/or modified process facilities, equipment and systems in order to ensure their ‘Ready for Safe Start-
up’ status prior to introduction of ‘Hazardous Substances’. 
 
All PSSR carried out for ADNOC Group shall be in accordance with the requirements of this Standard. 
 
3. 
SCOPE 
This document stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to ADNOC Group and its Contractors.  
 
ADNOC Group and Contractors shall ensure that all requirements listed herein are fully understood, 
complied with, and monitored at all times.

7. 
REQUIREMENTS  
A PSSR shall be conducted for: 
 
(i) 
Any new process facility, equipment or system installed as part of a Project or Development 
activity (including any existing facility, equipment or system to be operated at a different 
location, such as well testing operations); 
(ii) 
Any change to existing facilities, equipment or systems initiated through the Management of 
Change (MOC) process. The need for conducting a PSSR in the MOC process shall be 
established in accordance with the HSE Requirements in ADNOC Management of Change 
(MOC); and 
(iii) 
After completion of turnarounds or major non-routine maintenance. 
PSSR team shall be provided with all required information for carrying out the PSSR including any issues 
that were encountered during pre-commissioning stage. 
 
Carrying out PSSR is not a requirement for processes and systems that do not handle ‘hazardous 
substances’. Hazardous substance definition also includes processes such as high pressure systems 
(e.g. air, nitrogen, steam), cryogenics etc. 
 
Person in-charge of Project/MOC/Turnaround (e.g. Project Manager) shall be responsible for initiating, 
forming PSSR team and scheduling PSSR prior to introduction of ‘hazardous substance’ in to the process, 
system, and or equipment. 
 
Person in-charge of the operations and facility shall sign-off ‘Statement of Fitness’ after ensuring that all 
pre start-up actions are resolved and facility is ready for start-up. ‘Statement of Fitness’ template is 
provided as Appendix 4. 
 
7.1. 
PSSR TYPES 
The three types of PSSRs conducted are: 
 
(i) 
Project PSSRs; 
(ii) 
Management of Change (MOC)/ Modification PSSRs; and 
(iii) 
Turnaround PSSRs. 
7.1.1. PROJECT PSSR 
Project PSSR shall be performed for any new facility, any new equipment or system that is 
managed as a project. 
Project PSSR shall be conducted: 
 
(i) 
Prior to  introducing hazardous substances in any of the system; 
(ii) 
After all punch list items identified in the mechanical completion walk-through are either 
implemented or resolved; and 
(iii) 
As a team exercise with the aid of Project PSSR checklist provided as Appendix 1. 
The PSSR team shall ensure that facilities under review meet all relevant ADNOC Standards, Industry 
Codes and Standards. 
 
For Projects with multiple sub-systems (e.g. refinery, oil/gas processing facility) separate PSSRs should 
be conducted for the different sub-systems of the facility rather than a single PSSR for the entire facility.

A Project PSSR shall include review of the action items from the PHSER-5 - Commissioning stage.

be authorized by relevant personnel in charge of operations and facility within the concerned entity 
(Directorate, Functions, Divisions, etc.) across ADNOC Group.  
 
7.1.2. MOC/Modification PSSR 
MOC PSSR shall be performed for any change or modification to a facility initiated through MOC process.  
 
MOC PSSR shall be conducted: 
 
(i) 
Prior to  introducing hazardous substances in any of the systems; 
(ii) 
After all punch list items identified in the mechanical completion walk-through are either 
implemented or resolved; and 
(iii) 
As a team exercise with the aid of MOC PSSR checklist. MOC PSSR checklist is provided 
as Appendix 2. The Project Manager or PSSR Team Leader may use the Project PSSR 
Checklist depending on the complexity of the change. 
The PSSR team shall ensure that facilities under review meet all relevant ADNOC Standards, Industry 
Codes and Standards. 
 
Actions required for addressing all non-compliances and or gaps identified during PSSR shall be recorded 
as pre start-up or post start-up actions items section of the PSSR checklist, as appropriate. 
 
Hazardous substances shall only be introduced into an equipment or a system when pre start-up actions 
are either implemented or resolved. 
 
All post start-up action items shall be resolved within 30 days of introducing hazardous substances to the 
facility or equipment; any dispensation for extension and time line for implementation post 30 days shall 
be authorized by relevant personnel in charge of operations and facility within the concerned entity 
(Directorate, Functions, Divisions, etc.) across ADNOC Group.  
 
7.1.3. Turnaround PSSR 
Turnaround PSSR shall be performed after completion of turnarounds or major non-routine maintenance. 
A major maintenance and inspection activity requiring this type of PSSR should involve prolonged (more 
than one week shutdown) period with contractor(s) involvement and major maintenance work (confined 
space, DCS modification, etc.). 
 
Turnaround PSSR shall be conducted: 
 
(i) 
Prior to  introducing hazardous substances in any of the systems; and 
(ii) 
As a team exercise with the aid of Turnaround PSSR checklist. Turnaround PSSR checklist 
is provided as Appendix 3.

Basic PSSR work flow steps are as follows: 
 
(i) 
Determine the type of PSSR to perform – Project PSSR or MOC PSSR or Turnaround PSSR; 
(ii) 
Form the PSSR team led by a team leader and relevant SMEs; 
(iii) 
Schedule Team meetings by PSSR team. (One meeting may be sufficient for some PSSRs; 
multiple meetings may be needed for others.); 
(iv) 
Identify and assemble documentation, records that are required to be reviewed. All 
documents which are required for PSSR shall be provided to the PSSR team; 
(v) 
Identify field visit requirement for verification; 
(vi) 
Conduct document review and mark comments in the check list; 
(vii) 
Conduct field visit verification and mark comments in the check list. The verification shall 
include review of items such as safety systems/signage, access/escape routes, emergency 
facilities, and operability issues; 
(viii) Hold progress meetings as needed for the PSSR; 
(ix) 
Identify and record pre and post start-up actions required; 
(x) 
When all pre start-up action items are implemented or resolved, obtain authorization to start-
up (introducing hazardous substances), new facility, modification, process or turnaround;  
(xi) 
Complete the PSSR Documentation including Statement of Fitness as shown in Appendix 
4; and 
(xii) 
Track post start-up PSSR action items (if any). 
Note: Use lessons learned from both the PSSR successes and setbacks as events worthy of evaluation. 
These will help determine which programmatic aspects work and which aspects don't work in the PSSR 
process. 
 
7.3. 
PSSR TEAM  
The PSSR shall be completed by a cross functional team that is competent to review the project/MOC 
documentations and installed equipment/systems to decide if it is safe to start. The PSSR team shall 
comprise of subject matter experts from Project, HSE and Operations teams with skill and experience 
related to the issues involved in the modification or new equipment/ system/ process or turnaround. The 
PSSR team  
 
The team shall comprise:  
 
(i) 
PSSR Team Leader; and 
(ii) 
PSSR Team Members.  
Although the PSSR team composition and number of team members depends on the scope of the 
Project/Modification /Turnaround, the typical participants should include representatives from the 
following groups: 
 
(i) 
Engineers (Operation Engineer, Process Engineers and/or Reliability Specialists); 
(ii) 
Individuals familiar with the design and construction (Engineering/Construction Team); 
(iii) 
Operations representative; 
(iv) 
Maintenance Engineers (Mechanical, Electrical, Instruments & Control); 
(v) 
Technical HSE Engineers; 
(vi) 
Field HSE representatives;

7.3.1. PSSR Team Leader 
Each PSSR will have a Team Leader who is responsible for conducting, documenting the review and 
issuing report.   
 
The PSSR Team Leader shall be a subject matter expert having more than 10 years of experience in 
Projects/Operations and shall be independent from the Project/ MOC/ Turnaround Team.  
 
The PSSR Team Leader can either be a Third Party Consultant or selected from the technical expertise 
available within ADNOC Group.   
 
The responsibilities of a PSSR Team Leader shall include: 
 
(i) 
Determine the type of PSSR to be performed; 
(ii) 
Verifying that the scope of the PSSR adequately covers all requirements; 
(iii) 
Deciding to either start the PSSR study as scheduled or delay it if all of the information is 
not available; 
(iv) 
Assigning tasks to PSSR team members for document review and field verification; and 
(v) 
Ensuring timely completion of PSSR and issuing report. 
7.3.2. PSSR Team Members 
PSSR Team Members' responsibilities shall include: 
 
(i) 
Reviewing documents, records relevant to PSSR; 
(ii) 
Participating in all PSSR activities and providing necessary input; 
(iii) 
Reviewing PSSR draft report and providing comments, if any; and 
(iv) 
Ensuring timely completion of assigned actions, if any.

PSSR ACTION TRACKING 
Person in charge of the Project/MOC/Turnaround in the respective Company/Facility shall be responsible

implemented or resolved with an auditable, fully documented record of all actions taken, decisions and 
alternative solutions.

ADNOC will conduct compliance audit of this standard at approximately three-year intervals, These Audits 
will be in addition to GC’s internal audits and where required, ADNOC will take steps to minimise overlap 
and duplication between ADNOC Corporate and Group Companies internal audits. 
 
The main audit deliverable is a formal and structured report for the attention of GC Management and the 
respective ADNOC Directorate.
