1. 
INTRODUCTION 
This Standard sets out the minimum ADNOC requirements for HSE management in contracts. It identifies 
and describes the activities associated with the Management of Contractor HSE within the formal 
framework of a contract, both prior to and after award.  
 
Specific objectives, mandatory requirements for each phase are stated and associated appendices 
provide guidance for implementation at each contracting phase. 
 
Although the activities of Group Companies and some of their Contractors are carried out in diverse areas, 
some of which are remote, it is important that irrespective of location of its activities, ADNOC Group and 
its Contractors (while engaged in contractual obligation) have a Duty of Care to maintain the on-going 
health, safety and wellbeing of their employees including visitors. 
 
2. 
PURPOSE 
The purpose of this Standard is to: 
 
 
Define the minimum HSE objectives to be met at each stage of a contract; 
 
Develop a strategy for proactive management of Contractor HSE; 
 
Describe a planned approach to Management of Contractor HSE that will ensure a continual 
improvement in HSE performance for all Contractor activities and facilities; 
 
Describe the roles and responsibilities of key personnel in Contractor HSE Management; 
 
Provide means to monitor the implementation of HSE requirements; and  
 
Provide prescribed means to rectify contractor company HSE non-compliances or in extreme cases 
exercise intervention and stop the work if required. 
This Standard is targeted principally at Contract Administrators at ADNOC Group and Contractor 
personnel tasked with planning HSE requirements in contracts, tender preparation and evaluation, 
contract award and subsequent Contractor management.  
 
All of the requirements set out in this standard are mandatory and it is the responsibility of ADNOC Group 
senior management to ensure full implementation and ensure that the Contractors are aware and 
compliant to these requirements. Any deviation requests shall be formally raised and decided upon by 
ADNOC HSE Function. 
 
3. 
SCOPE 
This document stipulates the mandatory requirements applicable to ADNOC Group and its Contractors.  
 
ADNOC Group and Contractors shall ensure that all expectations listed herein are fully understood, 
implemented and thoroughly monitored. 
 
This Standard is applicable to Health, Safety, and Environmental aspects for all types and forms of 
contract services within the ADNOC Group.

7.1.2. CONTRACTOR HSEMS 
Contractors are a key resource that increases the organization’s capability when deployed to provide 
services to ADNOC Group. As such, they shall demonstrate that they have the necessary organization, 
values, leadership, culture, resources, capability, communication etc., to undertake the scope of work in 
a safe, sustainable and responsible manner, and to identify and mitigate all the associated risks. 
 
Contractors shall have an HSE Management System (HSEMS) that aligns with international standard 
while meeting ADNOC HSE requirements. It is preferred that Contractors have the ISO certifications such 
as ISO 45001, ISO 14001 as part of their HSE Management System. However, contractor HSE 
Management System shall be sufficient to provide adequate controls and barriers for the risk profile of 
the project and work being carried out. Refer to ADNOC HSE Management System (HSE-GA-ST02). 
 
Where a Contractor intends to subcontract, the Contractor shall have a demonstrable robust 
subcontractor HSE Management System, associated programme in place, and implemented. 
 
Service contracts are often used as a means of executing work. Initially such contracts often have no 
defined scope beyond a general description of the services, skills, plant, etc. that the Contractor shall be 
able to provide. However, the ability of the Contractor to manage HSE on a job-by-job basis is an essential 
requirement. For example, the Contractor shall be able to make hazard identification and risk assessment, 
apply and enforce controls to manage residual HSE risks and effects, apply local HSE legislation correctly, 
respond effectively to emergency situations, etc. These abilities shall be demonstrated prior to contract 
award. 
 
Bridging/Interface documents shall be developed when all or part of the scope of work is to be performed 
using the Contractor’s HSE Management System on the basis that it meets the ADNOC Group 
requirements. The HSE interface information shall provide a list of the Contractor’s specific HSE policies, 
processes and practices that the Contractor proposes to follow during the work activity. The benefit of 
this approach is to allow the Contractor’s staff to follow their own HSE Management System and 
associated processes and practices without having to change their approach to work with contract 
changes. 
 
7.1.3. CONTRACTOR HSE PLAN 
Contractor HSE Plan shall be updated as required and approved by Company for the execution stage. 
Refer to ADNOC Project HSE Plan Standard. [Ref. 2] for expectations on HSE Plan 
 
The HSE Plan shall demonstrate how: 
 
the Contractor and Subcontractor (where applicable) has an effective HSE Management System 
applicable to the specific work contracted, that is appropriate to the complexity and the phase of 
the contract execution 
 
risks, impacts or threats related to occupational health and safety, environmental and social 
responsibility, process safety, quality and security associated with the contracted work have been 
identified, assessed and shall be controlled by the implementation of control measures where and 
when required 
 
the responsibilities for the execution and maintenance of all control and recovery measures relating 
to the contracted work are assigned to specific, named persons throughout the life cycle of the 
contract, and  
 
risks have been evaluated and measures taken to eliminate or reduce the risks to an acceptable 
level that is As Low As Reasonably Practicable (ALARP).

7.7.3. APPLICATION OF HSE PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT MECHANISM 
Contractor HSE Performance Management Mechanism applies to contractors executing their contractual 
scope of work within the ADNOC Group controlled sites.  
 
The Contractor HSE performance Management Mechanism does not apply to contractors who do not 
carry out any work activities within ADNOC Group controlled sites i.e. all activities for the contract are 
completed remotely from ADNOC Group location and then the deliverable is provided to ADNOC Group 
for approval.  
 
7.7.4. COMPETENCE ASSURANCE 
During execution of the work, the ADNOC Group shall monitor the competence assurance, associated 
training programme of the Contractor, and ensure that it meets the requirements of the HSE Plan. Where 
necessary the ADNOC Group shall also determine any additional needs, which may occur because of 
local circumstances. Monitoring by the ADNOC Group shall include confirmation of the Contractor's 
commitment to the following: 
 
 
Ensure Contractor Competence Assurance System (CAS) is implemented, in particular verifying 
Contractor assurance systems are working effectively and that the process is robust; 
 
Adherence to the Contractor's personnel recruitment criteria; 
 
Demonstration of personnel qualifications, ability and skills; 
 
Provision of the necessary induction program; 
 
Training of Contractor's personnel in the standards and procedures to be followed; 
 
Completion of the necessary HSE and job-related training; 
 
Provision where appropriate for Contractor's personnel to attend on-going HSE courses (preferably 
as mixed groups of ADNOC Group and Contractor staff); and 
 
Availability of HSE documents, instruction and information leaflets and newsletters with special 
attention to use of local language and simple visual messages. 
 
7.7.5. MEDICAL FITNESS / FITNESS TO WORK  
Contractor shall ensure that all personnel engaged in the WORK are medically fit for the job to be 
undertaken.  
 
Contractor shall be able to demonstrate that personnel, selected for positions for which Fitness to Work 
medical evaluation is required, follow the guidance as laid down in ADNOC Group Medical Fitness 
Assessment Guidelines.  
If in the opinion of ADNOC and reflected in relevant medical report, any personnel are not medically fit to 
work, then ADNOC reserves the right to refuse permission for that personnel 
 
7.7.6. SUPERVISION 
ADNOC Group HSE matters shall be visibly demonstrated in contract execution by providing an ADNOC 
Group representative(s) from the line management to fulfil the HSE obligations of the ADNOC Group as

ADNOC Group Contract Administrator or Representative(s) may seek advice, support or services from 
an ADNOC Group HSE representative on a periodic basis but the responsibility for HSE matters on the 
contract shall remain with the assigned Contract Administrator or representative(s).

7.7.7. HSE INSPECTION, AUDITS AND REVIEWS 
Inspection and audit provide the methods for monitoring Contractor HSE activities. Regular inspections 
provide the day-to-day means of checking compliance with contract requirements. Audit provides the 
more formal and comprehensive assessments of adherence to the written framework of the contract (the 
Contractor's HSE Plan). ADNOC HSE Audit & Assurance Standard (HSE-GA-ST09) shall be 
implemented as applicable for HSEMS Audit Process. 
 
The Contractor will have a defined audit/review program as part of their HSE Plan for work including that 
of their Subcontractors. The documented evidence of these audits shall be available to the ADNOC Group 
together with the follow-up action taken. 
 
Inspections and Audits shall cover aspects such as: 
 
 
Compliance with Work Management System (WMS)/PTW system; 
 
Operational condition of HSE equipment /HSECES criticality and category; 
 
HSE features on plant and equipment; 
 
Reliability, serviceability, availability and maintenance of work tools and equipment; 
 
Emergency and contingency plan readiness and drills including use of fire-fighting equipment and 
first aid; 
 
Availability and correct use of protective equipment; 
 
Employee HSE awareness and demonstrated use of safe work practices; and 
 
Any other required areas. 
The effectiveness of inspections and audits can often be enhanced by the use of unannounced spot 
checks and involvement of the Contractor's responsible supervisory personnel. 
 
Inspection and audit are the primary responsibility of the Contractor. However, ADNOC Group 
supervisory staff shall be tasked to monitor the implementation of the Contractor's HSE Plan and ensure 
that the Contractor's systems are in place.  
 
Auditing practices may be used to examine specific technical areas in more detail or to provide in-depth 
HSE management assessments (e.g. the Contractor's internal HSE controls).
