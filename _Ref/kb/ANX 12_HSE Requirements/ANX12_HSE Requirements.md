BASIC REQUIREMENTS FOR HSE IN DESIGN 
The basic design provided with the Scope of Work includes various safety features. CONTRACTOR shall 
ensure that these features are maintained and improved upon during the detailed design phase in 
compliance with the Design HSE Philosophy. 
Contractor to comply with applicable ADNOC HSE Standard, Project Specification and applicable 
international standards as defined in the AGREEMENT.

stages and CONTRACTOR shall execute these studies. 
2.0 
BASIC REQUIREMENTS FOR CONTROL OF POLLUTION 
The design of the PROJECT must be on minimum pollution basis and include all the features required to 
ensure that control of all forms of pollution is of the highest standard and will comply with COMPANY, 
Federal and Local Governmental Authorities requirements. 
CONTRACTOR shall ensure that the pollution control standards provided in the basic design are 
maintained or improved during the detailed design phase. Air Quality Standard, GHG and Emission limits 
can be found in ADNOC Environmental standards and design HSE Philosophy. 
Hydrocarbons or odorous substances shall not vent to atmosphere during normal operation. 
CONTRACTOR shall follow the requirements as listed / provided in the below mentioned documents, in 
their latest Revision, or elsewhere in the AGREEMENT.

HAZID/ENVID/OHID

5.3 
NOC FROM ABUDHABI ENVIRONMENT AGENCY (EAD) 
CONTRACTOR shall note the following process with regard to obtaining No Objection Certificate from 
Abu Dhabi Environment Agency (EAD) for compliance. 
1. CONTRACTOR shall prepare EIA Terms of Reference (TOR) and shall be submitted 
to COMPANY Project Manager who shall forward to COMPANY’s HSE Division, who 
shall review and forward to ADNOC for approval. 
2. Upon receipt of TOR approval, CONTRACTOR shall prepare an EIA Report in the 
EAD Format, utilizing the EAD approved agencies listed in enclosed Attachment-E. 
3. The CONTRACTOR shall submit an EIA Report as part of HSEIA Report for 
COMPANY’s HSE Division review and comment. 
4. CONTRACTOR shall incorporate comments, if any, and update the HSEIA report 
and resubmit to COMPANY. COMPANY’s HSE Division will submit the updated 
HSEIA Report to ADNOC and EIA Report to EAD with ADNOC cover letter to obtain 
their NOC.  
5. NOC permit from EAD will be followed up and received directly by COMPANY’s HSE 
Division. Once the NOC is received by COMPANY’s HSE Division, the same shall be 
issued to the CONTRACTOR by COMPANY.
