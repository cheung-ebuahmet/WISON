3. The inclusion of a consultancy office in the list does not indicate an automatic approval of the studies submitted. Rather, it shows that the listed office possesses the minimum knowl

4. Project designers and project managers are prohibited from rendering environmental consulting services to the projects they are designing or managing.
5. It is the responsibility of the owner and the project manager to ensure that the consultancy offices are registered in the approperiate fields of work.
