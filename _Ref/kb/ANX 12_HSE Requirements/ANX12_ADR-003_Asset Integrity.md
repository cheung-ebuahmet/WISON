Contractor shall note that HSECES management standard HSE-OS-ST29 has been superseded 
with GP&E-GAIPS/STD-07 (ADNOC Group HSECES Management standard).  
In addition to GP&E-GAIPS/STD-07, Contractor shall comply with the requirements specified in 
Integrity management standard (AGAI-STD-0002) and Asset Integrity for Projects AGAI-GDL-
0029 (as applicable). 
Accordingly, and in line with GP&E-GAIPS/STD-07, Contractor shall note that Technical Integrity 
Verification (TIV) shall be conducted for all identified HSECES. This activity shall be performed 
by appointing an independent Verification Body (IVB).  
IVB will be appointed by the COMPANY. 
IVB scope of work detailing TIV requirements shall be prepared by Contractor.
