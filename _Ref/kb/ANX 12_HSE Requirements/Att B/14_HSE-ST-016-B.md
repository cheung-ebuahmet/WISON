RED 
ZONE
RED ZONES are the areas where potential exists for exposure to such high concentrations of 
H2S that fatality may occur despite relatively short exposure (minutes) due to insufficient 
time to protect (i.e. don SCBA) when H2S is released. 
Toxic LSIR Level is 1E-3 per year.
AMBER 
ZONE
An Amber zone is considered to exist where persons affected would have time to don PPE 
(other than interfering with process equipment with H2S content) which is carried with them 
and ready for use, but would not have time to reach nearest PPE location.
Toxic LSIR Level is 1E-4 per year. 
YELLOW 
ZONE
Yellow Zones are the areas where the risks of fatal concentrations of H2S are low enough, so 
that personnel can reach to designated Emergency Escape Breathing Device (EEBD) 
locations and don the mask before being overcome. 
Toxic LSIR level is 1E-5 per year.
GREEN 
ZONE
Green Zones are the areas outside the Yellow zone where personnel would be able to safely 
evacuate in the event of an accidental release without the need for PPE, but where exposure 
above the limit is possible. 
Toxic LSIR level is 1E-6.
