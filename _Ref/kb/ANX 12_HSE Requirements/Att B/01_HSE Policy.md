HEALTH, SAFETY & ENVIRONMENT (HSE) POLICY

The Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) and its Group of 
Companies (together the ADNOC Group) are committed to the 
highest standards of HSE excellence by protecting our people, 
communities and the environment; ensuring effective security 
and business continuity measures; and following sustainable 
business practices.
The ADNOC Group is committed to:
1	
Pursuing the goal of no harm to people and the community.
2	
Protecting the environment and supporting the UAE’s 
commitment to address the global challenge of climate change.
3	
Implementing cost-effective measures to improve energy 
efficiency and promote the use of renewable energy.
4	
Ensuring compliance with all applicable laws, regulations, 
policies and procedures relating to HSE.
5	
Having world-class emergency response, crisis 
management and business continuity measures in place.
6	
Managing risks through effective controls and minimizing 
impacts to our businesses.
7	
Focusing on incident prevention by managing process 
safety and the integrity of our assets.
8	
Promoting sustainability, corporate social responsibility 
and the welfare of our workforce.
9	
Embedding a 100% HSE culture that empowers
employees and contractors to intervene and stop
any unsafe work. 
10	 Developing and sustaining HSE critical competencies
in our employees. 
11	 Holding all levels of management, supervisors,
and employees accountable for HSE performance.
12	 Setting targets for HSE performance and
continuous improvement.
13	 Engaging business partners who are also committed
to 100% HSE.
The ADNOC Group will implement this Policy through a 
comprehensive HSE management system.
