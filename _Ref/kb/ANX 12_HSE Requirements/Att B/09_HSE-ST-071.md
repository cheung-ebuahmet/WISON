2. 
PURPOSE 
The purpose of this Standard is define minimum mandatory requirements with respect to the 
accommodation facilities, welfare arrangements and general working conditions of Workers 
engaged in GASCO Projects and residing in the permanent, semi-permanent or temporary 
accommodations provided by  Contractors & their Sub-Contractors. 
3. 
SCOPE AND APPLICATION  
This standard applies to all Contractors and their Sub-Contractors who are contracted by 
GASCO to carry out works under Major or Minor Projects; shutdowns and maintenance 
activities at sites, pipelines or residential areas. 
4. 
DOCUMENT CHANGE / CONTROL 
The custodian of this document is GASCO Vice President HSE Division [VP (HSE)]. 
This document shall be reviewed by HSE Division as and when required but not less than once 
in three years.  
For suggesting any changes in this document, the ‘Document Change/ Initiation Request’ 
(DC/IR) form should be completed by the concerned party and provided to HSE Division. 
Amendment of document shall be agreed by VP (HSE). 
5. 
WELFARE MANAGEMENT PLAN 
A detailed ‘Welfare Management Plan’ proposed for implementation, shall be submitted to 
GASCO for approval by all EPC Bidders and Contractors prior to Contract award. The Welfare 
management Plan shall include as a minimum the following: 
 
A written Welfare policy statement signed by Contractor management demonstrating the 
commitment to worker Welfare and compliance with UAE Law with regard Pay, overtime 
rates, leave entitlement, rest days, working hours and overtime. 
 
An employment policy stipulating procedures to regulate employer-employee industrial  
relationship; 
 
Detailed Camp Facilities Health/Hygiene, Safety, Environment and Security Management 
Plan; 
 
Detailed plan outlining and describing actions to provide and to maintain quality 
accommodation standard, as well as recreational, medical, psychosocial and nutritional 
elements, in line with GASCO and ADNOC requirements; 
 
Detailed plan outlining and describing the provision and maintenance of transportation for 
workers in  compliance with GASCO Standards (HSE-ST-006) and approved Contractor 
Journey Management Plan; 
 
It shall be ensured that the submitted plan is assessed against the GASCO Standards, Project 
Contractual requirements, applicable UAE Laws and ADNOC COPs & Group Guidelines.

7. 
WELFARE ORIENTATION  
All workers shall be provided with a proper welfare orientation at their initial arrival by the 
welfare management team of the Camp facilities.  
The orientation shall be based on the theme ‘Your Rights and Responsibilities’. Records of 
orientation shall be maintained in the camp welfare management office. The 
orientation/induction program shall be submitted to GASCO for review. 
8. 
CAMP WELFARE COMMITTEE 
A Workers’ welfare committee consisting of representatives from the GASCO PMT, Camp 
Welfare Management Team and workers representatives shall be nominated. The workers 
members shall represent all nationalities residing in the camp.  
The Welfare Committee is responsible for protecting the interest of Workers and resolving 
welfare issues  
8.1 
Terms of Reference of Welfare Committee 
1. To conduct camp visits, including a site review of the Contractor’s facilities, to ensure that 
the right services are provided. 
2. To investigate camp incidents and issues. 
3. To provide leadership and guidance on recreation activities 
4. To hold Monthly meetings to discuss and resolve all issues and ensure that corrective 
actions have been taken. 
The minutes of meetings shall be recorded and records maintained in the Camp welfare 
management office with copies to the attendees, senior contractor management and 
Camp HSES team.

10. WELFARE – GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 
Contractors and Sub-Contractors shall treat Workers on the basis of the following principles 
for maintaining high welfare standards: 
 
Ensure that Workers are informed about their rights and their entitlements under the  UAE 
Law, ADNOC CoPs, AGGs as referred to in this Standard; 
 
Provide initial and periodic medical exams at no cost to all employees  
 
Exert the due diligence to ensure that the dignity of Workers is protected and preserved 
throughout their recruitment, placement , work and return; 
 
Do not allow or tolerate any forced, compulsory labor, child , human trafficking practices, 
or other violations of labor rights; 
 
Comply with fair employment practices and standards for all Workers; 
 
Create a safe, healthy and worker-friendly working environment; 
 
Create a decent, clean, safe, healthy and quality living environment; 
 
Treat all Workers equally and fairly, irrespective of their nationality, gender, ethnic origin, 
race or religion; 
 
Ensure that Workers receive equal pay for equal work; 
 
Do not allow or tolerate employment of persons who are underage (Child Labour) as per 
UAE Labor Law; 
 
Do not allow or tolerate retaliation against Workers for exercising any of their rights under 
the Law including raising any form of grievance, deciding to resign or refusing to perform 
work that poses a safety risk.

11.1 Workers’ Salary Payments 
All workers shall be paid with their basic due salaries, cash allowances and other entitlements 
without delay. The basic salary and cash allowances which are payable to the workers shall 
exclude the payment or provision of other entitlements such as overtime, air tickets, leave 
salary, medical cover and end of service benefits, which shall be provided in addition to the 
basic salary and cash allowances. Any deductions from workers’ salaries or allowances shall be 
strictly in accordance with U.A.E labor law. No deductions shall be made from the monthly 
salaries or allowances of its personnel on account of food or accommodation for weekly off-
days/rest days/holidays/annual leave given to its personnel. Any charges required for the 
maintenance of salary accounts of workers shall be paid by the Contractor and/or their sub-
contractors.

11.2 Overtime Payments (part of worker’s salary payments) 
All "Overtime" dues shall be paid to the workers along with their due monthly salary. Payment 
for "Overtime" shall be in accordance with the U.A.E. Labor Law. During the holy month of 
Ramadan, the working hours shall be reduced to six (6) hours per day. However, all workers 
shall continue to be paid their salaries based upon the normal working hours, that is, eight (8) 
hours per day.  
12. END OF SERVICE BENEFITS 
The end of service benefit shall be calculated in accordance with the U.A.E. Labor Law and paid 
separately to all workers within fifteen calendar days from end of their service or end of 
assignment of the Project. 
A proof of payment shall be issued for the end of service benefits all workers for justification 
of actual payments made. 
13. LEAVES, WORK ROTATIONS, AND TIME-OFF 
Allowance shall be made for replacement of staff during leaves, work rotation and time-off, on 
one to one basis, of equal status and grade, in order to continue the work without interruption 
or disruption. Workers shall not be transferred or removed without prior written approval of 
GASCO Project Management. The C.Vs of such replacement staff shall be submitted for 
GASCO’s review and approval, in such cases. Leaves, work rotation and time off schedules of 
all workers shall be planned in such a way that personnel going on leave, work rotation or 
taking time off is released only upon arrival of their leave relief at work site. A leave, work 
rotation and time-off schedule shall be provided to GASCO site representative upon official 
request. 
14. LEAVE RETURN AIR TICKET  
Leave entitlement shall be taken annually. All workers of different categories shall be provided 
with annual return air ticket. Air tickets shall be provided to nearest airport in base country. 
 Air tickets for rotation work cycle shall be as per Contractor Procedure and in line with UAE 
regulations

16. MEDICAL EMERGENCIES, FIRST AID FACILITIES AND TRAINING 
First aid and first aid facilities, for all personnel employed, shall be provided in the camp 
location. Such facilities shall conform to the laws and regulations of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi 
and the U.A.E and requirements of ADNOC and GASCO. 
If the number of Camp residents is more than 100 persons a medical clinic with qualified nurse 
shall be provided 
If the ADNOC clinic or hospitals are located more than 10 km from the camp an ambulance 
must be provided. 
16.1 Medical Emergency Plan  
A medical emergency plan shall be prepared and incorporated in the ‘welfare management 
plan’. This plan shall specify means of implementing applicable requirements including contact 
details of medical emergency services, communication and transportation needs.  
16.2 Mutual-Aid Agreement  
A mutual-aid agreement may with the near-by resident companies in order to seek support 
during a medical emergency. 
16.3 First Aid and Medical Resources 
Adequate facilities (first aid and camp clinic) and trained personnel with all necessary 
resources shall be available for implementation of the medical emergency response plan. 
Doctors and/or male nurses, depending upon the capacity of camp residents, shall be provided 
and they shall be licensed as per HAAD (Health Authority of Abu Dhabi) requirements. At least 
10% of all camp residents shall be trained in basic first aid requirements from an approved 
third-party. 
16.4 Medical Wastes 
Medical wastes generated at the camp is considered hazardous and  shall be segregated, 
collected and disposed according to the UAE Legal, ADNOC (COPV2-05) and GASCO waste 
management requirements. 
17. TRANSPORTATION OF CONTRACTOR’S PERSONNEL 
Suitable transportation for the workers, for the purpose of performing the works and services, 
from the camp to respective field/work areas, office buildings or workshops and back to the 
camp shall be provided. It shall be ensured that all personnel are transported back to their 
camp at the end of their daily duties, after normal and overtime hours, safely and without any 
delay. For personnel working in remote areas, commuting busses shall be provided to allow 
visiting Medina Zayed, Abu Dhabi or Dubai over the weekend at least once per month, if 
desired by Camp Residents. 
17.1 Condition of the Vehicles and Driver’s Requirements  
All vehicles shall be safe, in good condition, fit for the weather and shall be in proper air 
conditioned buses, with an "In Vehicle Monitoring Device" (IVMD) installed and of the kind 
that meets the Personnel Transportation Regulations of U.A.E. All vehicles shall be properly 
maintained and tires shall be changed as per GASCO/ADNOC standards.

17.2 Construction of Buildings 
All structures shall be of sound construction and of suitable materials, secure, safe and 
weatherproof. Internally, walls and ceilings shall be constructed of durable materials and shall 
have lightly colored, smooth, and easy to clean surfaces. The walls to a height of 1.2m in toilet 
rooms and 1.8m in shower rooms shall be impervious to water. The floors of service buildings, 
shower/toilet blocks etc. shall have a smooth but nonslip, durable and easy to clean surfaces. 
All openings to the outside shall have tight - fitting, self-closing doors and glazed windows. Fly-
screen doors may be fitted to kitchen doors or to doors which are used frequently to prevent 
the entrance of insects and rodents. No asbestos materials or paints containing lead shall be 
utilized in the building construction. All sites used for camps shall be adequately drained. They 
shall not be subject to periodic flooding, nor located to surface collection of water. All sites 
shall be adequate in size to prevent overcrowding of structures. 
All requirements of UAE legislative bodies, ADNOC regulations (CoPs and Group Guidelines) 
and GASCO contractual requirements shall be complied with.  
18. ACCOMMODATION  
Shared rooms of Contractors and Sub-Contractors shall allow a maximum of two 
craftsmen/supervisors per room and three laborers/helpers per room space. Furniture and 
other amenities shall comply with Company/ADNOC requirements. Floor space in sleeping 
rooms shall be not less than 4.5 m2 per occupant, calculated only where the ceiling height 
exceeds 2.1m. Requirements of ADNOC CoPV1-10 (food, water safety and welfare) shall be 
followed. 
Contractor Shall Comply with Labor Accommodation Federal Law  (2009) and Abu Dhabi EHS 
Codes for labour camp (2012)

19. VENTILATION/TEMPERATURE  
The temperature in all habitable and other rooms, in which personnel live and work, shall be 
maintained at a level conducive to the healthy performance of the activity conducted in the 
area. Air-conditioning units and heating units for use during summers and winters shall be 
provided in all rooms throughout the camp for all residents. All habitable rooms shall be 
ventilated by means of natural or mechanical ventilation. All bathrooms, toilet rooms, laundry 
rooms and similar areas shall be provided with an exhaust ventilation system connected 
directly to the outside of the building.

21. WATER SUPPLY 
An adequate supply of potable water shall be provided to the camp, which shall conform to 
GASCO/ADNOC Drinking Water Quality Standards. Potable water shall be distributed in a 
properly designed and constructed closed system. Potable water, including water for bathing 
shall be chlorinated to provide a free chlorine residual of at least 0.2 mg/l (ppm) at the 
furthest point of use. Transported water shall be obtained from an acceptable ADNOC/GASCO 
approved source; delivered in a properly designed water tanker used only for this purpose; 
stored and distributed in an approved manner; and containing a free chlorine residual of 0.5 
mg/l (ppm). A non-potable water system is permitted only for purposes such as air 
conditioning and fire protection. Water should be tested at least once a month with samples 
sent to ADNOC’s Occupational Health Laboratory. Note: non-potable water shall not be used 
for washing or bathing. Outlets of non-potable water shall be identified accordingly.

22. TOILET AND WASHING FACILITIES 
Toilet and shower rooms shall be conveniently located at a distance of not more than 120m 
from the furthest habitable room and shall be accessible at all times. Toilet and shower 
facilities shall be arranged to ensure privacy for the user. They and the rooms containing them 
shall be ventilated and lit and kept in a clean and orderly condition. Electrical bonding of water 
pipes in shower rooms and toilet facilities is required. The minimum number of wash hand 
basins required is 1 per 10 residents up to 100, and 1 per 15 thereafter. Washing facilities shall 
be provided in the immediate vicinity of every sanitary convenience, whether or not provided 
elsewhere. Mirrors and adequate lighting shall be provided near wash hand basins. The 
number of toilets and showers required is a minimum of 1 of each per 10 camp residents. The 
number of urinals required is one per 25 camp residents. Liquid soap, hand dryers or 
disposable hand towels shall be provided and replenished regularly. Hot and cold or a 
tempered water supply shall be provided to all wash hand basins and showers. 
23. NOISE 
Noise levels shall be monitored and controlled. Compliance to the requirements of noise levels 
throughout the camps shall be maximum 50 Decibels in rooms and shall comply with the 
requirements of ADNOC CoP V1-10. 
24. SEWAGE DISPOSAL 
Sewerage disposal systems will be designed, sized and installed to carry all wash and 
wastewater, sewage etc. Where a public sewer system is available, all plumbing fixtures & 
sewers shall be connected to it. Sewage shall be collected and treated prior to disposal. 
Effluent shall comply with the ADNOC and GASCO Standards for Emissions into the 
Environment. In no case shall sewage or liquid waste of any type be discharged until it has 
been properly treated. If conditions are not suitable for provisions of a treatment system, a 
holding tank (cesspool) may be used with the effluent being trucked to an approved sewage 
treatment plant. Leaking cesspools shall not be permitted. Emptied excess water to the desert 
from these cesspools shall not be permitted. Where a treatment system cannot be provided, 
mobile treatment facilities shall be utilized even in the case of temporary camps.

25.1 Communication and Internet 
Access to public communication facilities shall be available at all time within the camp. 
Internet facility shall be provided for all workers. Internet café /clusters shall be provided 
suitable to the number of residents. 
A direct line telephone shall be available at all times for calling the emergency services. This 
facility must be centrally located, conspicuous and all personnel on site are to be notified of 
the facility. An information sheet (in all appropriate languages) with all emergency contact 
numbers is to be positioned at each phone location. 
25.2 Prayer Facilities  
Ablution and prayer areas with related hygiene facilities shall be provided, suitable for the 
number of users.  
25.3 Recreation and Sports Facilities 
Spacious Recreation Halls with indoor games, large TV screens and access to internet at the 
Camp shall be provided. One or more gymnasiums depending on the number of residents at 
Camp shall be provided. Common field sport facilities covering football & cricket, volleyball 
and basketball, tennis court(s), badminton court(s) shall be provided for major projects. The 
number of such facilities should be based on the number of residents and as approved by 
GASCO. Sports facilities shall be provided. Such facilities shall be in usable state. Indoor 
exercise facilities with suitable sport equipment shall be provided for this purpose. 
25.4 Grocery 
A grocery store shall be provided inside the camp which shall cater to the needs of all camp 
residents and shall comply with the legal and regulatory requirements.  
25.5 Cash Withdrawal and Money Transfer (proposed for approval) 
Provision of Cash withdrawal facilities (ATM machine) at Camp in agreement with local banks 
shall be provided. International Money transfer facilities shall be provided in agreement with 
the available money transfer service providing companies. 
25.6 Laundry 
Laundry facilities shall be provided to wash residents’ clothes and their bed linens, the laundry 
to be equipped with sufficient washing and drying machines, suitable to accommodate the 
number of camp residents and free of charge. A minimum of twice a week laundry service to 
be provided for labors 
25.7 Barber Shop 
A barber shop shall also be provided for the camp residents and all requirements of Abu Dhabi 
Municipality, other requirements of UAE public health and ADNOC CoPV1-10 (food, water 
safety and welfare) shall be complied with.

26.1 Food Safety and Food Premises 
 
Compliance shall be exhibited to the relevant provisions related to Health and food hygiene 
for Catering contained in ADNOC Code of Practice on Occupational Health Risk Management – 
Food Safety and Welfare (ADNOC-COPV3-07) and ADNOC CoPV1-10 including but not limited 
to Food Hygiene Standards and Health Guidance for Catering. 
26.2 Food Handlers 
All food handlers must be in possession of a valid U.A.E. Government-issued Food Handler’s 
Certificate while on duty at any location, which shall be renewed periodically as per 
Government requirement.  A complete medical examination (including X-ray, laboratory tests) 
shall be carried out on all staff prior to arriving at site. The medical reports shall be submitted 
to GASCO Medical Department prior to arrival of personnel on site through GASCO 
representative.  The complete medical examination shall be repeated annually, and every six 
months for all food handlers, waiters and office boys providing tea, coffee and services. 
Catering staff, both food handlers and non-food handlers, who are returning to from home 
country leave, shall undergo routine medical examination prior to resumption of duties. No 
person shall work in a food establishment if he is infected with a communicable disease, is a 
carrier of organisms that can cause a communicable disease, is afflicted with a boil, has an 
infected cut or wound, or has an acute respiratory infection. The operator of a food 
establishment shall provide an on-going Food Safety Training Program for all employees. Each 
of the food handlers shall be provided with at least two uniforms, aprons, hair net (caps as 
second alternative) and non-slip foot wears appropriate to the hazards. 
26.3 Food Handling and Quality 
At all times during storage, preparation, display, transportation and serving, food shall be 
protected from contamination. Food must be prepared, displayed and served with the least 
possible manual contact, with suitable utensils and on surfaces that have been cleaned and 
sanitized prior to use. Raw vegetables shall be washed before being cooked.  Those served raw 
shall be sanitized in a solution containing 50-100 mg/1 (50-100ppm) chlorine with a minimum 
contact time of 10 minutes. The temperature of potentially perishable foods shall be 5°C or 
below when refrigerated, -18°C or below when frozen and 63°C or above when in hot storage. 
Wherever possible, perishable foods shall be thawed in refrigerated units so that the 
temperature of the food does not exceed 5°C. 
26.4 Equipment and Surfaces 
Equipment, utensils and food contact surfaces shall be made of and repaired with safe 
materials.  They shall be corrosion resistant, non-absorbent, smooth, easily cleanable and 
durable. Floor mounted equipment, unless readily movable shall be sealed to the floor or 
installed on raised platforms to facilitate cleaning. Kitchenware and food contact surfaces shall

26.5 Equipment and Utensil Washing 
A sink shall be used to manually clean and sanitize submersible equipment, or parts thereof

26.6 Sanitary Conveniences and Washing Facilities 
Suitable and sufficient wash hand basins shall be located to permit convenient use by all 
employees. Sinks used for food preparation should not be used for hand washing. Each wash 
hand basin shall be provided with hot and cold running water, suitable means of cleaning such 
as soap/detergent and suitable means of drying such as disposable towels. Sanitary 
conveniences shall be appropriately located, of sufficient number and accessible at all times.  
They shall be kept in a clean and orderly condition.  Rooms containing them must be 
sufficiently lit and ventilated to the outside. No food, equipment or utensils shall be stored in 
sanitary conveniences or washing facilities. 
26.7 Smoking 
Food handlers shall not engage in smoking while on duty, and smoking is prohibited in all food 
rooms. Rest areas may be provided for the benefit of smokers, but must take into 
consideration suitable arrangements to protect non-smokers from discomfort caused by 
passive smoking. Smoking is prohibited in restaurants and food serving areas. 
26.8 Food Safety Inspections and Audits 
Contractor shall ensure that monthly food safety inspections and quarterly in-house audits are 
conducted and recorded. GASCO shall carry out food safety audits in all camps and share the 
findings with the concerned for maintenance of high and uniform food safety standard across 
all accommodation camps. 
27. GENERAL REQUIREMENTS 
No cooking shall be undertaken in habitable rooms or rooms immediately adjacent to any 
habitable rooms. Floors, hallways, walls, ceilings etc. shall be maintained in a sanitary 
condition. Each room shall be cleaned after each change of occupancy. Common areas shall be 
cleaned daily. Hallways, entrances to fire escapes and stairways must be kept free of 
obstructions.

29. LPG STORAGE, SUPPLY AND DISTRIBUTION 
In all camps, new or existing, Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) Storage tank with adequate safety 
and firefighting protection   shall be provided. Where existing camps have cylinders located in 
an internal store, bottom and top ventilation is to be provided. Where there is an existing 
internal store, the cylinder store shall be accessible direct from the outside without traveling 
through an internal room. It is essential that the internal wall (adjoining the remainder of the 
building) of the store is fire resistant to not less than 30 minutes standard (BS 467).  
Ventilation of the storage area shall be provided at high and low level. Ventilation shall be 
directly to the outside. 
A notice or the symbol for a gas container locker should be permanently fixed to the outside of 
the store indicating the presence of gas cylinders (Sign to comply with BS 5378). The storage of 
full and empty cylinders is to be separate. 
The gas connection from supply to the unit e.g. cooker; the length of the flexible hose must 
not exceed 1 meter. It is also recommended to use steel armored type hose to reduce the risk 
of mechanical damage. In addition to the main control valve at the cylinder, each appliance 
and gas burner fitting must be provided with a shut off tap. A shut-off valve must be provided 
at the point of entry of the gas pipe into the building.  
Gas detection and alarm system shall be provided in the storage area and the kitchens. 
Gas installations shall be inspected on a monthly basis paying particular attention to cylinder 
storage and flexible hoses. The camp manager shall keep records of these inspections and 
available at all times for inspection by Company.

30. FUEL STORAGE  
All fuel storage containers or vessels shall be remote from residential areas. Tanks at risk from 
lightning strike shall be suitably protected by means of earthing/grounding systems. Warning 
signs (including no smoking, no mobiles etc.) relative to the risk shall be provided.  
Portable cans used for the storage of petroleum products shall be kept in a suitable location 
remote from other flammable liquids, gases or solids.  
The delivery valves of tanks shall be in good working order and leaks should be prevented. The 
storage tanks shall be installed inside containment bunds which shall be designed to contain 
110% of the total volume of the tanks. A metal drip tray is to be provided underneath the 
filling points.  
Where a fuel tank is part of an indirect supply system (e.g. to a generator), there should be a 
means of isolating the fuel from the system. The valve must be clearly marked and indicated. 
Firefighting appliances shall be provided in suitable and sufficient numbers/types. Where tank 
supplies fuels to an indoor facility then a fire break wall 60 minutes is to be provided between 
the tank and the room. 
All the design, supply and erection requirements of ADNOC, GASCO and Civil defense shall be 
complied with. 
31. GARBAGE/REFUSE 
UAE Legislative, ADNOC (CoP V2-05 on Waste Management) and GASCO Waste Management 
hierarchy of controls shall be applied. Garbage/refuse storage rooms, areas or enclosures shall 
be large enough to store the garbage/refuse containers that accumulate between disposal 
periods. The room shall be insect-and rodent-proof and shall be cleaned daily and maintained 
in good repair. All garbage/refuse containers shall be made of durable, non-absorbent, easily 
cleanable materials that are impervious to insects and rodents. They shall be designed and 
constructed so that they do not leak and shall be provided with tight-fitting covers that must 
be kept in the closed position when not in use.  
Smaller containers shall be lined with heavy-duty plastic bags to minimize cleaning 
requirements and facilitate easy removal of contents. Garbage and refuse shall be disposed 
frequently to prevent the development of odors and attraction of insects and rodents. 
Garbage and refuse generated by the residents of field areas must be disposed-off in the local 
municipality approved areas only. Disposal of garbage and refuse into an open area in the 
desert shall not be permitted. 
32. PEST CONTROL 
All buildings, structures and associated facilities shall be insect and rodent proof, free of 
vermin before occupancy and maintained free of insects and rodents. No person shall place, 
leave, dump or permit to accumulate garbage, refuse or litter in any building or on any 
premises or open site, in a manner that will afford food and harborage for insects and rodents. 
Regular fumigation shall be conducted by a local authorities approved fumigation services 
provider. Requirements of pest control as per ADNOC CoP V1-10 shall be complied with.
