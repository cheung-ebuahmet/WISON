3.2 Payment Deduction Towards Not Meeting the UAE National Numbers
In the event the Contractor fails to meet the required number of new UAE Nationals stipulated in Clause 2 of
Scope of Work at APPENDIX 6 of RFT / Clause 2.6 of the Scope of Work at ANNEXURE 3 of AGREEMENT,
ADNOC shall deduct the corresponding amounts from Contractor·s payments. The deductions shall be calculated
as under:
(a) For each headcount below the stipulated targets in Clause 2 of Scope of Work at APPENDIX 6 of RFT/
Clause 2.1 of the Scope of Work at ANNEXURE 3 of AGREEMENT, the deduction shall be at the rate
of AED 20,000/- per National per month. Such deductions shall commence after 3 months from the
Contract Effective Date.
(b) ADNOC shall review the Emirati headcounts on monthly basis (the Emirati headcount required as per
the Scope of Work versus actual headcount as per MOHRE Report in the month). In case of a negative
- variance i.e., the actual Emirati headcount as per MOHRE Report are less than the required headcount,
the amount shall be calculated and deducted from Contractor·s invoices.
