4. 
PRESERVATION OF RIGHTS 
4.1 
This GUARANTEE is irrevocable and unconditional and neither the obligations of the GUARANTOR 
under this GUARANTEE nor the rights, powers and remedies conferred upon the COMPANY by this 
GUARANTEE will be discharged, impaired or otherwise affected by: 
(a) 
any suspension, or variation to or amendment of the AGREEMENT, or any suspension, or 
variation to or amendment of any WORKS required to be performed under the AGREEMENT 
(including any extension of time for performance and adjustment to the amount payable to the 
CONTRACTOR; 
(b) 
the termination of the AGREEMENT; 
(c) 
any release, waiver or forbearance of any right of action or remedy the COMPANY may have 
against the CONTRACTOR, or neglect by the COMPANY in enforcing any right of action or 
remedy afforded under the AGREEMENT; 
(d) 
any bond, security or guarantee (other than this GUARANTEE) held or obtained by the 
COMPANY in respect of the obligations of the CONTRACTOR under the AGREEMENT, or 
any payment to the COMPANY under such bond, security or guarantee (to the extent such 
payment was not sufficient to fully discharge the CONTRACTOR’s OBLIGATIONS); 
(e) 
any of the obligations of the CONTRACTOR under the AGREEMENT being or becoming 
illegal, invalid, void, voidable, unenforceable or otherwise ineffective in any respect and for 
whatever reason; 
(f) 
any breach of the AGREEMENT by CONTRACTOR or the COMPANY or other default of the 
CONTRACTOR or the COMPANY; 
(g) 
the DISSOLUTION of the CONTRACTOR or any other PERSON;  
(h) 
any other act, event or omission which might operate to discharge, impair or otherwise affect 
any of the obligations of the GUARANTOR under this GUARANTEE or any of the rights, 
powers and remedies conferred upon the COMPANY by this GUARANTEE; or 
(i) 
any incapacity or lack of power, authority or legal personality, or change in the status, function, 
control or ownership of the CONTRACTOR.

4.2 
The COMPANY will not be obliged, before enforcing any of its rights or remedies conferred upon it by 
this GUARANTEE or by LAW, to: 
(a) 
grant any time or indulgence to the CONTRACTOR;

4.3 
If the COMPANY brings proceedings against the CONTRACTOR, the GUARANTOR will be bound 
by any findings of fact, interim or final award or judgment made by an arbitrator or arbitrators or the 
courts in such proceedings, and (if applicable) any settlement or agreement made between the

5. 
NO COMPETITION 
5.1 
Until all OBLIGATIONS and all obligations of the GUARANTOR under this GUARANTEE have been 
satisfied and performed in full, the GUARANTOR will not, as a result of this GUARANTEE or any 
payment or performance under this GUARANTEE, be subrogated to any right or security of the 
COMPANY or claim or prove in competition with the COMPANY against the CONTRACTOR or any 
other PERSON, or demand or accept repayment of any monies or claim any right of contribution, set-off 
or indemnity, and any sums received by the GUARANTOR in breach of this Clause 5.1 will be held by 
the GUARANTOR in trust for and must be promptly paid to the COMPANY. 
5.2 
The GUARANTOR will not hold any security from the CONTRACTOR in respect of this 
GUARANTEE and any such security which is held in breach of this Clause 5.2 must be held by the 
GUARANTOR in trust for and must promptly be transferred to the COMPANY. 
6. 
GUARANTOR’S OBLIGATIONS 
6.1 
The GUARANTOR’S obligations are: 
(a) 
in addition to and independent of any other security which the COMPANY may at any time 
hold in respect of any of the CONTRACTOR’s obligations under the AGREEMENT; and 
(b) 
continuing and, subject to Clause 2.1(b), will remain in full force and effect until all 
OBLIGATIONS and all obligations of the GUARANTOR required to be performed and 
observed under this GUARANTEE have been satisfied or performed in full. 
7. 
PAYMENT 
7.1 
All sums payable by the GUARANTOR under this GUARANTEE must be paid in full without any set-
off or counterclaim and free and clear of all deductions or withholdings for or on account of any present 
or future taxes, duties and/or other charges, other than those required by LAW.  If, at any time, any LAW 
requires the GUARANTOR to make any deduction or withholding from a sum payable under this 
GUARANTEE, the GUARANTOR must gross up the payment so that the net sum received by the 
COMPANY will equal the full amount that the COMPANY would have received if the payment had 
been made without such deduction or withholding. 
7.2 
Any demand sent by the COMPANY to the GUARANTOR which requires the payment of money under 
this GUARANTEE must specify the: 
(a) 
amount to be paid; 
(b) 
currency of payment; 
(c) 
mode of payment; and

GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION 
12.1 
This GUARANTEE shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of ABU DHABI

12.2 
Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this GUARANTEE shall be finally settled by arbitration

be made to such court for juridical recognition of the award and an order of enforcement, as the case may 
be.

instrument titled “Amendment to GUARANTEE”.

exclusive of any rights or remedies provided by LAW.

notice to the other PARTY of the possibility that any provision or part-provision of this GUARANTEE 
is illegal, invalid or unenforceable, the PARTIES shall within fourteen (14) DAYS negotiate in good 
faith to amend such provision so that, as amended, it is legal, valid and enforceable, and, to the greatest 
extent possible, achieves the intended commercial result of the original provision.   
13.5
