# Key Personnel — 关键人员

> **Topics:** Personnel | Sub-Clause 4.2 | Sub-Clause 4.3
> **Library ID:** CL-KP-001

---

## 条款概述

分包合同对关键人员（Key Personnel）施加了严格的全职驻场义务。未经承包商批准的任何离岗或替换均构成 **Material Breach**。

---

## 核心条款文本

### Sub-Clause 4.3.3 — 临时离岗通知义务

> If SUBCONTRACTOR'S PROJECT MANAGER is or any KEY PERSONNEL are to be temporarily absent from the PROJECT SITE during the execution of the WORK, CONTRACTOR shall be notified by SUBCONTRACTOR of such absence in writing and a suitable temporary replacement person shall be appointed by SUBCONTRACTOR, subject to the prior APPROVAL of CONTRACTOR.

**要点：** ① 书面通知 ② 临时替代人员 ③ 承包商事先批准。三项缺一不可。

### Sub-Clause 4.3.6 — 全职驻场义务

> All key personnel of the SUBCONTRACTOR, once approved by the CONTRACTOR, shall be assigned on a full-time basis to the PROJECT site for the entire duration of their respective assignments. No substitution, reassignment, or part-time engagement of such key personnel shall be made without the prior written approval of the CONTRACTOR.

### Sub-Clause 4.3.7 — 违规即根本违约

> Any absence, replacement, or substitution of the approved key personnel without prior written approval shall constitute a material breach of this SUBCONTRACT, and the CONTRACTOR shall be entitled to take appropriate actions, including but not limited to imposing penalties, withholding payments, or instructing the immediate removal of the non-compliant personnel.

### 附件5 Appendix — 关键人员名单

每份分包合同在 Attachment 5 的 Appendix [Key Personnel & Liquidated Damages] 中列明 7 个关键职位及各自违约金。
